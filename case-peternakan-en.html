From Ear Tag to Halal Certificate

Livestock operations face complexity across animal identity, pen capacity, feeding, health, and halal traceability. Xerpium ERP provides a unified platform to manage every stage from purchase to sale with immutable records and HPP.

  • Per-animal identification via ear tag, RFID, or QR code
  • Structured origin tracking from province, district, and source farm
  • Batch-level fattening periods with FCR, ADG, and mortality KPIs
  • Halal slaughter records and blockchain-ready certificates

Daily Feed, Weighing, and Health Control

Daily feeding records, automatic ADG calculation, and medical event tracking keep every animal productive and compliant.

  • Feed formulas with TMR, dry matter tracking, and cost per kg
  • Weighing records with auto ADG and duplicate prevention
  • Health events with medicine tracking and withdrawal dates
  • Daily auto-created SOP checklists per pen

Immutable Traceability and Automatic HPP

Immutable chain-of-custody records and automatic HPP computation turn livestock data into audit-ready financial control.

  • Append-only SHA-256 hashed trace events per animal
  • Halal slaughter checklist and blockchain certificate verification
  • Auto HPP per head and per kg from batch costs
  • Sales journal posting when animals are marked sold

Scale Multi-Location with AI and Consolidated Accounting

Xerpium supports multi-location farms with consolidated accounting, AI dashboards, and natural-language reporting for fast decisions.

  • Multi-branch cost allocation and consolidated profit/loss per batch
  • AI-generated dashboards and NL-to-SQL real-time queries
