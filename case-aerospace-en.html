Aerospace Manufacturing Demands End-to-End Control

Xerpium ERP centralizes aircraft type, configuration, and serialized part data, giving manufacturers one source of truth from design to delivery.

  • Manage fixed-wing, rotary-wing, UAV, and glider configurations as-designed, as-built, and as-maintained.
  • Track part numbers with ITAR/EAR controls, material specs, alternates, and drawing revisions.
  • Monitor serial numbers with status (available, installed, in repair, scrapped), flight hours/cycles, and overhaul due dates.

Engineering Change and Material Traceability Without Gaps

ECOs and material records link directly to BOMs, part numbers, and serial numbers to keep every revision and batch auditable.

  • Control ECO types (design, process, material, tooling, documentation) through Draft → Under Review → Approved → Implemented → Closed.
  • Link ECOs to affected part numbers and BOM revisions for closed-loop change management.
  • Trace raw material to finished part via batch number, heat treatment number, material certificate, and chemical composition.
  • Maintain full audit trail from supplier certificate to installed serial number.

Production Execution with Mandatory Quality Gates

Manufacturing and work orders enforce sign-offs, tooling assignments, and serial/lot quality release for aerospace compliance.

  • Generate MOs by aircraft type and configuration with customer, priority (AOG/Critical/High/Normal/Low), and quality release flag.
  • Assign qualified operators and calibrated tooling per work order with digital sign-off timestamps.
  • Resolve non-conformances through NCR dispositions: use-as-is, rework, repair, scrap, or return-to-supplier.
  • Complete AS9102 First Article Inspection with pass/fail characteristics and overall results.

Scale with Real-Time KPIs and Native Integration

Xerpium ERP brings aerospace COA, inventory, purchasing, and HR records together on KPI dashboards for operational control.

  • Monitor total MO by state, production efficiency, work center downtime, open NCRs, and FAI status.
  • Automate WIP and COGS journal entries with a dedicated aerospace chart of accounts.
  • Manage supplier AS9100D certifications, quality ratings, and on-time delivery for approved parts.
  • Use AI Assistant to search part numbers, check manufacturing order status, and generate reports.
