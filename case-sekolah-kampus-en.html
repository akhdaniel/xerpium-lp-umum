Schools & Universities

Xerpium ERP unifies admissions, academics, payments, and portals for schools.

From First Application to Graduate Transcript

Schools and universities manage complex admission waves, student data, and academic placement. Xerpium ERP brings these processes into a single, configurable system.

  • Define admissions by academic year with waves and capacity tracking.
  • Capture student details: NISN/NIS, birth data, religion, ethnicity, hobby, and economic status.
  • Record parent/guardian education, occupation, income, and status.
  • Upload required documents: KTP, KK, birth certificate, diploma, photo, and more.

Admissions Selection and Curriculum Setup

Move applicants from evaluation to classroom placement with controlled workflows. Configure curriculum, teachers, and assessment criteria per academic year.

  • Use five custom test-score fields and two custom contribution fields per admission process.
  • Manage admission groups with capacity and filled count; filter pass/fail status.
  • Place eligible students into target classes, creating active student records automatically.
  • Map subjects to teachers per semester, generate AI-assisted RPP drafts, and set KKM per subject, level, and year.

Assessment, Attendance, Scheduling, and Student Records

Run grading and report card workflows from draft to done, with automatic calculations. Manage daily operations—attendance, schedules, class movement, and graduation—within one audit trail.

  • Grade with configurable weightings for quizzes, assignments, practice, midterms, and finals; compute numeric and letter grades automatically.
  • Record class and lesson attendance, auto-populate student lists, and calculate per-student totals for present, sick, excused, absent, and leave.
  • Build academic calendars, class schedules, and teacher schedules; calculate total teaching hours per teacher.
  • Handle class promotion, student transfers, dropout statuses, graduation, and transcript drafts with chatter audit trails.

Payments, Parent/Student Portals, and Scalable Impact

Xerpium connects financial operations to Xendit for online payments and reconciliation while giving parents and students real-time access. The same system scales from teacher extended data to total workload recaps.

  • Create Xendit invoices via API v2 with QRIS, virtual account, and credit card options; PAID webhooks auto-reconcile and send email.
  • Offer recurring invoices for monthly SPP, education fees, and re-registration.
  • Give students a portal for schedules, assignments, grades, attendance, and messaging.
  • Give parents dashboards for schedules, attendance, grades, invoices, and development statistics.
