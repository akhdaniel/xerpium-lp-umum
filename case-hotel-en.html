One ERP for End-to-End Hotel Operations

Xerpium ERP brings room configuration, multi-channel reservations, guest profiles, housekeeping, and folio billing into a single system.

  • Manage room types, amenities, facilities, and per-floor room counts
  • Track reservations from walk-in, OTA, corporate, and website channels
  • Connect front-office billing to accounting, inventory, and purchase modules

Front Office: Rates, Reservations, and Check-In/Out

From dynamic rate plans to auto-created folios, the front-office workflow keeps rooms and invoices aligned.

  • Rate plans by room type, date range, and channel: rack, corporate, OTA, package, promo
  • Reservation workflow: Draft → Confirmed → Checked-In → Checked-Out / Cancelled / No-Show
  • Check-in auto-creates folio and updates room status to occupied; check-out closes folio and marks room dirty
  • Folio billing supports room, F&B, laundry, service, minibar, transport, and payment methods

Housekeeping, Maintenance, and Guest Services

Daily schedules, room assignments, and service orders flow directly into billing and room status.

  • Housekeeping schedules by shift (06–14, 14–22, 22–06) with task types, progress, and supervisor
  • Maintenance requests with priority, assignment, and automatic room status updates
  • Guest service orders for F&B, laundry, spa, transport, tours with posting to folio
  • Housekeeping line status: Pending → In Progress → Done → Inspected; room returns to available

Operational Control and Scalable Back Office

Configurable stages and native accounting, inventory, and purchase links let hotels scale without losing control.

  • Reservation and folio workflows powered by configurable dynamic stages, kanban, and RBAC
  • Automatic invoice creation from folio; room charges and services become journal entries
  • Minibar, F&B ingredients, laundry supplies, and housekeeping items tracked in inventory
  • Amenity reorders, vendor management, bulk purchasing, and blanket orders for recurring supplies
