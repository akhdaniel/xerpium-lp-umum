Hospital & Pharmacy

Unified ERP for patient care, pharmacy, and billing

One system for hospital and pharmacy operations

Xerpium ERP connects patient records, clinical workflows, pharmacy dispensing, and billing in a single platform tailored for hospitals and pharmacies.

  • Patient profile with MR number, NIK, BPJS, insurance, demographics, allergies, and emergency contact
  • Doctor master with SIP/STR license tracking, consultation fees, and department assignments
  • Ward and room management with capacity, nightly rates, status, and occupied count
  • Integrated billing from consultations, procedures, medicines, lab, radiology, and room charges

Clinical workflows from queue to discharge

Queue, outpatient visits, lab/radiology orders, inpatient admission, and vital sign monitoring run on structured, configurable workflows.

  • Queue management with automatic daily numbers, priority levels, and status flow from waiting to done
  • Outpatient visit records with vital signs, anamnesis, ICD-10 diagnosis, SOAP notes, and referrals
  • Lab and radiology orders with sample types, workflow stages, result values, and critical flags
  • Inpatient admission with ward, bed, expected discharge, auto-computed length of stay, and discharge condition

Pharmacy control and accurate billing

Prescriptions, drug dispensing, and patient billing work together with built-in checks for allergies, stock, and insurance coverage.

  • Prescription with dosage, route, duration, diagnosis link, allergy notes, and configurable workflow
  • Drug dispensing with pharmacist, batch/lot tracking, qty prescribed vs dispensed, and automatic stock picking
  • Patient bill lines covering consultation, procedure, medicine, lab, radiology, room, and nursing charges
  • Payment methods including cash, transfer, BPJS, insurance, and debit/credit card

Operational control and scalability

Xerpium standardizes master data and workflows across departments, supporting compliance, patient safety, and growth from a single clinic to a multi-department hospital.

  • Doctor license tracking with SIP and STR expiry monitoring
  • Controlled substance tracking for narcotics and psychotropics
  • Lifetime patient history with visit, admission, and bill counts
  • Configurable workflow stages for prescriptions and inpatient admissions
