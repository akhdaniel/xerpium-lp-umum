Privacy Policy

The Xerpium POS app works offline and keeps all store data on your own device.

Summary

Xerpium POS is a point-of-sale app that runs entirely on your device. We operate no servers, require no account, and collect no data from your store.

Data we collect

None. The app does not send data over the internet. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party service receiving your data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — products, prices, transactions, customers, receivables, stock, deliveries, store settings, and store logo — is stored in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • The database is encrypted (SQLCipher) with a key kept in the device Keystore.
  • Backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and only leave the device when you choose to share them, for example via WhatsApp or your own cloud storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of that data from the device.

Permissions and how they are used

  • Camera — scanning product barcodes, photographing delivery proof, and capturing product and store logo images.
  • Location — recording the drop-off point and checking the distance to the customer’s address.
  • Storage/photos — choosing product photos, the store logo, and CSV import or backup files.
  • Bluetooth — prepared for printing receipts to a Bluetooth thermal printer.

All of this is processed on the device only and is never sent to us.

Data sharing

The app never shares data on its own. Data leaves the device only when you choose an action such as “share receipt via WhatsApp”, “export PDF”, or “save backup”. The destination app you pick is governed by its own privacy policy.

Security

Cashier PINs are stored as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, never as plain text. The database and backups are encrypted. Keep your backup password somewhere safe: without it, the data cannot be recovered.

Children

This app is intended for business owners and shop staff, 18 years and older.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date below will be updated. Significant changes will be announced on the app’s store page.
