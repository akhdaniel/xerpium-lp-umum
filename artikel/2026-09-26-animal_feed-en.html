# Animal Feed ERP: Managing Feed Formulation, QC, and Distribution with Xerpium

Feed mill operators work in an environment where a small variation in moisture, protein, or pellet durability can affect animal performance, customer trust, and margin per ton. At the same time, procurement teams negotiate volatile commodity prices, production teams manage multi-level formulas, and finance teams track distributor credit across multiple channels. Without a connected system, these functions often operate in silos, creating stock discrepancies, formulation errors, traceability gaps, and delayed financial visibility.

Xerpium ERP addresses this by connecting raw material procurement, multi-warehouse inventory, feed formulation, production, quality control, sales distribution, and financial reporting in one platform. For animal feed manufacturers, this means every batch can be traced from commodity receipt to finished feed delivery, while cost and profitability remain visible at the ton level.

Common Challenges in Animal Feed Manufacturing

Feed mills face several recurring operational pressures:

  • Commodity price volatility across corn, soybean meal, bran, pollard, CPO, molasses, minerals, vitamins, and additives.
  • Multi-level production complexity from grinding to mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging, each with different work centers and quality checks.
  • Strict quality requirements at receiving, in-process, and finished product stages, including moisture, protein, fat, fiber, ash, particle size, mixing uniformity, and pellet durability index.
  • Multi-warehouse inventory spread across corn silos, SBM warehouses, premix cold storage, and finished feed warehouses.
  • Diverse sales channels such as distributors, direct farmers, feed stores, modern trade, and makloon, with different credit terms and collection risks.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Animal Feed Operations

Xerpium ERP provides modular capabilities that directly map to feed mill workflows.

Raw Material & Procurement

The system handles commodity purchasing with multi-supplier purchase orders, long-term contracts, and commodity price tracking. Raw material categories include corn, soybean meal, premix, bran, pollard, CPO, molasses, minerals, vitamins, and additives. Inventory can be tracked across multiple warehouses, including corn silos, SBM warehouses, premix cold storage, and finished feed storage. At receiving, quality control sampling records moisture, protein, fat, and fiber content before material is accepted into stock.

Production & Formulation

Feed formulation supports nutritional composition per feed type, including broiler, layer, ruminant, and aquaculture feeds. Each formula is structured as a multi-level bill of materials covering grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging. Manufacturing orders are generated directly from formulations and routed through work centers such as grinding, pellet, and bagging. Quality control checkpoints can be recorded at each production stage, and costing uses a per-ton HPP model with cost rollup from raw materials, labor, and overhead.

Quality Control Lab

Raw material QC captures moisture percentage, protein percentage, fat percentage, fiber percentage, and ash percentage at receiving. In-process QC tracks particle size, mixing uniformity, and pellet durability index. Finished product analysis verifies nutrient composition guarantees. A Certificate of Analysis can be generated per batch, with lot number traceability linking finished feed back to incoming raw materials and production records.

Sales & Distribution

Xerpium ERP manages product categories including broiler starter, grower, and finisher feeds, layer feed, ruminant feed, aquaculture feed, and premix. Sales channels include distributors, direct farmers, feed stores, modern trade, and makloon. The system tracks distributor receivables, credit limits, due dates, and aging reports. Delivery documents, surat jalan, and fleet tracking support distribution to farmers and distributors.

Financial Reporting

Financial statements reflect the operational realities of a feed mill. The balance sheet includes current assets such as raw material inventory, work-in-progress, and finished feed inventory, as well as fixed assets such as grinding, pellet, and bagging equipment, liabilities, and equity. The profit and loss statement supports revenue per feed type—broiler, layer, ruminant, and aquaculture—alongside HPP per ton, operational expenses, and gross profit. The trial balance uses six-digit account codes across 32 account groups. Tax handling includes PPN 11% and, where applicable, an exempt fiscal position for animal feed. Gross profit, operating profit, and net profit are calculated automatically from the report structure.

KPI Widgets and AI Assistant

Dashboard Studio includes 14 KPI widgets relevant to feed mill management: total revenue, total orders, raw material cost, distributor receivables, stock value, total customers, pending material purchase orders, invoices this month, revenue trend over 12 months, raw material cost trend over 12 months, top products, and top customers. Xerpium’s AI Assistant allows users to check stock levels, commodity prices, and order status using natural language, while NL-to-SQL capabilities support ad hoc dashboard generation.

Cross-module integration connects accounting with a feedmill chart of accounts, inventory across multiple warehouses, purchase orders with QC receiving, manufacturing orders from formulations, sales distribution, and dashboard analytics. This removes the need for separate spreadsheets and manual reconciliation.

A Day in the Feed Mill: End-to-End Use Case

A feed mill receives a shipment of corn into the silo. QC records moisture and protein percentages during receiving, and the material is accepted into stock. The production team creates a manufacturing order from a broiler starter formulation. The multi-level BOM routes the order through grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging work centers. In-process QC checks particle size and pellet durability before finished product is released with a final nutrient analysis and Certificate of Analysis.

The sales team then processes a distributor order. The system checks the distributor’s credit limit and outstanding aging before confirming delivery. A delivery order and surat jalan are issued, and the fleet is tracked to the distributor location. Finance posts the invoice, and the transaction flows into revenue, distributor receivables, and P&L. The KPI dashboard updates total revenue, stock value, and top products, while management can ask the AI Assistant for current raw material cost or pending purchase orders without opening multiple screens.

With connected modules and real-time KPI visibility, feed manufacturers can reduce operational blind spots and make more confident decisions from receiving to delivery.
