# Streamlining Production with Xerpium ERP: A Practical Guide to Manufacturing (MRP)

Manufacturers today operate under constant pressure. Customer demand shifts quickly, raw material costs fluctuate, and production schedules must stay tight to protect margins. When production planning relies on spreadsheets, disconnected tools, or manual tracking, errors multiply: materials run short at the wrong moment, work orders stall, and the true cost of each finished product becomes difficult to calculate. These gaps quietly erode profitability and strain customer relationships.

The Manufacturing (MRP) module in Xerpium ERP addresses these problems by bringing bill of materials, routing, work orders, and production costing into a single connected system. Because it is a base module that integrates with the rest of the Xerpium suite, manufacturers gain a unified view of operations — from the shop floor to the financial statements — without stitching together separate applications.

Common Challenges in Manufacturing Operations

Production teams and operations managers frequently face recurring obstacles that slow output and cloud decision-making:

  • Inaccurate material planning. Without a reliable bill of materials and real-time visibility, teams either overstock components or run short mid-production.
  • Opaque production costs. When labor, materials, and routing steps are tracked separately, calculating the true cost of a finished product becomes guesswork.
  • Disconnected work orders. Production instructions that live outside the core system make it hard to track progress, bottlenecks, and completion status.
  • Complex multi-entity operations. Businesses running several companies or working across currencies struggle to consolidate manufacturing data consistently.
  • Limited audit and traceability. Regulatory requirements and internal controls demand a clear record of changes, but manual processes rarely provide one.

Relevant Xerpium Modules and Solutions

Xerpium's Manufacturing (MRP) module is designed as a base component of the ERP suite, meaning it works natively with the other modules rather than as a bolt-on. Here is what it delivers.

Bill of Materials (BOM) and Routing

The module supports structured BOM definitions and routing so every product has a clear recipe of components and a defined sequence of production steps. This foundation ensures that material requirements and process stages are documented, repeatable, and ready for planning.

Work Orders

Work orders in Xerpium translate production plans into actionable instructions. Each order ties directly to its BOM and routing, giving teams a consistent reference for what to produce, in what order, and against which materials.

Production Costing

Because BOM, routing, and work orders are managed in one place, the module can calculate production costs accurately. This gives finance and operations a shared, grounded understanding of what it actually costs to manufacture each item.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

For organizations operating across multiple legal entities or international markets, the module supports multi-company and multi-currency setups. A built-in audit trail records activity, supporting internal governance and compliance needs.

Real-Time Dashboards and Reporting

Manufacturing data flows into real-time dashboards and reports, so managers can monitor production status and performance as it happens rather than relying on end-of-period summaries.

Integration with the Xerpium AI Assistant

The Manufacturing (MRP) module connects to the Xerpium AI Assistant, allowing teams to interact with production data more naturally and surface insights without navigating complex screens manually.

A Unified, Integrated Foundation

As a base module, Manufacturing (MRP) is built to merge with every other Xerpium module. This integration means production activity is not isolated — it connects to the broader ERP environment, keeping information consistent across the business.

Example Use Case: From Order to Finished Product

Consider a mid-sized manufacturer producing custom industrial components across two legal entities, each operating in a different currency.

A new production requirement is created and linked to its defined bill of materials, which lists every component needed, and its routing, which outlines the production steps. The system generates a work order that gives the shop floor clear, consistent instructions.

As production progresses, activity is recorded against the work order. The production costing feature calculates material and process costs, giving finance an accurate figure for the finished item. Because the business runs two entities, the multi-company and multi-currency capabilities keep each company's data correctly separated and valued, while the audit trail captures changes for later review.

Throughout the process, managers monitor progress on real-time dashboards, spotting delays before they escalate. When they need a quick answer about status or cost, they turn to the Xerpium AI Assistant to retrieve the information directly. Because the Manufacturing (MRP) module is integrated with the rest of the suite, the production data feeds seamlessly into the broader ERP environment — no re-entry, no reconciliation gaps.
