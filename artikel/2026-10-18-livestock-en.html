Livestock ERP: Managing Cattle Operations, Feed, and Halal Traceability with Xerpium

# Livestock ERP: Managing Cattle Operations, Feed, and Halal Traceability with Xerpium

Livestock operations are more complex than they often appear from the outside. A single animal may need to be identified, tracked by origin, assigned to a pen and batch, weighed repeatedly, fed according to a specific ration, treated with medicine, and eventually sold or slaughtered under halal requirements. Multiply that by hundreds or thousands of animals across multiple locations, and the operational burden becomes clear. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated Livestock module that connects animal registry, pen management, feeding, weighing, health, costing, traceability, and halal certification in one system.

Common challenges in livestock operations

Many cattle and livestock businesses still rely on fragmented spreadsheets, paper records, or disconnected software. That creates recurring problems:

  • Animal identity and origin data are scattered across ear tags, RFID codes, QR labels, supplier files, and farm records.
  • Feed, weighing, FCR, ADG, and HPP calculations are time-consuming and prone to manual error.
  • Feed and medicine stock at pen level is difficult to track accurately, leading to shortages, waste, or expired inventory.
  • Halal chain of custody and certification require strong documentation and tamper-proof evidence.
  • Profitability per batch or per pen remains unclear because feed, medicine, labor, overhead, and revenue are not unified.

Xerpium ERP provides a structured answer to these challenges through a set of connected livestock workflows.

How Xerpium ERP addresses livestock operations

Animal registry and origin tracking

Xerpium ERP starts with individual animal identification. Each animal can be recorded with an ear tag, RFID code, or QR code. The registry supports cattle breeds such as Bali, Madura, PO, Limousin, Simmental, Brahman, Angus, and FH. Origin tracking is structured by province, regency, and source farm. The system also stores photos and documents per animal, plus purchase information including supplier, purchase order, buy price, initial weight, and entry date.

Pen and batch management

Pens or kandang can be classified by type: fattening, quarantine, isolation, and holding. Each pen records capacity, current animal count, utilization percentage, location or block, area in square meters, and a responsible person assigned from employee data. Pens are linked to livestock batches through a many-to-many relationship, allowing flexible assignment and movement.

Batch management covers the fattening period with start date, end date, and state tracking from draft to active to done. Each batch holds pen and animal assignments, performance KPIs such as FCR, average ADG, total weight gain, and mortality rate, and full costing for feed, medicine, labor, overhead, and purchases. Xerpium automatically computes HPP per animal and per kilogram of live weight.

Feed, feeding, and FCR

The feed module supports common ingredients such as forage, concentrate, BIS, bran, tofu waste, molasses, and minerals. Users can create TMR feed formulas with dry matter percentage and cost per kilogram. Feed stock is tracked per pen with quantity on hand, and daily feeding records can be captured per batch or pen for morning, afternoon, and evening feeding times.

Feeding records work in two modes. Formula mode pulls quantity automatically from the daily feed per head setting. Ingredient mode records direct ingredients with automatic cost calculation. Dry matter is calculated automatically from the ingredient or formula, and feed per head or dry matter per head is generated automatically. FCR is calculated from the feed and weight data already captured in the system.

Weighing and ADG tracking

Weighing records capture each animal’s weight by date and method, whether manual, RFID, or digital. The system automatically calculates average daily gain from the weight difference divided by the interval in days. A growth curve line chart is available per animal, and previous weight plus interval days are carried forward automatically. A duplicate check prevents the same animal from being weighed twice on the same date.

Health, pharmacy, and SOP control

Health events cover vaccination, treatment, sickness, death, quarantine, routine checks, and surgery. Medicine tracking includes product, dose, unit, cost, and withdrawal period. The withdrawal date is recorded to identify when an animal is safe to sell after medication. Preventive vaccination schedules can be set per species with cron-based reminders. Photo documentation is available for conditions before and after medical actions.

Pharmacy stock is tracked per pen and in central warehouse. The system records medicine receipts, issues, and adjustments, with minimum stock levels and automatic low-stock flags. Expiry tracking is available for medicine batches. When a medical record is created, pharmacy stock is deducted automatically.

SOP management covers feed, health, hygiene, weighing, vaccination, biosecurity, and slaughter processes. Each SOP consists of checklist items with guidance, estimated time, and photo support. Daily SOP executions can be created automatically for every pen, with status tracking from draft to in progress to done or skipped, plus automatic completion percentage.

Traceability, halal chain, and blockchain-ready certificates

Xerpium records the chain of custody for each animal from intake to active to sold or slaughter. These records are append-only and use SHA-256 hashes; they cannot be deleted after hashing. The hash chain structure is blockchain-ready, with each record referencing the previous hash.

Halal slaughter records include checklist items such as ante-mortem, bismillah, qiblat, blood drained, and post-mortem. Halal compliance is computed automatically and requires all mandatory checklist items to be fulfilled. Carcass data includes live weight, carcass weight, dressing percentage, and offal. The record supports SHA-256 hashing and blockchain transaction hash readiness, with the slaughter person assigned from employee data.

Halal certificates can be registered with number, institution such as MUI, LPPOM, or BPJPH, type, and validity period. A SHA-256 record hash is generated automatically upon validation. After blockchain submission, the transaction hash is stored. The certificate is tamper-evident, and a cron job automatically marks expired certificates based on their validity date.

Purchase, sales, accounting, and integrations

Xerpium connects livestock processes to the broader ERP. For purchases, a special PO Bakalan can carry species and expected weight. When the PO is approved, animals are created automatically from line items, pen and batch assignments are filled from the PO, and origin province is taken from the supplier address. An intake traceability event is also recorded automatically.

On the sales side, the system supports sales orders with animal selection. When an animal is marked sold, the sales journal is posted automatically, and delivery order or surat jalan integration is available. Inventory integrations manage feed stock per pen, pharmacy stock, and product consumption. Accounting integrations handle automatic HPP per batch, automatic sale journal posting, and analytic distribution per batch. HR integrations support labor assignment, veterinarian and officer assignment, and responsible person per pen. Maintenance, quality, and blockchain integrations extend the system to facility upkeep, halal compliance monitoring, and immutable audit trails.

HPP, profitability, and AI dashboard

The profitability engine consolidates feed costs, medicine costs, labor costs, overhead, and revenue. It calculates gross profit and margin percentage per batch automatically. The accounting module supports multi-branch livestock operations, analytic accounts per batch or pen, and profit and loss reporting per batch.

For management visibility, the AI dashboard can auto-generate dashboards from natural language using DeepSeek, convert natural language into SQL queries, and analyze trends, peaks, anomalies, and recommendations. A growth chart component displays interactive growth curves per animal, and conditional formatting highlights values dynamically.

Business benefits

  • A single source of truth from animal intake to sale, slaughter, and halal certificate.
  • Automated ADG, FCR, HPP, and profitability calculations reduce manual work and errors.
  • Pen-level feed and pharmacy stock control with low-stock and expiry tracking.
  • Tamper-evident traceability and blockchain-ready halal certificates.
  • Clear gross margin visibility per batch, pen, and branch.
  • AI-assisted dashboards for faster operational insight.

Example workflow

A cattle operation in Indonesia creates a PO Bakalan for Limousin cattle. Once the PO is approved, Xerpium automatically creates individual animal records, fills the intended pen and batch, and records origin province from the supplier address. The animals enter a quarantine or fattening pen, and daily feeding records use a TMR formula with dry matter tracking. Routine weighing records feed automatic ADG calculations, while any medical event deducts pharmacy stock and sets a withdrawal date. When the animals are sold, the sales order selects specific animals, marks them sold, and posts the sale journal. The batch profitability engine then calculates feed, medicine, labor, overhead, revenue, and gross margin in one view.

Instead of managing isolated spreadsheets, teams can run daily operations with automatic calculations, controlled stock, audit-ready records, and real-time profitability insight.
