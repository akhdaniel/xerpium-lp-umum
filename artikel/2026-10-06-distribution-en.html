# Distribution ERP: How Xerpium Streamlines Territory Management, Delivery, and Field Sales

Distribution businesses run on complex, interdependent operations: sales territories must stay balanced, principals and discount schemes need tight control, daily delivery routes require real-time visibility, and field teams need reliable tools for visits, orders, and payment collection. When these functions sit in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, errors multiply, approvals slow down, and seasonal demand becomes difficult to manage.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated distribution suite that connects territory planning, principal management, delivery execution, inventory transfer, forecasting, field sales, collection, and performance reporting in one environment. Instead of treating distribution as an afterthought, Xerpium makes it the operational backbone of your sales and fulfillment process.

Common Distribution Challenges

Distribution companies typically face several recurring problems:

  • Territory overlap and unclear assignment: Without a structured hierarchy, multiple salespeople can claim the same area, while other zones remain uncovered.
  • Manual discount and approval errors: Principal discount schemes are often applied inconsistently in sales orders, leading to pricing disputes and delayed approvals.
  • Weak delivery visibility: Daily routes, manifests, and proof of delivery are hard to track, especially when drivers and vehicles are managed outside the core system.
  • Field activity blind spots: Sales visits, on-site orders, and payment collections are difficult to monitor in real time, making it hard to measure execution against plan.
  • Seasonal stock imbalances: Forecasting demand for seasonal periods such as Ramadan, Lebaran, rainy season, or back-to-school cycles is often reactive rather than data-driven.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses Distribution Complexity

Territory Structure: Region, Area, Zone, and Salesman Assignment

Xerpium provides a clear geographic hierarchy: Region → Area → Zone. Regions represent large territories such as Sumatera, Jawa, or Kalimantan, each with a unique code. Areas break regions into intermediate sub-regions and link back to the parent region. Zones are the smallest distribution units, linked to areas and to customer records through a one-to-many relationship.

This structure gives distribution managers an automatic count of areas per region, zones per area, and customers per zone. Each zone can also carry its own notes for special handling. Salesman territory assignment is managed separately with date ranges, and the system enforces a unique constraint so that one salesman is assigned to one zone at a time. Related area and region fields are derived from the zone, keeping reporting consistent.

Principal and Discount Management

The principal module records the principals or suppliers behind distributed goods. More importantly, it manages the mandatory discount scheme applied from principal to customer. When a sales order is created, the principal discount is automatically applied based on the scheme. The same principal relationship flows into purchase orders and can be linked to specific product templates, reducing manual pricing work and ensuring that customer pricing reflects the agreed principal terms.

Daily Delivery Route and Manifest

Xerpium supports daily delivery planning through route management. Delivery orders are generated from stock picking with a sequence, and a daily manifest collects delivery orders by route and day. Proof of delivery is captured digitally, creating a reliable audit trail for completed shipments. Fleet integration allows vehicles and drivers to be assigned through the fleet module, while a wizard simplifies the creation of deliveries directly from sale orders.

Inventory Transfer and Warehouse Control

For businesses operating multiple warehouses or branches, Xerpium supports internal transfers with dual approval. Barcode scanning is available for locations and products through the barcodes module, and location labels can be printed as barcode labels to speed up warehouse operations. The inventory workflow can also be configured per company using dynamic stock stages, giving operations teams flexibility without custom development.

Seasonal Stock Forecasting

The forecast module helps distribution teams plan for seasonal demand using categories such as Ramadan, Lebaran, rainy season, and back-to-school periods. Xerpium predicts stock requirements based on seasonal patterns and can compute forecasts automatically through cron jobs. A wizard generates forecasts from historical data, and the results integrate with inventory to produce purchase recommendations, helping buyers act before demand spikes.

Field Sales Execution: Visit Plan, Visit Log, and Collection Payment

Field teams are supported from planning through execution. The Visit Plan module schedules daily salesperson visits, supports visit types such as sales call, canvassing, collection, and survey, and helps optimize route order by zone. Visit status moves from Planned to Done or Cancelled.

The Visit Log captures real-time on-site reports, photo evidence of display and store conditions, and order capture directly from the field. Geo tagging records visit coordinates for accountability. The Collection Payment module enables on-site payment collection with methods including cash, transfer, and QRIS, links payments to customer invoices, and updates payment status in real time for faster settlement.

Sales Targets and Performance Visibility

Xerpium tracks monthly sales targets per salesman and per principal, then compares realization against target. Reports are available through the distribution report integration, and a dashboard view visualizes sales performance versus target. This helps managers identify underperforming territories and adjust field priorities quickly.

Approval Workflows and Accounting Integration

Sales order approval supports automatic approval for orders below a configured limit, credit checks against customer receivable limits, and validation for discounts outside the allowed range. Purchase order approval includes budget checks, principal purchase limits, and multi-level approval through Supervisor, Manager, and Director stages. Both workflows can be customized per company using dynamic stage configuration.

On the accounting side, Xerpium supports automatic principal discounts, multi-warehouse valuation, field collection payment recording, consignment tracking, and salesperson commission calculation. Automatic journal entries from sales and purchase orders keep financial records aligned with distribution activity.

AI Assistant and Real-Time Dashboards

Xerpium’s AI dashboard features allow users to generate dashboards from natural language using DeepSeek, convert natural language into SQL queries in real time, and receive AI-driven chart insights on trends, peaks, and anomalies. A copilot chat with streamed responses works within the dashboard context. Distribution teams can ask questions such as “Show this month’s sales target for salesman X,” “Create a delivery order from SO/001,” “Show the Ramadan stock forecast,” or “Show visit realization vs plan this week.” Distribution KPIs are available through the distribution dashboard, including sales target achievement and delivery performance metrics.

Business Benefits

  • Clear territory ownership with region, area, zone, and unique salesman assignment.
  • Faster order processing through automatic principal discount application and configurable approval limits.
  • Better delivery control with daily manifests, digital proof of delivery, and fleet assignment.
  • More accurate seasonal planning through automated stock forecasts and purchase recommendations.
  • Real-time field visibility from visit plans, visit logs, geo tagging, and on-site payment collection.
  • Stronger financial reconciliation with automatic journals, collection payment updates, and commission calculation.
  • Faster decision-making using natural-language dashboards and distribution KPIs.

A Practical Distribution Workflow in Xerpium

A typical field-to-cash flow in Xerpium might look like this:

  1. A salesperson starts the day with a visit plan already routed by zone.
  2. During a store visit, they log the activity, capture photo evidence, and record a new order on-site.
  3. The sales order automatically applies the relevant principal discount; if the value is below the approval limit and the customer passes the credit check, the order is approved.
  4. The delivery team uses a wizard to create a delivery order from the sale order, assigns the route, vehicle, and driver, and generates the daily manifest.
  5. After delivery, proof of delivery is captured digitally.
  6. During a later visit or at the point of collection, the salesperson records payment by cash, transfer, or QRIS, and the invoice status updates in real time.
  7. Accounting receives the automatic journal entries, and managers monitor sales target achievement and delivery performance on the distribution dashboard.

Conclusion

Distribution is more than moving goods from warehouse to customer. It requires disciplined territory design, accurate principal and discount handling, reliable delivery execution, active field sales management, and strong financial control. Xerpium ERP brings these capabilities together in a modular distribution suite that reduces manual work, improves approval accuracy, and gives distribution leaders real-time visibility into field and delivery performance.

If your distribution operation needs a more connected ERP approach, Xerpium provides the tools to manage territories, deliveries, field visits, collections, and seasonal demand in one integrated system.
