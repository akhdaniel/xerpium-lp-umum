# Hospital & Pharmacy ERP: Unifying Patient Care, Pharmacy, and Billing with Xerpium

Hospitals and pharmacies operate in a high-stakes environment where patient safety, regulatory compliance, and financial control must move together. Too often, outpatient clinics, inpatient wards, pharmacy counters, laboratories, and billing teams work with disconnected records. Xerpium addresses this with an integrated Hospital & Pharmacy ERP module that connects patient administration, clinical documentation, pharmacy dispensing, inpatient management, billing, and health-system integrations such as BPJS Kesehatan and SatuSehat/FHIR.

Common challenges in hospital and pharmacy operations

  • Fragmented patient data: medical records, insurance details, allergies, and visit history scattered across systems.
  • Manual queue and polyclinic coordination causing delays and unclear patient status.
  • Pharmacy complexity: controlled substances, expiry tracking, batch tracking, stock integration, and dispensing accuracy.
  • Inpatient ward billing and discharge processes requiring accurate room charges, procedures, medicines, and length of stay.
  • Compliance with BPJS and SatuSehat/FHIR reporting, which demands structured data and reliable API synchronization.

Xerpium modules for hospital & pharmacy

Patient & Medical Record

The Patient & Medical Record module extends the ERP contact record into a full medical profile. Each patient can have an automatic medical record number from a sequence, NIK, BPJS number, insurance name, and policy number. It stores demographics such as age, gender, blood group, religion, and nationality; medical information covering allergies, chronic conditions, and medical notes; and emergency contact details. The system also maintains a lifetime history including visit count, admission count, and bill count.

Doctor & Polyclinic

Doctor Master records include name, SIP, STR, specialization, department, consultation fee, phone, email, photo, and active/inactive practice status. License tracking covers SIP number and expiry and STR number. Departments and polyclinics include Rawat Jalan, Rawat Inap, UGD, Lab, Radiology, Pharmacy, Surgery, and ICU, with head of department and computed visit count per doctor.

Ward & Inpatient Rooms

The ward module supports room types such as VIP, VVIP, Kelas 1, Kelas 2, Kelas 3, ICU, NICU, Isolasi, and UGD. It records capacity, rate per night, status such as Available, Occupied, or Maintenance, floor and building location, department link, occupied count, and facility notes. This creates a real-time view of bed availability for admissions.

Queue Management & Outpatient Visit

Queue Management automatically generates a daily queue number per date and polyclinic, with priority such as Normal, Urgent, or Emergency. The status flow moves from Waiting to Called, In Progress, Done, or Skipped, and records arrival time and call time. Staff can create a visit directly from the queue and set payment type as Umum, BPJS, or Asuransi.

Outpatient Visit captures the visiting doctor, polyclinic, payment type, vital signs including blood pressure, pulse, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2, weight, height, and BMI. It also records anamnesis, multi diagnosis with ICD-10 and a primary flag, main diagnosis, SOAP notes, and referrals to specialists, hospital, lab, or radiology. Visit status follows Draft, Waiting, In Examination, Done, or Cancelled.

Pharmacy, Prescription & Dispensing

Medicine Catalog manages drug categories such as obat keras, bebas, bebas terbatas, narkotika, psikotropika, alkes, and BHP. Each item can include generic name, brand, BPOM registration, dosage form, strength, manufacturer, therapeutic class, minimum stock alert, and expiry tracking.

Prescriptions record medicine, dosage such as 3x1, route such as Oral, IV, IM, SC, or Topical, duration, instructions, linked diagnosis, and allergy warnings. The workflow moves from Draft to Confirmed, Dispensing, Dispensed, or Cancelled, with configurable stages.

Drug Dispensing links to the prescription and tracks pharmacist, batch/lot number and expiry date, quantity prescribed versus dispensed, unit price, and stock integration that automatically creates stock picking from the pharmacy. Dispensing status includes Draft, Dispensed, Partially Returned, and Cancelled.

Inpatient Admission, Lab, Radiology, Procedures, and Monitoring

Inpatient Admission records the attending doctor, ward, bed number, payment type, admission and discharge diagnosis with ICD-10, anamnesis, treatment summary, expected discharge date, and discharge condition such as Recovered, Improved, Referred, AMA, or Died. Length of stay is auto-computed. Workflow stages include Draft, Admitted, In Ward, ICU, Discharged, or Cancelled.

Lab & Radiology Orders track patient, doctor, test type, sample type, result values with normal range and flags, clinical notes, and result timestamps. Lab workflows move from Draft, Ordered, Sample Collected, Processing, Resulted, to Verified. Radiology orders are also managed.

Medical Procedure provides a procedure catalog with code, category, price, duration, and department. Procedure records capture doctor, nurse, date, duration, price, status, notes, and linked product for invoicing. Categories include General, Surgery, Diagnostic, Therapy, Emergency, and Nursing.

Vital Sign Monitoring records periodic blood pressure, pulse, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2, blood glucose, urine output, pain scale, consciousness level from Compos Mentis to Coma, nurse, and timestamp.

Billing, Accounting, and Compliance

Billing & Invoice builds patient bills from consultation, procedure, medicine, lab, radiology, room, and nursing lines. It calculates subtotal, discount, tax, total, paid, and due, supports payment methods such as Cash, Transfer, BPJS, Asuransi, and Debit/Credit Card, tracks insurance coverage, and can automatically add room charges on admission discharge. Bills can generate account.move invoices, feeding Hospital ↔ Accounting for revenue tracking per department or polyclinic and payment status.

BPJS Kesehatan Integration supports eligibility checks, BPJS referrals, claims, SEP numbers, and claim status from Draft to Paid, using VClaim and PCare APIs for participant checks, billing insert, and referral creation. SatuSehat/FHIR Integration syncs patients, encounters, practitioners, locations, organizations, conditions, medications, observations, procedures, service requests, and clinical impressions through FHIR resources. API Management handles sandbox/production environments, OAuth token caching, automatic retry for errors such as 429/500/502/503, API logs, sync states, CRUD FHIR operations, and multi-company configuration.

Business benefits of Xerpium Hospital & Pharmacy ERP

  • A single patient record from registration through billing and discharge.
  • Faster outpatient flow and clearer queue status per polyclinic.
  • Safer pharmacy operations with allergy checks, controlled substance tracking, batch/expiry tracking, and stock integration.
  • Accurate inpatient charges with auto room charges and computed length of stay.
  • Structured BPJS and SatuSehat/FHIR compliance with controlled synchronization and error tracking.
  • Complete revenue visibility by department, polyclinic, and payment method.

Example patient flow

A BPJS patient registers at the front office. Xerpium creates a patient profile with an automatic MR number and BPJS data, then the patient enters the polyclinic queue with a daily queue number. The outpatient visit records vital signs, anamnesis, ICD-10 diagnosis, and SOAP notes. The doctor writes a prescription; the pharmacy checks allergies and dispenses medicine with batch and expiry tracking. The bill is generated with consultation, procedure, and medicine lines, and the BPJS claim is created through the VClaim/PCare integration. If the patient is admitted, ward status updates, length of stay is tracked, and discharge generates the discharge summary and final invoice.
