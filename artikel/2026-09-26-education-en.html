# Managing Schools and Vocational Institutes with Xerpium Education ERP

Schools and vocational education institutions operate in a uniquely complex administrative environment. They manage admissions waves, student records, curriculum planning, teacher assignments, daily attendance, report cards, student transfers, and recurring tuition payments—often across disconnected spreadsheets and manual workflows. Without a unified system, staff spend too much time on data re-entry, parents lack visibility, and financial reconciliation becomes error-prone.

Xerpium ERP provides a dedicated education module that brings these processes into one connected platform. From the first PSB registration to report cards and Xendit payment reconciliation, the system supports schools, vocational institutions, and administrators with structured workflows, role-based access, and built-in audit trails.

Common Challenges in Education Administration

Many schools and vocational institutions face similar operational bottlenecks:

  • Manual or fragmented student admissions processes with limited visibility into wave capacity and document completeness.
  • Disconnected curriculum data, making it difficult to link teachers, lesson plans, KKM, and assessment criteria.
  • Time-consuming grade input, report card generation, and monthly attendance recaps.
  • Limited parent and student access to schedules, grades, attendance, and billing information.
  • Complex tuition collection and reconciliation, especially for recurring SPP and registration fees.

Core Modules for Schools and Vocational Institutions

Admissions and Student Placement

Xerpium structures the full PSB process per academic year, including admission waves and capacity tracking. Prospective student records capture NISN, NIS, place and date of birth, gender, blood type, religion, ethnicity, hobbies, and economic condition. Parent and guardian data includes education, occupation, income, and status. Address data uses a tree selection for province, city, district, sub-district, and postal code.

Documents can be uploaded and classified by type, such as KTP, family card, birth certificate, diploma, and photos. Selection uses five customizable PSB score fields and two customizable donation fields per process. Admission groups show capacity and filled count. Placement moves through Draft, Open, and Done stages, allowing staff to filter eligible students and place them into destination classes. Passed and not-passed student views are generated automatically, and once placement is completed, active student records are created for academic use.

Curriculum, Lessons, and Teaching Management

The curriculum module manages compulsory and additional subjects, subject codes, and links to teachers and report card grades. Teacher-subject assignments are defined per semester with decree references and effective dates. Rencana Pelaksanaan Pembelajaran or RPP records include weight per level and semester, while KKM records define minimum passing criteria per subject, level, and academic year.

Testing types such as UTS, UAS, quizzes, assignments, and practice can be configured per subject. Assessment aspects include affective, cognitive, conceptual understanding, psychomotor, and practical skills. Xerpium also offers AI-assisted RPP generation using a GPT model, allowing teachers to create draft lesson plans faster.

Grading, Report Cards, and Attendance

Grading workflows follow Draft, Open, and Done stages. Teachers input grades per student and per RPP with multiple test types. Each grade detail includes weight, date, and notes, and average scores are calculated automatically. Grading rules are configurable, with a standard range such as A 90–100, B 80–89, C 70–79, D 60–69, and E 0–59.

Report card generation calculates final numeric and letter grades automatically. Teachers can add subject comments and attitude comments covering spiritual and social aspects with grade ranges. AI-assisted question generation is available and can be linked to surveys for online exams.

Attendance is handled through class-level monthly records and per-session lesson attendance. Class attendance supports statuses for present, sick, permission, absent, and leave. Lesson attendance records each session with subject, teacher, class period, material, and teacher notes. The system automatically computes per-student totals and active school days. Teachers can also auto-populate all students in a class using a search button.

Scheduling and Student Lifecycle

Academic calendars track events such as national holidays, exams, and report card distribution. Learning hour definitions cover period 1 through N with start and end times. Class schedules combine day, period, subject, and teacher, while teacher schedules are generated automatically per teacher. A teacher schedule recap workflow calculates total teaching hours per teacher.

Student lifecycle tools support class transfers, grade promotion, student mutations, graduation, and transcript generation. Each process follows structured stages and records activity in a mail thread for audit purposes. Grade promotion filters students who advance or remain, then places them automatically. Mutation reasons include dismissal, resignation, death, and school transfer.

Payment Collection with Xendit

Xerpium integrates with Xendit as a payment gateway using public key, secret key, and webhook token configuration. Invoice creation uses Xendit API v2 and provides a redirect URL for payment. Webhook events automatically update statuses: PAID becomes done with auto-reconciliation and email notification, PENDING remains pending, and EXPIRED or FAILED cancels the invoice. Transactions are linked to account.move records, and the invoice URL remains accessible from the form.

The system supports recurring payments such as monthly SPP, education fees, and registration fees. Parents and students can pay through multiple methods, including QRIS, virtual account, and credit card.

Portals for Students, Parents, and Teacher Data

The student portal provides a dashboard with schedule, recent grades, and announcements. Students can view lesson schedules, attendance, grades, report cards, and assignments. They can upload assignment answers and communicate with teachers and homeroom teachers.

The parent portal gives guardians a dashboard with their child’s schedule, attendance, and invoices. Parents can view daily schedules, monthly attendance summaries, semester grades, and report cards. They can also see SPP and payment bills, pay online through Xendit, monitor grade and attendance statistics, register a new prospective student, and message teachers or school staff.

Teacher data extends employee records with NIP, NUPTK, NRG, academic titles, personal details, religion, ethnicity, department, employment status, and start date. Teaching load is captured through semester assignments, teacher schedules, total teaching hours, lesson attendance, and RPP records.

Business Benefits of Xerpium for Education

  • Faster admissions processing with clear wave capacity, document tracking, and automatic student placement.
  • Better curriculum control through connected subjects, teacher assignments, RPP, KKM, and assessment rules.
  • Reduced administrative workload through automated grade averages, attendance recaps, and report card generation.
  • Transparent payment collection and reconciliation through Xendit integration and webhook-driven invoice status updates.
  • Stronger parent and student engagement through dedicated portals for schedules, grades, attendance, and billing.
  • Audit-ready student and teacher records with structured workflows and activity logs.

Example Workflow: From Admission to Paid SPP in One System

A vocational school opens a new PSB process for the academic year with two admission waves and defined capacity. Prospective students submit personal data, parent information, address details, and required documents. Staff review the five PSB score fields and two donation fields, then filter eligible students. Placement is moved from Draft to Open to Done, creating active student records in the correct classes.

Teachers use AI-assisted RPP drafts, input grades per RPP and test type, and the system calculates averages and final report card grades. Finance staff create recurring SPP invoices through Xendit. Parents receive the invoice, pay via QRIS or virtual account, and the webhook automatically marks the invoice as paid and reconciles the transaction. Attendance is recorded monthly and per lesson, with students and parents viewing real-time summaries through their portals.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP helps schools and vocational institutions move from fragmented administrative work to a connected education management system. By combining admissions, curriculum, grading, attendance, student lifecycle, payment collection, and parent-student portals in one platform, institutions can reduce manual effort, improve data accuracy, and provide a more transparent experience for staff, teachers, students, and parents.

If your school or vocational institution is ready to modernize its education operations, Xerpium ERP can help you consolidate admission, academic, financial, and communication workflows in one scalable system.
