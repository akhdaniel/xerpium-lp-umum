# Animal Feed ERP: How Xerpium Streamlines Feed Mill Operations from Procurement to Distribution

Animal feed manufacturers operate in a demanding environment where raw material costs shift constantly, nutritional specifications must be precise, and every batch needs reliable traceability. Ingredients such as corn, soybean meal, premix, rice bran, pollard, CPO, molasses, minerals, vitamins, and additives move through multiple warehouses and production stages before reaching distributors or farmers. Without an integrated system, feed mills often struggle with disconnected purchasing, formulation, quality control, inventory, and financial data.

Xerpium ERP addresses these challenges with a unified platform designed for feed mill operations. It connects raw material procurement, formulation-based manufacturing, QC lab processes, sales distribution, and financial reporting in one system.

Common Challenges in Animal Feed Manufacturing

  • Volatile commodity prices and multi-supplier procurement with long-term contracts.
  • Complex feed formulations with multi-level bills of materials across grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging.
  • Strict quality control from raw material receiving through in-process checks to finished product guarantees.
  • Multi-warehouse inventory management for corn silos, soybean meal warehouses, premix cold storage, and finished feed warehouses.
  • Distributor credit management, delivery tracking, and financial visibility per feed type.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Animal Feed Operations

Raw Material & Procurement

Xerpium manages the commodity materials that feed mills depend on, including corn, soybean meal, premix, rice bran, pollard, CPO, molasses, minerals, vitamins, and additives. The purchase module supports purchase orders for raw materials with multiple suppliers, long-term contracts, and commodity-based pricing.

Inventory is tracked across multiple warehouses, including corn silos, soybean meal warehouses, cold storage for premix, and finished feed warehouses. During receiving, QC sampling records moisture, protein, fat, and fiber content so quality issues are identified before materials enter production.

Production & Formulation

The formulation module stores nutritional composition per feed type, covering broiler, layer, ruminant, and aquaculture feeds. Each formula is built on a multi-level bill of materials that follows the production sequence: grinding → mixing → pelleting → cooling → bagging.

Manufacturing orders are generated directly from formulations, with work centers for grinding, pellet production, and bagging. Quality checks can be recorded at each stage. Costing calculates HPP per ton of feed using cost rollup from raw materials, labor, and overhead, giving manufacturers accurate product costs.

Quality Control Lab

Xerpium supports a complete QC lab workflow. At raw material receiving, the system records moisture percentage, protein percentage, fat percentage, fiber percentage, and ash percentage. During production, in-process QC tracks particle size, mixing uniformity, and pellet durability index.

For finished products, the system manages final product analysis and nutrient composition guarantees. Each batch can generate a Certificate of Analysis, with lot number traceability that connects finished feed back to the original raw material inputs.

Sales & Distribution

Xerpium handles the full range of animal feed product categories, including broiler starter, grower, and finisher feeds, layer feed, ruminant feed, aquaculture feed, and premix. Sales channels cover distributors, direct farmers, feed stores, modern trade, and toll manufacturing.

Customer management includes distributor receivables, credit limits, due dates, and aging reports. Delivery functions support delivery orders, surat jalan documents, and fleet tracking to farmers and distributors.

Financial Reporting & Tax

The accounting module provides a feed mill chart of accounts with 179 accounts, six-digit account codes, and 32 account groups. The balance sheet covers current assets such as raw material inventory, WIP, and finished feed, as well as fixed assets for grinding, pellet, and bagging equipment, liabilities, and equity.

Profit and loss reporting shows revenue per feed type, including broiler, layer, ruminant, and aquaculture, alongside HPP per ton, operating expenses, and gross profit. Gross profit, operating profit, and net profit are calculated automatically from the report structure. Tax handling includes PPN at 11% and fiscal position exempt settings for animal feed.

Dashboards and AI Assistant

Dashboard Studio provides 14 KPI widgets relevant to feed mill management. Key metrics include Total Revenue from confirmed or done sales orders, Total Orders, Raw Material Cost from purchase orders, Distributor Receivables from outstanding AR invoices, Stock Value across all warehouses, Total Customers, Pending Material POs, Invoices This Month, Revenue Trend over 12 months, Raw Material Cost trend over 12 months, Top Products, and Top Customers.

The AI assistant adds operational speed by allowing users to check stock levels, prices, and order status through natural language. Dashboards can be auto-generated, and natural language queries are converted into SQL for deeper analysis.

Example Workflow in a Feed Mill

A feed mill receives a shipment of corn. QC records moisture and protein content during receiving, and the purchase is matched to the supplier PO. The formulation team prepares a broiler starter formula, and the system generates a manufacturing order. Production records the grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging steps. In-process QC checks mixing uniformity and pellet durability, and the finished batch receives a Certificate of Analysis with lot traceability.

The sales team confirms an order from a distributor. Xerpium checks the distributor’s credit limit, creates the delivery order and surat jalan, and tracks the fleet movement. Accounting posts the transaction with PPN 11% and the exempt fiscal position for animal feed. The dashboard immediately updates revenue, raw material cost, stock value, and distributor receivables.
