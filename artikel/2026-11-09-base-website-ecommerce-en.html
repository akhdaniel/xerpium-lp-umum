Unifying Website & eCommerce Operations with Xerpium ERP Software (Built on Odoo)

Most B2B and retail organizations did not plan their digital presence — it accumulated. A marketing website built by one agency, an online store hosted on a separate platform, a payment gateway connected through a plugin, and an inventory spreadsheet that someone updates manually at the end of each day. Each piece works in isolation, and each handoff between them is a place where orders get lost, stock levels drift, and finance teams spend hours reconciling what was sold against what was actually shipped and paid for.

The cost of this fragmentation is rarely visible on a single invoice. It shows up as oversold products, delayed order confirmations, pricing inconsistencies between channels, and a customer service team that cannot answer a simple question without opening four systems. For companies operating across multiple entities or currencies, the problem compounds quickly.

Xerpium approaches Website & eCommerce differently. Rather than treating the storefront as an external channel that needs to be integrated, Xerpium delivers a website builder, online catalog, and checkout as a base module that sits inside the same ERP as every other business function. The store is not connected to the ERP — it is the ERP.

Common Challenges in Website and eCommerce Operations

  • Disconnected storefront and back office. Product information, pricing, and stock availability live in one system while fulfillment and accounting live in another, forcing manual synchronization or fragile integrations.
  • Catalog maintenance overhead. Every product update has to be entered twice — once for operations, once for the website — which guarantees that the two versions eventually diverge.
  • Checkout data that does not flow downstream. Orders captured online still require re-entry before they can be invoiced, fulfilled, or recognized as revenue.
  • Limited visibility across entities and currencies. Businesses selling through multiple legal entities or in multiple currencies struggle to see consolidated online performance without building reports by hand.
  • No reliable audit trail. When a price changed, who approved a discount, or when an order status was modified becomes difficult to reconstruct during an audit or a customer dispute.

Relevant Xerpium Modules and Capabilities

Website Builder

Xerpium includes a website builder that lets teams create and maintain their public web presence without depending on an external development cycle for every change. Because the builder is part of the ERP platform, the pages it produces draw on the same underlying business data used elsewhere in the system — not a copy of it.

Online Catalog

The online catalog presents products directly from Xerpium's product records. There is no separate catalog database to maintain and no export-import routine to schedule. What the operations team defines in the system is what customers see on the storefront, which removes the most common source of discrepancy between published and actual product information.

Integrated Checkout

Checkout is built into the same module rather than bolted on. Orders placed by customers enter Xerpium as native transactions, which means the information captured at checkout is immediately available to the rest of the platform instead of waiting in a queue for manual transfer.

A Base Module That Connects to Every Other Module

Website & eCommerce is a base Xerpium module that integrates natively with all other modules. This is the structural difference that matters most. The storefront is not an endpoint — it is one entry point into a single shared data model. An order does not have to travel across system boundaries to reach fulfillment, finance, or reporting, because there are no boundaries to cross.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium supports multi-company and multi-currency operation, so organizations running several legal entities or selling into different currency markets can operate them within one platform rather than maintaining parallel installations. The built-in audit trail records system activity, giving finance, compliance, and customer service teams a traceable history when questions arise.

Real-Time Dashboards and Reporting

Real-time dashboards and reports give management current visibility into online performance rather than a view assembled from last week's exports. Because the reporting layer reads the same records the storefront writes to, the numbers reflect the present state of the business.

Xerpium AI Assistant

The Website & eCommerce module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant, allowing teams to interact with their commerce data through the assistant alongside the rest of the ERP suite.

Business Benefits

  • A single source of product, pricing, and order data across the website and the back office
  • Elimination of manual re-entry between the online store and internal operations
  • Consolidated visibility across multiple companies and currencies from one platform
  • Traceable records through the built-in audit trail for compliance and dispute resolution
  • Current, real-time operational reporting instead of periodic manual consolidation
  • Faster catalog updates, since changes made in the system appear directly on the storefront

Example Flow: From Catalog Update to Reported Revenue

Consider a distributor operating two legal entities, one selling domestically and one serving export customers in a different currency.

A product manager updates specifications and pricing on a product record in Xerpium. Because the online catalog reads directly from that record, the updated information is reflected on the storefront without a separate publishing step or synchronization job.

A customer browses the catalog and completes checkout on the website. The order is created inside Xerpium as a native transaction, carrying the correct company assignment and currency from the start — no conversion step, no re-keying.

From there, the order is immediately available to the connected modules in the suite. Finance and operations teams work from the same record the customer created, rather than from a copy that arrived hours later.

Meanwhile, the management team monitors a real-time dashboard that reflects activity across both entities. Because multi-currency is handled within the platform, consolidated figures do not require a manual spreadsheet exercise. If a question arises about when a price changed or who modified an order, the audit trail provides the record.

Conclusion

A website and an online store are not side channels. For many organizations they are now the primary way customers encounter, evaluate, and transact with the business — which makes the gap between the storefront and the ERP an operational liability rather than a technical detail.

Xerpium closes that gap by making Website & eCommerce a base module within the ERP itself, built on Odoo and connected natively to every other part of the suite. The result is one catalog, one order record, one audit trail, and one set of real-time numbers.
