# Electronic OEM Manufacturing with Xerpium ERP: Control BOM, Export, KITE, and Shop Floor in One System

October 8, 2026

Electronic OEM manufacturers operate in a demanding environment: customer-specific product models, version-controlled bills of materials, export documentation, duty exemption facilities, strict quality sampling, and full lot traceability. When these processes live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, errors in BOM versions, shipping documents, or KITE reporting quickly become compliance and margin risks.

Xerpium ERP is an enterprise ERP suite with an AI assistant built for electronic OEM operations. It connects product models, BOMs, OEM customers and suppliers, export operations, trade finance, manufacturing orders, quality control, import supply chains, and OEM-specific accounting in one system.

Common Challenges in Electronic OEM Manufacturing

  • Frequent BOM and customer specification changes across draft, approved, and obsolete versions.
  • Complex export documentation that must remain consistent across delivery orders, packing lists, PEB, certificates of origin, and commercial invoices.
  • KITE facility management with target ratio tracking and actual realization reporting.
  • Shop floor visibility across SMT, assembly, injection molding, calibration, QC inspection, and packing.
  • Lot traceability from component lot to finished lot, manufacturing order, and customer PO.
  • Maintaining accurate landed cost and OEM-specific tax treatment such as PPN and PPh.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses Electronic OEM Complexity

Product Model and BOM Management

Xerpium manages product models with customer part number, HS code, FOB price in USD, weight, volume, and linked products. BOM versions are version-controlled as draft, approved, or obsolete, unique per model, and linked to mrp.bom. Each BOM line includes component, quantity, scrap rate percentage, supplier, and notes. Customer specifications are stored per BOM version, and HS codes support customs descriptions.

Customer and Supplier Management

Xerpium supports OEM customer types including Korean HQ, Korean Subsidiary, Local, and Export, with credit limit, incoterm, and port of loading/discharge. OEM suppliers can be classified as Korean, Local, Chinese, or Singapore, with supplier code, partner type, and payment terms. Supplier ratings track quality, delivery, and price scores into an average total score. Supplier certificates such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001, RoHS, REACH, UL, and CE include expiry tracking. Partner extensions capture is_oem_customer/supplier, parent corporation country, bank account, and SWIFT details.

Export Operations and Compliance

Export delivery orders record container, vessel, port of loading/discharge, and incoterm. PEB records the DJBC registration number and includes CEISA integration for submission. Packing lists capture total packages, gross/net weight, and volume per product model. Certificates of origin support Form E for ASEAN-China, Form AK for ASEAN-Korea, and non-ASEAN documents. Export invoices are issued as commercial invoices in USD with FOB value and incoterm.

Trade, Finance, and KITE

The KITE facility supports import duty exemption with a target ratio of 1.5 and periodic realization tracking. KITE realization compares import value against export value and shows actual ratio versus target. Letters of credit handle sight or usance terms for import and export, with issuance, expiry, and shipping dates. TT/remittance records outgoing and incoming payments with bank references and status tracking. Landed cost is calculated as FOB plus freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling.

Manufacturing Orders and Shop Floor Control

Manufacturing orders extend with customer PO ID and line, product model, FOB price in USD, and target ship date. Work centers include SMT, Assembly, Injection Molding, Calibration, QC Inspection, and Packing. Shift management defines start and end times and assigns shifts per manufacturing order. Yield records capture input quantity, output quantity, scrap quantity, and yield percentage per MO and shift. Downtime records classify machine breakdown, setup change, material missing, quality issue, and power outage. Work orders include cycle time target, scrap budget percentage, and sign-off.

Quality Control and Lot Traceability

Inbound AQL inspection supports levels 1.0, 2.5, and 4.0 with sample size and accept/reject decisions. Quality checks extend xerpium_mrp_quality with AQL, sample size, and reject count. Lot traceability links component lots to finished lots, manufacturing orders, and customer POs. Customer complaints classify defect types such as cosmetic, functional, dimension, missing, and packaging, and track root cause, action, and status. Supplier certificate expiry tracking helps maintain ISO, RoHS, REACH, UL, and CE compliance.

Import and Supply Chain

Import POs are supplier purchase orders with USD currency, incoterm, port, ETD/ETA, and LC number. Inbound inspection performs AQL sampling when goods arrive and records pass/fail results. Supplier rating, landed cost, and supplier certificate expiry tracking apply across the import supply chain.

Accounting and COA

Xerpium provides 168 accounts in a chart of accounts specific to electronic OEM operations. Revenue accounts cover export thermostat or parts, local sales, scrap, and discount. COGS includes raw material, import raw material, direct labor, overhead, and variance. Expenses cover selling costs such as freight and commission, and administrative costs such as salary, BPJS, depreciation of SMT/Assembly/Calibration machines, and ISO, RoHS, and CE certification. The balance sheet tracks current assets such as raw, WIP, and finished inventory, and fixed assets such as SMT, Assembly, Injection, and QC machines. Tax handling includes PPN 11%/12%, PPh 21/23/25/26/Final, and corporate tax.

KPI Dashboard

The dashboard tracks total revenue month to date, open customer POs, active product models, active BOM versions, active work centers, OEM customers, OEM suppliers, and HS codes. A monthly revenue line chart shows 12 months of performance, and a top customers bar chart displays the top 5 by revenue.

AI Assistant Tools

The AI assistant includes five electronic OEM tools: electronic_product_model to search product models by code or name, electronic_supplier to search OEM suppliers by name or country, electronic_customer_po to search customer POs by reference or customer, electronic_suggest_hs_code to suggest HS codes from descriptions, and electronic_check_kite_ratio to check KITE ratio by facility number. Users can query these tools through natural language chat.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth from customer BOM to export invoice reduces version and compliance errors.
  • Export documentation flows from DO to Packing List, PEB, Certificate of Origin, and Invoice, with CEISA integration for PEB submission.
  • KITE ratio tracking against the 1.5 target supports duty exemption compliance.
  • Lot traceability from component to finished good supports customer audits and complaint resolution.
  • Landed cost and the 168-account COA give finance better visibility into margin and tax obligations.
  • AI assistant reduces lookup time for product models, suppliers, POs, HS codes, and KITE ratios.

Example Workflow: From Customer PO to Export and KITE Reporting

A Korean subsidiary customer issues a purchase order. The planning team creates a manufacturing order linked to customer_po_id/line and selects the approved BOM version for the product model. SMT and Assembly shifts are assigned, and yield and downtime records capture shop floor performance. Inbound components pass AQL inspection before use. Once production is complete, export operations generates the delivery order, packing list, PEB through CEISA integration, Form AK certificate of origin, and USD export invoice. Finance records landed cost and tracks KITE realization against the target ratio. The dashboard updates open customer POs and monthly revenue for management visibility.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP brings electronic OEM manufacturing processes into one connected system, from version-controlled BOMs and customer specifications to export documentation, KITE management, shop floor control, and OEM-specific accounting. For manufacturers looking to reduce compliance risk and improve operational visibility, Xerpium offers a practical ERP foundation with AI-assisted business tools.
