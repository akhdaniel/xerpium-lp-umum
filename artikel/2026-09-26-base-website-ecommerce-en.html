# Website & eCommerce ERP: Unify Online Sales and Back-Office Operations with Xerpium

Managing a modern B2B website is rarely just about publishing pages. Product catalogs, customer-specific pricing, multi-currency orders, real-time inventory visibility, and clean transaction records all need to work together. When the website operates separately from the ERP, teams often face manual data entry, inconsistent product information, and delayed reporting. Xerpium ERP addresses this with its Website & eCommerce module, a base module designed to connect digital sales directly into the wider ERP environment.

This article explains the common challenges in website and eCommerce management, how the Xerpium Website & eCommerce module works, and the business benefits of running these operations on a unified ERP platform.

Common Challenges in Website & eCommerce Management

Many B2B organizations recognize that a website is no longer a standalone marketing asset. It is part of daily commercial operations. However, without the right ERP connection, several recurring problems tend to appear:

  • Manual re-entry of orders, customer data, and product information between the website and back-office systems.
  • Difficulty maintaining consistent catalogs, pricing, and product availability across different markets.
  • Limited support for multi-company and multi-currency transactions, which slows down international sales.
  • Weak audit trails that make it hard to trace changes, approvals, or transaction history.
  • Delayed or incomplete reporting because website data is not synchronized with finance, inventory, or sales modules.

These issues are not just operational inconveniences. They create risk, reduce team productivity, and make it difficult for management to trust the numbers coming from the online channel.

How Xerpium’s Website & eCommerce Module Solves These Challenges

The Xerpium Website & eCommerce module is part of the core Xerpium ERP system. It combines essential eCommerce capabilities with the operational depth of an ERP, so online activity and back-office processes remain connected.

Website Builder, Online Catalog, and Integrated Checkout

Xerpium provides a website builder, an online catalog, and an integrated checkout as part of the Website & eCommerce module. This means businesses can manage their digital storefront, present products in a structured online catalog, and accept orders through a checkout process that is connected to the rest of the ERP.

Because checkout is integrated rather than bolted on, order data can flow into the relevant ERP processes without requiring separate middleware or manual export. This reduces the gap between what customers see online and what internal teams process offline.

Built as a Base Module Connected to the Entire Xerpium ERP

One of the most important characteristics of the Website & eCommerce module is that it is a base module in Xerpium ERP. It is designed to unify with all other modules rather than exist as an isolated application.

This unified design supports multi-company and multi-currency operations. Businesses that operate multiple legal entities or sell across different markets can manage website and eCommerce activity within the same ERP environment. The module also supports audit trail functionality, giving organizations a clear record of relevant changes and transactions.

Real-Time Dashboards, Reporting, and Audit Trail

Xerpium includes dashboards and real-time reporting as part of its ERP capabilities. For website and eCommerce teams, this means they do not have to wait for batch updates or separate reporting tools to understand what is happening online.

Real-time dashboards help operational teams monitor activity, while reporting provides a clearer view of performance across the digital channel. Combined with audit trail support, this makes it easier to maintain accountability and review historical activity when needed.

Xerpium AI Assistant Integration

The Website & eCommerce module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This connection allows users to work with AI assistance within the ERP environment, supporting day-to-day tasks and helping teams access relevant information more efficiently.

Because the AI Assistant is part of the same system, it can operate alongside the website and eCommerce data without requiring a separate integration project.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Website & eCommerce

Using the Xerpium Website & eCommerce module as part of a unified ERP can deliver several practical benefits:

  • Reduced manual work by connecting website, catalog, and checkout processes to ERP data.
  • Greater consistency across online catalogs and internal product information.
  • Simplified multi-company and multi-currency management within one platform.
  • Clearer accountability through audit trail support.
  • Faster visibility into digital sales performance through real-time dashboards and reports.
  • More responsive daily operations with AI Assistant integration.

These benefits are most relevant for B2B companies that need their online channel to behave like a controlled business process, not just a marketing site.

A Short B2B Use Case

Consider a B2B distributor operating across multiple countries and currencies. The company uses the Xerpium Website & eCommerce module to maintain an online catalog and accept orders through the integrated checkout. Because the module supports multi-company and multi-currency scenarios, different regional entities can operate within the same ERP structure.

When an order is placed, the information remains connected to the wider Xerpium system. This type of setup replaces fragmented processes with a single operational flow.
