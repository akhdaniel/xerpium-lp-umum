Streamlining Logistics Operations with Xerpium ERP: Shipment, Fleet, and Cold Chain Control

# Streamlining Logistics Operations with Xerpium ERP: Shipment, Fleet, and Cold Chain Control

Logistics teams today manage a highly interconnected chain of shipments, routes, vehicles, containers, warehouses, and customer expectations. When these functions live in separate systems, businesses struggle with poor visibility, inconsistent pricing, delayed invoicing, and limited control over costs. Xerpium ERP addresses this by providing an integrated logistics suite that connects shipment execution, fleet management, container tracking, warehouse operations, and financial reporting in one platform.

This article explores how Xerpium ERP supports modern logistics operations, from route planning and rate calculation to proof of delivery and claims management.

Common Challenges in Logistics Operations

Before implementing a unified logistics ERP, many organizations face recurring operational issues:

  • Shipment, route, and cost data are fragmented across spreadsheets or disconnected tools.
  • Pricing is inconsistent because rates are managed manually or without clear validity periods.
  • Real-time shipment status and proof of delivery are difficult to capture and share.
  • Fleet, container, and warehouse activities are planned separately, causing delays and underutilization.
  • Revenue, cost, and profit margin per shipment are not visible until long after delivery.

Xerpium ERP is designed to resolve these challenges through a modular logistics system that works alongside sales, inventory, fleet, accounting, and AI-powered dashboards.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Logistics Operations

Shipment and Route Management

Xerpium’s shipment module covers the full lifecycle from draft to completed delivery. Users can define shipment types such as pickup, delivery, transfer, return, or multimodal, and assign service types including trucking, forwarding, warehousing, last-mile, project, and cold chain.

Each shipment moves through clear states: Draft, Confirmed, Assigned, In Transit, Delivered, and Completed. Origin and destination details include full addresses and cities, while routes are linked directly to the logistics route master.

The route module stores origin and destination cities with automatic naming, distance in kilometers, transit time in hours, route type such as road, sea, air, rail, or multimodal, estimated cost, and shipment count per route. This structure helps planners compare routes and manage transportation more consistently.

Service Categories and Rate Cards

Service categories allow companies to group logistics activities by type, including trucking, forwarding, warehousing, last-mile, project, and cold chain. Each category has a unique code per company, accounting mappings for income, expense, and COGS, a default product for billing, and a shipment count.

Rate cards provide the pricing engine for logistics services. Xerpium supports pricing dimensions such as price per kilometer, kilogram, cubic meter, or hour. Vehicle types range from motorcycle to container, covering nine vehicle types in total. Rate cards also include minimum charge rules, validity periods, and an auto compute function that calculates shipping prices based on weight, volume, and distance.

Shipment Operations, Invoicing, and Profit Margin

Within each shipment, teams can manage multiple shipment lines with item details, weight, volume, and price per line. Total weight, volume, pieces, and price are automatically summarized. Planned pickup and delivery schedules are recorded alongside actual pickup and delivery times for tracking and performance review.

Financially, Xerpium calculates profit margin as revenue minus actual cost automatically. Shipments can be converted into invoices directly through the create invoice action, reducing manual billing effort and improving cash flow.

Fleet and Container Management

The fleet module integrates vehicle master data with driver management. Drivers are flagged as drivers within the partner system, and each shipment can have a vehicle and driver assigned. Fleet operations also include maintenance schedules, fuel tracking, and utilization analytics.

Container management supports standard ISO container types including 20ft, 40ft, 40HC, and 45HC. The system covers ten container categories such as dry van, reefer, open top, flat rack, tank, ventilated, bulk, car carrier, insulated, and custom. Containers are tracked by ISO 6346 number, ownership type such as owned, leased, or customer-owned, and current status including available, in use, in transit, under maintenance, damaged, lost, or retired.

Location tracking covers depot, customer site, port, terminal, warehouse, in transit, and maintenance facility. Container movements such as gate in/out, transfer, loading, and discharge are recorded with before and after status and condition. Maintenance activities include preventive, corrective, inspection, and certification work with parts, labor, and cost tracking. Certification records such as CSC plate and ISO certification include expiry tracking. Financial information includes purchase price, current value, lease rate, and insurance. For full container load shipments, containers can be linked through a many-to-many relationship on the shipment.

Freight Forwarding and Warehouse Operations

For international and multi-leg movements, the freight forwarding module supports multimodal combinations of road, sea, air, and rail. It handles consolidation of multiple shipments per container, documentation such as Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, and packing list, and customs tracking for PIB/PEB in international shipments. Cost tracking covers ocean freight, handling, and documentation fees.

Warehouse operations include storage zones within warehouses, cross-docking for fast transfer from inbound to outbound, putaway strategies for optimized storage allocation, wave and batch picking, and inter-warehouse stock transfers. This integration connects physical warehouse work directly with outbound shipments.

Real-Time Tracking, Proof of Delivery, and Claims

Xerpium’s tracking module records logistics events such as created, confirmed, picked up, in transit, at hub, out for delivery, delivered, exception, and returned. Each event includes geolocation coordinates, timestamp, photo evidence, status color indicators such as normal, warning, or danger, and the reporting driver or hub personnel.

Proof of delivery captures recipient information, digital signature, actual delivery date and time, and multiple delivery photos. POD status can be pending, delivered, partial, or refused. When a POD is confirmed, the shipment is automatically marked as delivered.

The claim management module supports claim types including damage, loss, delay, shortage, wrong delivery, and other. Claims follow a status flow from open to investigating, then resolved or rejected, and finally closed. Each claim has priority, claim amount, resolved amount, evidence attachments, and a responsible person assigned to handle the case.

Cold Chain and AI Dashboard Features

For temperature-sensitive logistics, Xerpium includes cold chain capabilities with minimum and maximum temperature ranges per shipment, a cold chain flag with validation, real-time temperature logs during transit, and alert triggers when temperatures fall outside the allowed range. This supports compliance for industries such as food, pharmaceutical, and chemicals.

AI dashboard features enable logistics teams to auto-generate dashboards from natural language using DeepSeek, query data with natural language to SQL, and receive AI-driven chart insights on trends, peaks, and anomalies. Copilot chat with SSE streaming provides dashboard context, while conditional formatting highlights values dynamically.

Accounting, Integration, and Workflow Automation

Xerpium connects logistics to the rest of the ERP. Sales orders can automatically create shipments, shipping prices can be pulled from rate cards, and tracking numbers can be attached to sales orders. Inventory stock picking can trigger shipments, and warehouse zone management works with cross-dock and transfer operations.

Accounting integration supports shipment invoicing, multi-service category account mapping, estimated versus actual cost tracking, real-time profit margin per shipment or route, analytic distribution for cost centers, and fleet cost allocation including maintenance, fuel, and driver costs.

The logistics dashboard provides KPI visibility such as on-time delivery metrics and profitability per route. Dynamic stages allow configurable shipment workflows with role-based access control and Python hooks on stage entry. Notifications can be triggered for real-time tracking updates, POD confirmation, and claim status changes.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Logistics ERP

  • A single system connects shipment, route, fleet, container, warehouse, and financial data.
  • Rate cards automate shipping price calculation and reduce pricing errors.
  • Real-time tracking and digital proof of delivery improve customer confidence and reduce disputes.
  • Cold chain monitoring and container certification tracking support compliance in regulated industries.
  • Invoicing and profit margin are generated directly from shipment data, accelerating financial close.
  • Fleet and container utilization become visible and manageable within the same ERP environment.

Example Use Case: From Sales Order to Delivered Shipment

A B2B distributor receives a sales order. The sales order automatically creates a shipment and applies the appropriate service category. The route is selected, and the rate card calculates the shipping price based on weight, volume, and distance. Warehouse staff pick the items using a wave or batch picking strategy, and the goods are cross-docked for outbound loading. A vehicle and driver are assigned from the fleet module.

During transit, the driver records tracking events with geolocation and photo evidence. If the shipment is cold chain, temperature readings are logged and alerts are triggered if values fall outside the allowed range. On arrival, the recipient signs digitally, and the proof of delivery is confirmed, automatically completing the shipment. The system generates an invoice from the shipment lines, calculates profit margin, and updates the logistics dashboard with on-time delivery and route profitability.

Xerpium ERP brings logistics execution, fleet and container management, warehouse operations, cold chain compliance, and financial control into a single connected system. By automating shipment creation, pricing, tracking, proof of delivery, and invoicing, businesses can reduce manual work, improve visibility, and make more informed operational decisions.

If your logistics operation needs better integration across shipments, vehicles, containers, and financial reporting, Xerpium ERP provides the tools to manage the entire process from origin to delivery.
