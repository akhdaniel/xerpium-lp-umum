Sales Solutions with Xerpium: An Odoo-Based ERP Software for Pipeline, Retail, and Order Fulfillment

Sales teams rarely lose deals because of a lack of effort. They lose them because information is scattered. The pipeline lives in a spreadsheet, customer history sits in a messaging app, stock availability is confirmed by walking over to the warehouse, and marketplace orders arrive in a separate dashboard that nobody reconciles until month-end. By the time a sales representative confirms a quantity to a customer, the number may already be wrong.

For B2B distributors, retailers, and manufacturers operating in Indonesia, this fragmentation has a direct commercial cost: delayed quotations, overselling, manual re-entry of orders, and forecasts built on incomplete data. Xerpium, an Odoo-based ERP suite, addresses the problem by placing Sales & CRM on the same platform as Inventory, Purchasing, Manufacturing, and Retail & POS — with an AI Assistant reading the resulting sales data to produce insight, forecasts, and recommendations.

This article outlines the common obstacles sales organizations face and the specific Xerpium modules designed to resolve them.

Common Challenges in Sales Operations

  • Disconnected pipeline and customer data. Opportunities, contact history, and loyalty status are tracked in separate tools, so no one has a complete view of a customer relationship at the moment it matters most.
  • Sales promises that inventory cannot keep. When the sales process is not linked to stock records, orders are confirmed against quantities that are no longer accurate, leading to backorders and cancellations.
  • Multi-channel sprawl in retail. Physical outlets, Shopee, Tokopedia, and food delivery channels each generate orders in their own system, forcing manual consolidation.
  • Manual handoff between sales, purchasing, and production. A confirmed order has to be re-keyed before procurement or manufacturing can act on it, adding delay and transcription errors.
  • Forecasting based on intuition. Without structured analysis of historical sales data, planning becomes guesswork, and sales targets are set without an evidence base.

Xerpium Modules That Support the Sales Function

Sales & CRM

The Sales & CRM module manages the pipeline, customer records, product recommendation, and loyalty programs from a single platform. Rather than treating lead management and customer retention as separate disciplines, Xerpium consolidates them so that a sales representative sees the opportunity stage, the customer's history, recommended products, and loyalty standing in one place.

Product recommendation supports upselling and cross-selling during an active conversation instead of after the fact. Loyalty program management, handled in the same system, means repeat-customer incentives are applied consistently across the sales process rather than administered through side files.

Retail & POS

For businesses with a retail footprint, the Retail & POS module covers point-of-sale operations, marketplace connections to Shopee and Tokopedia, multi-outlet management, and GoBiz GoFood integration. This matters because retail sales today are rarely single-channel. An order may originate at a counter, through a marketplace listing, or via a food delivery platform — and each channel draws on the same stock.

Multi-outlet support allows businesses running several locations to operate under one system rather than maintaining isolated installations per store. Marketplace and GoFood integration brings externally generated orders into the same operational flow as in-store transactions.

Integrated Stock and Order Flow

Sales in Xerpium connects directly to Inventory and continues into Purchasing or Manufacturing, producing a continuous order-to-delivery process. This is the structural difference between an ERP approach and a standalone CRM tool.

When a sales order is confirmed, it does not sit in isolation waiting for someone to notify the warehouse. The order moves through the same system that holds stock data, and where fulfillment requires procurement or production, the process continues into Purchasing or Manufacturing without a manual bridge. Sales commitments and operational capacity stay aligned because they are recorded in one place.

AI-Assisted Sales Analysis

The Xerpium AI Assistant reads sales data to generate insight, forecasts, and recommendations. Because the assistant works on data already captured through the Sales, Retail, and Inventory flow, the analysis reflects actual transactions rather than a separately maintained reporting file.

For sales management, this supports a shift from retrospective reporting to forward-looking planning: understanding which patterns are emerging, what demand is likely to look like, and where attention should be directed.

Business Benefits

  • A single source of truth for customer and pipeline data, reducing time spent searching across systems before a customer conversation.
  • Accurate order commitments, because sales is connected directly to Inventory rather than relying on verbal stock checks.
  • Consolidated multi-channel retail operations, with POS, marketplace, and delivery-platform orders handled in one application.
  • Faster order-to-delivery execution, as confirmed sales orders flow into Purchasing or Manufacturing without re-entry.
  • Evidence-based forecasting and recommendations through the AI Assistant's analysis of sales data.
  • Scalable multi-outlet management for businesses expanding their retail presence.

Example Flow: From Opportunity to Delivery

Consider a distributor with a showroom, two retail outlets, and active Shopee and Tokopedia storefronts.

A lead enters the Sales & CRM pipeline. The sales representative opens the customer record, reviews purchase history, and uses product recommendation to propose a complementary item alongside the requested product. The customer's loyalty status is visible in the same view, so the applicable program benefit is applied during quotation rather than negotiated separately.

When the customer accepts, the order is confirmed in the system. Because Sales is linked to Inventory, available stock is reflected immediately. Part of the order can be fulfilled from existing stock; the remainder requires replenishment, so the process continues into Purchasing — or, for manufactured items, into Manufacturing — without anyone re-entering the order details.

Meanwhile, the retail outlets continue transacting through POS, and marketplace orders from Shopee and Tokodepia arrive through the same integrated environment, drawing on the same inventory records.

At the end of the period, the AI Assistant analyzes the combined sales data across channels, surfacing insight on performance patterns and producing a forecast that informs the next planning cycle.

Conclusion

Sales performance depends less on adding tools than on removing the gaps between them. Xerpium brings pipeline management, customer loyalty, multi-channel retail, inventory, procurement, and production into one Odoo-based ERP system, with an AI Assistant that turns the resulting sales data into usable insight.
