# Generic ERP Without the Gaps: How Xerpium Unifies Sales, Operations, and Finance

Many growing businesses look for a generic ERP to replace disconnected spreadsheets and point tools. The goal is simple: one system for sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting, HR, and operations. In practice, many generic ERP platforms stop at basic record-keeping. Teams still handle approvals, multi-warehouse logistics, local payroll, quality control, and financial reporting outside the system.

Xerpium takes a different approach. It is an enterprise ERP suite with deep, connected modules and an AI assistant that turns natural language into real business actions. This article explains the common gaps in a generic ERP environment and how Xerpium closes them.

Common Challenges in a Generic ERP Environment

Many teams experience the same limitations when a generic ERP is treated as a full operational platform:

  • Core modules are disconnected, so sales, inventory, and accounting data require manual reconciliation.
  • Approval and validation workflows are shallow, creating bottlenecks in order confirmation, purchasing, and stock movements.
  • Reporting is slow or too limited, especially for multi-company consolidation and financial statements.
  • Industry-specific operations such as lot and serial tracking, BPJS and PPh21 payroll, quality control, and project profitability are missing.
  • User adoption suffers when teams must switch between tools because the ERP cannot handle CRM, e-commerce, or natural-language commands.

Xerpium Modules That Turn Generic ERP into a Unified Operating System

Xerpium covers the full ERP scope without forcing teams into generic, one-size-fits-all processes.

Sales & CRM

Xerpium includes lead pipeline management, opportunity kanban, probability tracking, expected revenue, lost reason analysis, activity planning, and email integration. Quotations support revision tracking and automatic cancellation of expired quotes. Sale orders run through an automatic workflow covering validation, invoicing, payment, and stock updates with multi-level approval. Pricing supports global or fixed discounts, pricelist surcharges, product recommendations, loyalty programs, and coupons. Delivery can be split by date or multi-address, with lot and serial selection and per-line warehouse sourcing. Invoicing includes advance payment, invoice plans, forced invoicing, split payment, and payment sheets.

Purchase & Inventory

Purchase requests follow a full workflow from draft through approval, in progress, and done, with allocation to purchase order lines. RFQs and purchase orders support blanket orders, order types, multi-tier approval, auto-approval, approval blocks, and exception rules. Down payment features include advance payment, deposit invoices, invoice plans, and work acceptance for government or construction scenarios. Supplier management covers rating, certificates, inbound AQL inspection, allowed products, and supplier calendars.

Inventory is built for multi-warehouse operations with location zones, areas, racks, and bins. It includes putaway rules, ABC strategy, stock movements with dual approval, barcode scanning, and WMS mobile features such as cluster picking, GS1 barcode support, and dangerous goods handling. Lot and serial tracking supports auto-creation, import, filtering, return propagation, archiving, and locking. Cycle counting includes scheduled rules, discrepancy tracking, lockdown, and verification requests. Valuation offers FIFO reports through SVL, stock cards per product per warehouse, landed cost, and available-to-promise quantities.

Accounting & Finance

Xerpium provides general ledger, trial balance, partner ledger, and aged accounts receivable and payable reports with XLSX export. Cash and bank management includes cash books, bank statement import through OFX, CSV, or PDF, and reconciliation. Invoicing supports multiple layouts, credit limits, follow-up, post-dated cheques, and journal audit. Fixed assets include a registry, linear or degressive depreciation, and disposal. Budgeting compares budget versus actual with percentage achievement and analytic views.

Turbo Report uses direct SQL to generate financial reports in under one minute. It includes balance sheet, profit and loss, trial balance, partner ledger, cash book, and bank book. Multi-company consolidation, inter-company elimination, month-over-month and year-over-year comparison, report caching, and an automated year-end closing wizard are also available. The system includes 32 ready-to-use charts of accounts for industries such as manufacturing, retail, cooperatives, hospitals, and hotels.

HR & Payroll

Employee management covers personal data, family records, documents, PKWT and PKWTT contracts, and mutation history. Recruitment includes applicant tracking, AI CV screening, AI interview support, and a question bank. Attendance handles check-in and check-out, calendar-based overtime, workdays, and holidays. Leave management supports annual leave, sick leave, and permission leave with email-to-leave workflows.

Payroll Indonesia is built in, including BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan components such as JKK, JKM, JHT, and JP, as well as PPh21 using TER and Pasal 17 methods. Employee loans and salary advances can be deducted automatically from payslips. KPI tracking supports performance indicators per position, contract-based evaluation, and batch processing. Offboarding includes resignation, exit checklists, and relieving letters.

Manufacturing, Quality & Project Management

Manufacturing covers manufacturing orders, multi-level BOMs, work centers, work orders, and routing. Quality control uses control points, checks, alerts, and AQL. PLM includes engineering change orders, BOM versioning, and approval workflows. Maintenance supports equipment registries and preventive or corrective maintenance work orders. Costing includes WIP journals, cost rollup for material, labor, and overhead, and automatic journal entries from component consumption through WIP to finished goods and COGS.

Project management supports Scrum, Waterfall, and Kanban. Teams can plan sprints, backlogs, velocity, phase gates, milestones, and deliverables. Task boards include story points, priority, type, tags, and assignee tracking. Team allocation manages employee roles, date ranges, and hours per week. Timesheets support weekly or bi-weekly submission, approval, billable and non-billable work, and portal access. Budget and billing cover fixed price, time and materials, milestone billing, budget versus actual, and profitability percentage.

AI Assistant, Dashboard Studio, Dynamic Stage, and Commerce

The AI assistant works across the entire suite through natural language. Users can create sales orders, purchase orders, and invoices by chat, look up products, customers, and stock, and trigger MRP intents such as creating a manufacturing order or confirming a work order. It includes more than 100 tools, supports 11 languages, and can process spreadsheet, PDF, and image attachments.

Dashboard Studio offers 19 chart types, AI-generated dashboards, natural-language-to-SQL queries, ORM model queries, raw PostgreSQL SQL, global filter bars, and 33 ready-to-use industry dashboards. Dynamic Stage provides configurable stages for models such as sales orders, purchase orders, and stock movements. It includes Python hooks, server actions, RBAC, kanban drag-and-drop views, and automatic synchronization between stages and native state fields.

For commerce, Xerpium includes point of sale with multi-cashier and offline mode, GoBiz GoFood integration with OAuth 2.0 and QRIS payment, Shopee and Tokopedia marketplace synchronization, a website builder with cart and checkout, and a customer portal for order history and invoice downloads.

Example Flow: A Sales Order That Runs Through the Entire System

A sales team creates a quotation with revision tracking. Once confirmed, the sale order enters an automatic workflow with multi-level approval. The delivery is split by commitment date or address, with warehouse sourcing and lot or serial selection on the sale order lines. When goods are delivered, stock is updated and valuation is recorded.

The invoice can be generated automatically or through an invoice plan with advance payment. Accounting entries post to the general ledger. A dashboard then shows revenue, stock availability, and cash flow in real time. Meanwhile, the AI assistant can create the sales order, look up product details, or ask for a report using natural language. This is not a generic handoff between separate tools. It is one connected process.
