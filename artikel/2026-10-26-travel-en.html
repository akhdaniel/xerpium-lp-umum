# How Xerpium ERP Transforms Hajj & Umrah Travel Operations from Registration to Return

Managing a Hajj or Umrah travel program is one of the most operationally demanding tasks in the travel industry. Beyond booking flights and hotels, travel operators must coordinate hundreds of pilgrims, track sensitive documents, manage installment payments, distribute equipment, and comply with complex Indonesian tax regulations — all while keeping families informed and regulators satisfied. Spreadsheets and disconnected tools simply cannot keep up. Xerpium ERP offers a purpose-built solution that unifies every dimension of Hajj and Umrah operations into a single, intelligent platform.

---

Common Challenges Facing Hajj & Umrah Travel Operators

  • Fragmented pilgrim data. Jamaah identity, passport validity, visa status, and health information are often scattered across multiple files and staff members, creating dangerous blind spots before departure.
  • Document compliance risk. Tracking nine document types per pilgrim — each with its own expiry date and approval status — manually leads to missed renewals and last-minute rejections.
  • Payment collection complexity. Installment schedules involving down payments, interim payments, and final settlements are difficult to manage without automated invoicing and overdue alerts.
  • Equipment distribution errors. Allocating gender-aware pilgrim kits across hundreds of participants without a system leads to missing items, wrong sizes, and untracked distributions.
  • Tax and accounting compliance. Indonesian travel businesses must apply multiple tax types — PPN, PPh 23, PPh 26, and others — correctly across vendor payments and customer invoices, which is error-prone without proper configuration.

---

How Xerpium ERP Addresses These Challenges

Pilgrim (Jamaah) Management

Every pilgrim profile in Xerpium stores a full set of structured data: 16-digit NIK, date and place of birth, gender, education, occupation, blood type, wheelchair needs, and special dietary requirements. Passport validity is checked automatically — the system flags any passport expiring within six months. Visa numbers, issue dates, and expiry dates are tracked with automated status monitoring. For female pilgrims, a mahram linkage connects them to the corresponding male guardian in the same record. Emergency contacts are stored per pilgrim, and a computed field called document_complete confirms whether all four critical documents — KTP, passport, photo, and vaccine certificate — have been approved.

Document Workflow

Xerpium manages nine document types per jamaah, including KTP, Passport, Photo 3×4, Sertifikat Vaksin, Akta Nikah, Akta Lahir, KK, SK Sehat, and additional attachments. Each document follows a structured workflow: Pending → Approved or Rejected, with verifier identity and timestamp recorded at every step. If a document needs revision, it can be reset to Pending for re-verification. The system prevents duplicate document types per pilgrim, and automated email notifications inform pilgrims when their documents are approved or rejected — including rejection notes.

Package & Departure Management

Xerpium supports three package tiers — Reguler, Plus, and VIP — each with defined durations, quotas, assigned hotels in Makkah and Madinah, number of nights, and room pricing across four configurations: Quad, Triple, Double, and Single. Departures are auto-coded in the format KEP/YYYY/NNNN and progress through eight dynamic stages from Persiapan through to Selesai or Dibatalkan. Quota utilization is tracked in real time — showing used seats from confirmed sale orders, available seats, and fill percentage. Each departure can assign a group leader and multiple guides or muthawwif from the HR employee database.

Registration & Payment Schedule

When a pilgrim registers, the operator selects a package, departure, and room type. Pricing is populated automatically. Upon confirmation, Xerpium generates a three-term payment schedule: DP 30%, Angsuran 40%, and Pelunasan 30%. Each term can be invoiced with a single click, and the system tracks total invoiced, total paid, and remaining balance per pilgrim. An overpayment prevention mechanism validates that total invoiced amounts never exceed the package price.

Purchase Integration

Xerpium connects directly to procurement through four purchase order types: Hotel Makkah, Hotel Madinah, Flight, and Visa. A guided wizard auto-fills vendor information from the hotel master and calculates quantities based on confirmed pilgrim counts. Duplicate PO prevention ensures the same type cannot be created twice for the same departure. Purchase status and count are visible directly from the departure record.

Equipment Distribution

Each package tier has its own equipment template covering 15 or more items — luggage, ihram cloth for men, mukena for women, batik uniform, guidebook, sandals, money belt, ID card, and first aid kit, among others. Gender-aware filtering ensures male pilgrims receive ihram and female pilgrims receive mukena automatically. Clothing sizes from S through XXXL are recorded per pilgrim. Distribution follows a workflow from Draft through Confirmed, Packed, and Distributed, with automatic stock picking that reduces inventory and tracks acceptance per individual pilgrim.

Tax Configuration & Accounting Integration

Xerpium includes a travel-specific chart of accounts with 180 accounts across 33 groups, and nine pre-configured tax types covering PPN 11%, PPh 23 at 2% and 4%, PPh 26 at 20% for foreign parties such as Saudi airlines, PPh Final for building rental, PPN reverse charge for foreign services, and stamp duty. A dedicated fiscal position for Hajj and Umrah operations applies PPN exemption where applicable. Financial reports including Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, and Trial Balance are available out of the box.

Automated Reminders & Dashboard

A daily cron job checks passport expiry and sends email alerts for passports expiring within six months. Departure reminders are sent at H-30 and H-7. The management dashboard displays 15 KPI tiles covering total pilgrims, total revenue, active departures, pending documents, passports near expiry, and more. Charts include registration trends, revenue trends, document status distribution, and upcoming departures — with AI-assisted dashboard generation available through Xerpium's built-in AI Assistant.

Example Use Case: A Travel Bureau Managing 200 Pilgrims

A travel bureau in Jakarta opens a VIP Umrah departure for the upcoming season. The system auto-generates a departure code, sets the quota from the VIP package, and assigns flights from Garuda Indonesia displayed as "Garuda Indonesia GA-981 (Jakarta → Jeddah)." As pilgrims register, payment schedules are created automatically. The document team reviews uploads and approves or rejects each one — pilgrims receive instant email notifications. Thirty days before departure, the system sends automatic H-30 reminders. On packing day, the equipment distribution module generates individual kits with correct gender filtering and clothing sizes. Finance issues invoices per payment term with one click, and management monitors revenue realization from the dashboard in real time.
