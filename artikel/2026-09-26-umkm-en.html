# Xerpium for SMBs: A Unified ERP with Built-in AI Assistant for Growing Teams

Small and medium-sized businesses often run sales, stock, and finance in separate applications. Those fragmented tools create extra manual work, make data inconsistent, and limit how quickly owners can understand business performance. Xerpium ERP addresses this by unifying Sales, CRM, Purchasing, Inventory, and Accounting in one centralized platform. It also includes an AI Assistant directly inside the ERP and offers a pricing model designed for SMB budgets, starting at Rp150,000 per user per month with Xerpium Base.

Common challenges for SMBs

  • Many SMBs depend on multiple disconnected applications for selling, managing stock, and handling finances.
  • Data is spread across systems, so teams often re-enter information and reconcile records manually.
  • Traditional ERP options can feel costly or complex, especially when each module requires a separate purchase.
  • Lengthy setup and configuration can delay the time to start using a new system.
  • Without integrated analytics, business owners may struggle to read daily data and spot trends quickly.

How Xerpium ERP addresses these challenges

One platform for Sales, CRM, Purchasing, Inventory, and Accounting

Xerpium brings the core operational functions into one system. Instead of maintaining separate tools, SMB teams can work with the same centralized data across modules. Each module supports a key part of the business:

  • Sales: sales workflows sit alongside customer, inventory, and accounting data.
  • CRM: customer records remain connected to daily transactions.
  • Purchasing: purchasing activity feeds the same system used for inventory and accounting.
  • Inventory: stock data is maintained centrally with sales and purchasing records.
  • Accounting: financial records are supported by operational data from the same platform.

Because these modules share one environment, SMBs can reduce duplicate entry and keep their records aligned as transactions happen.

SMB-friendly pricing with all modules included

Xerpium Base starts at Rp150,000 per user per month. Each company receives 500 AI credits per month. One license covers all modules, and there are no hidden fees. For SMBs, this means the cost is predictable and the team does not have to choose between essential capabilities based on budget.

Instant onboarding with automatic baseline setup

Xerpium reduces the typical setup burden by preparing the system based on the type of business. When a company selects its business type—such as retail, services, distribution, or others—Xerpium prepares the chart of accounts, applications, and baseline system automatically. The team can then directly import data and start transacting. This onboarding approach is designed to help SMBs move from setup to operational use more quickly.

AI Assistant for daily operational support

The AI Assistant in Xerpium is available directly within the ERP. It supports sales analysis, operational automation, and helps users read business data. For an SMB without a large analytics team, this built-in support can make daily data reviews more accessible and actionable.

Business benefits of Xerpium for SMBs

  • A single centralized platform replaces separate applications for sales, stock, and finance.
  • Predictable per-user pricing starts at Rp150,000 per user per month.
  • One license includes Sales, CRM, Purchasing, Inventory, and Accounting.
  • 500 AI credits per company per month support AI-assisted sales analysis and operational automation.
  • Automatic chart of accounts, applications, and baseline system speed up onboarding.
  • No hidden fees make budgeting simpler for growing teams.
