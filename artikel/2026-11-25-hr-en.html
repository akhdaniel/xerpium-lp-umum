Common HR Challenges in Growing Organizations

Fragmented Data Across Tools

Most HR teams start with spreadsheets and add point solutions as headcount grows. The result is duplicated master data: an employee's name and join date may exist in three places, and none of them updates the others. Payroll errors usually trace back to this fragmentation rather than to calculation mistakes.

Manual Payroll and Statutory Components

Payroll in Indonesia involves statutory elements such as BPJS and PPh21. Calculating these manually each cycle consumes significant time and creates audit risk, particularly when headcount changes mid-month or when employees move between pay grades.

Administrative Load on HR for Routine Requests

Leave requests, expense claims, and pay slip copies are low-value tasks that still require HR attention when there is no self-service channel. For example, an HR officer may spend part of each week printing pay slips or chasing managers for approval signatures.

Slow, Inconsistent Recruitment Screening

Screening a large volume of CVs manually is slow and difficult to keep consistent across reviewers — especially when applications arrive in more than one language.

Xerpium HR Modules and What Each One Does

Complete HR and Payroll

The core module covers personnel administration (SDM), payroll including BPJS and PPh21, attendance, timesheet, and KPI. Because these functions sit in one module rather than in separate systems, attendance and timesheet data feed payroll directly, and KPI records stay attached to the same employee profile used for compensation.

This also means HR reporting draws from a single dataset. For example, a manager reviewing a team member's KPI is looking at the same record that payroll and attendance reference.

ESS Mobile App for Employee Self-Service

Xerpium provides an employee self-service application built with Flutter, designed to stay lightweight on staff devices. Employees can record attendance from mobile, submit leave and permit requests, file expense claims, view pay slips, and process approvals.

The practical effect is that routine HR transactions move to the people who originate them. For example, a field sales representative can clock in, submit a travel expense, and check last month's pay slip without contacting the HR office.

AI Recruitment with the Xerpium AI Assistant

The AI Assistant supports the hiring process through CV screening, AI interviews, and candidate assessment, with multilingual capability. This helps recruiters work through applicant volume more consistently and makes it easier to evaluate candidates who apply in different languages.

Recruiters still make the hiring decision; the AI Assistant handles the repetitive first-pass work and produces structured assessment output for review.

Why the Accounting Integration Matters

Payroll Cost Posting Without Re-Entry

Salary costs from the HR module post automatically into accounting journals. There is no export-and-import step between payroll and the general ledger, and no risk of a payroll run being approved but never recorded in the books.

More Accurate and Timely Financial Reporting

Because payroll expense reaches the ledger as part of the payroll process itself, financial reports reflect actual labour cost for the period. Finance teams close faster, and management reviews payroll cost alongside other expense lines in the same reporting structure.

Business Benefits of a Connected HR System

  • Less duplicate work. Attendance, timesheet, and payroll share one data source, removing manual transfer between systems.
  • Lower administrative burden. Self-service shifts routine requests and approvals to employees and managers.
  • Faster, more consistent hiring. AI-supported screening and interviews standardize the early stages of recruitment.
  • Reliable financial reporting. Automatic journal posting of payroll costs keeps accounting aligned with HR activity.

Example Flow: From Attendance to Journal Entry

Consider a monthly payroll cycle in a company using Xerpium.

Throughout the month, employees record attendance through the ESS Mobile App and submit leave requests, which managers approve in the same app. Project staff log hours against timesheets. When payroll is processed, the HR and Payroll module uses this attendance and timesheet data together with each employee's compensation record, and calculates BPJS and PPh21 components.

Pay slips become available to employees in the ESS app. At the same time, the resulting salary cost posts into the accounting journals, so the finance team sees the payroll expense in the period's reports without a separate entry.
