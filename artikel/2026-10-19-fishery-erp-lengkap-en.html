Complete ERP solution for Fishery: End-to-End Traceability, Grading, and Cold Chain Management with Xerpium

October 19, 2026

Fishery companies often run separate tools for vessel trips, receiving, grading, processing, cold storage, accounting, and export documents. Catch data is rekeyed, traceability breaks between departments, and profitability per trip or lot becomes difficult to prove. Xerpium ERP replaces those disconnected workflows with one suite that connects master data, operational transactions, quality, inventory, accounting, fleet, HR, and customs documentation in a single traceability chain.

Tantangan umum di Fishery

  • Catch data is scattered across trip sheets, spreadsheets, and warehouse logs, with no link from vessel, area, and species to stock lots.
  • Manual grading creates inconsistent grade, size, and price decisions because temperature and freshness are not connected to pricing rules.
  • Cold chain breaks are discovered too late when cold room temperatures are logged manually or not monitored in real time.
  • Processing yield, byproducts, and waste are not tied back to the receiving batch, so true cost per finished product is unclear.
  • Traceability stops at the warehouse door, making it hard to give buyers or regulators a public, verifiable lot history.

Cakupan solusi Xerpium

Fishery Master Data

Xerpium structures the core fishery data before transactions begin. Fish Species holds scientific name, unique code for lot numbering and traceability, default product mapping, and multi-language names. Fish Vessel stores registration, vessel type, captain relation to HR employee, nominal capacity in kilograms, and fleet integration with fleet.vehicle. Fish Area records fishing ground, GPS reference, area classification based on regulation, and track record per trip. Fish Grade & Size manages Grade A/B/C with min/max score, size classification per species by weight range, automatic grading rules, and pricing rules per kilogram based on grade and size.

Fishing Trip and Crew Management

The Fishing Trip Management module follows a lifecycle from Draft → Departed → Returned → Received → Closed. It supports crew management with roles such as captain, engineer, and crew, including share percent. Each trip tracks catch estimate per species versus actual weight, fuel and ice quantity, plus cost tracking for fuel, ice, logistic, wage, and maintenance. Profitability is calculated automatically as total cost versus revenue, giving profit per trip without manual allocation.

Receiving, Grading, and Pricing

Fish Receiving & Grading uses a controlled flow: Draft → Weighing → QC → Approved → Posted. Each line item records gross, tare, and net weight, temperature log, and freshness score. Auto-grade rules use temperature and freshness to assign grade; if a line does not match any rule, it can be auto-rejected or put on hold. The receiving QC status moves from pending to pass, hold, or reject.

Auto Pricing System calculates price per kilogram from the most specific matching rule. Priority logic checks supplier match first, then grade match, then size match, then default. Multi-supplier pricing and seasonal date ranges are supported, and pricing can be applied directly on the receiving line.

Processing, Cold Storage, and Traceability

Processing Batch manages yield from input weight to output plus waste with yield percent. Supported process types include fillet, loin, steak, block, whole frozen, and fish meal. Multiple output products and byproducts are tracked, expected yield per species is compared with actual, and the batch can auto-create mrp.production from manufacturing integration. A QC stage runs before the processing batch is completed.

Cold Storage & Cold Chain covers cold room types such as chilled, frozen, and blast freezer. Temperature thresholds have min and max alerts. Capacity management compares capacity in kilograms with current weight and utilization percent. IoT integration enables automatic temperature logging, with logs recording source as manual, IoT, or import. Cold chain events track receiving → storage → transfer → delivery movement.

Lot & Traceability extends stock lots with species, vessel, trip, area, grade, and temperature fields. Parent-child lot relations preserve upstream/downstream traceability through processing. QR code generation creates public traceability, covering vessel → receiving → processing → delivery → customer, including cold chain events per lot and a public URL endpoint per lot/QR.

Enterprise and Fishery Accounting

Xerpium provides enterprise-level capabilities: multi-warehouse hierarchy, multi-company or branch operations with inter-company transactions, role-based access control, multi-currency with exchange rate sync and unrealized gain/loss, and consolidation reports for P&L and Balance Sheet with inter-company elimination.

Fishery accounting features auto-create vendor bills from receiving lines and auto-post stock movement with journal entries when receiving is posted. Crew cost accrual is tracked per trip, multi-currency handles export transactions, cost per lot or traceability batch supports HPP, and analytic distribution assigns costs to vessel or trip cost centers.

Fishery Integrations

  • Sales: fish products appear on sale order lines, traceability QR is generated from delivery orders, and cold chain events auto-log from outgoing picking.
  • Purchase: fish receiving originates from purchase orders, vendor bills auto-create, and supplier grading and pricing rules apply.
  • Inventory: stock lots are extended with fishery fields, serial/lot tracking covers species, grade, and trip, and cold storage locations are managed.
  • Fleet: vessels are managed through fleet.vehicle, captain and crew link to HR employees, and maintenance scheduling is available per vessel.
  • HR: crew assignment uses HR employees, wage tracking follows trip crew, and role-based access separates captain, crew, and admin.
  • Quality: quality alerts arise from cold room temperature, grading rules act as QC checkpoints, and freshness scoring and defect tracking are supported.
  • Bea Cukai: export documents for fishery products, HS Code tracking for fish export, and INATRADE integration for export documentation are included.

AI Dashboard Features

AI-assisted dashboards generate complete dashboards from natural language, translate natural language into real-time SQL queries, provide chart insights that analyze trends, peaks, and anomalies with recommendations, offer Copilot chat with SSE-streamed responses in dashboard context, and apply conditional formatting with dynamic colors based on values.

Alur end-to-end

A purchase order is created for a supplier. The receiving clerk records the fish receiving line with species, vessel, trip, area, grade, and size. QC auto-grades based on temperature and freshness score, then auto pricing computes the price per kilogram. Posting receiving auto-creates the vendor bill and stock movement journal. The lot enters cold storage with IoT temperature logs and cold chain events. A processing batch converts the lot into fillet or block outputs, tracks yield and waste, and creates parent-child lots with QR codes. The sales team creates a sale order for those output lots, and delivery generates traceability QR and outgoing cold chain events. Accounting recognizes revenue, cost per lot, crew cost accrual, and analytic distribution per vessel or trip in the same dataset.

Manfaat bisnis

  • One master data set for species, vessel, area, grade, and size removes duplicate entry and manual mapping.
  • Auto-grading and pricing rules make receiving consistent, auditable, and faster.
  • Cold room temperature alerts and capacity utilization reduce cold chain losses.
  • Full lot traceability from vessel to customer with QR codes builds buyer and regulator confidence.
  • Per-trip and per-lot costing reveals true profitability and waste.
  • Multi-company, multi-currency, and consolidation support export-oriented fishery operations.

Langkah implementasi

  1. Data preparation: enter fish species with scientific name, unique code, and default product; register vessels and captains; define fishing areas with GPS references; configure grade/size groups and pricing rules.
  2. Accounting and parameters: set chart of accounts, analytic accounts per vessel and trip, multi-currency, warehouse and cold storage locations, and user roles for captain, crew, QC, and admin.
  3. Pilot one cycle: run one trip from receiving through grading, pricing, vendor bill, cold storage, processing, and delivery. Validate auto-grading, yield, and traceability outputs.
  4. Rollout to all locations: import open trips and historical lots, connect IoT temperature sensors, enable auto-alerts, and train users according to role-based access.
  5. Evaluate and refine: review dashboards, trip profitability, QC reject/hold rates, cold chain events, and pricing rule performance. Adjust grading and pricing rules before scaling further.
