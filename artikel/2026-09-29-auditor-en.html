# Audit and Tax Consultant ERP: How Xerpium Streamlines Engagements, Working Papers, and Compliance

Audit firms and tax consultants operate in a demanding environment where documentation, compliance, time tracking, and billing must work together without friction. Yet many practices still rely on disconnected spreadsheets, shared folders, and manual review processes. This creates delays, weak visibility into profitability, and higher risk when managing complex client work.

Xerpium ERP offers an integrated solution designed for audit and tax practices. It brings engagement management, working paper documentation, time tracking, billing, compliance, and reporting into a single system. With built-in AI advisory and a client portal, Xerpium helps firms run engagements more efficiently and with stronger control.

Common Challenges for Audit Firms and Tax Consultants

Firms handling audit, tax, and advisory work often face similar operational bottlenecks:

  • Multiple engagement types — audit, tax, advisory, due diligence, and forensic work — are managed in separate systems or files, making it difficult to see the full picture.
  • Working papers, evidence, and checklists are scattered across email, local drives, and paper, causing version control issues and slow partner review.
  • Timesheets and billing are manual, making it hard to track billable hours by staff level, calculate cost rates, or measure profit margins accurately.
  • Compliance checklists for ISA, SAP, PPh, and UU Perpajakan require consistent sign-off and follow-up, which is difficult without structured workflows.
  • Firm leaders lack real-time visibility into engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses These Challenges

Engagement Management

Xerpium treats every audit, tax, or advisory project as a structured engagement. Each engagement can be set up per client and per year, moving through stages from draft to planning, execute, review, and complete.

Within each engagement, teams define scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, and team assignment. A dedicated Client Portal allows clients to upload documents, view engagement progress, and download final reports. Multiple engagement types — audit, tax, advisory, due diligence, and forensic — can be managed in one system. Budget tracking compares estimates against actual cost and shows profit margin per engagement.

Working Paper & Documentation

Working papers are organized by audit area, including income tax, balance sheet, and internal control. Teams can upload supporting evidence such as documents, photos, emails, and notes directly into the system.

Standard audit checklists support compliance checks and sign-off. Partners and directors can review each working paper and approve or reject it with a clear workflow. Findings are recorded with severity levels, recommendations, and follow-up status, so issues do not get lost.

Time Tracking & Billing

Timesheets allow staff to log hours per engagement, per task, and per staff member. Billable hours are automatically calculated using rates for each staff level, such as partner, senior, and staff.

Xerpium also tracks internal cost rates, making it easier to evaluate profitability by comparing billable value against internal cost. Invoices can be generated automatically from timesheets or milestone billing. Fee proposals can be prepared as quotations before the engagement begins, creating a clear link between proposed fees and actual billing.

Compliance & Reporting

Compliance checklists are built around regulations such as ISA, SAP, PPh, and UU Perpajakan. This helps teams follow required procedures consistently and maintain audit trails.

The report structure includes 179 accounts specifically designed for Indonesian audit firms. Turbo Report can generate client financial statements in under one minute, reducing the time spent on report preparation. The dashboard provides visibility into engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff. AI advisory supports recommendations for computerization and tax planning using natural language.

Integrated Modules for End-to-End Control

Xerpium ERP connects audit work to accounting, project management, HR, and dashboards, creating a complete operational view.

Auditor ↔ Accounting

Xerpium includes a 179-account chart of accounts designed for audit firms, covering balance sheet and profit and loss. Invoices can be generated from timesheets and milestone billing, while journals handle fee and cost allocation.

Auditor ↔ Project

Each engagement functions as a project, and each task behaves like a working paper. Timesheets are tracked per task, budgets are monitored, and review and approval workflows run within the project structure.

Auditor ↔ HR

Staff structures reflect audit firm roles such as partner, senior, and staff levels. Each level has its own rate, and Xerpium compares billable rates against cost rates. Performance review data is also available per staff member.

Auditor ↔ Dashboard

Dashboards display engagement status, billable hours, and revenue. AI can auto-generate dashboard views, helping partners and managers monitor firm performance without manual report building.

Business Benefits of Xerpium for Audit and Tax Firms

  • Single source of truth for engagements, working papers, evidence, timesheets, and invoices.
  • Better profitability visibility through budget vs actual comparisons and billable vs cost rate tracking.
  • Faster review and sign-off workflows reduce delays in completing working papers.
  • Stronger compliance readiness with structured checklists and a report structure built for Indonesian audit firms.
  • Improved client communication through a portal for document upload, progress tracking, and report delivery.
  • Reduced manual reporting effort with Turbo Report and AI advisory.

Example Workflow: One Engagement from Start to Invoice

A tax advisory engagement begins with a fee proposal in Xerpium. Once approved, the firm creates the engagement with defined scope, timeline, budget, and assigned team members.

Staff log hours against specific tasks in the timesheet. Senior staff and partners review working papers, upload evidence, and complete compliance checklists. Findings are recorded with recommendations and follow-up actions.

Because billable hours are tracked by staff level, Xerpium automatically calculates the invoice value from timesheets or milestone billing. The client can follow progress through the client portal and download the final report. At the same time, the dashboard shows engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff, giving partners a clear view of both delivery and profitability.
