Complete ERP solution for University Management: Unifying Academics, Finance, and Operations with Xerpium

October 27, 2026

Running a modern university means orchestrating thousands of moving parts at once: faculties and study programs, academic years and semesters, course enrollment, grading, thesis supervision, lecturer workloads, classroom scheduling, and tuition collection. In many institutions, each of these lives in a separate system—an academic information system here, a finance spreadsheet there, a standalone scheduling tool somewhere else. The result is duplicated data entry, reconciliation headaches, and decisions made on information that is already out of date.

When student records, grades, and tuition invoices don't talk to one another, every reporting cycle becomes a manual stitching exercise. A unified ERP changes that equation. Xerpium brings university operations into one connected platform so academic data, financial transactions, and operational scheduling flow together without re-keying. This article walks through how Xerpium covers the University Management vertical end to end.

Common Challenges in University Management

  • Fragmented academic data. Faculty, department, student, and course records sit in disconnected systems, making it hard to track a student's full journey from admission to graduation.
  • Manual KRS and grading workflows. Course enrollment (KRS), prerequisite checks, and grade computation are often handled in spreadsheets, increasing the risk of errors in SKS totals and GPA calculation.
  • Disconnected tuition and accounting. Tuition plans, invoices, and payment tracking rarely post automatically to the general ledger, forcing finance teams to reconcile SPP collections by hand.
  • Room and schedule conflicts. Without real-time overlap detection, lecture halls and labs get double-booked, disrupting classes and exams.
  • Limited visibility for leadership. Deans and rectors lack a single dashboard showing accreditation status, enrollment, GPA distribution, and tuition arrears at a glance.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Xerpium's university modules map directly to the structure of an academic institution, from the organizational hierarchy down to individual student transactions.

Academic Structure

The foundation starts with Faculty (uni.faculty), which manages faculty names, unique codes, and leadership assignments (Dean, Vice Dean, Secretary) linked to hr.employee, with automatic department counts. Program Studi (uni.department) handles degree levels (S1, S2, S3, D3, D4), accreditation tracking (A/B/C/Unranked with history), head-of-department assignment, and vision and mission records.

Time is structured through Tahun Ajaran (uni.year.ajaran)—academic years such as 2025/2026 with a Draft → Active → Closed workflow—and Semester (uni.semester), which defines Ganjil, Genap, and Short semesters, KRS periods, and an active flag for the running term.

Students and Enrollment

Mahasiswa (uni.student) stores NIM, biodata, parent information, cohort, and live status (Active, Graduated, Withdrawal, Frozen, and more), along with real-time cumulative GPA (IPK) and total credits (SKS). Course enrollment runs through KRS (uni.enrollment) with course selection, automatic SKS totals, section assignment, prerequisite validation, and a Draft → Submitted → Approved → Rejected workflow.

Grading and Thesis

Nilai & IPK (uni.grade.scale / uni.course.grade) applies a 4.0 grade scale with weighted score components (UTS 30%, UAS 40%, Tugas 20%, Partisipasi 10%), automatic grade letters and points, and produces the KHS (study result card) and full transcript grouped by semester. Skripsi / Thesis (uni.thesis) tracks the full lifecycle from Proposal to Graduated, including Advisor I & II, progress percentage with per-meeting notes, and defense panels with examiners and rooms.

Courses, Lecturers, and Scheduling

Mata Kuliah (uni.course) manages course codes, SKS (1–6 with lecture/lab/tutorial hours), categories, prerequisites, and course sections. Dosen (uni.lecturer) records NIDN/NIP, lecturer type (Permanent, Contract, Guest), teaching load, academic advisor role, and current semester SKS. Jadwal Kuliah (uni.schedule.line) and the Academic Calendar (uni.academic.calendar) handle day/time scheduling with conflict detection, section links, and semester events like KRS, UTS, UAS, holidays, and graduation with color coding and notifications.

Tuition and Facilities

Tuition Plan (uni.tuition.plan) supports per-SKS or flat-fee (SPP) models, department-specific plans, and multi-currency. Tuition Invoice (uni.tuition.invoice) auto-generates invoices, tracks payments and overdue amounts, supports Bank Transfer, Cash, Virtual Account, and E-Wallet, and posts automatically to account.move. Room Management (uni.room) and Room Booking (uni.room.booking) manage building hierarchies, room types, capacity, equipment, and real-time overlap protection.

Intelligence and Cross-Module Integration

The AI Dashboard can auto-generate dashboards from natural language via DeepSeek, convert natural language to SQL, surface chart insights, and provide a Copilot chat. Across the suite, Xerpium connects University to HR (lecturers linked to payroll and timesheet), Accounting (tuition invoices to account.move, budget tracking per faculty), Inventory (lab assets and library stock), CRM (PMB admissions from leads), Portal (online KRS, KHS, grade input, and SPP payment), and Email/Notification (announcements and WhatsApp reminders for tuition). The AI Assistant answers requests like "Find student NIM 12345678" or "Show an empty room on Monday at 8."

End-to-End Flow

Consider a single semester cycle. When the new Tahun Ajaran is set to Active and the odd Semester opens its KRS period, students log into the Portal to submit their KRS. Xerpium validates prerequisites automatically and totals the SKS. Approved enrollments link to Course Sections, which feed the Jadwal Kuliah and Room Booking with conflict detection ensuring no double-booked halls.

Based on each student's SKS and their program's Tuition Plan, a Tuition Invoice is generated and posted to Accounting via account.move; payment reminders go out through email and WhatsApp. As the semester progresses, lecturers enter scores; Nilai & IPK computes grade points, produces the KHS, and updates the student's cumulative IPK in real time. Leadership then reviews enrollment, GPA distribution, and tuition arrears through the AI Dashboard—all without exporting a single spreadsheet.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth connecting academic records, finance, and scheduling.
  • Automated GPA and SKS computation that reduces grading and transcript errors.
  • Faster tuition collection with auto-generated invoices and automatic ledger posting.
  • Conflict-free scheduling through real-time room and timetable overlap detection.
  • Data-driven leadership with AI dashboards covering accreditation, enrollment, and arrears.
  • Lower administrative load via student and lecturer self-service portals.

Implementation Steps

  1. Data preparation. Gather and clean master data: faculties, departments, courses with prerequisites, lecturers (NIDN/NIP), students (NIM, cohort), and existing GPA records for migration.
  2. Configure parameters and accounting. Define degree levels, grade scales, score component weights, tuition plans (per-SKS or flat fee), payment methods, and analytic distribution per faculty for cost-center tracking.
  3. Run a pilot. Select one faculty or program to test the full cycle—KRS, scheduling, grading, and tuition invoicing—and validate prerequisite checks and GPA computation against known results.
  4. Roll out institution-wide. Extend to all faculties, activate student and lecturer portals, and enable email and WhatsApp notifications for announcements and tuition reminders.
  5. Evaluate and optimize. Use AI dashboards to review each semester's metrics, refine tuition and scheduling parameters, and expand integrations such as CRM-based admissions (PMB).
