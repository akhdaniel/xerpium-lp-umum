# Building Management with Xerpium ERP: Unifying Property Operations, Billing, and Maintenance

Managing apartments, offices, malls, hotels, hospitals, schools, industrial estates, or government facilities creates ongoing operational pressure. Property teams must keep unit data accurate, track owners and residents, bill monthly IPL and utilities, respond to maintenance requests, coordinate security and cleaning, and manage rental contracts. When these functions run on spreadsheets or disconnected tools, small errors become expensive problems: incorrect invoices, missed SLA deadlines, unclear maintenance costs, and slow reporting.

Xerpium ERP provides a building management suite that connects property, tower, floor, unit, resident, billing, maintenance, security, and facility data in one system. It is designed for B2B property operations teams that need tighter control, automation, and financial visibility.

Common challenges in building and property operations

  • Fragmented property and unit data across towers, floors, unit types, owners, and residents.
  • Manual monthly IPL billing and utility calculations that slow collections and increase errors.
  • Reactive maintenance and complaints without clear priority, SLA, assignment, or cost-to-invoice flow.
  • Limited visibility into security patrol completion, cleaning quality, and facility booking.
  • Rental contracts and unit occupancy status that fall out of sync, causing double-booking or billing mistakes.

How Xerpium ERP addresses building management

Property, tower, and unit structure

Xerpium ERP starts with a clear hierarchy. Each Property stores name, code, address, manager, and property type, including apartment, office, mixed, mall, school, hospital, hotel, industrial, and government. Within a property, teams define Tower records with unit allocation, Floor management with numbers and codes, and Unit Type records such as studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, and penthouse. Configuration also links IPL products, sinking fund, and utility settings.

The Unit Management module tracks each unit by name, code, property, tower, floor, and unit type. It records owner information with multi-owner history, status such as Available, Occupied, Reserved, Maintenance, or Inactive, usage type Residential or Commercial, size in sqm, and bedroom/bathroom counts. Ownership history is automatically recorded when the owner changes.

Resident and occupancy management

Resident Management stores identity data from KTP or passport, phone, email, and the related unit. It supports resident types Owner, Tenant, Family, and Staff, and statuses Active, Moved Out, and Blacklisted. Move-in and move-out tracking includes dates and history. The module also manages family members within a unit and registers vehicles per resident.

For commercial units, Rental Contract manages lease contracts with start and end dates, automatic overlap validation, annual rent, down payment, and balance tracking. DP invoices and annual rent invoices are created through accounting. Contract statuses include Draft, Active, Expired, and Cancelled. The system automatically syncs unit status to occupied or available based on the contract.

Billing, IPL, and utilities

Billing & IPL generates monthly IPL billing per unit or resident. Charge types include Service Charge, Sinking Fund, Water, Electricity, Gas, Internet, and Parking. Meter reading captures utility consumption, and the system calculates usage from previous and current readings. Invoices are generated automatically to accounting, with due dates and outstanding tracking. A monthly wizard generates mass billing for all units.

Utility Management supports water, electricity, gas, and internet per property. Meter readings can be entered manually or imported. The module calculates previous versus current consumption and applies tariffs per utility type, including tiered pricing. Consumption automatically becomes a billing line.

Maintenance requests and work orders

Maintenance Request allows residents to create requests with priority Low, Normal, High, or Urgent. Each priority triggers an automatic SLA; urgent requests use a 1-hour response and 8-hour completion deadline. Requests can be assigned to technicians from HR, with schedule and dispatch details. Each request supports checklist lines, before/after photo documentation, and can create invoices and purchase orders from maintenance work.

Work Order extends maintenance execution with work order types Electrical, Plumbing, Civil, HVAC, Cleaning, Landscaping, and IT. Work orders can be assigned to employees or vendor contractors. The module records technician time, material usage from stock products, task checklists with completion status, and before/after photos.

Facility, security, cleaning, parking, and complaints

Facility Booking manages facility lists per property such as gym, pool, and function room. It tracks maximum capacity, hourly rate plus deposit, and a booking calendar for residents. Booking integrates automatically to billing for invoicing.

Security Incident covers incident types including Theft, Vandalism, Fire, Flood, Noise, Trespassing, and Accident. Users report incidents with location details down to property, tower, or unit. The workflow moves from Draft to Investigated to Resolved or Cancelled, with recorded action taken.

Security Patrol defines patrol routes with QR-code checkpoints. Security staff scan each checkpoint during patrol. Schedules are organized by morning, afternoon, and night shifts. The system tracks completion rate, scan timestamps, photos, and notes per checkpoint.

Cleaning Management manages cleaning areas such as corridor, lobby, toilet, and parking. Schedules are set per shift and area. Supervisors inspect quality and record a score from 1 to 5, while completion rate shows the percentage of areas completed per schedule.

Parking & Visitor tracks parking slots per property with types car, motorcycle, and bicycle, and statuses Available, Occupied, Reserved, and Maintenance. Visitor management supports pre-registration, check-in, check-out, and QR code passes. Lost & found items follow a claim and dispose workflow.

Complaint Management uses categories with their own SLA and priority levels Low, Normal, High, and Urgent, each with different SLA hours. Complaints can be auto-assigned to employees or escalated. The system sets SLA deadlines automatically, tracks resident feedback on a 1–5 scale, and auto-detects overdue complaints.

Dashboard and spare parts

Dashboard Properti gives property managers a real-time view of unit status as a pie chart of occupancy, outstanding billing per month, open versus closed maintenance requests, complaint trends and SLA compliance, and security incidents per property.
