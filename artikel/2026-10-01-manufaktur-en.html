# Manufacturing ERP Solutions: How Xerpium Connects Production, Quality, and Inventory

Manufacturing operations rarely fail in just one place. A material shortage delays a work order, a quality issue surfaces after a batch is already packed, or production output sits unrecorded while purchasing teams reorder based on outdated information. These disconnects add cost and erode delivery reliability.

Xerpium is an enterprise ERP suite with an AI assistant that offers manufacturing solutions designed to connect planning, execution, quality, and inventory in one system. With capabilities for bills of materials, work orders, production schedules, material requirements planning, quality control, by-product and waste tracking, and direct links to stock and purchasing, Xerpium helps manufacturers run more controlled, transparent operations.

Common Manufacturing Challenges

Many production teams face recurring operational issues that slow throughput and increase cost:

  • Material shortages or late arrivals because material planning is disconnected from production schedules.
  • Quality problems discovered too late, after products are already completed or shipped.
  • By-products and process waste that are not tracked, making true production costs harder to manage.
  • Manual handoffs between production output, inventory records, and purchasing requests.
  • Limited visibility into how work orders, materials, and finished goods fit together.

These challenges are not solved by adding more spreadsheets. They require a connected system where production data flows into quality, inventory, and procurement automatically.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses Manufacturing Challenges

Xerpium provides a focused set of manufacturing capabilities that link core production processes.

Manufacturing & MRP

Xerpium supports Bill of Materials, work orders, production schedules, and MRP to help ensure materials are available at the right time. Teams can define what goes into each product, plan production work, and let material requirements drive timely preparation. This reduces the risk of starting a work order only to find that key components are missing.

Quality Control

Quality control in Xerpium is integrated directly into the work order. Inspection findings are recorded in the context of production, so issues can be addressed immediately before products leave the facility. This helps manufacturers prevent quality problems from moving downstream to customers.

By-Product & Waste Tracking

Xerpium tracks by-products and waste from the production process. By recording these outputs alongside main production results, manufacturers gain better visibility into what is being produced, what is being lost, and where cost efficiency can be improved.

Connected to Inventory & Purchasing

Production results in Xerpium enter inventory automatically. At the same time, material requirements can flow down into purchase requests. This creates a tighter loop between what is produced, what is in stock, and what needs to be bought, reducing manual data entry and helping purchasing teams respond to actual production demand.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Manufacturing Solutions

With these capabilities working together, manufacturers can expect several practical benefits:

  • Better material readiness through MRP and connected production schedules.
  • Fewer quality escapes because inspections are part of the work order.
  • Greater cost clarity with by-product and waste tracking.
  • Faster and more accurate inventory updates after production.
  • More responsive purchasing through automatic purchase requests tied to material needs.
  • A clearer end-to-end view from Bill of Materials to finished goods.

A Typical Production Flow with Xerpium

Consider a manufacturer preparing a production run. A planner creates or opens a work order based on a Bill of Materials. MRP checks material availability against the production schedule. If a required material is insufficient, the system can route the need into a purchase request.

During production, quality inspections are recorded directly within the work order. Any finding is addressed before the batch is finalized. When production is complete, finished goods enter inventory automatically. By-products and waste from the process are also recorded, giving the team a full picture of the run’s output and cost.

This flow removes many of the manual steps that typically separate planning, production, quality, and inventory control.

Conclusion

Xerpium brings manufacturing, quality, and inventory processes into one connected ERP environment. By combining MRP, work orders, production schedules, integrated quality control, by-product and waste tracking, and automatic inventory and purchasing updates, Xerpium helps manufacturers reduce disconnects and operate with greater control.
