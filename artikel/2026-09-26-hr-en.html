# HR Solutions with Xerpium ERP: Unifying Payroll, Employee Self-Service, and AI Recruitment

HR departments in growing organizations often operate across disconnected tools. Payroll administrators export attendance data from one system, finance teams manually rekey salary journals, employees email leave requests, and recruiters screen CVs without a consistent process. This fragmentation creates errors, compliance risk, and unnecessary administrative work.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with an integrated HR suite that connects payroll, employee self-service, recruitment, and accounting in one system. Instead of maintaining separate solutions, HR teams can manage the entire employee lifecycle from a single platform.

Common HR challenges

Many HR teams face similar operational bottlenecks:

  • Employee data is scattered across payroll, attendance, timesheet, and KPI records.
  • Payroll processing becomes complex when managing BPJS and PPh21 obligations manually.
  • Routine requests such as leave, permission, expense, and approval add heavy administrative load.
  • Recruitment screening and candidate assessment are slow and inconsistent without standardized tools.
  • Salary costs are not automatically reflected in accounting, delaying accurate financial reporting.

These challenges are not isolated. When HR and Finance operate in silos, even a small payroll adjustment can require multiple manual steps before it reaches the general ledger.

Key Xerpium ERP HR solutions

Xerpium ERP provides several connected modules designed to solve these challenges.

HR & Payroll complete

The HR & Payroll module brings employee data, payroll with BPJS and PPh21, attendance, timesheet, and KPI into one module. Because attendance, timesheets, payroll, and performance indicators share the same source of data, HR teams no longer need to reconcile separate spreadsheets or export files between systems.

The module is directly connected to Accounting. This connection allows payroll results to flow into the financial system without manual journal creation.

ESS Mobile App

Xerpium’s ESS Mobile App gives employees self-service access through a lightweight Flutter application. Employees can record attendance via mobile, submit leave and permission requests, manage expenses, view pay slips, and handle approvals directly from their devices.

By moving routine HR transactions to employees and managers, the app reduces back-and-forth emails and manual data entry. The Flutter-based design keeps the application lightweight, so staff can use it comfortably even on standard devices.

AI Recruitment

The AI Recruitment module uses Xerpium’s AI Assistant to support CV screening, AI interviews, and candidate assessment in multiple languages. Recruiters can standardize early-stage evaluation and focus their time on the most relevant candidates.

Multilingual support helps organizations recruit across regions without language becoming a barrier during initial screening and assessment. This creates a more consistent and efficient hiring process from application to shortlist.

Integrated with finance

Xerpium ERP connects HR and payroll directly to accounting. When payroll is processed, salary costs are automatically posted to accounting journals. This integration ensures that financial reports reflect payroll activity accurately without manual rekeying or separate reconciliation processes.

For finance teams, this means month-end closing becomes smoother. For HR teams, it means payroll updates no longer require a separate handoff to accounting.

Business benefits of Xerpium ERP HR solutions

Using Xerpium ERP for HR delivers several practical benefits:

  • One unified employee record covers HR data, payroll, attendance, timesheet, and KPI.
  • Payroll processing supports BPJS and PPh21 requirements within the same module.
  • Employee self-service through the ESS Mobile App lowers HR administrative workload.
  • AI Recruitment accelerates CV screening and provides consistent multilingual candidate assessment.
  • Automatic salary journal posting keeps financial reports accurate and up to date.
  • Mobile-first self-service increases accessibility for both office and field-based staff.

Example workflow: from attendance to accounting

Consider a company preparing its monthly payroll.

Employees record attendance through the ESS Mobile App. Managers approve leave and expense requests directly from their devices. HR reviews attendance and timesheet data inside the HR & Payroll module, runs payroll with BPJS and PPh21 calculations, and finalizes the pay run.

Once payroll is confirmed, Xerpium posts salary costs to the accounting journal automatically. Finance sees updated figures without creating manual entries.

At the same time, for an open position, the AI Assistant screens incoming CVs, conducts multilingual AI interviews, and scores candidates. HR reviews the shortlist and schedules final interviews.

The entire process leaves a clear data trail across recruitment, HR, payroll, and finance—without exporting files or switching between disconnected tools.

Conclusion

HR teams cannot operate efficiently when payroll, attendance, recruitment, and finance live in separate systems. Xerpium ERP combines HR & Payroll, ESS Mobile App, AI Recruitment, and direct accounting integration into one connected solution.

The result is faster payroll processing, lighter HR administration, more consistent hiring, and financial reports that reflect payroll reality. Contact Xerpium to explore how these HR solutions can fit your organization’s needs.
