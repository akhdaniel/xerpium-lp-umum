# Coal Mining ERP: How Xerpium Connects Mine Site, Production, Quality, and Compliance

Coal mining operations are inherently complex. A single company may manage multiple mine sites, dozens of blocks, several active seams, continuous 24/7 shifts, heavy equipment fleets, stockpile movements, quality laboratories, export documentation, and strict ESDM reporting obligations. When these functions run on disconnected spreadsheets or generic systems, the result is fragmented data, slow reconciliation, and compliance risk.

Xerpium ERP provides a coal mining-specific suite that links the operational side—site, block, seam, production, stockpile, quality—with accounting, trade, export, HSE, and government reporting. This article explains how the platform helps coal producers manage the full mine-to-report cycle in one system.

Common Challenges in Coal Mining Operations

  • Site and reserve data is scattered across permits, block estimates, seam records, and geological zones.
  • Production tonnage and quality parameters such as CV, ash, and moisture are difficult to consolidate across shifts and equipment.
  • Stockpile inventory changes frequently, and weighted average quality must be tracked as material moves in and out.
  • ESDM reports, royalty calculations, and export documentation often require manual data collection and re-checking.
  • Financial control is difficult when production, delivery, revenue, royalty accrual, and cost tracking are not connected.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Coal Mining

Mine Site, Block, and Seam Management

coal.mine.site records each mine site with a unique site code, GPS coordinates, total area, province, district, address, and permit details such as permit number, type (IUP/IUPK/IPR), issue date, expiry, and computed days remaining. Site state can be Active, Suspended, or Closed with workflow actions. The site also shows block count and total reserve tonnage computed from linked blocks.

coal.mine.block manages individual mining blocks. Each block has a unique code per site, area, depth, mining type (Open Pit, Underground, Alluvial), geological zone reference, estimated reserve tonnage, estimated CV, linked seam, and a status flow from Exploration to Development, Production, and Depleted.

coal.seam records coal seam reference data, including seam code, thickness, depth from/to, typical CV, ash percentage, moisture percentage, volatile matter, and sulfur. This data is used in block estimation and production quality tracking.

Production Orders, Production Lines, and Shift Operations

coal.production.order handles mining production orders with an auto-sequence format such as CPO/2026/00001. Each order links to the mine site, production block, seam, required shift, production date, and responsible employee. The order tracks planned tons versus actual tons with computed achievement percentage. Quality at extraction—actual CV, ash, and moisture—is recorded directly on the production order. Total fuel liters are computed from production lines, and a target stockpile can be linked so that tonnage is automatically added when the order is done.

coal.production.line captures detail for each production activity. Lines link equipment from coal.equipment, assign an operator from employee records, and record start/end times. Duration hours are computed from the start-end datetime. Each line records output tons extracted and fuel used in liters, with optional operational notes.

coal.cost.center and coal.shift support 24/7 operations through Day, Night, and Evening shift management, and link cost centers to production orders for budget tracking per site or block.

Stockpile, Equipment, and Quality Control

coal.stockpile tracks maximum capacity and current tonnage. Weighted average CV, ash, and moisture are maintained automatically. The _add_tons method is called when production is done, and stockpile movement tracks in/out with weighted average quality.

coal.equipment manages heavy equipment types such as Excavator, Dump Truck, Loader, Bulldozer, Grader, Drill Rig, and Compactor. The registry includes asset code, brand, model, year, plate number, serial/engine number, status (Active, Under Maintenance, Idle, Retired), current hours, purchase value, residual value, insurance, maintenance schedule and history, total maintenance cost, total fuel liters, and total operating hours.

For quality control, coal.sample supports auto-sequence numbering such as CS/2026/00001, sample sources (mine face, stockpile, delivery, rehandle), sample types (Chip, Grab, Channel, Composite), collection date, collector, location, and links to production orders or stockpiles. State moves from Draft to Submitted, Analyzed, and Reported.

coal.lab.result records proximate analysis including total moisture, ash, volatile matter, and fixed carbon; ultimate analysis including carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen, and sulfur; and calorific value with gross CV and net CV in kcal/kg. The result includes the assigned analyst and analysis date, with states from Draft to Done and Verified.

coal.quality.certificate generates certificate numbers linked to samples. Grade classification can be Grade A/B/C, Sub-bituminous, or Lignite based on CV. The certificate records CV, ash, moisture, sulfur, weight tonnage, and customer for delivery certificates. State moves from Draft to Issued and Revoked.

Delivery, Sales, and Export Operations

coal.delivery.order creates unique delivery order numbers linked to sale orders. It records transport details such as truck number, driver name, and vessel for barging. Weight tracking includes gross weight, tare weight, and computed net weight. Quality at delivery—CV, ash, and moisture—is captured for quality certificates. The order tracks dispatch date and delivery date with states Draft, Dispatched, and Delivered.

coal.sale.order and coal.vessel manage customer contracts, pricing per ton, quality specifications, vessel or barge registry with capacity and dimensions, invoice integration through account.move, and ordered versus delivered tonnage tracking.

For export, Xerpium supports Export DO with container, vessel, port loading/discharge, and incoterm. PEB includes DJBC registration number and CEISA integration. Packing List records total packages, gross/net weight, and volume per product. Certificate of Origin supports Form D for ASEAN, non-ASEAN, and preferential tariff. Export Invoice handles commercial invoice in USD, FOB value, and incoterm.

HSE, Government Reporting, and Royalty

coal.hse.incident manages incident reports with types including Personal Injury, Near Miss, Property Damage, Environmental, Fire, Vehicle, Chemical Spill, and Structural. Severity levels are Low, Medium, High, and Critical. Each incident can track root cause analysis, corrective action, preventive action, lost time days with a non-negative constraint, first aid, hospitalization, witnesses, photos, attachments, and a workflow from Draft to Reported, Investigating, and Closed.

coal.safety.inspection and Permit to Work cover daily, weekly, and monthly inspection checklists with auto-computed score percentage. Permit types include Hot Work, Confined Space, Working at Height, and Excavation. PTW records start/end datetime, area, responsible person, environmental monitoring, waste management, and reclamation tracking.

coal.govt.report supports ESDM report types: Production Report, Sales/PLN Report, Environmental Report, Royalty Report, and Export Report. Reports include period start/end, site-specific data, and company. The system auto-computes total production tonnage, delivery tonnage, revenue, average CV/ash/moisture, production by block, order count, sales/export data by customer or vessel, environmental incident counts by type and severity, and royalty calculated from production multiplied by rate per ton. The _generate_report_json() method produces structured data per report type.

coal.royalty.rate configures royalty rates per coal type, rate per ton, and effective date. Rate history is tracked by effective date, and the configured rate is used automatically in royalty report generation.

Accounting, Trade, and AI Assistant

The accounting side connects mining operations to finance through cost centers per site, automatic journal entries when coal moves to stockpile, revenue recognition when delivery is confirmed, royalty accrual per production period, asset depletion for coal resources, and multi-company support for multi-site operations.

Trade and finance features include LC sight/usance for import/export, TT/remittance with bank reference and status tracking, landed cost calculation from FOB plus freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling, USD/IDR forex tracking with realized versus booking gain/loss, and bank reconciliation auto-matching payments to LC/TT references.

The AI Dashboard allows users to auto-generate a coal mining dashboard with commands such as “Buat dashboard produksi batubara,” convert natural language to SQL, analyze production trends and CV decline with chart insight, and use Copilot Chat streamed with SSE for mine dashboard context. The AI Assistant can answer operational questions such as “Produksi hari ini berapa ton?” and “Rata-rata CV bulan ini,” or generate ESDM production reports.

Example Use Case: From Production Order to ESDM Report

A mine planner creates a coal.production.order for a production block and seam, selects the shift and date, and assigns a responsible employee. Operators record production lines with equipment, start/end times, extracted tons, and fuel used. When the order is marked done, stockpile tonnage is automatically added through the _add_tons method and weighted average quality is updated.

A sample is collected from the mine face or stockpile, submitted to the laboratory, and analyzed. The lab result feeds the quality certificate. A delivery order is created from the sale order, net weight is computed from gross and tare, and delivery quality is recorded. Once delivered, revenue recognition and invoice integration follow.

Finally, the ESDM production report is generated with production tonnage, delivery tonnage, revenue, average CV, ash, moisture, and royalty amounts. During the entire cycle, HSE incidents can be recorded, investigated, and closed, while the AI Assistant answers ad hoc questions about daily production, monthly CV averages, or report generation.
