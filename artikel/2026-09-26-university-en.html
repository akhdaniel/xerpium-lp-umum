# University Management with Xerpium ERP: From Academic Records to Tuition Automation

Universities operate in a uniquely complex administrative environment. They must coordinate faculties and departments, academic years and semesters, student enrollment, grading, thesis supervision, lecturer workloads, room scheduling, and tuition payments—often across multiple campuses and companies. When these processes run on disconnected spreadsheets or legacy systems, staff spend too much time on manual reconciliation, students face unnecessary friction in KRS and payments, and leadership lacks reliable academic and financial visibility.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a unified University Management suite built on a modern ERP foundation. It connects academic operations, tuition finance, facilities, portals, and AI-assisted insight in one system, making university administration more accurate, transparent, and scalable.

Common Challenges in University Administration

Before exploring the solution, it is useful to recognize the operational pain points many institutions face.

  • Academic records are fragmented across faculties, study programs, cohorts, and grading systems.
  • KRS processes are prone to errors in prerequisite checks, section selection, and total SKS calculation.
  • Tuition invoicing and overdue SPP tracking require significant manual effort and often lack accounting integration.
  • Room scheduling creates conflicts when lecture, exam, event, and meeting bookings are not centrally validated.
  • Leadership lacks real-time KPIs on enrollment, academic progress, room utilization, and payment status.

How Xerpium ERP Supports the Academic Core

Faculty and Study Program Governance

Xerpium models the academic structure from the faculty level down. The uni.faculty module records faculty name, unique code, and sequence, while dean assignments—including Dean, Vice Dean, and Secretary—are linked to hr.employee. The system automatically counts departments or study programs within each faculty. Mail tracking and activity mixins keep collaboration visible in one thread.

At the program level, uni.department manages degree levels such as S1, S2, S3, D3, and D4 through configurable options. It tracks accreditation status—A, B, C, or Unranked—including accreditation history, assigns a Head of Department or Kaprodi, and records the program’s vision, mission, and outcomes.

Academic Year and Semester Control

The uni.year.ajaran module provides structured academic year management, for example 2025/2026 with code 2526. Each academic year follows a workflow from Draft to Active to Closed, with start and end date validation. One2many integration connects the academic year to its semesters, ensuring reporting and configuration stay aligned.

Within each year, uni.semester handles semester types: Ganjil, Genap, and Singkat or Short. Each semester can define a KRS registration period, carry an academic calendar for events, and use an active flag to indicate the current running semester.

Student Records and KRS Enrollment

The uni.student module stores the complete student master data. It includes a unique NIM per company, biodata such as name, photo, gender, religion, place and date of birth, and parent or guardian contact details. Student status covers Active, Inactive, Graduated, Withdrawal, Frozen, and Expelled. The cohort or angkatan is based on entry year, while IPK and total SKS are computed in real time.

For course registration, uni.enrollment manages KRS. Students select courses per semester, and the system automatically calculates total SKS from enrollment lines. A workflow moves from Draft to Submitted to Approved or Rejected. Section assignment lets students choose a course section, and prerequisite validation enforces academic rules before registration is accepted.

Grading, KHS, and Transcript

Xerpium supports a standard grade scale using uni.grade.scale and uni.course.grade. Grade letters such as A, B+, B, C+, C, D, and E are mapped to grade points on a 4.0 scale. Score components are weight-based: UTS 30%, UAS 40%, assignments 20%, and participation 10%. The system automatically converts total scores into grade letters and grade points. It also generates KHS or Kartu Hasil Studi with semester IPS and cumulative IPK, as well as transcripts grouped by semester.

Thesis and Academic Delivery

The uni.thesis module manages the full thesis lifecycle: Proposal, Ongoing, Submitted, Defended, and Graduated. It supports Advisor I and Advisor II assignments with advisor types, tracks progress percentage and meeting notes, and schedules defense panels with examiners and rooms. Workflow stages are configurable through xerpium_dynamic_stage, making the process adaptable to each institution.

For course catalogs, uni.course records unique course codes, SKS values from 1 to 6, lecture, lab, and tutorial hours, and categories such as Compulsory, Elective, and General. Prerequisites are many2many relationships, and course sections represent class offerings per semester with capacity and schedule data.

Lecturer Workload

uni.lecturer links faculty members to HR records using NIDN and NIP. Lecturer types include Permanent, Contract, and Guest. The module tracks teaching load per semester, maximum SKS, advisor assignments, and current SKS computation for the active semester.

Scheduling and Room Management

The uni.schedule.line model records day and time from Monday to Saturday with start and end times, room assignment, and conflict detection. Each schedule line links to a course section and syncs with the XERPIUM calendar. The uni.academic.calendar module defines events per semester—KRS, UTS, UAS, holidays, and graduation—with automatic date validation, color coding, and notifications to students and lecturers.

Room planning is handled in uni.room, with building hierarchy from building to floor to room, room types such as Lecture Hall, Lab, Seminar, Library, and Auditorium, capacity and equipment details, and automatic overlap checking. The related uni.room.booking module validates start and end datetimes, supports section-linked lecture bookings, runs a Draft to Confirmed to Cancelled workflow, and protects against double booking in real time.

Tuition and Financial Operations

Xerpium’s education finance capabilities begin with uni.tuition.plan. Institutions can configure payment per SKS or flat fee, link plans to specific study programs, support multiple currencies, and define flexible amounts. The uni.tuition.invoice module then auto-generates SPP invoices from those plans. Payment tracking shows amount paid, residual, and overdue detection. Supported payment methods include Bank Transfer, Cash, Virtual Account, and E-Wallet. Invoices move through Draft, Posted, Paid, Partial, and Overdue statuses, with automatic account.move posting into accounting.

For the broader accounting function, Xerpium supports mass invoice generation per semester, multi-payment methods, overdue tracking, reporting per academic year or semester, analytic distribution by faculty or study program, and multi-company consolidation for foundations with multiple campuses.

AI Dashboards and Intelligent Assistance

Xerpium includes AI-powered dashboard features that help university leaders move from raw data to insight quickly. Users can auto-generate a complete dashboard from natural language using DeepSeek, convert natural language into SQL queries in real time, and receive chart insights that analyze trends, peaks, anomalies, and recommendations. A Copilot chat with SSE streaming provides dashboard-aware assistance, and conditional formatting dynamically colors values based on thresholds.

Education-specific dashboards such as vit_dashboard_university and vit_dashboard_education provide academic KPIs and analysis. The AI assistant can answer commands like “Cari mahasiswa NIM 12345678”, “Buat KRS untuk semester ini”, “Tampilkan IPK mahasiswa atas nama X”, or “Cari ruangan kosong hari Senin jam 8”.

A Connected University Ecosystem

University Management in Xerpium is not isolated. It integrates with HR, Accounting, Inventory, CRM, Portal, Email, and notifications.

  • HR: Lecturers connect to hr.employee for payroll, teaching load integrates with timesheet, and lecturer contract management runs through HR.
  • Accounting: Tuition invoices become account.move automatically, SPP payments use the payment register, and budget tracking is available per faculty or program.
  • Inventory: The system tracks room assets, laboratory inventory, equipment, and library stock.
  • CRM: New student admissions or PMB can originate from leads and track through crm.stage, with marketing campaigns per study program.
  • Portal: Students access KRS and KHS online, lecturers input grades, and a payment portal supports online SPP.
  • Email and Notification: Academic models include mail threads and activities, automated announcements, and WhatsApp integration for SPP reminders.

Business Benefits

  • Unified academic data with workflow controls reduces duplication and manual errors.
  • Real-time GPA, SKS, and payment status improve student service and administrative follow-up.
  • Automatic prerequisite, schedule, and room conflict checks prevent operational clashes.
  • Tuition plan and invoice automation accelerate billing and strengthen accounting accuracy.
  • AI dashboards give leadership clear visibility into academic and financial trends.
  • Multi-campus and multi-company consolidation supports foundation-scale university governance.

A Practical Use Case

Consider a university opening KRS for the odd semester of the 2026/2027 academic year. The academic year is Active, and the odd semester is flagged as active with a defined KRS period. Students log into the portal and select courses. The system validates prerequisites and calculates total SKS before the enrollment moves through the approval workflow. Approved KRS lines map to course sections, while room assignments check for conflicts and sync to the academic calendar.

Simultaneously, tuition plans linked to each study program generate SPP invoices based on per-SKS or flat fee configuration. Students pay through virtual account or e-wallet, the payment register updates the invoice status, and the transaction posts automatically to accounting. Throughout the process, lecturers see their teaching load, students receive schedule and payment reminders, and administrators monitor KRS completion, room utilization, and payment performance through AI-assisted dashboards.

Conclusion

University management is more than record-keeping. It requires academic governance, financial control, facility coordination, and stakeholder access to work as one connected operation. Xerpium ERP brings those areas together in a unified platform, with workflow-driven academic modules, automated tuition and accounting integration, room and schedule conflict protection, student and lecturer portals, and AI-assisted decision support.
