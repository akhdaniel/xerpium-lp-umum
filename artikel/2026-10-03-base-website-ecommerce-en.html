# Unified Website & eCommerce ERP: Streamline B2B Operations with Xerpium

In B2B commerce, the gap between a company’s website and its ERP system is often where growth slows down. Orders captured online still need to be manually transferred into back-office systems. Product catalogs are updated in one place but not another. Finance teams wait for reconciled data, while sales teams lack a real-time view of transactions. Xerpium addresses this disconnect with a Website & eCommerce module that is built directly into the ERP suite, not bolted on as an afterthought.

By bringing website management, online catalogs, and checkout into the same system as the rest of the business, Xerpium helps B2B companies run a more connected and transparent digital sales operation.

Common Challenges in Website & eCommerce Operations

Many B2B organizations struggle with similar issues when running their website and eCommerce channels separately from their ERP:

  • Disconnected storefront and back office: Orders from the website often require manual re-entry into the ERP, increasing the risk of errors and delays.
  • Inconsistent product data: Online catalogs can quickly fall out of sync with the product information used by internal teams, leading to incorrect pricing, descriptions, or availability.
  • Complex multi-entity structures: Supporting multiple companies and currencies becomes difficult when eCommerce tools are not designed for that level of operational complexity.
  • Limited audit visibility: Without a clear audit trail, businesses may find it harder to track changes, ensure compliance, and resolve disputes.
  • Delayed reporting: When website data lives outside the ERP, decision-makers cannot easily see real-time performance alongside the rest of the business.

These challenges are not just operational annoyances. They create friction for customers, increase manual work, and slow down decision-making.

Xerpium Website & eCommerce Module: Core Capabilities

Xerpium’s Website & eCommerce module is designed to remove the gap between digital sales and core business operations. The module includes several built-in capabilities that work together as part of the broader Xerpium ERP suite.

The module provides a website builder that allows businesses to create and manage their digital storefront within the same environment as their ERP. Xerpium includes an online catalog that is part of the ERP environment. This means product information presented to customers online can remain aligned with the data used across the organization. Integrated Checkout

The checkout process is integrated directly into the ERP. When a customer completes a purchase on the website, the transaction flows into the system without requiring a separate handoff. This creates a smoother order process and gives internal teams faster access to sales data.
