# Coal Mining ERP: Managing Reserves, Production, Quality, and Compliance with Xerpium

Coal mining is a high-volume, operationally intensive business. Every day, mine operators must coordinate site permits, reserve estimates, 24/7 shift production, heavy equipment, fuel consumption, stockpile quality, HSE incidents, government reporting, and export documentation. When these processes run on disconnected spreadsheets or separate systems, errors multiply: production tonnage does not match stockpile movement, quality data is inconsistent, royalty calculations become manual guesswork, and ESDM reporting consumes days of back-office effort.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated coal mining suite built into a broader enterprise platform. It connects mine sites, blocks, seams, production orders, stockpiles, laboratory results, delivery orders, government reports, and accounting in one auditable workflow.

Common Challenges in Coal Mining Operations

  • Managing multiple mine sites and blocks with different permits, geological zones, and reserve estimates.
  • Tracking 24/7 shift production, heavy equipment usage, fuel consumption, and actual versus planned tonnage.
  • Maintaining consistent coal quality from mine face to stockpile, delivery, and customer certification.
  • Calculating royalties and generating structured reports for ESDM production, sales, environmental, and export obligations.
  • Controlling HSE compliance, permits to work, incident investigations, and corrective actions.
  • Coordinating export documentation and trade finance, including PEB, certificate of origin, LC, and landed cost.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Coal Mining

Mine Asset and Reserve Management

Xerpium starts with coal.mine.site, which captures site code, GPS coordinates, total area, province, district, address, and permit data such as permit number, type (IUP/IUPK/IPR), issue date, expiry, and computed days remaining. Sites have workflow states—Active, Suspended, or Closed—and calculate block count and total reserve tonnage from linked blocks.

Within each site, coal.mine.block records block code, area, depth, mining type (Open Pit, Underground, or Alluvial), geological zone, estimated reserve tonnage, and estimated calorific value (CV). Each block links to a coal.seam, which stores seam thickness, depth from/to, and typical quality parameters such as CV, ash percentage, moisture percentage, volatile matter, and sulfur. Block status follows an exploration-to-depletion path: Exploration → Development → Production → Depleted.

Production and Operational Control

Daily operations run through coal.production.order, with an auto-sequence format such as CPO/2026/00001. Each production order links to a mine site and an active block, references a seam, and requires shift, production date, and responsible employee. The order tracks planned tonnage versus actual tonnage and computes achievement percentage. Quality at extraction is captured directly on the order, including actual CV, actual ash percentage, and actual moisture percentage. Total fuel liters are computed from production lines.

Under each production order, coal.production.line captures equipment, operator assignment, start and end time, computed duration hours, tons extracted, fuel used, and operational notes. coal.cost.center and coal.shift support analytic accounting per site or block and Day/Night/Evening scheduling for 24/7 operations.

coal.equipment manages heavy assets such as excavators, dump trucks, loaders, bulldozers, graders, drill rigs, and compactors. Each asset includes registry details, status (Active, Under Maintenance, Idle, Retired), current hours, purchase value, residual value, insurance, maintenance schedules, total maintenance cost, and total fuel and operating hours.

Stockpile, Quality, and Certification

When a production order is done, coal.stockpile automatically adds tonnage through the _add_tons method. Stockpiles track maximum capacity, current tonnage, and weighted average CV, ash, and moisture. Movements in and out maintain weighted average quality, so inventory remains consistent with production and delivery.

Quality assurance uses coal.sample, which supports sources such as mine face, stockpile, delivery, and rehandle, and sample types including chip, grab, channel, and composite. Samples move through Draft → Submitted → Analyzed → Reported. coal.lab.result records proximate analysis, ultimate analysis, gross and net calorific value, analyst, and analysis date, with a workflow from Draft → Done → Verified. coal.quality.certificate then links to a sample and issues grade classifications such as Grade A/B/C, Sub-bituminous, or Lignite based on CV, including parameters and customer linkage.

Delivery, Sales, and Export Operations

coal.delivery.order creates a unique delivery order linked to a sale order. It captures truck number, driver, vessel for barging, gross weight, tare weight, computed net weight, delivery quality parameters, dispatch date, and delivery date, with states Draft → Dispatched → Delivered. coal.sale.order and coal.vessel manage customer contracts, pricing per ton, quality specifications, vessel registry, invoice integration, and ordered versus delivered tonnage.

For export, Xerpium supports export delivery orders with container, vessel, port loading/discharge, and incoterm. The platform also manages PEB with DJBC registration number and CEISA integration, packing lists, certificate of origin such as Form D for ASEAN or non-ASEAN preferential tariff, and USD export invoices with FOB value. Trade finance includes LC sight/usance, TT/remittance, landed cost calculation, and USD/IDR forex tracking.

HSE, Government Reporting, and Accounting

coal.hse.incident records incident types from personal injury and near miss to environmental, fire, vehicle, chemical spill, and structural. It tracks severity, root cause analysis, corrective and preventive actions, lost time days, first aid, hospitalization, witnesses, photos, and attachments. States move from Draft → Reported → Investigating → Closed. coal.safety.inspection and Permit to Work manage daily, weekly, and monthly checklists with auto-computed scores, plus permit types such as Hot Work, Confined Space, Working at Height, and Excavation.

coal.govt.report covers production, sales/PLN, environmental, royalty, and export reports. It auto-computes total production tonnage, delivery tonnage, revenue, and average CV/ash/moisture using the _generate_report_json() method, structured per report type. coal.royalty.rate configures royalty rates per coal type with effective dates and history, and royalty amounts are computed automatically in government reports.

Mining-specific accounting includes cost centers per site, automatic journal entries when coal moves to stockpile, revenue matching on confirmed delivery, royalty accrual per production period, asset depletion, and multi-company support for multi-site operations.

AI Dashboard and Assistant

Xerpium includes AI dashboard features that generate full production dashboards from a natural language prompt, convert natural language to SQL for questions such as “Produksi bulan Januari per site,” analyze production trends and CV declines, and stream Copilot chat responses with mining dashboard context. The AI assistant can answer questions like “Produksi hari ini berapa ton?” or “Rata-rata CV bulan ini,” and generate ESDM production reports.

Business Benefits

  • Faster ESDM reporting with structured, auto-generated data per report type.
  • Consistent royalty accrual and government compliance through configured royalty rates and effective dates.

  • Integrated export and trade finance documentation reduces shipment delays and billing errors.

Example Operational Flow

A production supervisor opens a production order for an active open pit block. Day shift operators log production lines with excavator and dump truck assignments, start/end times, tons extracted, and fuel used. When the order is completed, tonnage is automatically added to the target stockpile, and extraction quality data—actual CV, ash, and moisture—is recorded on the production order.

A mine face sample is collected, submitted, analyzed in the lab, and reported. The stockpile quality updates with weighted average parameters. A customer delivery order linked to a sale order records truck and net weight, dispatches the coal, and confirms delivery. A quality certificate is issued based on the sample and delivery quality. Meanwhile, the government report aggregates production tonnage, delivery tonnage, revenue, and royalty automatically from the same data, and accounting posts production, revenue, and royalty accrual entries without rekeying.
