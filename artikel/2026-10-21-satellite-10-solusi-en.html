10 Best ERP Solutions for Satellite City Developer Operations

October 21, 2026

Satellite city developers operate across large land banks, multiple clusters, long-running PPJB agreements, installment receivables, infrastructure contractors, unit handover checklists, community facilities, and recurring IPL billing. When these processes run on separate spreadsheets, mismatched unit stock, overdue payment schedules, invisible infrastructure progress, and weak handover evidence become daily problems. The list below focuses on ten operational modules in the Xerpium Satellite suite that directly support satellite city developer workflows, from master project data to recurring community billing.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. sc.project — Project / Township: Centralizes township master data, timeline state, and owner assignment to prevent version-control gaps.
  2. sc.cluster — Cluster / Block: Tracks block-level area, type, and computed sold/available counts to stop manual stock errors.
  3. sc.kavling — Kavling / Unit: Manages individual lots, pricing, physical attributes, and status across sales, handover, and billing.
  4. sc.kavling.type — Tipe Kavling: Standardizes unit templates and links products for faster quotation and sales order generation.
  5. sc.customer.agreement — PPJB: Controls binding sales agreements, payment schemes, down payment, and installment logic in one lifecycle.
  6. sc.payment.schedule — Jadwal Pembayaran: Auto-generates installment and fine lines with invoice links to prevent missed billings.
  7. sc.infrastructure — Infrastruktur Proyek: Tracks infrastructure types, budgets, contractors, and auto-computed progress.
  8. sc.handover — Serah Terima Unit: Captures handover schedule, condition assessment, checklist, and auto-updates unit status.
  9. sc.facility — Fasilitas Komunitas: Manages community facility capacity, operational status, and monthly maintenance costs.
  10. sc.ipl.billing.line — Billing Line: Automates bulk recurring IPL charges and invoice creation for sold or handed-over units.

1. sc.project — Project / Township

satellite city developers need a single master record for every township. sc.project provides that control point. Each project receives an auto-sequence code in SC.PRJ/#### format, a type such as Residential, Commercial, Mixed-Use, or Industrial, a location with city/district and total area in hectares, and a master plan document link. The timeline tracks commencement date, target completion, and state from draft to planned, in_progress, and completed. The developer or owner is linked through res.partner, so project-level reporting and document ownership are not scattered across files. This solves the common operational problem of missing version control and unclear project ownership.

2. sc.cluster — Cluster / Block

sc.cluster organizes township land into planned areas. Each cluster has a unique code per project, area in hectares, and a type matching residential, commercial, mixed-use, or industrial. Clusters are linked as a one2many field from sc.project with cascade delete, so removing a project does not leave orphan cluster records. Computed stats via @api.depends provide kavling_count, available_count, and sold_count, and the display format is [Code] Cluster Name. This gives real-time block-level sales visibility and prevents manual stock counting errors. Satellite city developers can compare cluster performance without exporting data to external spreadsheets.

3. sc.kavling — Kavling / Unit

sc.kavling manages individual lots as the core stock item. Lot numbers are unique per cluster and displayed as cluster-code-number. Each kavling links to a kavling type for template dimensions, and land price plus building price are auto-computed as total price. Status moves from Available to Reserved, Sold, Handed Over, or Cancelled, while physical attributes include land area, building area, facing direction, corner lot, and facing road. Relations to agreement_id, partner_id, handover_id, and sale_order_id connect the unit across sales, handover, and billing, eliminating duplicate unit records and reducing the chance of selling the same lot twice.

4. sc.kavling.type — Tipe Kavling

sc.kavling.type standardizes unit templates before individual lots are created. Each type code is unique with sequence order and records dimensions: building area, land area, bedrooms, bathrooms, and floors. A linked product from product.product enables direct integration with sale.order, so approved PPJB agreements can create sales orders without rekeying product data. The display format is Type Name (100m² / 50m²), making it easy for sales teams to identify standard sizes. This reduces inconsistency in unit specifications and speeds up quotation and contract generation for satellite city developers.

5. sc.customer.agreement — PPJB

sc.customer.agreement manages the binding sales agreement between buyer and developer. Agreement numbers auto-sequence as SC.AGR/#### with tracking enabled, and parties include buyer, project, kavling, and kavling type. Pricing captures land price, building price, discount, and net price with auto-compute. Payment schemes support Cash, Installment 12/24/36/48/60 months, and Custom. Down payment percentage automatically calculates DP amount, and installment amount is computed as (net - DP) / installment_count. The state flow Draft, Confirmed, Approved, Payment In Progress, Completed, Handed Over ensures every PPJB moves through a controlled lifecycle, and action approve auto-creates a sale.order.

6. sc.payment.schedule — Jadwal Pembayaran

sc.payment.schedule keeps installment receivables on schedule. When an agreement is completed, payment lines are auto-generated with types Down Payment, Installment, Fine, and Other. Each line stores due date, amount, and currency with multi-currency support, and links to account.move through invoice_id. Payment status is computed from invoice payment_state, and related fields store partner_id, project_id, and kavling_id for reporting. Cash scheme creates a single down payment line equal to net price, while installment scheme creates DP at sequence 10 and installment lines at 11, 12, 13, and so on. This prevents missed billings and late payment fines.

7. sc.infrastructure — Infrastruktur Proyek

sc.infrastructure tracks the delivery of physical works such as roads, bridges, water supply, electricity, drainage, gas distribution, telecom, and green space or park. Each infrastructure item records length, width, budget amount, and actual cost, plus construction start, target completion, and actual completion dates. Progress is auto-computed from milestone percentages via @api.depends milestone_ids.progress_pct. A contractor is linked through res.partner, and state moves Planned, In Progress, Completed, or Cancelled. This gives satellite city developers a single control point for infrastructure budget, timeline, and contractor accountability instead of separate project spreadsheets.

8. sc.handover — Serah Terima Unit

sc.handover controls unit delivery to buyers. Handover numbers auto-sequence as SC.HDO/####, and the name format is [No] - Buyer Name. Relations connect buyer, agreement, kavling, and project. The schedule stores planned date and actual date, with actual date auto-set when complete. Condition assessment captures Good, Minor Defect, or Major Defect, and a checklist boolean plus attachment count supports quality control. The action_complete auto-update changes kavling status to handed_over and agreement status to handed_over. This ensures defects are recorded and handover evidence is retained before unit status closes.

9. sc.facility — Fasilitas Komunitas

sc.facility manages community assets after handover. Facility types include Club House, Swimming Pool, Gym, Playground, Mosque/Musholla, Park, Security Post, and Commercial Area. Each facility records capacity, operational status, opening date, and monthly maintenance cost. Facilities are linked as a one2many field from sc.project with cascade delete. This helps satellite city developers plan facility operations, track when each asset becomes operational, and monitor recurring maintenance costs across projects. It solves the common issue of completing infrastructure but losing visibility into facility readiness and operating cost.

10. sc.ipl.billing.line — Billing Line

sc.ipl.billing.line automates recurring IPL charges for sold or handed-over units. Lines are generated per kavling that is sold or handed_over and has a partner. Owner is related from kavling, amount is a monetary value per kavling per month, and invoice link connects account.move when action_post runs. The action_generate_lines method searches all sold kavlings, skips existing lines, and creates new ones in bulk. The _create_invoice method then creates out_invoice records with the IPL product. This removes manual monthly invoicing and ensures no occupied unit is missed in community fee billing.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Start by mapping your operational lifecycle from project to recurring billing, then select modules that cover project, cluster, kavling, PPJB, payment schedule, infrastructure, handover, facility, and IPL. Prioritize solutions with auto-sequence numbering and computed fields because they reduce manual data entry and status errors. Look for native links to sale.order, account.move, and res.partner so approved agreements, invoices, and contractor or buyer records flow without rekeying. Finally, confirm that the solution supports multi-project, multi-currency, and configurable workflow needs for a growing township portfolio, especially if you manage separate companies or mixed-use developments.

Conclusion
