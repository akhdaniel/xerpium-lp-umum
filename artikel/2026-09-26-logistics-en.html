# Logistics ERP: End-to-End Shipment, Fleet, and Cold Chain Control with Xerpium

Logistics operations rarely break in just one place. A shipment may begin as a sales order, move through route selection, rate calculation, vehicle assignment, multi-modal handoffs, temperature monitoring, proof of delivery, invoicing, and claims handling—while finance waits for cost and margin visibility. When these functions live in separate tools, operations teams lose time reconciling documents, tracking statuses, and manually re-entering data.

Xerpium ERP solves this by bringing shipment management, routing, rate cards, fleet operations, container tracking, warehousing, cold chain compliance, and logistics accounting into one connected system. The result is a logistics operation that can plan, execute, track, bill, and analyze from a single source of truth.

Common Logistics Challenges

Without an integrated logistics ERP, B2B teams typically face several recurring problems:

  • Shipment, fleet, warehouse, and accounting data are scattered across disconnected applications.
  • Pricing varies inconsistently across routes, vehicle types, and service categories.
  • Real-time visibility is limited, and proof of delivery is often paper-based or delayed.
  • Invoice creation, cost recognition, and profit-margin calculation require manual reconciliation.
  • Cold chain compliance, container utilization, and claim management become difficult to control at scale.

How Xerpium ERP Solves Logistics Complexity

Xerpium Logistics handles the full shipment lifecycle using purpose-built modules that work together rather than as isolated add-ons.

Shipment and Route Planning

The Shipment module captures shipment type—Pickup, Delivery, Transfer, Return, or Multimodal—and service type such as Trucking, Forwarding, Warehousing, Last-Mile, Project, or Cold Chain. Each shipment follows a defined state flow from Draft through Confirmed, Assigned, In Transit, Delivered, and Completed.

Shipments link to origin and destination addresses, a route, and assigned vehicle and driver records from fleet management. The Route module stores origin and destination cities, distance, transit time, route type—Road, Sea, Air, Rail, or Multimodal—estimated cost, and shipment count per route.

Rate Cards and Service Categories

Xerpium rate cards make pricing transparent and repeatable. Teams can define pricing dimensions per kilometer, kilogram, cubic meter, or hour, across vehicle types from motorcycle to container. Each rate card supports minimum charges and validity periods. The system can automatically compute shipping price from weight, volume, and distance using compute_shipping_price, reducing manual quotation errors.

Service categories map each service type to a unique company code, income, expense, and COGS accounts, and a default billing product. Shipment counts per category give finance and operations a clear view of volume by service line.

Shipment Operations and Accounting

Shipment lines handle multiple items with weight, volume, pieces, and price per line, with automatic totals. Teams can schedule planned pickup and delivery times and record actual times for tracking. Revenue minus actual cost is computed automatically, giving a real-time profit margin per shipment and per route.

Invoicing is directly connected to logistics. Users can create an invoice from a shipment using action_create_invoice, so shipment lines flow into billing without re-keying. Cost tracking compares estimated versus actual cost per shipment, while analytic distribution supports cost centers by project or department.

Fleet and Container Operations

Fleet management integrates vehicle master data, driver records with an is_driver flag, and vehicle and driver assignment per shipment. Maintenance schedules, fuel tracking, and utilization analytics help teams control fleet costs.

For containerized operations, Xerpium supports standard ISO container types—20ft, 40ft, 40HC, 45HC—across ten categories including Dry Van, Reefer, Open Top, Flat Rack, Tank, Ventilated, Bulk, Car Carrier, Insulated, and Custom. Container records include ISO 6346 numbers, ownership status, and location tracking across depots, ports, terminals, warehouses, and maintenance facilities.

Container movements capture gate in/out, transfer, loading, and discharge with before/after status and condition. Preventive, corrective, inspection, and certification maintenance can be tracked with parts, labor, and cost. Financial fields include purchase price, current value, lease rate, and insurance. Full Container Load shipments integrate through container_ids many2many fields.

Freight Forwarding and Warehouse Operations

For freight forwarding, Xerpium supports multi-modal movements combining road, sea, air, and rail. Consolidation allows multiple shipments per container, with documentation such as Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, and packing list. Customs tracking for PIB/PEB is available for international shipments, alongside cost tracking for ocean freight, handling, and documentation fees.

Warehouse operations include storage zones, cross-docking for fast inbound-to-outbound transfer, putaway strategies for optimized storage allocation, and wave or batch picking. Inter-warehouse stock movement is supported as part of stock transfer operations.

Cold Chain Compliance

Cold chain logistics is managed through dedicated controls. Shipments can be flagged as cold chain with min/max temperature range validation. Real-time temperature logs track readings during transit, and alerts trigger when temperatures move outside the acceptable range. The module supports food, pharma, and chemicals industries with regulatory compliance in mind.

Real-Time Tracking, Proof of Delivery, and Claims

Tracking events include Created, Confirmed, Picked Up, In Transit, At Hub, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Exception, and Returned. Each event can record latitude and longitude, timestamp, photo evidence, status color, and the reporting driver or hub personnel.

Proof of delivery captures recipient information, digital signature, delivery datetime, and multiple delivery photos. POD statuses include Pending, Delivered, Partial, and Refused. When POD is confirmed, the shipment can automatically move to Delivered.

Claims management covers Damage, Loss, Delay, Shortage, Wrong Delivery, and Other. Claims follow a status flow from Open to Investigating, then Resolved or Rejected, and finally Closed. Priorities range from Low to Urgent, with claim amount, resolved amount, photos, documents, and responsible PIC tracked.

AI Assistant and Dashboard Intelligence

Xerpium’s AI capabilities support logistics through natural language dashboard generation, natural language to SQL queries, chart insight for trend and anomaly analysis, and Copilot chat with dashboard context. Conditional formatting adds dynamic visual cues based on values.

The AI assistant can answer operational questions such as “Cari shipment dengan nomor resi X” or “Tracking terakhir shipment Z,” and execute tasks like creating a shipment for a customer or generating revenue summaries per route.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth from sales order to shipment, POD, and invoice.
  • Transparent pricing through rate cards and automatic shipping price computation.
  • Real-time tracking and digital proof of delivery improve customer confidence.
  • Faster invoicing and cost recognition with account mapping by service category.
  • Real-time profit-margin visibility per shipment and per route.
  • Better control of fleet, containers, warehouse zones, and cold chain compliance.

Example Workflow: From Sales Order to Paid Invoice

A B2B distributor receives a sales order for a cold chain pharmaceutical shipment. Xerpium automatically creates a shipment from that sales order. A road route is selected with known distance and transit time. The rate card computes the shipping price based on weight, volume, and distance, including any minimum charge.

A refrigerated vehicle and driver are assigned from fleet records. Warehouse staff complete picking using wave picking, then the shipment moves through tracking events: Picked Up, In Transit, At Hub, and Out for Delivery. Cold chain temperature logs record conditions throughout transit, with alerts if the range is breached.

At delivery, the recipient signs digitally and photos are captured. POD confirmation automatically marks the shipment as Delivered. An invoice is generated from shipment lines, actual costs are matched, and the profit margin updates in real time on the logistics dashboard. If damage occurs, a claim is opened with photo evidence and tracked to resolution.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP gives logistics teams a connected platform for planning, execution, tracking, billing, and analysis. Instead of managing shipments in one system, vehicles and containers in another, and financials in a third, logistics leaders can run operations from a single integrated suite.
