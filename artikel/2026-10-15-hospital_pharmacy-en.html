# Hospital & Pharmacy ERP: Unifying Clinical, Pharmacy, and Billing Operations with Xerpium

Hospitals and pharmacies manage high-volume patient flows, strict clinical documentation, controlled substances, insurance claims, and national health interoperability. When patient data, prescriptions, wards, billing, and regulatory reporting live in separate systems, care teams lose time and financial visibility. Xerpium’s Hospital & Pharmacy ERP addresses this by connecting patient administration, clinical workflows, pharmacy, inpatient management, billing, and BPJS/SatuSehat integration in one enterprise suite.

Common challenges in hospital and pharmacy operations

  • Fragmented patient and medical records: MR number, BPJS, insurance, allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contacts scattered across departments.
  • Manual queues and visit statuses: polyclinic queues, priority handling, and visit creation without a single operational view.
  • Pharmacy complexity: generic and brand medicines, BPOM registration, dosage forms, controlled substances, batch expiry, and stock integration.
  • Inaccurate billing: consultation, procedure, medicine, lab, radiology, room, and nursing charges not always captured or reconciled with accounting.
  • Compliance and interoperability: BPJS Kesehatan claims, SEP numbers, and SatuSehat/FHIR resources must be managed reliably.

Core Xerpium capabilities for hospital and pharmacy

Patient, doctor, and polyclinic management

Xerpium extends the partner record into a full patient profile: MR number, NIK, BPJS number, insurance name and policy, demographics, blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, emergency contact, and lifetime visit, admission, and bill counts. MR numbers can be generated automatically from a sequence. Doctor master records track name, SIP, STR, specialization, department, consultation fee, contact, photo, active/inactive status, and visit count. Departments and poli include Rawat Jalan, Rawat Inap, UGD, Lab, Radiology, Pharmacy, Surgery, and ICU, with head-of-department assignments.

Ward and inpatient room management

Ward records cover room types such as VIP, VVIP, Kelas 1, Kelas 2, Kelas 3, ICU, NICU, Isolasi, and UGD. Each ward has capacity, rate per night, status including Available, Occupied, and Maintenance, floor and building location, department link, occupied count, and facility notes.

Medicine catalog, pharmacy master, and ICD-10

The medicine catalog supports hard drugs, over-the-counter medicines, limited over-the-counter medicines, narcotics, psychotropics, medical devices, and consumables. For each item, Xerpium tracks generic name, brand, manufacturer, BPOM registration, dosage form, strength, storage, therapeutic class, minimum stock, and expiry. Controlled substance tracking supports narcotics and psychotropics. An ICD-10 catalog provides international diagnosis codes for clinical and billing workflows.

Queue management and outpatient visits

Polyclinic queues generate automatic daily numbers per date and department. Staff can set priority Normal, Urgent, or Emergency, record arrival time and call time, move patients through Waiting, Called, In Progress, Done, or Skipped, and create an outpatient visit directly from the queue. Payment type can be Umum, BPJS, or Asuransi. Outpatient visits capture date, doctor, poli, payment type, vital signs, anamnesis, ICD-10 diagnoses with a primary flag, main diagnosis, SOAP notes, and referrals to specialists, hospitals, labs, or radiology.

Prescription, dispensing, lab, radiology, and procedures

Prescriptions are created per doctor with medicine, dosage, route, duration, and instructions. Routes include Oral, IV, IM, SC, and Topical. The prescription links to a diagnosis and checks patient allergy notes. Workflow can move from Draft to Confirmed, Dispensing, Dispensed, or Cancelled, with configurable stages. Drug dispensing from pharmacy links to the prescription, tracks pharmacist, batch or lot and expiry, quantity prescribed versus dispensed, and automatically creates stock picking from pharmacy.

Lab orders support sample types such as Blood, Urine, Stool, Sputum, and Swab, with workflows from Draft through Ordered, Sample Collected, Processing, Resulted, and Verified. Results store value, normal range, and flag normal, low, high, or critical. Radiology orders and medical procedure records are also covered, including procedure catalog, category, price, duration, product link for invoicing, and auto-fill from catalog.

Inpatient admission, vital sign monitoring, and discharge

Inpatient admission records include attending doctor, ward, bed number, payment type, admission and discharge diagnoses with ICD-10, expected discharge date, and workflow stages such as Draft, Admitted, In Ward, ICU, Discharged, or Cancelled. Length of stay is auto-computed. Discharge condition includes Recovered, Improved, Referred, AMA, or Died. Vital sign monitoring captures periodic BP, pulse, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2, blood glucose, urine output, pain scale, consciousness level, nurse record, and timestamps. Discharge summary includes discharge diagnosis, treatment summary, procedures, medications, follow-up plan, auto LOS, and ward release back to available.

Billing, invoice, and accounting alignment

Patient bills include subtotal, discount, tax, total, paid, and due amounts. Bill lines can cover consultation, procedure, medicine, lab, radiology, room, and nursing. Payment methods include Cash, Transfer, BPJS, Asuransi, and Debit/Credit Card. Xerpium supports insurance coverage, automatic room charges on admission discharge, adding procedures and medicines to the bill, and generating account.move out_invoice entries. Accounting visibility includes payment tracking and revenue analysis per department or poli.

BPJS Kesehatan and SatuSehat/FHIR integration

For Indonesian healthcare compliance, Xerpium supports BPJS Kesehatan eligibility checks through VClaim and PCare APIs, BPJS referral creation, SEP numbers per visit, and claims for outpatient, inpatient, UGD, procedures, medicine, and lab. Claim status can move from Draft to Submitted, Processing, Approved/Rejected, or Paid. Referral management records referral letters to specialists, hospitals, labs, or radiology, BPJS referral format, diagnosis and reason, anamnesis summary, treatment given, status, auto sequence, and doctor information.

For SatuSehat and FHIR interoperability, Xerpium syncs Patient, Encounter, Practitioner, Location, Organization, Condition, Medication, and Observation resources. FHIR resources also include Composition, Clinical Impression, Service Request, Procedure, Allergy Intolerance, Medication Request, and Medication Dispense. API management includes sandbox and production environment switching, OAuth 2.0 token caching, retry logic for errors 429/500/502/503, API logs, sync state, CRUD FHIR operations, error tracking, and multi-company configuration.

Business benefits

  • Single patient record from registration through discharge and pharmacy dispensing.
  • Faster polyclinic queues and fewer manual handoffs between front office, nurse, doctor, and pharmacy.
  • Stronger pharmacy control with BPOM registration, expiry tracking, controlled substance tracking, and stock integration.
  • More accurate billing with automatic room charges, procedure and medicine capture, insurance coverage, and accounting invoices.
  • Improved compliance with BPJS Kesehatan and SatuSehat/FHIR, including audit logs and sync error tracking.

Example use-case: outpatient visit to pharmacy and billing

A patient registers with an auto-generated MR number and BPJS eligibility is checked. The polyclinic queue creates a daily queue number and payment type. The doctor records vital signs, anamnesis, ICD-10 diagnosis, SOAP notes, and a prescription. The pharmacist dispenses based on the prescription, tracking batch and quantity, and pharmacy stock picking is created automatically. Lab or radiology orders are processed with results. The patient bill collects consultation, procedure, medicine, and lab or radiology lines, applies insurance coverage, and generates an invoice. For BPJS patients, SEP and claim status follow the configured workflow. For SatuSehat, relevant FHIR resources are synced with sync state and API logs.
