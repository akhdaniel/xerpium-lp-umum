# Manufacturing (MRP) with Xerpium ERP: A Smarter Way to Manage BOM, Routing, and Work Orders

Manufacturing operations become more complex every year. Product structures change, production routing must stay consistent, work orders need clear oversight, and production costs must be calculated accurately. When these elements live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, manufacturers face delayed decisions, margin erosion, and audit pressure.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with its Manufacturing (MRP) module. As a base module, it unifies bill of materials (BOM), routing, work order, and production cost calculation in one system. Because it is built to connect with all other Xerpium modules, manufacturing data no longer sits in isolation from the rest of the business. Multi-company, multi-currency, audit trail, real-time dashboards, and the integrated AI Assistant make Xerpium MRP a practical foundation for modern production management.

Common Manufacturing Challenges

Many manufacturers recognise these pain points:

  • BOM changes are not consistently reflected in production, leading to incorrect material usage or outdated product definitions.
  • Routing steps vary between teams or sites, making it difficult to standardise production processes and compare performance.
  • Work orders are tracked in scattered systems, so production status and completion data are hard to consolidate.
  • Production cost calculation is manual or fragmented, making product-level profitability unclear.
  • Managing multiple companies and currencies adds complexity, especially when auditors require consistent traceability.

Without a connected ERP foundation, these issues compound as production volumes and business structures grow.

How Xerpium Manufacturing (MRP) Solves These Challenges

Xerpium Manufacturing (MRP) is designed as a base module that integrates with the entire Xerpium ERP suite. This means production data can flow into the broader business context without duplicate entry or disconnected processes.

Bill of Materials (BOM)

The BOM capability gives manufacturers a controlled way to define what goes into a product. With a central product structure, production planning and costing work from the same reference point. This reduces the risk of outdated or inconsistent product definitions affecting production orders.

Routing

Routing functionality helps standardise the sequence of production steps for a manufactured item. When routing is defined consistently, work orders can follow a clear process, and production cost calculation can reflect the intended production flow. This supports more predictable operations and better cost transparency.

Work Order

Work order management connects the planned BOM and routing to actual production execution. Instead of managing production instructions outside the ERP, teams can work from a unified production record. This creates a clearer link between what was planned, what is being produced, and what the production cost should be.

Production Cost Calculation

Xerpium MRP combines BOM and routing data to support production cost calculation. This gives operations and finance teams a consistent basis for understanding production costs. Rather than relying on separate spreadsheets, manufacturers can calculate costs using the same product and process data used in production.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium MRP supports multi-company and multi-currency environments. For manufacturers operating across entities or regions, this reduces the need for separate reporting structures. The audit trail provides a record of changes, strengthening traceability for internal reviews and external compliance requirements.

Real-Time Dashboard and Reports

Manufacturing teams need visibility without waiting for end-of-period reports. Xerpium MRP includes real-time dashboards and reports, allowing production and management teams to monitor relevant data as it changes. This supports faster, more informed operational decisions.

AI Assistant Integration

The Manufacturing (MRP) module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This allows users to access manufacturing information through AI-supported interaction, reducing the time spent searching for data. Teams can work more efficiently by using the assistant to surface relevant production information within the ERP environment.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Manufacturing (MRP)

  • One connected manufacturing base — BOM, routing, work order, and production cost calculation are unified within Xerpium ERP.
  • Stronger cross-module integration — as a base module, MRP connects with all other Xerpium modules, reducing data silos.
  • Simplified multi-entity operations — multi-company and multi-currency support helps manufacturers manage complex group structures more effectively.
  • Improved traceability — audit trail capabilities support accountability and compliance across production processes.
  • Real-time visibility — dashboards and reports give production teams a current view of manufacturing data.
  • Faster data access — integration with the AI Assistant helps users find relevant information without manual searching.
  • More consistent costing — production cost calculation is based on the same BOM and routing data used in production.

Example Use Case

Consider a manufacturer that produces assembled goods across multiple companies.

The engineering team defines the BOM and routing in Xerpium MRP. A production planner creates a work order using that product structure. As the work order moves through production, the routing remains visible, so the team knows which production steps are expected.

Production cost calculation uses the BOM and routing data, giving management a consistent cost view. Because the company operates in more than one location, the multi-company and multi-currency support keeps data aligned without separate spreadsheets. An auditor can later review the audit trail to see what changed and when.

Meanwhile, a production manager opens a real-time dashboard to monitor current production information. Instead of asking another team for a report, they use the Xerpium AI Assistant to retrieve the relevant manufacturing data quickly.

Conclusion

Xerpium Manufacturing (MRP) is more than a standalone production tool. It is a base ERP module that connects BOM, routing, work order, and production cost calculation with the wider business environment. Multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail, real-time dashboards, and AI Assistant integration make it a practical option for manufacturers that need greater control without adding system fragmentation.
