# Data Center & IT System Management with Xerpium ERP: A Unified Financial and Operational Platform

Published: October 5, 2026

Data center and IT service providers operate in a demanding environment. They must manage racks, power, cooling, servers, network devices, service contracts, SLAs, incident tickets, change requests, and recurring billing — often across disconnected spreadsheets and legacy tools. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a localized data center and IT system suite that connects facility operations, service delivery, financial accounting, and analytics in one platform.

Common Challenges in Data Center and IT Service Operations

  • Managing IT assets across full lifecycles, from draft and available to installed, active, maintenance, decommissioned, and disposed.
  • Tracking power, cooling, bandwidth, and rack capacity while preventing overcommitment.
  • Handling SLA compliance, NOC/SOC incident management, escalation, and resolution time.
  • Billing recurring contracts, usage-based services, overage charges, and prepaid annual revenue correctly.
  • Maintaining Indonesian-standard financial reporting with a 6-digit chart of accounts and PPN 11 percent tax defaults.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Data Center & IT System

Operational Control: Facility, Assets, Contracts, and Tickets

The core xerpium_data_center_it module covers facility management for data centers, rooms, racks, and power/cooling tracking. IT assets — servers, network equipment, storage, and security devices — are managed through their full lifecycle. Service contracts support colocation, managed services, cloud, support, and SLA tiers. Service tickets handle NOC/SOC incident management with automatic escalation. Capacity planning monitors power, cooling, and space utilization. Change management provides IT change requests with an approval workflow. Subscriptions enable recurring billing through xerpium_subscription, while usage billing meters power in kWh, bandwidth in GB, and storage in TB.

Stage-Driven Workflows for Assets, Tickets, Contracts, and Changes

The xerpium_dynamic_stage_data_center_it module adds structured stage transitions. Asset lifecycle stages run from Draft to Available, Installed, Active, Maintenance, Decommissioned, and Disposed. Ticket pipeline stages include New, Open, In Progress, Pending, Resolved, Closed, and Cancelled. Contract stages move from Draft to Active, Expired, and Cancelled, with automatic activation on entering the relevant stage. Change workflow stages include Draft, Submitted, Approved, In Progress, Completed, and Cancelled. On-enter methods execute business logic automatically when an object enters a stage, and stage configuration supports user groups, confirmation messages, and back/forward rules per stage.

Localized Financial Management and Reporting

Xerpium includes an Indonesian data center and IT system chart of accounts template with 6-digit account codes using the dc_XXXXXX prefix. Receivable and payable accounts are predefined as dc_120100 for AR and dc_210100 for AP. Revenue streams cover colocation, cloud hosting, managed services, and license revenue. Tax defaults are pre-configured for PPN 11 percent on both sale and purchase transactions. Currency exchange differences use dc_710200 for gains and dc_720200 for losses, while cash differences use dc_710300 for income and dc_720300 for expense.

Financial reporting follows data center standards. The balance sheet groups IT assets such as servers and network equipment. The profit and loss statement breaks down revenue per service type with COGS for bandwidth, power, cooling, and maintenance. The cash flow statement covers operational IT services, investment through IT capital expenditure, and financing. Reports also separate recurring and one-time revenue, service revenue per type and client, and capital expenditure for IT infrastructure. The report structure auto-syncs account_group_ids from the COA template through an XML parsing process and a _post_load_data hook when the chart template is applied.

Dashboard, Analytics, and AI Tools

The vit_dashboard_data_center_it module provides an IT services dashboard with KPI tiles for revenue, contracts, capacity, uptime, and SLA compliance. Charts show monthly revenue, cost trends, top clients, and service breakdown. Data sources include sale_order, project.project, and account_move, with SQL sources for monthly metrics, top clients, and service revenue analytics. A global date filter applies automatically and refreshes every 5 minutes. The dashboard uses a sky blue color theme and a 12-column responsive grid layout from y=0 to y=14.

Dashboard analytics help operators track monthly recurring revenue from active contracts, SLA compliance as uptime target versus actual per month, rack utilization, bandwidth usage, power consumption, top 10 clients by revenue, revenue per service type, incident volume and resolution time, and project status with planned versus actual hours.

The llm_tool_data_center_it module adds AI-powered tools. The Ticket Pattern Analyzer analyzes ticket patterns, auto-categorizes incidents, and suggests resolutions. Capacity Forecast predicts power, cooling, and space needs from usage trends. Contract Summarizer summarizes terms, identifies renewal risks, and highlights SLA obligations. Asset Lookup enables natural language search for IT assets by type, location, or status.

Service-Specific Coverage

Xerpium supports core data center services directly. Colocation services include rack rental for full, half, and quarter racks, power allocation in kW per rack with usage monitoring, included bandwidth plus overage billing, public IP address allocation, 24x7 client access, and remote hands for on-site hardware maintenance.

Cloud hosting covers VPS and virtual machines with CPU, RAM, and storage; SSD, HDD, and backup storage; VLAN, firewall, and load balancer networking; daily backup and disaster recovery; auto-scaling of CPU/RAM based on usage; and API access for provisioning and management.

Managed services include server management with OS patching, monitoring, and troubleshooting; database administration for MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB; security with firewall, IDS/IPS, and vulnerability scanning; 24x7 monitoring with alert notification; SLA-based incident response; and reporting on monthly uptime, incidents, and performance.

Professional services support consulting, infrastructure design, architecture review, server and cloud migration projects, new server deployment, network setup, disaster recovery planning and failover testing, IT team training, and IT security or compliance audits.

Example Use Case: Colocation Client Incident and Billing

A colocation client reports a hardware issue through the NOC. Xerpium creates a service ticket in the New stage and assigns it according to SLA tiers. The ticket moves through In Progress and Pending stages until resolved, with automatic escalation rules applied. The engineer logs time on the incident, and service work is linked to the client contract.

At the end of the month, usage data from power, bandwidth, and storage metering flows into usage billing. The client is invoiced for rack rental, included services, and any overage charges. Subscription billing handles the recurring colocation contract, while prepaid annual revenue is deferred and recognized according to accounting rules. The dashboard updates revenue, SLA compliance, and incident trends for management visibility.
