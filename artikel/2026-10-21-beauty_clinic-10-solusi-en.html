10 Best ERP Solutions for Beauty Clinic Operations

October 21, 2026

Running a beauty clinic involves more than booking appointments. Patient histories, treatment catalogs, multi-session plans, membership packages, doctor commissions, prescriptions, and consumable stock all need to move together. When these functions live in separate spreadsheets or standalone tools, staff double-enter data, sessions get missed, inventory disappears, and doctor commissions become a monthly dispute. A well-scoped ERP for beauty clinic operations connects the front desk, treatment rooms, pharmacy, inventory, and accounting in one flow. The ten solutions below are selected from the Xerpium beauty clinic suite because they cover the core operational chain: patient identity, service definition, scheduling, visit execution, treatment plans, package management, prescriptions, commissions, consumption, and daily control.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. Patient Management — centralizes patient identity, biodata, emergency contacts, and medical timeline.
  2. Treatment Catalog — standardizes treatments, pricing, duration, category, and doctor commission rules.
  3. Appointment & Queue — manages calendar-based booking, queue numbers, statuses, and email confirmations.
  4. Admission / Visit — converts walk-ins and appointments into sale orders with consultation, doctor, and chart links.
  5. Treatment Chart — tracks multi-session plans, down payment, invoicing, and completed sessions.
  6. Membership Package — manages package sessions, validity, auto-deduction, and membership status.
  7. Prescription & Pharmacy — digitizes doctor prescriptions, medicine preparation, and dispensing flow.
  8. Doctor Commission — auto-calculates doctor commissions from invoice posting and creates vendor bills.
  9. Product Consumption — records consumable usage per visit, auto-fills cost, and updates stock.
  10. Dashboard Klinik — monitors daily visits, revenue, commissions, memberships, and treatment popularity.

1. Patient Management

Patient Management inherits res.partner with an is_patient flag and auto-sequence patient code. It stores date of birth, gender, blood group, allergies, medical notes, and emergency contact name and phone. The module builds a medical timeline from appointments, treatment charts, and prescriptions, and tracks admission count per patient. For beauty clinic operations, this removes scattered spreadsheets and missing histories. Front desk and clinicians see one reliable patient record, reducing intake time and improving continuity across consultations, follow-up treatments, and emergency contact handling.

2. Treatment Catalog

Treatment Catalog defines treatments with name, code, description, duration in minutes, and price. It classifies services into categories: Consultation, Facial, Injectable, Laser/Device, Body, Hair, and Other. Each treatment links to a product.template for invoicing, carries doctor commission as a fixed amount or percentage of price, and has an is_active flag to disable inactive treatments. This keeps the clinic’s service list consistent across booking, sales, and commission calculation, preventing mismatched prices or forgotten commission rules when new treatments launch or seasonal services are deactivated.

3. Appointment & Queue

Appointment & Queue inherits calendar.event to manage patient booking. The user must select a patient filtered by is_patient, choose a treatment from the catalog, and assign a doctor filtered by is_doctor. It auto-assigns a queue number on admission and applies category-based colors—consultation/facial=1, injectable=2, laser=3. Status moves from Draft to Confirmed, In Progress, Done, or Cancelled; confirming sends an email notification automatically. This solves double-booking and unclear daily flow, helping reception manage queues and treatment rooms with visible calendar colors and automatic patient communication.

4. Admission / Visit

Admission / Visit inherits sale.order with an is_beauty_admission flag. It supports walk-in or from-appointment admission types, auto-generates a queue number, and auto-adds a consultation line if no order line exists. Users can assign a doctor per admission and per treatment line, link to the treatment chart and membership, create a prescription directly from the visit, and track product consumption per visit. This unifies front-office service delivery with back-office sales, avoiding separate invoicing and clinical records. Every visit produces one traceable order with consultation, treatment, doctor, prescription, and consumables in context.

5. Treatment Chart

Treatment Chart manages multi-session plans such as six laser sessions with completed tracking. It links each chart to a patient and treatment, auto-calculates total amount as treatment price multiplied by planned sessions, and records down payment amount for multi-session packages. The module creates invoices per chart to accounting and tracks status from Draft to In Progress, Done, or Cancelled, with sessions linked to calendar.event appointments. This solves the problem of tracking prepaid or planned treatment courses. Clinics can see remaining sessions, collect down payments, and invoice accurately instead of manually following up each session.

6. Membership Package

Membership Package defines package name, treatment, total sessions, price, and validity days, with a service product for invoicing package purchase. Per patient, membership tracks start/end date, remaining sessions, and state—Active, Completed, Expired, or Cancelled. It auto-deducts sessions when an appointment is done via sale order. This module addresses package-based beauty services where clients buy multiple sessions upfront. Front desk no longer manually updates remaining quota. It ensures membership status remains accurate, prevents overuse or expiry confusion, and connects package revenue to invoicing with clear session usage per patient.

7. Prescription & Pharmacy

Prescription & Pharmacy records doctor prescriptions with patient, doctor, date, and valid until. Lines include product, quantity, dosage, and instructions. Prescriptions link to appointment and sale order, and can add prescription products to an admission sale order. Medicine preparation moves through Draft, Preparing, Ready, and Dispensed, auto-loads lines from the prescription, and flags compounded products with is_manufactured. This closes the loop between consultation and pharmacy. Prescribed products become order lines and preparation tasks, reducing manual transcription errors and ensuring medicines or skincare products are prepared and dispensed according to the doctor’s notes.

8. Doctor Commission

Doctor Commission auto-creates commissions from invoice posting via the _post() hook. Calculation uses fixed amount per treatment or percentage of price, based on the doctor assigned per treatment line. The module consolidates commissions by doctor for vendor bills, with status Draft, Approved, and Paid. It creates vendor bills to pay doctor commissions and sets state to paid when the vendor bill is paid. This removes spreadsheet-based commission tracking and dispute-prone month-end calculations. Clinics get per-doctor, per-line commission visibility, automatic consolidation, and an accounting path through vendor bills, reducing payroll errors and improving doctor trust.

9. Product Consumption

Product Consumption tracks consumable or storable products used per visit. Consumption lines auto-fill standard_price from the product, calculate subtotal as quantity multiplied by cost price, and reduce product stock in real time. It uses an is_medicine flag in product.template for medicine items and supports cosmetic stock tracking. This helps clinics account for serums, masks, injectables, and medicines consumed during treatments. Without it, inventory shrink and cost of goods remain invisible. With this module, every visit records actual material usage, keeps stock accurate, and gives management consumption cost data per treatment visit without manual stock counts.

10. Dashboard Klinik

Dashboard Klinik gives daily visits, treatment revenue per period, doctor commission outstanding and paid, active membership and session usage, and treatment popularity. It consolidates operational data from admissions, invoices, memberships, and commissions into one clinic dashboard. This solves the problem of running a beauty clinic without clear daily control. Managers do not need to ask multiple staff for separate reports or reconcile spreadsheets. They can monitor patient flow, revenue trends, popular treatments, commission liabilities, and membership utilization, enabling data-driven decisions on staffing, promotions, and inventory.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Map your operational bottleneck first. If multi-session packages dominate revenue, prioritize Treatment Chart and Membership Package. If daily patient volume is high and queue confusion is frequent, start with Appointment & Queue and Admission / Visit. If doctors are paid per treatment and consumables are significant, include Doctor Commission and Product Consumption from day one. Second, check integration with existing accounting, inventory, and calendar. The suite should work through sale orders, invoices, stock, and calendar events rather than acting as a standalone booking tool. Third, evaluate the data you need for management control. A clinic that cannot see daily visits, revenue by treatment, outstanding commissions, and membership utilization will struggle to make operational decisions. Choose a scope that includes Dashboard Klinik once core transactions are stable. Finally, implement in phases. Start with Patient Management, Treatment Catalog, Appointment & Queue, and Admission / Visit. Then add Treatment Chart, Membership Package, Prescription & Pharmacy, Doctor Commission, Product Consumption, and Dashboard Klinik as staff become comfortable with the workflow.

Conclusion

A beauty clinic ERP is not just booking software. It must coordinate patient records, treatment catalogs, appointments, admissions, multi-session charts, membership packages, prescriptions, doctor commissions, consumable stock, and daily reporting. The ten solutions above cover the operational chain from first contact to paid doctor commission and inventory update.
