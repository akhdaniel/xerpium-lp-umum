# Website & eCommerce with Xerpium ERP: A Unified Platform for Digital B2B Sales

Published on October 22, 2026

B2B buyers increasingly expect the same digital convenience they experience as consumers: a clear online catalog, accurate pricing, and a smooth checkout process. For many companies, however, the website and the ERP system still operate as separate worlds. Product data, inventory information, pricing, and customer orders must be manually transferred between systems, creating delays, errors, and limited visibility.

Xerpium ERP addresses this gap through its Website & eCommerce module. As a base Xerpium module, it is unified with all other modules in the ERP suite. That means your website builder, online catalog, and checkout are not standalone tools—they are connected to the same operational and financial data that runs your business.

This article outlines the common challenges in website and eCommerce operations, explains how Xerpium ERP supports digital sales, and shows the benefits of running your online storefront within the ERP environment.

Common Challenges in Website and eCommerce Operations

Many B2B organizations face similar obstacles when managing their online sales channels:

  • Disconnected front-end and back-office systems force teams to rekey orders, update product data manually, and reconcile separate databases.
  • Multi-company and multi-currency complexity makes it difficult to show accurate pricing, taxes, and product availability across different business entities.
  • Limited audit trail creates compliance risks and slows down the resolution of order disputes or data discrepancies.
  • Delayed reporting prevents leaders from seeing real-time sales, order status, and website performance when they need it.
  • Separate tools and platforms increase maintenance effort, create data silos, and reduce overall operational efficiency.

These challenges are especially damaging for B2B companies that manage multiple legal entities, sell in different currencies, or operate in regulated industries.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses Website & eCommerce Requirements

Xerpium ERP includes a Website & eCommerce module that combines digital storefront capabilities with core ERP functionality. Because it is a base module, it is designed to unify with the rest of the Xerpium platform rather than function as a disconnected add-on.

Website Builder

The module includes a website builder as part of the core Website & eCommerce functionality. Instead of maintaining a separate content management system or external website platform, businesses can build and manage their digital presence within Xerpium ERP.

This approach keeps website operations connected to the same product data, customer records, and business logic used across the ERP. Changes made in the ERP can flow into the website environment without requiring a separate integration project.

Online Catalog

The online catalog feature gives B2B sellers a centralized way to present products and services to customers. Because the catalog is part of the ERP, it can reflect the correct product information, pricing structures, and availability for different business contexts.

The multi-company and multi-currency support in Xerpium means that catalog data can be presented according to the relevant entity and currency, helping businesses serve diverse customer segments without building separate storefronts for every market.

Integrated Checkout

The integrated checkout capability connects the online purchasing experience directly to ERP processes. When a customer completes a checkout, the transaction is captured within the Xerpium environment rather than passed through a separate eCommerce platform.

This integration reduces manual order entry and helps ensure that the order data available to downstream teams is consistent with what the customer actually submitted. Since the Website & eCommerce module is unified with all other Xerpium modules, order information can move through fulfillment, billing, and other operational workflows without being re-entered.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium Website & eCommerce supports multi-company and multi-currency operations. This is particularly relevant for B2B organizations that operate multiple legal entities, serve international customers, or need to manage digital sales under different corporate structures.

The audit trail capability provides a record of changes and transactions within the system. For website and eCommerce operations, this supports accountability, compliance, and traceability. Teams can review what changed, when it changed, and under which organizational context the activity occurred.

Real-Time Dashboard and Reporting

The module includes dashboard and reporting capabilities that provide real-time visibility into website and eCommerce activity. Instead of waiting for end-of-day reports or manual exports, businesses can monitor performance and operational data as it happens.

Real-time reporting helps teams identify issues earlier, respond to customer demand more effectively, and make decisions based on current information rather than delayed snapshots.

AI Assistant Xerpium Integration

Website & eCommerce is integrated with the AI Assistant Xerpium. This connects digital sales operations to Xerpium’s AI capabilities within the ERP environment. Teams can use AI-assisted support while managing website content, catalog data, checkout processes, and reporting.

The AI Assistant integration brings additional operational support to website and eCommerce workflows, making it easier for users to work with information and navigate the platform without leaving the ERP context.

Business Benefits

Combining website and eCommerce functionality with Xerpium ERP offers several operational advantages:

  • Single source of truth: website, catalog, checkout, and back-office data are unified in one ERP platform.
  • Reduced manual effort: integrated checkout and connected workflows lower the need for rekeying and manual data transfers.
  • Consistent multi-entity and multi-currency management: businesses can support multiple companies and currencies without fragmented digital storefronts.
  • Stronger traceability: the built-in audit trail improves compliance, accountability, and issue resolution.
  • Faster decision-making: real-time dashboards and reports provide current visibility into digital sales operations.
  • AI-supported workflows: the AI Assistant Xerpium integration adds efficiency to website and eCommerce tasks.

Example Flow: From Online Catalog to Integrated Checkout and ERP Visibility

Consider a B2B distributor that sells through multiple companies and transacts in more than one currency. With Xerpium ERP, the distributor uses the website builder to create a digital storefront and the online catalog to present products with the correct pricing for each entity and currency.

A customer selects products from the catalog and completes the order through the integrated checkout. The order is captured directly in Xerpium ERP, preserving the relevant company and currency context. From there, the order can move through downstream operational processes without manual re-entry.

Throughout the process, the audit trail records transaction activity, supporting transparency and compliance. Managers monitor sales and order activity through the real-time dashboard, and the team can use the AI Assistant Xerpium to help access information or guide follow-up actions. This reduces manual coordination and keeps digital sales aligned with the rest of the business.

With website builder, online catalog, integrated checkout, multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail, real-time reporting, and AI Assistant integration, Xerpium gives B2B organizations a more connected way to run digital sales.
