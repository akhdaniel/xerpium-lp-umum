# Purchase & Procurement ERP: How Xerpium Unifies PR, RFQ, PO, and 3-Way Matching

Procurement teams often operate in a fragile chain of spreadsheets, email threads, disconnected approvals, and manual matching. A delayed purchase requisition or a mismatched invoice can stall operations, strain supplier relationships, and create compliance risk. For companies running multi-entity operations, the difficulty grows: different currencies, different approval paths, and limited visibility make it hard to answer a simple question such as “What is our committed spend right now?”

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a Purchase & Procurement module that is built into the core ERP platform. It covers the full procurement cycle—from purchase requisition (PR), request for quotation (RFQ), and purchase order (PO) to vendor management and 3-way matching. Because the module is a base part of Xerpium, procurement data does not sit in a separate tool. It connects natively with the rest of the ERP and is supported by real-time dashboards, audit trails, multi-company and multi-currency capabilities, and the Xerpium AI Assistant.

Common Procurement Challenges

Before adopting a unified ERP approach, many B2B organizations face familiar pain points. These challenges are rarely isolated to procurement alone; they affect finance, operations, and supplier management.

  • Fragmented purchase requests: PRs arrive through email, chat, or paper forms, making prioritization and approval tracking difficult.
  • Unstructured supplier communication: RFQs are sent manually, and comparing vendor responses becomes a time-consuming, error-prone process.
  • Weak PO control: Purchase orders may be issued without clear links to approved requests or negotiated terms, increasing the risk of unauthorized spend.
  • Manual reconciliation: Matching purchase orders, goods receipts, and supplier invoices by hand delays payments and creates disputes.
  • Limited audit visibility: Without a central system, auditors cannot easily trace who requested, approved, or changed a procurement document.
  • Multi-entity complexity: Companies operating across multiple entities and currencies struggle to consolidate purchasing data and maintain consistent controls.

How Xerpium Purchase & Procurement Solves These Issues

Xerpium ERP provides one procurement environment that connects these activities end to end. Below are the key components within the Purchase & Procurement module.

Purchase Requisition (PR)

Xerpium captures purchase requests in a structured digital format. Instead of tracking requests through scattered messages, teams can create, review, and approve PRs inside the ERP. This gives procurement a clear starting point for demand and helps ensure that purchases follow the organization’s approval flow.

Request for Quotation (RFQ)

The module supports RFQ management as part of the procurement cycle. Teams can manage quotation requests and vendor responses within the same system that will later hold the selected purchase order. This reduces the disconnect between sourcing and purchasing and keeps supplier communication connected to the transaction record.

Purchase Order (PO)

Xerpium turns approved purchasing activity into purchase orders with a clear link back to the originating request or RFQ. Procurement teams gain better control over what is ordered, from which vendor, and under what terms. Since POs are part of the base ERP, the information is available to other business functions without duplication.

Vendor Management

Vendor management is included in the Purchase & Procurement module. This gives organizations a central place to work with supplier-related purchasing information as part of the broader procurement flow. Rather than maintaining separate supplier lists outside the ERP, teams manage vendor interactions in the same system where orders and invoices are recorded.

3-Way Matching

Xerpium supports 3-way matching, which compares the purchase order, the goods receipt, and the supplier invoice. This control helps finance and procurement teams identify discrepancies before payment. Discrepancies in quantity or price become visible in the system, reducing the risk of overpayment or paying for goods that were not received as expected.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

The Purchase & Procurement module is built for organizations that operate across more than one legal entity. Xerpium supports multi-company and multi-currency scenarios, so procurement data can be managed in the correct entity and currency. At the same time, the platform provides an audit trail for procurement activities. This helps internal teams and external auditors trace document changes, approvals, and transaction history without reconstructing events from emails.

Real-Time Dashboard and Reports

Procurement leaders do not need to wait for month-end spreadsheets. Xerpium provides dashboard and reporting capabilities that reflect procurement information in real time. Teams can monitor open purchase orders, pending approvals, vendor activity, and matching exceptions as part of their daily workflow.

Integrated AI Assistant

Xerpium’s Purchase & Procurement module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This means the procurement process sits within an ERP environment where AI-assisted support is available alongside the core workflows. Instead of switching between disconnected systems, users work in a unified experience that connects procurement data, ERP processes, and AI assistance.

Example Workflow: From Purchase Request to Payment

A mid-sized manufacturer needs to buy raw materials from an overseas supplier. The procurement team creates a purchase requisition in Xerpium for the required quantities. After approval, the team issues an RFQ to several vendors and records their responses in the system.

Once the best vendor is selected, the team converts the approved RFQ into a purchase order. The PO is created in the supplier’s currency, and the transaction remains visible under the correct company entity. When the goods arrive, the warehouse records the receipt in Xerpium. The supplier invoice is then received and compared against the PO and the goods receipt through 3-way matching.

Because the invoice matches the received quantities and agreed price, it moves to payment without manual back-and-forth. Throughout the process, the audit trail shows who approved the PR, who issued the PO, and when the goods were received. Procurement managers can view the status in the real-time dashboard instead of asking for updates by email.
