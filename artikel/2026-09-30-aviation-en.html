# Aviation ERP: How Xerpium Connects Airline, MRO, Cargo, and Crew Operations

September 30, 2026

Aviation operators manage multiple operational layers at once: aircraft airworthiness, crew licensing, flight schedules, passenger bookings, cargo waybills, maintenance work orders, and financial compliance. When these functions run on disconnected systems, the result is reactive decision-making, missed expiry dates, and poor visibility into true operating cost. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a unified aviation suite that combines flight operations, MRO, cargo, crew management, loyalty, accounting, inventory, and AI-assisted operational queries in one platform.

Common operational challenges

  • Fleet airworthiness and maintenance control across airframe hours, cycles, A/B/C/D-checks, AD/SB compliance, and component life limits.
  • Crew qualification and expiry tracking for licenses, medical certificates, passports, type ratings, line checks, and security training.
  • Revenue and cost fragmentation across passenger tickets, cargo, ancillary services, MRO, fuel, catering, and ground handling.
  • Flight schedule accuracy and on-time performance management, including delay attribution and load factor tracking.
  • Cargo and load control complexity around chargeable weight, ULD fill, and MTOW compliance.

Xerpium ERP aviation modules in practice

Airport, route, aircraft type, and fleet

Xerpium starts with accurate operational master data. Airport & Route records IATA/ICAO codes, city, country, timezone, GPS coordinates, elevation, and hub flag. Routes define origin and destination, distance in nautical miles, block time, and service type — domestic, international, regional, or charter — with validation such as origin not equal destination, three-letter IATA, and four-letter ICAO.

Aircraft Type & Fleet manages ICAO type, manufacturer, MTOW, MLW, maximum seats, range, cruise speed, fuel capacity, and crew requirements. Fleet records track tail number, type, operator, owner, and status including active, maintenance, AOG, stored, and retired. Airframe hours and cycles, last flight date, and next A/C-check dates are recorded. The real-time MRO Rollup shows YTD cost, 30-day cost, hours, and work order count per aircraft, while Seat Template defines First, Business, Premium Economy, and Economy rows, seats per row, pitch, width, and total seats.

Flight, schedule, crew, and passenger operations

Flight Master manages flight number, airline IATA, flight type — passenger, cargo, charter, or training — STD/STA, duration, and international flag. Service Category covers ten types: Passenger, Cargo, Ancillary, Charter, MRO, Ground Handling, Fuel, Catering, Training, and Loyalty, with income and expense account mapping per service type.

On the commercial side, Booking & Ticket handles PNR creation with an auto-generated 3–8 character code, route, cabin, and state flow from draft to confirmed, ticketed, checked in, boarded, flown, cancelled, or refunded. E-Ticket records include ticket number, passenger, cabin, fare, SSR, and baggage. The Refund Wizard manages amount, cancellation fee, and refund method, while the Check-in Wizard supports bulk check-in, seat assignment, and passenger lists.

Seat & Loyalty assigns rows and letters, cabin class, exit row, and extra legroom with a unique seat per aircraft and flight. Loyalty points accrue on flown segments, with redemption, adjustment, expiry, and ledger per partner. Miles are calculated as total amount multiplied by tier miles multiplier. Tier upgrade is automatic from cumulative points, with Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers defining minimum points, minimum spend, miles multiplier, and benefits.

Flight Schedule & Leg tracks schedule state from scheduled to boarding, departed, and arrived. Actual flight legs record ATD, ATA, block time, delay, OTP with on-time defined as ≤15 minutes, and load factor. IATA delay codes cover passenger, cargo, aircraft, weather, ATC, operations, and security. Departure triggers Aircraft Sync to auto-update airframe hours and cycles. Crew Assignment assigns Captain, FO, Relief, Cabin Chief, Cabin Crew, and Flight Engineer per leg, tracks report/release time, block time, flight time, and rest time, and automatically validates type rating for the leg aircraft type.

Maintenance, engineering, and compliance

Work Order & Tasks manages work order number, aircraft, program, type, and priority. Programs include A/B/C/D-check, AD, SB, and LLP, with types such as routine, non-routine, AOG, inspection, and modification. Estimates compare estimated hours and cost against actuals. Tasks track task cards, estimated and actual man-hours, mechanic, and state. CRS records Certificate of Release to Service sign-off by and date. Work order states move from Open through In Progress, Pending Parts, Inspection, Released, and Closed.

Components & Spare Parts tracks part number, serial number, aircraft, position, life limit in hours or cycles, remaining life, and overhaul due. Spare requirements show required, available, and to-order quantities, supplier, and auto-PO creation. Compliance & Tooling records AD/SB references, type, due date, recurring compliance, and state. Tooling includes tool number, type, calibration due, status, and location. Recurring items automatically reset to open after compliance date.

Pre-Flight Check supports preflight, hourly, transit, daily, and weekly checks across nine categories — Exterior, Engine, Cabin, Cockpit, Landing Gear, Fuel, Flight Controls, Safety Equipment, and Cargo. Status reflects Pending, OK, Fail, or N/A, with pass rate computed. Sign-off requires an inspector, date, and time, and blocks release if any item fails. Standard item templates auto-load on check start.

Cargo, ground handling, and load control

AWB manages air waybills with a three-digit prefix and eight-digit serial, shipper, consignee, agent, origin, destination, and leg. Weight records include pieces, gross weight, volume, and chargeable weight calculated as the greater of actual weight or volume multiplied by 167. Special handling codes cover General, Perishable, Valuable, Hazardous, DGR, Human Remains, and Vulnerable. The AWB workflow moves from Draft to Booked, Accepted, Loaded, Arrived, and Delivered.

ULD & Load Sheet tracks ULD types such as AKE, AKH, AKN, AAP, AMA, PLA, P1P, and DQP with maximum weight, tare, maximum volume, fill percentage, and overweight alerts. Load sheets record total passengers, baggage, cargo, and mail weight; ZFW, TOW, and LW; and enforce MTOW compliance. States move from Draft to Finalized and Dispatched. Fuel, Catering & GHA records fuel uplift in liters, density, mass, price, and total cost; catering meal counts, special meals, beverages, and supplier cost; and ground handling as full, self, or partial service with passenger count, baggage count, and cost.

Cross-module automation and intelligence

Xerpium connects aviation operations to Accounting with an Indonesian aviation chart of accounts and PPN 11%, covering revenue from tickets, cargo, MRO, catering, and fuel, with balance sheet, P&L, and trial balance. Inventory manages MRO spare parts, fuel stock, catering stock, lot/expiry tracking, and component life limits. Sales converts bookings to sale orders automatically, while Purchase handles purchase orders for fuel, catering, GHA, and MRO spare parts with auto-PO from spare requirements. HR links crew data from employee records with qualification, medical, and passport expiry.

The Dashboard provides 22 KPI widgets covering revenue and AR, flight ops OTP, booking, MRO, and cargo metrics. Eight AI tools — lookup_flight, pax_itinerary, awb_track, wo_status, otp_digest, crew_roster_summary, uld_availability, and parts_lookup — let teams query live operational data in natural language. The Portal supports passenger booking, ticket, loyalty, cargo AWB tracking, and self-service.

Business benefits

  • Single source of truth for fleet, crew, maintenance, cargo, and finance.
  • Lower compliance risk with automatic expiry alerts and qualification checks.
  • Better OTP visibility through flight leg tracking and IATA delay codes.
  • Real-time MRO cost control with work order, component, and airframe rollup data.
  • Automated booking-to-sale-order flow with aviation-specific accounting and PPN 11%.
  • Safer load planning through chargeable weight, ULD fill, and MTOW compliance.
  • Faster operational decisions through dashboards and natural-language AI queries.

Example use case

A regional carrier operates a passenger flight with cargo on the same leg. Booking creates the PNR and sale order automatically. The passenger checks in through the Check-in Wizard, receives a seat assignment unique to that aircraft and flight, and loyalty points accrue after the flown segment. Crew assignment validates the captain and first officer type rating for the aircraft before departure. Pre-flight inspection sign-off blocks release if any item fails. After departure, Aircraft Sync updates airframe hours and cycles. Fuel uplift, catering, and ground handling costs are recorded against the flight. In parallel, an AWB moves from Booked to Accepted, Loaded, Arrived, and Delivered, while the load sheet confirms MTOW compliance. The dashboard updates OTP, revenue, and MRO KPIs, and the AI assistant answers a natural-language query such as checking work order status or ULD availability.
