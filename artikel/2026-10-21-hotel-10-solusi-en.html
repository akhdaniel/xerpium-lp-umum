10 Best ERP Solutions for Hotel & Property Operations

October 21, 2026

Running a hotel or multi-property operation involves constant coordination between reservations, front desk, housekeeping, F&B, engineering, and accounting. When each team works from spreadsheets or siloed tools, room statuses become unreliable, folio charges get missed, rate plans are entered manually, and VIP guests receive inconsistent service. An ERP designed for hospitality connects these workflows in one system — from room configuration and multi-channel reservations to billing, payment, and housekeeping scheduling. Below are the ten most essential solutions from the Xerpium hospitality ERP that address the daily operational pain points of hotels and serviced properties.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. Tipe Kamar & Amenity — Centralizes room type definitions, base pricing, and amenity lists so every channel and team works from the same data.
  2. Kamar & Lantai — Tracks each room’s number, floor, type, status, and current folio in real time.
  3. Tamu & VIP — Builds a single guest profile with VIP level, stay history, and emergency contact for personalized service.
  4. Rate Plan & Harga — Manages dynamic pricing by room type, period, and booking channel to prevent rate leakage.
  5. Reservasi Multi-Channel — Orchestrates reservations from walk-ins, OTAs, corporate accounts, and direct channels with clear statuses.
  6. Check-In/Check-Out — Automates folio creation at check-in and room status updates at check-out.
  7. Folio & Billing — Produces complete guest bills with room charges, service lines, taxes, and payment tracking.
  8. Invoice & Payment — Converts folios into accounting invoices and supports discounts per line.
  9. Layanan Tamu (Service) — Captures F&B, laundry, spa, transport, and other guest charges directly into the folio.
  10. Housekeeping Schedule — Plans daily shifts, assigns rooms, and tracks cleaning progress to speed up turnover.

1. Tipe Kamar & Amenity

The Tipe Kamar & Amenity solution gives every room type a consistent definition that drives pricing, reservations, and channel listings. For each type you configure bed type (single, double, twin, king, queen), guest capacity, base price per night, and a rich amenity list such as AC, TV, WiFi, safe, and more. Hotel-level facilities — pool, gym, restaurant, spa — are also recorded, along with HTML descriptions and photos per room type. This removes the spreadsheet version of room details that often causes mismatched expectations, overbooked capacities, or wrong quotes when guests book through different channels.

2. Kamar & Lantai

Kamar & Lantai handles the physical inventory of rooms across floors and buildings. Each room carries a number, type, floor, capacity, and a real-time status: Available, Occupied, Dirty, Maintenance, or Blocked. A direct link to the room’s current folio lets front desk staff jump from room view to active charges. Floor records include building or tower, order, and computed room count. The system also exposes an is_available boolean based on status, so availability checks are always current. This eliminates the classic problem of selling a dirty or maintenance room because no one updated a whiteboard or spreadsheet.

3. Tamu & VIP

Tamu & VIP extends the standard partner profile with hospitality-specific fields: guest code, ID type (KTP, Passport, SIM, Lainnya), VIP level (Regular, Silver, Gold, Platinum), birth date, and emergency contact name and phone. Stay history is computed automatically — number of stays and total spending — so staff can recognize repeat guests without searching through old reservations. Quick actions link directly from the profile to past reservations and folios. This gives a single, reliable view of every guest, helping front desk and management deliver tiered service and capture preference data that would otherwise be scattered across inboxes and paper cards.

4. Rate Plan & Harga

Rate Plan & Harga brings order to pricing across periods and booking channels. Each rate plan ties a name, room type, validity date range, and rate type — Rack Rate, Corporate, OTA, Package, or Promo — to a specific price per night. Dynamic pricing means different rates can apply per period and per channel, including dedicated corporate and OTA rates. This prevents undercutting between direct and third-party channels and ensures the front desk no longer quotes yesterday’s rate or forgets a corporate agreement. Rate changes become structured, auditable records rather than ad-hoc edits in a spreadsheet.

5. Reservasi Multi-Channel

Reservasi Multi-Channel manages every booking from a single workflow: Draft → Confirmed → Checked-In → Checked-Out, with Cancelled and No-Show as terminal states. Sources include walk-in, phone, email, OTA, corporate, and website, and channels cover Direct, Booking.com, Expedia, Traveloka, Tiket.com, and Agoda. A single reservation can hold multiple rooms through reservation lines, with adult/child counts and special requests. Deposits are tracked with payment status. Because the workflow runs on Xerpium’s configurable dynamic stage mixin, hotel operators can adapt stages and stage-level access control, while reservation numbers generate automatically from an internal sequence.

6. Check-In/Check-Out

Check-In/Check-Out automates the two most critical front desk transitions. At check-in, the system creates a folio automatically, posts the room charge, and changes the room status to Occupied. At check-out, it marks the room Dirty and closes the folio for invoicing. The module also supports No-Show marking, a cancel guard that blocks cancellation once a guest has checked in, and a reset action that returns cancelled or no-show reservations back to Draft. Wizard-based flows make these steps fast for high-volume front desk staff, reducing keying errors and ensuring that room status and billing always move together.

7. Folio & Billing

Folio & Billing is the central guest account. Each folio includes subtotal, tax, total, amount paid, and remaining balance. Folio lines cover room, F&B, laundry, service, minibar, transport, and other charge categories, with tax computed per line using account.tax multi. Payment methods support cash, transfer, credit/debit card, OTA or virtual account, and corporate invoice. The checkout flow moves from Open → Checked-Out → Invoiced → Paid, and staff can post additional charges to an active folio at any time. Notes per folio allow internal remarks. This closes the common gap where minibar or laundry charges never make it onto the final bill.

8. Invoice & Payment

Invoice & Payment turns a completed folio into a formal accounting document. The system creates an account.move out_invoice directly from the folio, with invoice lines auto-generated from every folio line. Each line supports a percentage discount, and the subtotal is computed as quantity × price × (1 − discount/100), removing manual arithmetic. Every invoice line links back to its product record, and the system records who posted each charge. The folio retains a direct link to the generated Xerpium invoice, so front office and accounting always work on the same transaction, eliminating reconciliation headaches and disputed charges.

9. Layanan Tamu (Service)

Layanan Tamu (Service) ensures that every ancillary charge is captured. Service types include F&B/room service, laundry, spa and wellness, transport, tour and activity, and others. Each service order holds multiple product lines with quantity, price, and subtotal, and follows a workflow: Draft → Confirmed → Delivered → Posted to Folio, with cancellation supported. When a service is delivered, the system automatically creates the corresponding folio line and links the room and guest from the active folio. Service numbers generate automatically from an internal sequence. This prevents revenue leakage from minibar restocking, spa bookings, or airport transfers that are often logged on paper and forgotten.

10. Housekeeping Schedule

Housekeeping Schedule brings daily cleaning operations under control. Each daily schedule includes a date, shift (Pagi 06–14, Siang 14–22, Malam 22–06), supervisor, and a room list with assigned housekeeper, task type, status, and priority. Task types cover cleaning, turndown, deep cleaning, inspection, and checkout clean. The schedule computes total rooms, completed rooms, and progress percentage in real time, and follows a Draft → In Progress → Done workflow. By giving supervisors and front desk a shared view of housekeeping status, the module reduces guest wait times and prevents the front desk from selling rooms that have not been inspected.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Start with the operational bottleneck that costs the most — usually room status accuracy and folio completeness. If housekeeping turnover is the pain, prioritize a solution where room status updates automatically after each cleaning task and inspection. Next, evaluate whether the rate plan engine can handle your channel mix, including corporate and OTA rates with date ranges; a rigid price list will force manual workarounds. Third, check that folio and invoice workflows are connected to accounting, not just a front-office list, so payments and partials can be tracked without export. Finally, confirm that guest profiles and VIP levels are available to the front desk during check-in, because personalization depends on having the data at the moment of service, not buried in a CRM.

Conclusion

Hotels and serviced properties run on hundreds of small handoffs — from reservation to room assignment, folio to invoice, housekeeping to front desk. The ten solutions above cover that chain end to end: room definitions, inventory, pricing, reservations, guest check-in/out, billing, invoicing, service capture, and housekeeping. Choosing an ERP that treats these as one integrated environment, rather than ten separate modules with manual bridges, is what prevents revenue leakage and guest complaints.
