# Gold Mining & Refinery Operations with Xerpium ERP: End-to-End Control from Mine Block to Bullion Sale

Gold mining and refining is a data-intensive business. Exploration blocks evolve into production pits, ore moves into stockpiles, assay results determine grade, refinery jobs convert ore into bullion, and every gram must be traceable to a purity certificate, sale order, and accounting entry. When these processes run on spreadsheets or disconnected systems, mining operators lose visibility into recovery rates, inventory value, compliance, and margins.

Xerpium ERP provides a dedicated gold mining and refinery suite that unifies site management, block planning, production orders, equipment tracking, assay quality, refinery output, bullion inventory, pricing, sales, HSE, and financial accounting. The system also includes an AI assistant with industry-specific tools for gold prices, production summaries, refinery output, bullion inventory, pending assays, top buyers, and sales quotations.

Common Challenges in Gold Mining and Refinery Management

  • Permit and compliance tracking is fragmented, making it difficult to manage IUP, IUPK, and IPR permits with clear expiry and status workflows.
  • Grade and reserve estimates across blocks are not connected to actual production and stockpile movements.
  • Ore stockpiles and bullion inventory are difficult to reconcile, especially when grades are weighted averages that change after every addition.
  • Assay and purity certificate processes are manual, creating quality gaps and weak traceability from sample to bullion bar.
  • Equipment, shifts, and cost centers are recorded separately from production and accounting, obscuring true operating costs.
  • Refinery losses and recovery rates are not systematically monitored, leading to unexplained material loss and inaccurate profitability.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Gold Mining and Refinery

Mine Site, Block, and Ore Type Management

Xerpium starts with gold.mine.site, which stores site code, GPS coordinates, total area, province, district, and address. Each site tracks permit details with permit number, type, issue date, expiry date, and computed days remaining. The site state can be Active, Suspended, or Closed with workflow actions. Site statistics include block count and total reserve tonnage computed from linked blocks.

Within each site, gold.mine.block records the block or pit. Each block has a unique block code, sequence, area, depth, mining type, geological zone, ore type, estimated reserve in tons, grade in grams per ton, and estimated gold in kilograms. Block status follows a clear progression: Exploration, Development, In Production, and Depleted. gold.ore.type classifies ore by mineralogy and processing method, such as smelting, CIL, CIP, or heap leach, and is referenced in blocks and production orders.

Production Orders, Lines, and Stockpile Handling

gold.production.order manages mining production orders with an auto-sequence number such as PO/2026/00001, unique per company. Each production order links to a mine site and an in-production block, assigns a shift and production date, and records a responsible employee. It tracks planned tonnage versus actual tonnage with a computed achievement percentage, average grade, estimated gold content, and total fuel consumed. The order can link to a target stockpile and automatically add tonnage when the order is done.

Production detail is captured in gold.production.line, where equipment is linked from the active equipment registry for the site, operators are assigned from HR, and start/end times calculate duration. Each line records output tons and fuel used, with notes for operational issues.

gold.ore.stockpile tracks stockpile capacity, current tonnage, and weighted average grade per site. The stockpile is updated automatically when production orders are completed, and movements are tracked for reconciliation. Grade is recalculated after each addition to maintain an accurate weighted average.

Equipment, Cost Centers, and Shift Management

gold.equipment registers heavy equipment types including excavator, dump truck, crusher, mill, pump, loader, drill rig, bulldozer, and conveyor. Each asset includes asset code, brand, model, year, plate number, serial/engine number, current status, operating hours, purchase value, residual value, insurance expiry, and maintenance history. Computed performance covers total maintenance cost, total fuel, and total operating hours.

Financial tracking is supported by gold.cost.center, which links each site or block to an analytic account for cost tracking. gold.shift configures day, night, and evening shifts and links them to production orders.

Assay and Quality Control

gold.assay.sample manages sample assays with auto-sequence numbering, site and block links, sample types such as chip, core, grab, channel, and composite, and source types such as ore/rock, bullion, process stream, tailings, and soil. Collection details include date, collector, location, depth range, and sample weight. Results are stored as one2many entries with a computed result count and best grade.

gold.assay.result records test methods including Fire Assay, XRF, Wet Chemistry, AAS, and ICP-OES. Element values cover gold PPM, silver PPM, copper percentage, iron percentage, and sulfur percentage. Result grade in grams per ton is auto-computed from element values. Each result moves through Draft, Done, and Verified states.

Refinery and Bullion Traceability

gold.refinery.job manages refinery jobs with auto-sequence numbers such as REF/2026/00001, unique per company. Process types include Smelting, CIL, CIP, Heap Leaching, Amalgamation, and Electrolysis/Wohlwill. The job tracks input ore tonnage, input grade, theoretical gold input, gross bullion output, output purity, fine gold content, slag, other losses, total loss, recovery rate, and loss rate. Each job links to a purity certificate.

Refinery process types offer different recovery characteristics: smelting above 95 percent, CIL 90–97 percent for oxide ores, CIP 90–95 percent, heap leaching 50–80 percent for low-grade ore, amalgamation 60–90 percent, and electrolysis with 99.99 percent purity.

gold.purity.certificate issues unique certificate numbers with purity percentage, fineness label, weight in grams, troy ounces auto-computed, issue date, validity, issuing lab, and state transitions from Draft to Issued to Revoked.

gold.bullion.stock tracks each bullion bar by bar number, purity, weight, fine gold, troy ounces, linked refinery job, status, location, and linked purity certificate.

Price Board and Gold Sales

gold.price.board maintains prices per date and purity for 999, 995, 960, 916, 750, 585, and 375. It records buy price per gram, sell price per gram, computed spread, spot USD per troy ounce, USD/IDR rate, spot IDR per gram, and source method. A unique constraint applies per date, purity, and company. The API method get_latest_price retrieves the latest price by purity and company.

gold.sale.order records gold sales to customers, supports bullion bar or fine gold products, pricing from the price board based on purity, weight, fine gold, and troy ounce conversion, and integrates with account.move for invoicing.

HSE, Safety, and Permits

gold.hse.incident handles incident reports for personal injury, near miss, property damage, environmental, fire, vehicle, chemical spill, and structural incidents. Each incident includes severity, root cause analysis, corrective action, preventive action, lost time days, first aid, medical treatment, hospitalization, witnesses, and evidence attachments. Incident state moves from Draft to Reported, Investigating, and Closed.

gold.safety.inspection supports daily, weekly, and monthly checklists with score percentages. Permit to Work covers hot work, confined space, working at height, and excavation, with validity periods, area, and responsible person. Environmental monitoring includes waste management and reclamation tracking.

Accounting, AI Assistant, and Dashboards

Xerpium connects gold operations to accounting with cost centers per site, automatic journal entries when ore moves to stockpile, refinery costs capitalized to bullion inventory, revenue recognition and cost matching on bullion sales, royalty calculation as a percentage of revenue, and multi-company support for multi-site operations.

The AI assistant includes tools such as get_gold_price, get_production_summary, get_refinery_output, get_bullion_inventory, get_pending_assays, get_top_gold_buyers, and create_gold_sale_quotation. Dashboards use vit_dashboard_gold_mining for end-to-end KPIs, Dashboard Studio with 19 chart types, and AI-generated dashboards and insights.

Example Use Case: From Development Block to Bullion Sale

A mine site has an exploration block that moves to In Production. A production order is created for the night shift, linking the block, responsible employee, and target stockpile. Production lines record excavator and dump truck output with operator assignments and fuel usage. When the production order is completed, the stockpile automatically updates tonnage and weighted average grade.

A sample is collected from the ore stockpile and sent for assay. Results are verified using Fire Assay or XRF, and the best grade is computed. A refinery job is created with the input ore tonnage and grade, and the output bullion is recorded with purity and fine gold content. A purity certificate is issued, and the bullion stock entry is created with bar number, weight, and certificate link.

The sales team uses the latest price from the price board for the relevant purity and creates a gold sale order. The invoice is generated in account.move, revenue is recognized with cost matching, and royalty is calculated. Throughout the process, the AI assistant can provide production summaries, refinery output, bullion inventory, pending assays, and top buyer information.
