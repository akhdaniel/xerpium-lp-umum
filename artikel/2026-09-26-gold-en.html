# Gold Mining & Refinery ERP: Mine-to-Bullion Control with Xerpium

Managing a gold mining operation involves far more than moving ore. Operators must coordinate permits, geological blocks, production targets, equipment performance, assay data, refinery recovery, bullion inventory, HSE compliance, and government royalties. When these processes live in separate spreadsheets or generic ERP modules, reconciliation becomes slow and traceability suffers. Xerpium provides a purpose-built gold mining and refinery ERP suite that connects site operations, production, laboratory results, refinery jobs, bullion stock, sales, safety, and accounting in one system.

Common Challenges in Gold Mining and Refining

  • Permit complexity across IUP, IUPK, and IPR with manual expiry tracking
  • Grade and reserve data scattered by site and block, making production planning unreliable
  • Production, fuel, and equipment performance not linked to cost centers or shifts
  • Assay results and purity certificates disconnected from refinery output and bullion bars
  • Refinery loss, recovery, HSE incidents, and royalty calculations managed outside financial records

How Xerpium ERP Covers the Full Gold Value Chain

Site, Block, and Geological Data Management

Xerpium’s mine site module centralizes each site with a unique site code, GPS coordinates, total area, province, district, and address. Permit management tracks IUP, IUPK, or IPR with issue date, expiry date, and computed days remaining, while workflow actions support Active, Suspended, or Closed states. The block module records each pit or block with sequence, area, depth, mining type—Open Pit, Underground, or Alluvial—geological zone, ore type, estimated reserve in tons, and grade in g/ton. Estimated gold in kilograms is computed from reserve and grade. Block status moves from Exploration to Development, In Production, and Depleted. Ore type classification references mineralogy and processing method such as smelting, CIL, CIP, or heap leach, and is used in blocks and production orders.

Production Orders, Shifts, Stockpiles, and Equipment

Production orders auto-generate unique sequences such as PO/2026/00001 and link each order to site, block, shift, production date, and responsible employee. Planned ton versus actual ton is tracked with computed achievement percentage, average grade, and estimated gold content. Total fuel is computed from production lines. When production is done, the target stockpile tonnage updates automatically. Production lines record equipment, operator, start and end time, auto-computed duration, output tons, and fuel used. Cost center and shift management link analytic accounts per site or block and day, night, or evening shifts to production orders.

Stockpile management tracks capacity, current tonnage, weighted average grade, and in/out movements. Average grade is recomputed after each addition through the _add_tons method. The equipment registry covers excavators, dump trucks, crushers, mills, pumps, loaders, drill rigs, bulldozers, and conveyors, with asset codes, status, current hours, maintenance history, and computed performance metrics.

Assay, Purity, Refinery, and Bullion Traceability

Assay sample and result modules manage sample collection with types including chip, core, grab, channel, and composite, and source types ranging from ore/rock and bullion to process stream, tailings, and soil. Test methods include Fire Assay, XRF, Wet Chemistry, AAS, and ICP-OES. Elements such as Gold PPM, Silver PPM, Copper %, Iron %, and Sulfur % are recorded, and result grade in g/ton is auto-computed. Samples move through Draft, Done, and Verified states.

Refinery jobs support Smelting, CIL, CIP, Heap Leaching, Amalgamation, and Electrolysis/Wohlwill. Each job tracks input ore ton and grade, gross bullion output, output purity, fine gold, slag, other losses, recovery rate, and loss rate. Purity certificates record purity percent, computed fineness labels such as 999, 995, 960, 916, 750, 585, or 375, weight in grams and troy ounces, issuing lab, and validity. Bullion stock links each bar to a refinery job and certificate, with status Available, Reserved, or Sold and warehouse or vault location.

Pricing, Sales, HSE, and Accounting

The gold price board maintains buy and sell prices per date and purity, computed spread, spot USD per troy ounce, USD/IDR rate, and spot IDR per gram. It includes an API method to retrieve the latest price for a specific purity and company. Gold sale orders use price board data for bullion bars or fine gold, including gold weight, fine gold, troy ounce conversion, and invoice integration through account.move.

For safety, HSE incident reporting covers personal injury, near miss, property damage, environmental, fire, vehicle, chemical spill, and structural incidents. Each incident records severity, root cause analysis, corrective action, preventive action, lost time days, witnesses, and photo evidence. Safety inspections support daily, weekly, and monthly checklists, and Permit to Work covers hot work, confined space, working at height, and excavation with validity dates and responsible persons.

Accounting features include cost center per site, automatic journal entries when ore moves to stockpile, refinery costing capitalized into bullion inventory, revenue recognition when bullion is sold, royalty calculation as a percentage of revenue, and multi-company operations.

Dashboards and Configurable Workflows

A dedicated gold mining dashboard provides end-to-end KPI visibility. Dashboard Studio supports 19 chart types, and AI can auto-generate dashboards and insights. For production and refinery processes, Dynamic Stage offers configurable workflows with Python hooks, server actions, RBAC, and automatic sync between stage and native state.

Example Workflow: From Mine Block to Bullion Sale

A site in production has a block with estimated reserve and grade. A production order is created for a day shift, linking equipment and operator. Production lines record actual tonnage and fuel per equipment. When production is done, ore is added to the target stockpile and the weighted average grade updates automatically. Assay samples are collected and verified, providing grade data. A refinery job processes stockpile ore using CIL and records input ton, gold content, bullion output, purity, fine gold, slag, and recovery. The resulting bullion bar is registered with a purity certificate and marked as Available. The sales team creates a gold sale order using the latest price board rate, generates an invoice, and accounting matches revenue to bullion cost while calculating royalty.
