Complete ERP solution for University Management: End-to-End Academic, Finance, and Operations on Xerpium

October 19, 2026

Managing a university with disconnected systems creates friction between admissions, academic administration, finance, and facilities. Student records, KRS, schedules, grades, and tuition payments often live in separate spreadsheets or legacy applications. The result is duplicate data entry, inconsistent academic statuses, delayed revenue recognition, and limited real-time visibility for faculty and leadership. Xerpium ERP provides an end-to-end University Management vertical that connects academic master data, enrollment, scheduling, assessment, thesis, tuition, accounting, and AI-assisted analytics in one system.

Common Challenges in University Management

  • Academic and financial data are fragmented across departments, making consolidated reporting difficult.
  • Manual KRS and prerequisite validation processes create enrollment errors and increase administrative workload.
  • Tuition invoicing and payment tracking are disconnected from accounting, so overdue SPP and revenue reconciliation become slow.
  • Scheduling of lecturers, rooms, and academic events is prone to conflicts without central conflict detection.
  • Limited real-time visibility into student GPA, study progress, accreditation, and faculty workload weakens decision-making.

Scope of the Xerpium Solution

Academic Foundation and Master Data

Xerpium organizes the academic structure with Faculty records that include faculty name, unique code, sequence, dean assignment from HR employees, and automatic department count. Each Study Program manages degree level, accreditation history, head of department, vision and mission, and links to its faculty. The Academic Year model supports draft-to-active-to-closed workflow, date range validation, and semester integration for odd, even, and short semesters. Semester records define the KRS period, academic calendar events, and an active flag for the running semester.

For people, the Student master stores NIM per company, biodata, parent information, status, cohort, and real-time cumulative GPA and total credits. The Lecturer master stores NIDN/NIP, lecturer type, teaching load, current semester SKS, and academic advisor assignments.

Curriculum, Enrollment, and Scheduling

The Course model defines unique course codes, SKS, lecture/lab/tutorial hours, category, and prerequisite relationships. KRS (Enrollment) allows students to select courses per semester, calculates total SKS automatically, applies prerequisite checks, assigns course sections, and moves through draft, submitted, approved, and rejected statuses.

Schedule Line captures day and time, room assignment, section link, and integrated calendar sync with conflict detection. Academic Calendar records events by semester such as KRS, UTS, UAS, holidays, and graduation, with date validation, color coding, and notifications to students and lecturers.

Assessment, Grading, and Thesis

The grading framework uses a Grade Scale from A to E on a 4.0 scale. Score components are configured as UTS 30%, UAS 40%, assignments 20%, and participation 10%; the system automatically calculates grade letters and grade points. KHS shows semester GPA and cumulative GPA, while Transcript groups study results by semester.

For final projects, Thesis tracks the full lifecycle from proposal to ongoing, submitted, defended, and graduated. It supports two advisors, progress percentage with meeting notes, defense panels, room assignment, and configurable dynamic stages via the dynamic stage feature.

Tuition and Education Accounting

Tuition Plan supports payment per SKS or flat fee, department-specific plans, multi-currency, and flexible amount configuration. Tuition Invoice automatically generates SPP invoices from the tuition plan, tracks paid amounts, residual amounts, and overdue detection, and accepts bank transfer, cash, virtual account, and e-wallet payments. Invoices move from draft to posted, paid, partial, or overdue, and integrate with accounting through automatic account.move posting.

Education accounting in Xerpium includes mass SPP invoice generation, multi-payment methods, overdue tracking, reporting by academic year or semester, analytic distribution by faculty or study program, and multi-company consolidation for multi-campus foundations.

Facilities and Resource Management

Room Management defines building hierarchy from building to floor to room, room types, capacity, equipment, and booking types for lectures, events, exams, and meetings. Room Booking schedules start and end datetime, links to course sections, and enforces workflow from draft to confirmed or cancelled with real-time overlap protection.

Connected Applications, Portals, and AI

Xerpium connects University Management with other business areas. Lecturers are linked to HR employees for payroll, timesheet-based teaching load, and contract management. Tuition invoices post to Accounting automatically, and payments are processed through the payment register with budget tracking per faculty or study program. Inventory supports asset tracking, laboratory equipment management, and library book or stock management. CRM handles PMB from leads, admission stage tracking, and marketing campaigns per study program.

The Dashboard features university-specific KPIs through vit_dashboard_university and vit_dashboard_education. The AI dashboard can auto-generate dashboards from natural language, convert natural language to SQL, provide chart insight for trends and anomalies, and support copilot chat with conditional formatting.

Portals enable students to manage KRS and KHS online, lecturers to input grades, and students or parents to pay SPP through a payment portal. Email and notification tools use mail threads and activities per academic model, automatic email announcements, and WhatsApp integration for SPP reminders. The AI Assistant can find a student by NIM, create KRS for the current semester, show a student's GPA, or find an available room on Monday at 8 AM.
