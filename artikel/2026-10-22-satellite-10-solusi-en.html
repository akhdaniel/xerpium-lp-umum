10 Best ERP Solutions for Satellite City Developer Operations

October 22, 2026

Satellite city and township developers operate across a uniquely fragmented value chain. A single project spans land parcels, clusters, kavling inventory, PPJB agreements, installment schedules, infrastructure construction, unit handover, community facilities, and recurring IPL billing. When these functions live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, the result is data duplication, missed payment terms, inaccurate availability counts, and slow handover cycles. This article outlines ten ERP solutions that address these operational pain points directly, grounded in the XERPIUM Satellite City module set.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. sc.project — a single record of truth for every township and its timeline.
  2. sc.cluster — structured block management with live availability statistics.
  3. sc.kavling — complete unit inventory with pricing and status flow control.
  4. sc.customer.agreement — PPJB lifecycle management from draft to handover.
  5. sc.payment.schedule — automated installment schedules tied to invoices.
  6. sc.infrastructure — budget, progress, and milestone control for project infrastructure.
  7. sc.handover — structured serah terima with condition assessment and checklist.
  8. sc.facility — community facility tracking with maintenance cost visibility.
  9. sc.ipl.billing — bulk generation of recurring environmental maintenance invoices.
  10. KPI Dashboard & AI Dashboard — real-time operational insight from natural language.

The 10 Solutions in Detail

1. sc.project — Project / Township Management

Every satellite city operation needs a reliable master record. sc.project provides exactly that: an auto-sequenced project code in the SC.PRJ/#### format, project type classification (Residential, Commercial, Mixed-Use, Industrial), location data including total area in hectares and master plan documents, and a clear timeline with commencement date, target completion, and a state flow from draft through planned, in_progress, to completed. The developer or owner is linked via a res.partner relationship. This single source of truth eliminates scattered project files and gives leadership a dependable reference for every reporting layer built above it.

2. sc.cluster — Cluster / Block Management

Satellite cities are rarely sold as a single unit; they are organized into clusters or blocks. sc.cluster manages each cluster with a unique code per project, area measurement, and cluster type (Residential, Commercial, Mixed-Use, or Industrial). Clusters link one-to-many to their parent project with cascade delete, making the hierarchy explicit. Most operationally important, the module computes live statistics — kavling_count, available_count, and sold_count — through the @api.depends mechanism. Sales and marketing teams can therefore see real-time availability per cluster without manual tallying, while management views a clean display formatted as [Code] Cluster Name.

3. sc.kavling — Kavling / Unit Inventory

The kavling record sits at the heart of any township ERP. sc.kavling gives each unit a lot number unique within its cluster, displayed as cluster-code-number for instant recognition. It links to a kavling type for standardized dimensions, computes total price automatically from land price plus building price, and tracks a disciplined status flow: Available, Reserved, Sold, Handed Over, and Cancelled. Physical attributes — land area, building area, facing direction, corner lot flag, and facing road — are stored natively. Relations to agreement, partner, handover, and sale order connect the unit to the rest of the operational lifecycle without duplication.

4. sc.customer.agreement — PPJB Management

The PPJB (Perjanjian Pengikatan Jual Beli) is the binding document between developer and buyer, and it demands precision. sc.customer.agreement auto-sequences agreement numbers as SC.AGR/#### and links the buyer, project, kavling, and kavling type in one place. Pricing fields — land price, building price, discount, and auto-computed net price — remove calculation errors. The module supports Cash, Installment (12/24/36/48/60 months), and Custom schemes, with down payment percentage automatically calculating the DP amount from net price. A clear state flow moves agreements from Draft through Confirmed, Approved, Payment In Progress, Completed, to Handed Over, giving the legal and finance teams full auditability.

5. sc.payment.schedule — Payment Scheduling

Manual installment tracking is a frequent source of revenue leakage. sc.payment.schedule eliminates this by auto-generating payment lines the moment an agreement is completed. Each line carries a payment type — Down Payment, Installment, Fine, or Other — with due date, amount, and multi-currency support. Every line links to its generated account.move via the invoice_id field, and the is_paid flag is computed from the invoice payment state. For installment schemes, the sequence logic places the DP at sequence 10 and installments at 11, 12, 13 onward, while fine lines handle late payment penalties under a separate payment type. Finance teams gain a dependable receivables pipeline tied directly to customer invoices.

6. sc.infrastructure — Infrastructure Management

Roads, drainage, water supply, electricity, and telecom are substantial capital expenditures that often outlive the sales cycle. sc.infrastructure tracks each item by type — including Road, Bridge, Water Supply, Electricity, Drainage, Gas Distribution, Telecom, and Green Space/Park — with dimensions, budget amount, and actual cost. Progress is auto-computed from linked milestone percentages via @api.depends, so project managers see current state without manual aggregation. Each item links a contractor from res.partner and follows a Planned, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled state flow. The sc.infra.milestone sub-module adds per-item progress percentages, target versus actual, and milestone-level expense records, enabling precise infrastructure capitalization into assets.

7. sc.handover — Serah Terima Unit

Handover is the moment revenue is recognized and customer satisfaction is tested. sc.handover auto-sequences handover numbers as SC.HDO/#### and links the buyer, agreement, kavling, and project. It schedules a planned date and auto-sets the actual date on completion. The module records a condition assessment — Good, Minor Defect, or Major Defect — alongside a boolean checklist and attachment count for documentation. Critically, the action_complete method automatically updates the kavling status to handed_over and the agreement to handed_over, so downstream billing and reporting reflect reality without double data entry.

8. sc.facility — Community Facility Management

Operational satellite cities must deliver more than sold land; they must deliver livable amenities. sc.facility catalogs community assets by type — Club House, Swimming Pool, Gym, Playground, Mosque/Musholla, Park, Security Post, and Commercial Area — each with capacity, operational status, opening date, and monthly maintenance cost. Facilities link one-to-many to their parent project with cascade delete. This visibility lets operations teams plan staffing, track when each amenity becomes operational, and budget maintenance spend per facility — information that directly informs IPL rate-setting and resident communication.

9. sc.ipl.billing — IPL Billing Management

Recurring environmental maintenance fees (IPL) are the lifeblood of long-term township operations. sc.ipl.billing handles bulk generation: the action_generate_lines method searches all sold or handed-over kavling, skips records already billed, and creates one billing line per kavling per month with the owner pulled through the related partner field. The _create_invoice method then produces out_invoice documents automatically using the IPL product. Every line links to its account.move once posted, and payment status is tracked through invoice payment_state. The result is a repeatable, auditable monthly billing cycle that scales from a single cluster to an entire township.

10. KPI Dashboard & AI Dashboard

Executive oversight requires data that updates without manual report building. The KPI Dashboard consolidates kavling sales, infrastructure progress, IPL billing status, and project timelines in real time. The AI Dashboard extends this with natural language querying powered by DeepSeek: a request like "Tampilkan penjualan kavling bulan ini" is converted to SQL and rendered as a chart. Chart Insight analyzes sales trends and identifies slow-moving clusters, while Copilot Chat offers a streamed conversation with full property dashboard context. Conditional formatting highlights status colors, overdue alerts, and progress bars, turning raw operational data into decisions.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Selection should follow operational reality, not feature count. First, prioritize modules that match your current bottleneck — if handover delays dominate, sc.handover and sc.kavling status automation matter more than AI dashboards. Second, confirm that the agreement and payment modules support your actual payment schemes, including installment lengths and fine handling. Third, evaluate whether project hierarchy (project → cluster → kavling) maps cleanly to your land structure. Fourth, prefer solutions where modules share a common data model, so a kavling sale automatically flows into payment schedules, invoices, and IPL billing without re-entry.

Conclusion

Satellite city development demands an ERP that mirrors the physical and legal structure of the business: projects contain clusters, clusters contain kavling, kavling drive agreements, agreements drive payments, and completed units feed handover and IPL billing. The ten solutions above form a connected operational spine that reduces manual work, improves receivables visibility, and keeps every stakeholder — sales, finance, project management, and residents — looking at the same accurate data.
