From Pipeline to Cash: Building a Connected Sales & CRM System with Xerpium ERP Software

Most sales teams do not lose deals because they lack effort. They lose them because information is scattered. The pipeline lives in a spreadsheet, quotations sit in someone's email folder, order confirmations are typed again into a separate finance application, and nobody can say with confidence how much revenue is actually collectible this quarter.

This fragmentation gets worse as the business grows. Add a second legal entity, a few export customers paying in different currencies, and an auditor asking who approved a discount six months ago — and manual reconciliation becomes a full-time job. A connected Sales & CRM module inside an ERP system addresses this by keeping the entire journey, from first contact to cash received, on one data layer.

Common Challenges in Sales and Customer Management

Data that lives in too many places

When CRM and order processing are separate applications, the same customer record is created more than once. A sales representative updates the contact in the CRM tool, but the invoice still carries an outdated billing address. The correction happens after the customer complains.

Slow and inconsistent quotation cycles

Quotations are often assembled manually from price lists of uncertain vintage. Different representatives quote different terms for the same product, and approvals happen over chat. The result is margin leakage that is only visible long after the order is closed.

No single view of order to cash

Sales knows the order is confirmed. Operations knows it shipped. Finance knows whether it was paid. Without an integrated system, nobody holds the complete picture, and cash forecasting becomes guesswork.

Complexity across entities and currencies

Groups operating several companies, or selling across borders, need consolidated reporting without losing entity-level accuracy. Spreadsheets can approximate this, but they rarely survive an audit review.

How Integrated Sales & CRM Software Solves It

Pipeline management

The pipeline gives sales leaders a structured view of opportunities by stage, owner, and value. Instead of asking for a weekly status update, the manager opens the same record the representative is working in. Forecasting is based on live data, not a snapshot that was accurate on Monday morning.

Quotation handling

Quotations are generated from the same product and pricing data used downstream. When a customer accepts, the quotation converts into a sales order without re-entry, so the commercial terms agreed with the customer are the terms that reach fulfilment and invoicing.

Order to cash

Order to cash connects confirmation, delivery, invoicing, and payment into one continuous process. For example, a confirmed order can trigger the downstream steps handled by other Xerpium modules, with the sales record retaining visibility of what has been delivered and what remains open.

CRM as the customer system of record

The CRM side holds contacts, communication history, and account context. Because it is integrated rather than bolted on, the account manager sees the commercial relationship and the transactional history in one place — useful during renewals, escalations, or credit discussions.

Why a Base Module Matters in an Odoo-Based ERP Suite

Xerpium is built on Odoo, and Sales & CRM is positioned as a base module within the suite. That matters architecturally: a base module is not an add-on that synchronises data overnight. It shares the same database and the same master records as every other module in the system.

The practical effect is that a product, a customer, or a currency is defined once. When finance, operations, and sales all reference the same record, reconciliation effort drops because there is nothing to reconcile.

Multi-company, multi-currency, and audit trail

The module supports multi-company and multi-currency operations, which is relevant for groups with more than one legal entity or export revenue. Audit trail support means changes to records are traceable — useful when internal audit or an external reviewer asks who changed a price, and when.

Real-time dashboards and reporting

Dashboards and reports are generated from live transactional data. A sales director can review open opportunities, confirmed orders, and outstanding receivables without requesting a consolidated file from three departments.

A Short Example Flow

Consider a distributor with two legal entities selling to both domestic and export customers.

An inbound enquiry is logged as an opportunity in the pipeline and assigned an owner. The representative prepares a quotation in the customer's currency, using current product and pricing data. Once accepted, the quotation becomes a sales order under the correct legal entity, and order to cash proceeds through fulfilment and invoicing.

Throughout, the CRM record retains the full conversation history, and every change is captured in the audit trail. The sales director reviews a real-time dashboard covering both entities without consolidating anything manually.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sales & CRM have to be implemented together with other modules?

Sales & CRM is a base module in Xerpium that integrates with the rest of the suite, but organisations commonly begin with the processes that cause the most friction. Starting with pipeline, quotation, and order to cash is a reasonable first scope, with adjacent modules added as needs become clear.

How should we evaluate the cost of an ERP-based CRM?

Look beyond licensing at total cost of ownership: implementation, data migration, user training, and ongoing support. Compare that against the cost of your current situation — duplicate data entry, delayed reporting, and manual reconciliation across systems. The honest comparison is integrated system cost versus the running cost of fragmentation, not versus doing nothing.

Can it handle more than one legal entity?

Yes. The module supports multi-company operations, so entities can be managed within the same system while retaining separate records. Consolidated reporting becomes considerably easier than maintaining separate tools per company.

How does multi-currency work for export sales?

Multi-currency support allows transactions to be recorded in the currency the customer actually uses. This keeps quotations and orders consistent with what the customer agreed to, while reporting remains available at group level.

What does the audit trail actually cover?

Audit trail support means record changes are traceable within the system. For example, if a discount on a quotation is revised, the history of that record remains available for review rather than being overwritten.

What role does the AI Assistant play in daily sales work?

The Xerpium AI Assistant is integrated with the module and works from the same live data. It is intended to make information retrieval faster — surfacing pipeline or order status without manually building a report.
