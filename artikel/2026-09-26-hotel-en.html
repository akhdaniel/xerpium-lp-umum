# Hotel & Property Operations with Xerpium ERP: Unified Front Desk, Housekeeping, and Billing

Hotel and property operators often run separate systems for reservations, folios, housekeeping, maintenance, inventory, and accounting. That fragmentation creates double entry, delayed room status updates, billing errors, and limited visibility into daily performance. Xerpium ERP addresses this by bringing hotel-specific operations, guest management, and back-office accounting into one connected suite.

This article outlines the main operational challenges in hotel and property management and how Xerpium ERP modules for room configuration, reservations, folio billing, guest services, housekeeping, maintenance, and reporting help teams work more efficiently.

Tantangan umum

Many hotels still struggle with:

  • Room availability and status scattered across channels, leading to overbookings and slow front desk response.
  • Manual folio creation and tax calculation that increase the risk of billing mistakes.
  • Delayed housekeeping and maintenance coordination that keeps rooms unavailable longer than necessary.
  • Limited guest profile and stay history, making it difficult to personalize service or recognize VIPs.
  • Disconnected service orders, inventory, and accounting processes that require manual reconciliation.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Hotel & Property

Xerpium ERP includes a range of hotel and property capabilities that cover the full guest journey, from room definition and reservation to check-out, invoicing, and reporting.

Room Configuration, Floors, and Availability

The foundation of hotel operations starts with room types and physical room setup. Xerpium supports room types such as Single, Double, Twin, King, and Queen with bed type, guest capacity, base price per night, and amenity lists including AC, TV, WiFi, Safe, and more. Each room type can also include facility information for hotel amenities like Pool, Gym, Restaurant, and Spa, along with image and HTML description.

At the room level, users manage room number, type, floor, capacity, and status. Available statuses include Available, Occupied, Dirty, Maintenance, and Blocked. Each room can link to its current active folio. Floors can be organized by building or tower and order, with a computed room count per floor. Availability is presented as a computed boolean, so staff can quickly see whether a room is available.

Guest Profiles and VIP Management

Xerpium extends the standard partner profile with hotel-specific fields, including guest code, identification type such as KTP, Passport, SIM, or Other, VIP level such as Regular, Silver, Gold, or Platinum, birth date, and emergency contact name and phone number. Stay history and total spending are computed fields, giving front desk staff a quick view of guest value. Quick actions allow staff to view reservations and folios directly from the guest profile.

Rate Plans and Multi-Channel Reservations

Rate plans can be defined per room type, date range, and price per night. Supported rate types include Rack Rate, Corporate, OTA, Package, and Promo. Dynamic pricing allows different prices for different periods and booking channels, with dedicated corporate rates, OTA rates, and promo rates for specific periods.

Reservations follow a configurable workflow from Draft to Confirmed, Checked-In, and then Checked-Out, Cancelled, or No-Show. Reservation sources include Walk-In, Phone, Email, OTA, Corporate, and Website, while channels include Direct, Booking.com, Expedia, Traveloka, Tiket.com, and Agoda. One reservation can handle multiple rooms through reservation lines. Guest information such as number of adults, children, and special requests is captured, along with deposit amount and deposit payment status. Reservation numbers are generated automatically from the system sequence.

Check-In, Check-Out, and Folio Billing

Check-in is a key automated step: it creates a folio automatically, updates the room status to Occupied, and posts room charges. Check-out updates the room status to Dirty and closes the folio. The system prevents cancellation of reservations that have already checked in, while cancelled or no-show reservations can be reset back to draft. Check-in and check-out wizards simplify the workflow for front desk teams.

The folio contains the complete bill, including subtotal, tax, total, paid amount, and remaining balance. Folio lines can cover room charges, F&B, laundry, service, minibar, transport, and other items. Payment methods include Cash, Transfer, Credit/Debit Card, OTA/VA, and Corporate/Invoice. The checkout flow moves from Open to Checked-Out, Invoiced, and Paid. New charges can be posted to an active folio at any time, with tax computed per line and notes available on each folio.

Invoice and Payment

From a folio, Xerpium creates an outbound customer invoice automatically. Invoice lines are generated from folio lines, and the folio links directly to the related Xerpium invoice. Each line supports discount percentage, with automatic subtotal calculated as quantity × price × (1 - discount/100). Every billing line is connected to a product record, and the system tracks who posted each charge.

Guest Services

Service orders handle F&B and room service, laundry, spa and wellness, transport, tour and activity, and other services. Each service order can include multiple product items with quantity, price, and subtotal. The workflow moves from Draft to Confirmed, Delivered, and then Posted to Folio or Cancelled. When posted, service orders automatically create folio lines. Room and guest are automatically linked from the folio to the service order, and service numbers are generated automatically.

Housekeeping Scheduling and Line Management

Housekeeping schedules are organized by date and shift, with shifts covering Morning, Afternoon, and Night. Each schedule includes a room list with task type, housekeeper, status, and priority. Task types include Cleaning, Turndown, Deep Cleaning, Inspection, and Checkout Clean. Progress is computed based on total rooms and completed rooms, and the schedule workflow moves from Draft to In Progress to Done, with a supervisor assigned per schedule.

At the individual room line level, housekeeping assignments track room, housekeeper, task type, priority, and status flow from Pending to In Progress, Done, and Inspected. Start and finish timestamps are recorded per room. Once a room is done or inspected, the room status automatically becomes Available.

Maintenance Request

Maintenance requests capture location, type such as Corrective or Preventive, priority, and damage description. Priority levels include Normal, Priority, and Urgent. Each request records who reported it and who is assigned, along with report date, scheduled work date, and completion date. When a maintenance request is confirmed, the affected room status automatically changes to Maintenance. When maintenance is done, the room automatically returns to Available.

Back-Office Integration, Dashboards, and Reporting

Xerpium connects hotel operations directly to accounting, inventory, and purchasing. Folios generate customer invoices and journal entries automatically, with tax computed per folio line and payment tracked as paid, partial, or unpaid. Minibar items trigger stock deductions, F&B ingredients create stock moves, laundry supplies are consumed from stock, and housekeeping supplies are tracked in inventory.

For purchasing, amenity and supply reorders can generate purchase requests, vendor management covers hotel supplies, bulk purchasing handles F&B ingredients, and blanket orders support recurring supplies.

Hotel dashboards provide KPI visibility for occupancy rate, revenue, RevPAR, room status overview, and real-time housekeeping progress. Reporting includes registration card reports per reservation, folio detail reports, reservation summary reports, and payment receipt reports.

Business Benefits

Hotels using Xerpium ERP can expect:

  • Fewer billing errors through automatic folio creation, tax computation, and invoice generation.
  • Faster room turnaround with real-time housekeeping progress and automatic maintenance release.
  • Better rate management with dynamic, corporate, OTA, and promo rate plans.
  • Stronger guest relationships through VIP profiles, stay history, and direct access to reservations and folios.
  • A single source of truth for front desk, housekeeping, maintenance, inventory, accounting, and management reporting.

Example Workflow

A corporate guest books a room through a corporate rate plan. The reservation is confirmed with an auto-generated reservation number and deposit recorded. At check-in, Xerpium automatically creates the folio and updates the room status to Occupied. During the stay, a room service order for F&B is delivered and posted to the folio as a new charge. At check-out, the room status changes to Dirty and the folio is closed. An invoice is generated from the folio, payment is linked, and the housekeeping team receives a checkout clean task. Once housekeeping marks the room as inspected, the room automatically becomes Available for the next guest.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP gives hotel and property teams a unified platform for managing rooms, guests, rates, reservations, folios, services, housekeeping, maintenance, accounting, inventory, and reporting. Instead of patching together multiple systems, operators gain a connected workflow that reduces manual work, improves accuracy, and provides real-time visibility into daily performance.
