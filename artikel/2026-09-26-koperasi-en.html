# Koperasi ERP: How Xerpium Unifies Savings, Loans, and Multi-Business Operations

Koperasi today rarely operate as a single-function entity. A cooperative may run a savings and loan unit, a retail minimarket, a distribution business, agricultural activities, and service contracts at the same time. When those units run on separate spreadsheets or disconnected tools, cooperative managers struggle to consolidate financial reports, calculate interest accurately, distribute SHU fairly, and stay compliant with national cooperative reporting standards.

Xerpium ERP addresses that complexity with a unified B2B platform for cooperative operations. It combines member management, savings and loan processing, self-service access, multi-business operations, accounting, and consolidated reporting in one system built for Indonesian cooperatives.

Common Challenges in Cooperative Management

  • Member data is fragmented: NIK, KTP, address details, membership type, and KYC documents are often stored inconsistently across branches or files.
  • Savings and loan transactions are manual: interest accrual, penalties, grace periods, amortization schedules, and withdrawal approvals become error-prone and slow.
  • Multi-unit businesses lack consolidated reporting: retail, distribution, production, or agriculture units often operate on separate systems, making SHU calculation and compliance difficult.
  • Members have limited visibility: without a portal, members cannot easily view savings balances, loan schedules, or apply for loans online.
  • Reporting and compliance are time-consuming: balance sheets, P&L, NPL ratio, and SHU distribution require manual reconciliation across units.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Koperasi

Foundation: Member, Branch, and Document Management

The cooperative base module manages members as extended partner records with NIK, KTP, address details including RT/RW/kelurahan, member type, and status. It supports multi-branch structures such as a head office and multiple city branches. Required KYC documents—KTP, KK, salary slips, photos, NPWP, SIUP, and certificates—are stored centrally. Member sequences are auto-generated in the format KOP/YYYY/NNNNN. Access is controlled through four RBAC levels: Member, Staff, Manager, and Admin.

Savings and Loan Management

For savings, Xerpium supports five products: Pokok at 0%, Wajib at 4%, Sukarela at 5%, Berjangka 12 bulan at 7.5%, and Hari Raya at 6%. Each savings account uses the format SAV/YYYY/NNNNNN and stores balance and year-to-date accrued interest. Transactions—deposits, withdrawals, interest, fees, transfers, and corrections—use the TRX/YYYY/MM/NNNNN sequence. Withdrawals follow a workflow from draft to submitted, approved, completed, or rejected. Monthly interest posting runs automatically via cron, calculates daily interest, and posts GL entries.

For loans, Xerpium includes four products: Konsumtif at flat 1.5%, Produktif at flat 1.2%, Modal Usaha at reducing 18%, and Darurat at flat 1%. Loan applications move through draft, submitted, reviewed, approved, disbursed, or rejected. Loan records track principal, admin fee, due date, and outstanding balance. Flat loans use equal principal plus constant interest, while reducing loans use the PMT annuity formula. Daily cron jobs mark overdue installments, calculate penalties, and apply grace periods. Guarantor data, collateral descriptions, and document uploads are supported.

Member Self-Service Portal

The portal gives members a dashboard showing total savings, active loans, and the next installment. Members can view savings accounts, transaction details, loan schedules, and payment status. They can also submit loan applications online through responsive, mobile-ready pages with HTML5 validation. Portal routes include /koperasi, /koperasi/savings, /koperasi/loans, and /koperasi/apply.

Retail, Distribution, and Other Business Units

Xerpium also supports the business side of a cooperative. The Retail module includes multi-cashier POS, multi-payment methods—cash, card, QRIS, e-wallet, and potong simpanan—plus product catalog, stock management, membership discounts, loyalty points, promotions, receipts, and e-commerce with WhatsApp ordering. Distribution covers sales orders, route planning, fleet management, warehouse operations, customer portal, invoicing, and delivery analytics. Logistik handles shipments, freight forwarding, warehouse zones, cross-docking, cold chain, claims, and rate cards. Produksi supports BOM, manufacturing orders, work centers, quality control, cost accounting, raw material requests, and finished goods updates. Jasa covers project management, timesheets, service quotations, contracts, and expense tracking. Pertanian supports crop management, livestock, quality grading, cold storage, contract farming, and traceability. Kebersihan & Perawatan handles service scheduling, checklists, staff assignment, quality scores, and material usage.

Integrated Accounting and Consolidated Reporting

Xerpium uses a single chart of accounts for savings and loan activities plus operational business units. Consolidated reporting delivers combined balance sheet and P&L across all business units. Inter-unit transfers allow funds to move from retail to savings and loan. Member cross-sell connects savings and loan members to retail and distribution activities. The unified dashboard shows combined KPIs for savings, loans, sales, and stock. SHU distribution aggregates results from all business units and distributes to members. Financial reports follow national cooperative standards.

The accounting module generates double-entry GL journal entries automatically for every savings and loan transaction. The cooperative COA includes 26 special accounts and 52 account groups covering assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, and expenses. Reports include balance sheet, P&L, trial balance, comparative P&L, interest posting, SHU distribution, multi-currency with auto-synced exchange rates, and bank reconciliation.

KPI Dashboards and Cooperative Reports

Dashboards show total revenue from invoices, total purchases from POs, outstanding AR, top members by revenue, top suppliers by cost, and monthly revenue and purchase trends. Cooperative reports include a balance sheet with 52 account groups, P&L, comparative P&L, SQL views for savings reports, loan portfolio, member analytics, NPL ratio, and SHU distribution per member.

Business Benefits

  • Faster, more accurate savings and loan processing through automated interest posting, amortization, and overdue management
  • Single member, branch, and KYC record with a consistent document trail
  • Member self-service that reduces staff workload and improves member experience
  • Unified financial reporting across savings, loans, retail, distribution, production, agriculture, and service units
  • Accurate SHU distribution and cooperative compliance from one consolidated chart of accounts
  • Real-time visibility into KPIs such as savings balances, loan portfolio, NPL ratio, sales, stock, and outstanding receivables

Example Use Case: Loan Disbursement to Retail Cross-Sell

A member logs into the portal and submits a loan application online. Staff review the request, verify guarantor and collateral documents, and approve it. Upon disbursement, Xerpium automatically posts the GL entry debiting Piutang and crediting Bank. The member’s loan schedule is generated with the appropriate flat or reducing amortization method. Overdue installments are flagged daily by cron, with penalties calculated according to the configured grace period.

Later, the same member shops at the cooperative minimarket. At the POS, the member receives an automatic discount based on membership tier and earns loyalty points. Payment can be made by potong simpanan, which updates both the retail sale and the member’s savings balance. The transaction flows into the cooperative’s consolidated P&L, and the member’s savings, loan, and retail activity are visible in one unified dashboard.

Xerpium ERP provides that foundation—from member onboarding to loan disbursement, retail POS, stock management, consolidated P&L, and SHU distribution.
