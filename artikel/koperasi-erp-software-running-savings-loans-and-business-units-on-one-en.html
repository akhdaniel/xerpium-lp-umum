Koperasi ERP Software: Running Savings, Loans, and Business Units on One Xerpium System

Most cooperatives in Indonesia start simple: a member register, a savings ledger, and a spreadsheet for loan installments. The trouble begins when the cooperative grows — multiple branches, thousands of members, a retail store, maybe a distribution or farming unit. At that point, savings data lives in one file, loan schedules in another, and the store's sales somewhere else entirely. Month-end closing becomes a reconciliation exercise instead of a management review.

Xerpium is an Odoo-based ERP suite with a dedicated cooperative (koperasi) foundation. It connects member data, savings products, loan portfolios, and business units to a single double-entry general ledger — so the balance sheet reflects every unit without manual consolidation.

Key Takeaways

  • Cooperative operations fail at scale when savings, loans, and business units sit in separate systems with no shared ledger.
  • Xerpium provides four cooperative modules — koperasi_base, koperasi_savings, koperasi_loan, and koperasi_portal — covering membership, savings products, loan amortization, and member self-service.
  • Every savings and loan transaction automatically generates a journal entry against a cooperative chart of accounts with 26 dedicated accounts and 52 account groups.
  • Multi-business cooperatives (retail, distribution, agriculture, services) can consolidate into one balance sheet and distribute SHU across all units.

Common Operational Challenges in Cooperatives

Fragmented Member and Branch Data

A member may hold a mandatory savings account, a consumer loan, and a store membership card — tracked in three places. Without a single member record carrying NIK, KTP, address details, member type, and status, cooperatives duplicate data entry and lose the ability to see total exposure per member.

Multi-branch structures compound this. A cooperative operating a head office plus Jakarta Utara, Selatan, and Timur branches needs branch-level reporting without splitting the database.

Manual Interest and Penalty Calculation

Savings interest and loan penalties are time-consuming to compute by hand. Interest rates differ by product, penalties depend on grace periods, and errors erode member trust quickly.

Disconnected Accounting

When loan disbursement is recorded in a spreadsheet and the bank movement in accounting software, the two drift apart. Non-performing loan ratios and SHU calculations then rest on numbers nobody fully trusts.

Xerpium Cooperative Modules Explained

koperasi_base — The Foundation

This module extends the partner record into a cooperative member profile: NIK, KTP, RT/RW and kelurahan address, member type, and status. It handles multi-branch structures, KYC document storage (KTP, KK, payslips, photo, NPWP, SIUP, certificates), and auto-generated member sequences in the format KOP/YYYY/NNNNN.

Configuration is a singleton per company, holding savings rates, loan rates, penalty settings, and grace periods. Access is governed by four RBAC levels: Member, Staff, Manager, and Admin.

koperasi_savings — Savings Management

Five savings products are supported: Simpanan Pokok (0%), Simpanan Wajib (4%), Simpanan Sukarela (5%), Simpanan Berjangka 12 months (7.5%), and Simpanan Hari Raya (6%). Each account carries a stored balance and year-to-date accrued interest.

Transactions cover deposit, withdrawal, interest, fee, transfer, and correction. Withdrawals follow a controlled workflow — draft, submitted, approved, completed, or rejected. A monthly cron calculates daily interest and posts the GL entry automatically. Deposits debit Bank and credit Simpanan; withdrawals reverse it.

koperasi_loan — Lending Operations

Four loan products are available: Konsumtif (flat 1.5%), Produktif (flat 1.2%), Modal Usaha (reducing 18%), and Darurat (flat 1%). Applications move through draft, submitted, reviewed, approved, and disbursed or rejected.

Amortization supports both flat schedules (equal principal plus constant interest) and reducing-balance schedules using the PMT annuity formula. A daily cron marks overdue installments and calculates penalties against the configured grace period. Guarantor data, collateral descriptions, and supporting documents are attached to the loan record.

koperasi_portal — Member Self-Service

Members access a dashboard showing total savings, active loans, and the next installment due. They can review savings accounts and transaction history, check loan schedules and payment status, and submit a loan application online. The portal is responsive and reachable at routes such as /koperasi, /koperasi/savings, /koperasi/loans, and /koperasi/apply.

Software for Koperasi Simpan Pinjam and Serba Usaha

Many cooperatives are not purely savings-and-loan. Xerpium's integration layer addresses that directly.

A single chart of accounts combines simpan pinjam and operational business activity. Consolidated reporting produces a combined balance sheet and P&L across all units. Inter-unit transfers move funds between units — for example, from the retail unit into simpan pinjam. Member cross-sell turns savings members into retail or distribution customers, and SHU from all business units is distributed back to members.

Business unit modules include Retail (POS with multi-payment including savings deduction, stock management, promotions), Distribution (sales orders, route planning, fleet management), Logistik, Produksi, Jasa, Pertanian, and cleaning services.

Reporting and Accounting Integration

Every savings and loan transaction generates a double-entry journal entry. The cooperative COA includes 26 dedicated accounts across 52 account groups. Standard outputs include Balance Sheet, P&L, Trial Balance, and comparative P&L.

SQL views support savings reports, loan portfolio analysis, and member analytics. NPL ratio is calculated automatically, and SHU distribution is computed per member based on savings and loan activity. Bank reconciliation and multi-currency handling are also available.

Example Flow: From Application to Ledger

Consider a member applying for a Modal Usaha loan through the portal. The application is created as APP/YYYY/MM/NNNN and moves to submitted. Staff review the KYC documents and guarantor data already attached to the member record.

After manager approval, the loan is disbursed as LN/YYYY/NNNNN. The system generates a reducing-balance amortization schedule and posts DR Piutang / CR Bank. Each installment payment posts DR Bank / CR Piutang plus interest income. If an installment passes its grace period, the daily cron marks it overdue and applies the penalty — which then flows into the NPL ratio without anyone recalculating it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Xerpium handle a cooperative with multiple branches?

Yes. The koperasi_base module supports a multi-branch structure — for example, a head office with Jakarta Utara, Selatan, and Timur branches — while keeping member records and sequences centralized.

How is savings interest posted to accounting?

A monthly cron job calculates daily interest and automatically posts the GL entry, debiting interest expense and crediting interest payable on savings. No manual journal entry is required.

How should a cooperative evaluate ERP cost and total cost of ownership?

Scope drives cost more than licensing. A cooperative using only savings, loans, and the member portal has a narrower implementation than one adding retail POS, distribution, and manufacturing. Map your required modules first, then factor in data migration from existing spreadsheets, user training across your four RBAC levels, and ongoing support.

Can members apply for loans without visiting a branch?

Yes. The koperasi_portal module includes an online loan application form at /koperasi/apply, along with dashboard views of savings balances, loan schedules, and next installments. The interface is responsive for mobile use.

Does the system support both flat and reducing-balance loans?

Yes. Flat products generate equal principal plus constant interest, while reducing-balance products such as Modal Usaha use the PMT annuity formula for amortization.

Can store purchases be deducted from member savings?

The POS integration supports multiple payment methods including cash, debit and credit cards, QRIS, e-wallets, and deduction from member savings. Membership-tier discounts and loyalty points are applied automatically.
