Complete ERP solution for Animal Feed: An End-to-End Feedmill Platform

October 19, 2026

Animal feed producers operate in a complex environment: commodity prices shift, formulations must match nutritional guarantees, quality control is batch-critical, and distribution spans many channels. Many feedmills still run procurement, formulation, QC, production, sales, and finance as separate tools. That creates mismatched stock data, unclear HPP per ton, slow batch traceability, and a painful month-end close. Xerpium ERP closes these gaps by connecting the whole feedmill into one end-to-end system—from jagung and soybean meal procurement to distributor billing and financial reporting.

Common Challenges in Animal Feed

  • Volatilitas harga komoditas — jagung, soybean meal, premix, dedak, pollard, CPO, molasses, mineral, vitamin, and additive require multi-supplier purchasing and long-term contract tracking.
  • Different formulations across species — broiler, layer, ruminansia, and akuakultur feeds each need precise nutrient composition and multi-level BOM control.
  • Kualitas harus konsisten — moisture, protein, fat, fiber, and ash checks at receiving; particle size, mixing uniformity, and pellet durability during production.
  • Multi-warehouse complexity — silo jagung, SBM warehouse, cold storage premix, and finished feed warehouse all move at different speeds.
  • Distribution and customer credit — distributor, direct farmers, feed stores, modern trade, and makloon each create different delivery, credit limit, and aging-report needs.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Bahan baku & procurement

Xerpium covers commodity raw materials such as jagung, soybean meal, premix, dedak, pollard, CPO, molasses, mineral, vitamin, and additive. Purchase orders support multi-supplier buying, long-term contracts, and commodity price tracking. Inventory is managed across multiple warehouses: silo jagung, SBM warehouse, cold storage premix, and finished feed warehouse. Receiving includes QC sampling for moisture, protein, fat, and fiber content before material is accepted.

Produksi & formulasi

Formulasi ransum stores the nutrient composition for each feed type—broiler, layer, ruminansia, and akuakultur. The bill of materials is multi-level, covering grinding → mixing → pelleting → cooling → bagging. Manufacturing orders are generated from formulations and routed through work centers such as grinding, pellet, and bagging, with QC checks at each stage. Costing calculates HPP per ton using cost rollup from raw material, labor, and overhead.

Quality control lab

Raw material QC records moisture %, protein %, fat %, fiber %, and ash % during receiving. In-process QC tracks particle size, mixing uniformity, and pellet durability index. Finished product analysis verifies the final nutrient composition guarantee. Every batch can generate a Certificate of Analysis and maintain lot number traceability from receiving to finished feed.

Sales & distribution

Sales covers product categories including broiler starter/grower/finisher, layer, ruminansia, akuakultur, and premix. Distribution channels include distributor, peternak langsung, toko pakan, modern trade, and makloon. The customer module manages distributor receivables, credit limits, due dates, and aging reports. Delivery documents include DO and surat jalan, with fleet tracking to farmers and distributors.

Laporan keuangan & KPI

Financial reports are built for feedmill operations. The balance sheet tracks current assets such as raw material inventory, WIP, and finished feed, plus fixed assets for grinding, pellet, and bagging equipment. The profit and loss shows revenue per feed type, HPP per ton, operational expenses, and gross profit. Trial balance uses six-digit account codes across 32 account groups, with a feedmill chart of accounts of 179 accounts. Tax handling includes PPN 11% and fiscal position exempt for pakan ternak. Gross profit, operating profit, and net profit formulas are generated automatically from the report structure.

The Dashboard Studio includes 14 KPI widgets such as Total Revenue, Total Orders, Raw Material Cost, Distributor Receivables, Stock Value, Pending PO, Monthly Invoices, Revenue Trend, Raw Material Cost Trend, Top Products, and Top Customers. Dashboard Studio supports AI auto-generate and NL→SQL, while the AI Assistant can check stock, price, and order status through natural language.

End-to-End Flow

A distributor orders broiler finisher feed. The sales order checks the distributor credit limit and due date. Procurement creates a PO to a supplier for jagung, soybean meal, and premix based on formulation demand. Receiving runs QC sampling for moisture and protein; accepted material enters the silo or warehouse. Production generates a manufacturing order from the broiler formulation and routes it through grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging work centers. In-process QC checks particle size and pellet durability. Finished feed enters the finished goods warehouse and receives a Certificate of Analysis per batch. Delivery is processed with DO and surat jalan, and fleet tracking updates the distribution team. Finance posts the invoice with PPN 11% or exempt fiscal position. HPP per ton is rolled up from raw material, labor, and overhead, and the dashboard updates revenue, orders, stock value, and distributor receivables.

Business Benefits

  • One source of truth for raw material, WIP, and finished feed across silo, cold storage, and warehouse.
  • Accurate HPP per ton from cost rollup across raw material, labor, and overhead.
  • Clear batch traceability with Certificate of Analysis and lot numbers.
  • Stronger distributor credit control through limits, due dates, and aging reports.
  • Faster financial close with automatic P&L, trial balance, and KPI widgets.
  • Consistent QC visibility from receiving to in-process and finished product.

Implementation Steps

  1. Persiapan data — set up raw material masters, commodity categories, formulations, BOM, work centers, warehouses, customers, and the feedmill chart of accounts.
  2. Parameter & COA — configure PPN 11%, fiscal position exempt for pakan ternak, credit limits, and multi-warehouse mapping for silo, SBM, cold storage premix, and finished feed.
  3. Pilot — run one feed type or one production line end-to-end using real PO, receiving QC, manufacturing order, COA, delivery, and invoice posting.
  4. Rollout — expand to all feed categories, including layer, ruminansia, akuakultur, and premix, across all sales channels and warehouse locations.
  5. Evaluasi — review trial balance, HPP per ton, receivables aging, and the 14 KPI widgets after a defined posting period, then refine work centers and QC checkpoints.
