# Xerpium for SMBs: A Unified ERP Approach for Sales, Inventory, and Accounting

Small and medium-sized businesses often start with a collection of separate applications: one for sales, another for customer records, another for stock, and a spreadsheet or desktop tool for finance. The result is fragmented information, manual data transfers, and limited visibility. Xerpium ERP takes a different approach for SMBs. It brings Sales, CRM, Purchasing, Inventory, and Accounting together in one centralized system, so core business processes no longer have to live in disconnected tools.

Common Operational Challenges for SMBs

Growing SMBs face several recurring issues when core systems are disconnected:

  • Multiple applications create data silos across sales, stock, and finance.
  • Manual reconciliation between systems consumes time and increases error risk.
  • Decision-makers lack a single view of business performance.
  • Traditional ERP implementation can feel too complex or time-consuming.
  • Software costs become difficult to predict when each function requires a separate subscription.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses These Challenges

One Central Platform for Core Business Functions

Xerpium unifies the essential functions that SMBs rely on every day:

  • Sales – manage sales activity in the same system as the rest of the business.
  • CRM – keep customer-related information and interactions connected to operational data.
  • Purchasing – handle procurement without switching to a separate tool.
  • Inventory – maintain stock visibility alongside sales and purchasing.
  • Accounting – close the loop between operations and financial records.

Because these modules sit in one central platform, data flows within the same environment instead of being exported and re-entered across multiple applications. This gives teams a more consistent operational view and reduces the manual work that comes from maintaining separate systems.

Transparent Per-User Pricing

Xerpium uses a per-user pricing model designed for SMB budgeting. Pricing starts at Rp150,000/user/month for XERPIUM Base, which includes 500 AI credits per company per month. A single license includes all modules, and there are no hidden fees. This means SMBs do not have to purchase each function as an add-on or pay extra to unlock core modules.

Instant Onboarding for Different Business Types

Onboarding with Xerpium is structured to reduce setup time. Users select their business type—such as retail, services, or distribution—and Xerpium automatically prepares the chart of accounts (COA), applications, and baseline system. After that, businesses can import their data and begin transacting. Instead of starting from an empty system, SMBs get a configured baseline that fits their operational profile.

Built-in AI Assistant for Daily Operations

Xerpium includes an AI assistant inside the ERP. It helps with:

  • Sales analysis
  • Operational automation
  • Reading and understanding business data

Because these AI capabilities are available directly in the ERP, users do not need to move data into a separate analytics tool to get day-to-day support.

Business Benefits of Xerpium for SMBs

  • A single system connects sales, CRM, purchasing, inventory, and accounting.
  • Onboarding is faster because Xerpium prepares a baseline system for the selected business type.
  • Per-user pricing with all modules included supports simpler budgeting.
  • The built-in AI assistant offers daily help with analysis and operational tasks.
  • Businesses can import existing data and start transacting without lengthy customization projects.
  • The same platform can support different business types, including retail, services, and distribution.

Example Use Case: A Small Distribution Business

Consider an SMB in distribution that currently manages customers in one app, stock in another, and accounting in spreadsheets. With Xerpium, the team selects “distribution” as the business type during onboarding. Xerpium prepares the chart of accounts, applications, and baseline system automatically. The business imports its existing inventory and customer data, then starts transacting.

From there, sales and CRM work in the same system as inventory and purchasing. When the sales team records an order, the information remains in the centralized platform rather than being re-entered in separate tools. The accounting team does not need to key in the same transaction from a separate file. At the end of the week, the owner asks the AI assistant for a sales analysis to understand performance. Because the AI assistant is already inside the ERP, the owner gets support without exporting data to another tool.

This use case shows how Xerpium helps a small business move from disconnected processes to a more unified daily workflow.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP gives SMBs a practical way to consolidate sales, CRM, purchasing, inventory, and accounting in one platform. Its per-user pricing starts at Rp150,000/user/month for XERPIUM Base, with 500 AI credits per company per month and no hidden fees. The onboarding process prepares a configured baseline based on business type, so teams can import data and start transacting sooner. With the AI assistant built into the ERP, daily analysis and operational support are available in the same environment where work happens.

If your SMB is ready to reduce fragmented tools and bring core operations into one system, explore Xerpium ERP as your next step.
