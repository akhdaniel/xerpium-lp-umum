Complete ERP solution for Hospital & Pharmacy: Unified Clinical, Pharmacy and Billing Operations with Xerpium

October 19, 2026

Managing a hospital or pharmacy network with separate tools for patient records, outpatient queues, inpatient wards, medication dispensing, insurance claims and accounting creates daily friction. Medical staff retype data across systems, billing loses track of procedures, pharmacists struggle to verify prescriptions, and BPJS/SatuSehat reporting becomes a manual burden. Xerpium ERP for Hospital & Pharmacy brings these operations into one vertical-native suite — from the first patient registration to the final invoice and regulatory sync.

Common Challenges in Hospital & Pharmacy

  • Fragmented patient data: Medical records, visit history, allergies, and insurance details are scattered across spreadsheets or legacy modules, increasing clinical and administrative risk.
  • Manual queue and bed management: Without a structured queue and ward status system, waiting times rise and bed capacity is not visible in real time.
  • Prescription-to-pharmacy handoff errors: Handwritten or disconnected e-prescriptions lead to wrong dosage, missed allergy checks, and poor stock control.
  • Complex BPJS and SatuSehat compliance: Eligibility checks, referrals, claims, and FHIR resource sync require repeated API calls and manual reconciliation.
  • Disconnected clinical and financial data: Billing lines for consultation, procedures, medicines, lab, radiology, and room charges are often created manually after the service, causing underbilling and delayed revenue recognition.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Xerpium covers the full Hospital & Pharmacy vertical with modules that share a single data backbone based on res.partner for patient profiles and product for medicines and procedures.

Patient & Clinical Foundation

The solution builds on an extended Patient Profile including Medical Record number (auto-generated from sequence), NIK, BPJS number, insurance name and policy number, demographics (age, gender, blood group, religion, nationality), allergies, chronic conditions, emergency contact, and lifetime visit/admission/bill counts. Doctor Master tracks SIP, STR, specialization, department, consultation fee, active status, and visit count. Ward & Kamar Rawat Inap manages room types (VIP, VVIP, Kelas 1-3, ICU, NICU, Isolasi, UGD), capacity, rate per night, floor/building, department link, occupancy count, and status (Available, Occupied, Maintenance). An ICD-10 catalog provides international diagnosis codes used across visits, admissions, and discharge summaries.

Frontline & Outpatient Operations

Queue Management creates automatic daily queue numbers per department with priority levels (Normal, Urgent, Emergency), status flow (Waiting, Called, In Progress, Done/Skipped), call time, arrival time, payment type, and the ability to create a visit directly from the queue.

Outpatient Visit records date, doctor, polyclinic, payment type, vital signs (blood pressure, pulse, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2, weight, height, BMI), anamnesis, multiple ICD-10 diagnoses with a primary flag, SOAP notes, and workflow from Draft to Waiting, In Examination, Done or Cancelled. Referral Management creates referral letters to specialists, hospitals, labs, or radiology using BPJS-compliant formats, capturing diagnosis, treatment given, anamnesis summary, doctor information, and auto sequence numbers.

Pharmacy & Medical Inventory

Master Farmasi & ICD-10 handles medicines and medical devices with generic INN, brand, manufacturer, BPOM registration, dosage form (tablet, capsule, syrup, injection, cream, drops), strength, storage conditions, controlled substance tracking (narkotika/psikotropika), therapeutic class, minimum stock alert, and expiry tracking.

Drug Dispensing links directly to physician prescriptions, tracks batch/lot, expiry, quantity prescribed vs dispensed, pharmacist responsible, unit price from product.lst_price, and automatically creates a stock picking from the pharmacy. Workflow stages include Draft, Dispensed, Partially Returned, Cancelled.

Inpatient & Nursing

Inpatient Admission manages doctor on duty, ward, bed number, payment type, admission and discharge diagnoses with ICD-10, dynamic workflow (Draft, Admitted, In Ward, ICU, Discharged/Cancelled), auto-computed length of stay, discharge condition (Recovered, Improved, Referred, AMA, Died), expected discharge date, and treatment summary. Vital Sign Monitoring records periodic monitoring by nurses: blood pressure, pulse, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2, blood glucose, urine output, pain scale, consciousness level, and timestamps. Medical Procedure includes a catalog with code, category, price, duration, department, and auto-fill on procedure records linked to products for invoicing. Discharge Summary consolidates discharge diagnosis, treatment summary, procedures, medications, follow-up plan, and ward release.

Billing, Integration & Accounting

Billing & Invoice creates patient bills with subtotal, discount, tax, total, paid, due, and line types for consultation, procedure, medicine, lab, radiology, room, and nursing. Payment methods include Cash, Transfer, BPJS, Asuransi, and cards. Insurance coverage amount is computed against the bill. Auto room charge is generated at admission discharge. The system creates account.move out_invoice automatically.

BPJS Kesehatan Integration supports eligibility check via VClaim/PCare API, BPJS referral creation, claim submission for outpatient, inpatient, UGD, procedure, medicine, lab, claim status tracking, SEP number per visit, and configurable VClaim/PCare URLs, credentials, and faskes code.

SatuSehat / FHIR Integration provides FHIR Patient, Encounter, Practitioner, Location, Organization, Condition, Medication, Observation, and additional resources (Composition, Clinical Impression, Service Request, Procedure, Allergy Intolerance, Medication Request, Medication Dispense). OAuth 2.0 token management, sandbox/production environment, retry logic, API logs, sync state (Draft/Synced/Error), and multi-company config are handled in API Management.

Hospital & Accounting analyzes bill invoices, insurance coverage vs amount due, payment tracking, and revenue per department or polyclinic.

End-to-End Flow

A patient arrives at the front desk and receives an automatic Medical Record number and daily queue number for a polyclinic. The nurse calls the queue, creates an Outpatient Visit, records vital signs, and the doctor adds anamnesis, multiple ICD-10 diagnoses, and a SOAP note. The doctor prescribes medication; the system checks allergy notes and generates a Prescription with dosage, route, and instructions. Lab or radiology orders are created from the visit, with sample collection and result entry linked back to the patient record. The pharmacist opens the prescription in Drug Dispensing, sees batch/lot and expiry, dispenses the medicine, and the system creates a stock picking automatically. The outpatient bill is generated with consultation, procedure, medicine, and lab lines; insurance coverage is applied; an invoice is created in accounting. If the patient needs admission, an Inpatient Admission is created with ward and bed, vital signs are monitored periodically, procedures are recorded, and upon discharge the ward is released and the room charge is added to the bill. A Discharge Summary is issued, BPJS claim is submitted, and SatuSehat FHIR resources are synced in the background. Payment tracking updates paid, partial, or unpaid status, and revenue per polyclinic is visible in real time.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth for patient, clinical, pharmacy, and billing data — no double entry between departments.
  • Clinical safety through allergy checks, controlled substance tracking, vital sign history, and ICD-10 diagnosis linkage on prescriptions.
  • Operational efficiency with automatic queue numbers, ward occupancy counts, auto room charges, procedure catalog auto-fill, and prescription-to-dispensing stock integration.
  • Regulatory compliance for BPJS eligibility, referrals, claims, and SatuSehat FHIR resources with retry and logging.
  • Financial visibility via automatic invoicing, insurance coverage computation, payment tracking, and revenue analysis per department.
  • Traceability across every step — from queue call time to discharge summary and medication batch.

Implementation Steps

  1. Persiapan data master: Import patient profiles, doctor masters, ward/room definitions, medicine catalog with BPOM registration and ICD-10 codes, procedure catalog, and Chart of Accounts.
  2. Konfigurasi workflow & parameter: Set up queue priorities, prescription workflow stages, admission dynamic stages, ward statuses, billing line types, and insurance coverage rules.
  3. Integrasi BPJS & SatuSehat: Configure VClaim/PCare URLs, faskes code, credentials, sandbox environment, and OAuth tokens; test eligibility, referral, and claim submission.
  4. Pilot di satu poli/ward: Run a pilot outpatient queue and a small inpatient ward, validate prescription-to-dispensing and billing-to-invoice flows, and adjust workflows based on staff feedback.
  5. Rollout & evaluasi: Extend to all departments, train nurses, pharmacists, billing staff, and accounting; monitor sync errors, claim status, and revenue reports, then optimize room pricing, procedure catalogs, and stock thresholds.
