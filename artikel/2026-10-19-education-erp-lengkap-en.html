Complete ERP solution for Education (Schools & Vocational): A Unified Admissions-to-Graduation System with Xerpium ERP

October 19, 2026

Schools and vocational institutions run on a complex chain of processes: prospective student admissions, placement, curriculum planning, daily attendance, continuous assessment, report cards, tuition billing, and student administration. Too often, these areas live in separate spreadsheets, standalone applications, or manual paper trails. The result is fragmented data, repeated data entry, inconsistent grading rules, delayed payment reconciliation, and limited visibility for parents and teachers.

Xerpium ERP offers an end-to-end education solution that connects these functions in one system. From the first PPDB/PSB wave to graduation transcripts, the system gives school operators, teachers, finance teams, students, and parents a single source of truth.

Common challenges in Education (Schools & Vocational)

  • Admissions data is scattered across forms, files, and message threads, making it difficult to track waves, capacity, documents, and selection results.
  • Curriculum and grading vary by subject, teacher, assessment type, and academic year, requiring flexible weights, KKM thresholds, and configurable grade scales.
  • Attendance, class schedules, and teacher schedules are interdependent, so manual updates create conflicts and inaccurate workload reports.
  • Student transitions such as class promotion, transfer, mutation, and graduation are error-prone when handled outside a controlled workflow.
  • Tuition and recurring payments often lack automatic reconciliation, leaving finance teams to match invoices, payments, and bank records manually.
  • Students and parents have limited real-time access to schedules, grades, attendance, assignments, and invoices.

Xerpium solution scope

Admissions, selection, and placement

Xerpium manages the full PSB process per academic year with configurable waves and capacity limits. Candidate records include NISN, NIS, place and date of birth, gender, blood type, religion, and ethnicity. Parent and guardian data covers education, occupation, income, and status. Address data uses a tree selection for province, city, district, subdistrict, and postal code. The system also stores uploaded documents such as KTP, KK, birth certificate, diploma, and photos, plus student hobbies and economic condition.

Selection is structured but adaptable. Each PSB process can define five custom score fields and two custom donation fields. Candidate groups show wave capacity and filled count. The placement workflow moves through Draft → Open → Done, filters students who passed, and places them into destination classes. Passing and non-passing views are generated automatically. Once placement is completed, active student records are created and academic data becomes available.

Curriculum, lesson planning, and AI assistance

Subjects can be defined as mandatory or additional, linked to teachers and report card grades. Teacher-subject assignments are managed per semester with references such as SK and effective date. RPP records hold learning plans with weights per level and semester. KKM can be set per subject, level, and academic year. Assessment types include UTS, UAS, quizzes, tasks, and practical work per subject. Assessment aspects cover affective, cognitive, conceptual understanding, psychomotor, and practice.

For faster lesson preparation, AI RPP generates a draft RPP using a GPT model with deepseek-chat. Questions can also be generated through AI and linked to surveys for online examinations.

Grading, report cards, and assessments

The assessment workflow uses Draft → Open → Done stages. Teachers input scores per student, per RPP, and across multiple test types. Each entry captures weight, date, and notes, and the system calculates average scores automatically. Weight rules determine how quizzes, tasks, practical work, UTS, and UAS contribute to final results. Weight configurations move from draft to verified before use.

Grading is configurable. The default scale is A 90–100, B 80–89, C 70–79, D 60–69, and E 0–59. Report card grades follow a Draft → Open → Done workflow and automatically compute final numeric and letter grades. Teachers can add subject comments and attitude comments for spiritual and social aspects, including predicates and value ranges.

Attendance, academic calendar, and scheduling

Class attendance is recorded per month and per class with statuses for present, sick, permission, absent, and leave. Lesson attendance tracks each session by subject, teacher, period, material, and teacher notes. The detail view automatically computes totals per student for present, sick, permission, absent, and leave. Active school days per month are also computed automatically. Teachers can use a search button to auto-populate all students in a class.

The academic calendar records events such as national holidays, exams, and report card distribution. Study hour definitions create periods from period 1 to N with start and end times. Class schedules link day, period, subject, and teacher. Teacher schedules are generated from the same data, and a teacher schedule recap workflow calculates total teaching hours per teacher.

Student administration and transitions

Class transfers are handled in a Draft → Open → Done workflow, allowing mass student moves from source class to destination class. Grade promotion filters students who advance or remain and then places them automatically. Student mutation covers dismissal, resignation, death, and school transfer. Graduation workflow determines pass or fail status and supports transcript printing. Transcript records show total scores, averages, and semester results. Every process is recorded in a mail thread, creating an audit trail.

Billing, payments, and Xendit integration

Xerpium integrates with Xendit as a payment gateway using public key, secret key, and webhook token. Invoices are created through Xendit API v2 with a redirect URL for payment. Webhook statuses drive automatic actions: PAID marks the invoice done, reconciles it, and sends email; PENDING keeps it pending; EXPIRED or FAILED cancels it. Payment transactions link to account.move records, and the invoice URL is available on the form.

The system supports recurring payments for monthly SPP, education fees, and re-registration. Parents can pay through QRIS, virtual account, or credit card.

Student and parent portals

The student portal provides a dashboard with schedule, latest grades, and announcements. Students can view lesson schedules, attendance, and grades, access assignments from teachers, upload answers, review grade history per subject, see online report cards, and check monthly attendance recaps. They can also communicate with teachers and homeroom teachers.

The parent portal shows the child’s schedule, attendance, and invoices. Parents can view daily lesson schedules, attendance recaps, grades and report cards per semester, billing lists, and online payment status through Xendit. Statistics display charts of grade and attendance development. Parents can also register prospective students for siblings or other children and communicate with teachers and the school.

Teacher data and workload management

Teacher records extend the HR employee master with NIP, NUPTK, NRG, academic titles, call name, gender, place and date of birth, marital status, identity number, religion, and ethnicity. Department or division can reflect roles such as subject teacher, honorary staff, assistant, or administration. Teacher status is configurable. Start date and notes provide additional employment context.

Workload is tracked through teacher-subject assignments per semester, teacher schedules, total teaching hours, lesson attendance records, and RPP documents per teacher and subject. This creates a clear view of teaching assignments, assistance hours, and additional duties for each period.

End-to-end flow

A typical cycle starts with the admissions team defining a PSB process with waves and capacity. Prospective students are registered through the parent portal or by staff, and required documents are uploaded. After scores and selection data are entered, the placement workflow filters passing students and places them into classes. Placement completion creates active student records.

Next, the academic team configures subjects, teacher assignments, RPP, KKM, assessment types, grading scales, and weight rules. Teachers use AI-generated RPP drafts and later input assessment scores. The system calculates final report card grades and comments automatically.

During the term, class attendance, lesson attendance, schedules, and teacher workloads are maintained in one place. Parents and students see schedules, attendance, grades, and assignments through their portals. The finance team creates recurring SPP invoices, and Xendit processes QRIS, virtual account, or card payments. Paid invoices are reconciled automatically.

At year-end, promotion, transfer, mutation, or graduation workflows move students to the next stage. Transcripts and reports are generated, and the entire history remains traceable through mail threads.

Business benefits

  • One connected record from candidate registration to graduation, reducing duplicate data entry and inconsistencies.
  • Configurable admissions, grading, KKM, and weighting rules that match each school or vocational program.
  • Automatic final grade calculation and report card generation with configurable grading scales.
  • Integrated Xendit payments with automatic reconciliation for recurring tuition and education fees.
  • Real-time visibility for students and parents through role-based portals.
  • Clear teacher workload data through schedules, lesson attendance, and teaching-hour recaps.
  • Full audit trail for student transitions, grading, and payments through workflow states and mail threads.
  • AI support for RPP drafting and exam question generation to reduce teacher preparation time.

Implementation steps

  1. Prepare master data – Collect academic years, classes, subjects, teachers, employee data, student and parent records, address data, document types, and chart of accounts or payment items for tuition and fees.
  2. Configure parameters – Set PSB waves, capacity, score and donation fields, curriculum, RPP, KKM, assessment types, grading scale, weight rules, academic calendar, study hours, recurring billing products, and Xendit gateway keys and webhook.
  3. Run a pilot – Choose one level, program, or admission wave. Execute admissions, placement, scheduling, attendance, assessment, report cards, and Xendit payment testing before full rollout.
  4. Roll out to all programs – Expand to all classes and vocational programs. Train administrators, teachers, finance staff, and portal users. Publish schedules, invoices, and access for students and parents.
  5. Evaluate and refine – Review attendance recaps, grading consistency, payment reconciliation, promotion, and graduation results. Use the audit trail and statistics to adjust parameters, templates, and workflows.

Instead of managing disconnected tools, education teams can run standardized workflows with automated calculations, integrated payments, and transparent portals for students and parents.
