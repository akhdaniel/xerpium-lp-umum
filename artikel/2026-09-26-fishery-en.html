# Fishery ERP with Xerpium: Traceability, Grading, and Cold Chain Control for Seafood Operations

Seafood companies operate in one of the most operationally complex segments of the food supply chain. Every catch must be linked to a species, vessel, fishing area, trip, grade, and temperature history. At the same time, margins depend on precise grading, yield control, cold chain integrity, and cost allocation across vessels and trips. Xerpium ERP is an enterprise ERP suite with an AI assistant that brings fishery-specific capabilities into a unified business platform.

This article explains how Xerpium ERP helps fishery businesses manage species and vessel master data, fishing trips, receiving and grading, processing, cold storage, traceability, and accounting.

Common Challenges in Fishery Operations

Fishery and seafood processors typically face several recurring problems:

  • Fragmented traceability: vessel, trip, lot, grade, and temperature data are often stored in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems.
  • Inconsistent grading and pricing: manual grading rules create revenue leakage and buyer disputes.
  • Weak cold chain control: temperature deviations are recorded after the fact, or missed entirely.
  • Poor trip profitability visibility: fuel, ice, logistics, crew wages, and maintenance are not fully reconciled against revenue per trip.
  • Limited yield accountability: processing losses, byproducts, and waste are not tracked at lot level.

Xerpium ERP addresses these issues through a dedicated fishery layer that is integrated with core ERP workflows.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Fishery Operations

Master Data: Species, Vessels, and Fishing Areas

Xerpium ERP starts with clean master data that becomes the foundation for traceability and operational control.

  • Fish Species: each species can carry a scientific name, a unique code for lot numbering and traceability, a default product mapping, and multi-language names.
  • Fish Vessel: vessel records include registration number, vessel type, captain linked to an employee record, nominal carrying capacity in kilograms, and integration with fleet vehicle records.
  • Fish Area: fishing areas track the fishing ground per trip, GPS reference coordinates, area classification based on regulation, and trip history per fishing area.

Fishing Trip Management

Fishing trips follow a controlled lifecycle: Draft → Departed → Returned → Received → Closed.

During the trip, Xerpium ERP tracks:

  • Crew management with roles such as captain, engineer, and crew, including share percent.
  • Catch estimates per species versus actual weight received.
  • Fuel and ice quantities per trip.
  • Cost tracking for fuel, ice, logistics, wages, and maintenance.
  • Automatic profitability calculation: total cost versus revenue, with profit per trip.

This gives operators a complete view of trip performance without manual spreadsheets.

Receiving, Grading, and Auto Pricing

Receiving follows a structured flow: Draft → Weighing → QC → Approved → Posted.

For each receiving line, Xerpium ERP captures gross, tare, and net weight. It also records temperature at receiving and calculates a freshness score. The system can auto-grade fish based on temperature and freshness, with line-level QC status moving from pending to pass, hold, or reject.

The pricing engine calculates price per kilogram based on species, grade, and size. Pricing rules follow priority logic: supplier match > grade match > size match > default. Multi-supplier pricing is supported, and seasonal prices can be defined with start and end dates. The price can be computed directly on the receiving line.

Processing Batch and Yield Management

Once fish is received, Xerpium ERP supports processing batch management with yield control.

  • Tracks input weight, output, and waste with yield percentage.
  • Supports process types including Fillet, Loin, Steak, Block, Whole Frozen, and Fish Meal.
  • Captures multiple output products, byproducts, and waste tracking.
  • Compares expected yield percentage per species against actual yield.
  • Can auto-create manufacturing production orders from a processing batch.
  • Includes a QC stage before a processing batch is completed.

Cold Storage and Cold Chain Integrity

Cold chain control is a major requirement in fishery operations. Xerpium ERP includes:

  • Cold room types: Chilled, Frozen, and Blast Freezer.
  • Minimum and maximum temperature thresholds with auto-alert.
  • Capacity management based on capacity in kilograms versus current weight, with utilization percentage.
  • IoT integration for automatic temperature logging from sensors.
  • Temperature logs with history source tracking: manual, IoT, or import, including status.
  • Cold chain event tracking from receiving → storage → transfer → delivery.

Lot Traceability and Public QR

Traceability is extended directly into stock lots.

  • Extended stock lots contain species, vessel, trip, area, grade, and temperature fields.
  • Parent-child lot relationships support upstream and downstream traceability.
  • QR codes can be generated automatically for public traceability.
  • Full traceability covers vessel → receiving → processing → delivery → customer.
  • Cold chain events are tracked per lot for integrity verification.
  • A public URL endpoint provides traceability information per lot or QR code.

Enterprise, Accounting, and AI Dashboard Capabilities

Beyond fishery-specific operations, Xerpium ERP provides enterprise features for multi-warehouse management, multi-company or branch operations, role-based multi-user access, multi-currency transactions, and consolidation reports such as profit and loss and balance sheet with inter-company elimination.

For fishery accounting, Xerpium ERP supports:

  • Vendor bill auto-creation from receiving lines.
  • Picking auto-posting for stock movement and journal entries when receiving is posted.
  • Crew cost accrual per trip.
  • Multi-currency transactions for export operations.
  • Cost per lot for traceability batches.
  • Analytic distribution for cost center tracking per vessel or trip.

The AI dashboard can generate dashboards from natural language, convert natural language to real-time SQL queries, provide chart insight with trend, peak, and anomaly analysis, and offer a Copilot Chat with streamed responses. Conditional formatting is also supported for dynamic visual thresholds.

Cross-Module Integration

Fishery operations in Xerpium ERP are not isolated. The system connects with:

  • Sales: fish products become sale order line items; traceability QR can be attached to delivery orders.
  • Purchase: fish receiving can come from purchase orders; vendor bills can be auto-created.
  • Inventory: stock lots are extended with fishery fields; serial and lot tracking includes species, grade, and trip.
  • Fleet: vessel management is linked through fleet vehicle records; maintenance scheduling can be managed per vessel.
  • HR: crew assignment is linked to employee records; wage tracking per trip crew is supported.
  • Quality: quality alerts can be triggered from cold room temperature; grading rules act as QC checkpoints.
  • Bea Cukai: supports export documentation for fishery products, HS Code tracking, and INATRADE integration.

  • Stronger lot-level traceability from vessel to customer, including public QR access.
  • More consistent grading and pricing through rule-based automation.
  • Better trip profitability visibility with full cost tracking and crew accrual.
  • Improved cold chain compliance through IoT logging and temperature alerts.
  • Clearer yield accountability by species and process type.
  • Multi-entity and multi-currency support for growing fishery businesses.
  • AI-assisted reporting and dashboard generation for faster decision-making.

Example Use Case: From Vessel to Customer

A fishing company sets up species, grade, and size masters, then registers its vessels and fishing areas. A trip is created and crew members are assigned with their share percent. Fuel, ice, and other costs are recorded as the trip progresses.

When the vessel returns, receiving starts in Draft and moves through weighing, QC, and approval. Gross, tare, and net weights are recorded, temperature is logged, and freshness scoring triggers auto-grading. The pricing engine calculates the price per kilogram based on species, grade, and size.

The catch then moves into a processing batch for filleting, where yield and waste are tracked. Finished products enter cold storage, and IoT sensors continuously log temperature. A QR code is generated for each lot, allowing customers and regulators to view traceability data through a public URL. The delivery order includes the traceability QR, and the vendor bill and stock journal are generated automatically.
