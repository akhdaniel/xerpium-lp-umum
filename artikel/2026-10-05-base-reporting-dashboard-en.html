# Real-Time Reporting & Dashboard with Xerpium ERP: Turning Enterprise Data into Decision-Ready Insight

For many B2B organizations, reporting is still a backward-looking process. Data is gathered from different departments, manually reformatted in spreadsheets, and distributed days or weeks after the actual business activity. By the time leaders review the numbers, the operational reality has already changed.

Xerpium ERP addresses this challenge with a Reporting & Dashboard module that is a base module within the platform and is natively connected to all other Xerpium modules. It combines real-time KPIs, custom reports, cross-module analytics, multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail capabilities, and integration with the Xerpium AI Assistant.

This article explores the common reporting problems in multi-entity operations and how Xerpium ERP helps teams move from fragmented data to decision-ready insight.

Common Reporting Challenges in B2B Operations

Before implementing an ERP reporting solution, many organizations face similar obstacles.

  • Data is scattered across different systems and departments, making consolidation slow and error-prone.
  • Reports are often outdated by the time they are compiled, forcing teams to base decisions on historical snapshots rather than current conditions.
  • KPIs are defined differently across teams, leading to inconsistent metrics and conflicting interpretations.
  • Multi-company and multi-currency reporting adds complexity, especially when each entity uses its own format or exchange-rate approach.
  • A limited audit trail makes it difficult to trace how a report was built or which data source was used.

These issues are not just operational inconveniences. They create delays in decision-making and reduce confidence in the numbers being reported.

Inside Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard

Xerpium ERP positions Reporting & Dashboard as a base module that works with all other modules in the suite. This means reporting is not a separate add-on that requires complex integration; it is part of the core platform from the start. Below are the key capabilities drawn from the official Xerpium module description.

Real-Time KPI Monitoring

Xerpium provides KPI real-time visibility, allowing teams to track key performance indicators as data is updated within the system. Instead of waiting for a periodic report, users can monitor performance signals on an ongoing basis and respond to changes earlier.

Custom Reports

The module supports laporan custom, or custom reports, so teams can build reporting views that match their specific operational and management needs. This flexibility helps align reports with the metrics that matter most to each function without forcing a rigid, one-size-fits-all format.

Cross-Module Analytics

Because the module is integrated with all other Xerpium modules, it enables analitik lintas modul, or cross-module analytics. Users can bring together information from different areas of the platform and analyze it in one reporting context, reducing the need for manual data merging.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard supports multi-company and multi-currency reporting. This is particularly relevant for B2B organizations operating across entities or markets, where consolidated financial and operational views are needed. At the same time, the module supports audit trail, helping teams maintain traceability over data used in reporting and analysis.

Real-Time Dashboard and Report Delivery

The module offers dashboard and laporan real-time, meaning both dashboards and reports are available in real time. This supports faster internal reviews and gives stakeholders a current view of business performance rather than a delayed snapshot.

AI Assistant Integration

Reporting & Dashboard is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This allows reporting and dashboard data to be brought into AI-supported workflows, so users can work with insights in a more conversational and assisted way within the Xerpium environment.

A B2B finance team operates across several companies and transactions in more than one currency. Before Xerpium, the team exported data from different systems and manually aligned exchange rates to prepare a consolidated view. The process took several days and was difficult to audit.

With Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard, the team builds a real-time dashboard that presents KPIs across the entities.
