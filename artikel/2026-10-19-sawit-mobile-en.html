Palm Oil Mobile App for Accurate FFB Harvest Calculation (Perhitungan Panen TBS) with Xerpium Sawit

October 19, 2026

The weighbridge rarely lies, but the paper ticket often does. In oil palm estates, fresh fruit bunch (TBS) harvest calculation starts in the field—before trucks move, before mill reception, before OER and KER are calculated. When mandors and field clerks record bunches on paper at block level, the result is predictable: illegible tickets, uncertain TPH entries, disputed premiums, and difficult reconciliation against the mill weighbridge. Xerpium Sawit Mobile, the Android field application (aplikasi mobile sawit) for Xerpium Sawit ERP, changes that loop. It moves harvest ticket creation, bunch counting, kg estimation, premium calculation, transport documents, and weighbridge reconciliation into one auditable mobile flow that works in remote blocks without signal.

Common challenges in palm harvest calculation

  • Paper harvest tickets are easily damaged, lost, or rewritten before they reach the office.
  • Remote blocks often have no reliable connection, so field data enters the ERP hours or days late.
  • Bunch count and kg estimates are difficult to verify, leading to premium disputes between harvesters and management.
  • Transport documents and weighbridge results are recorded separately, creating reconciliation gaps.
  • Without a clear audit trail, corrections are hard to trace and field numbers are difficult to defend.

How the daily field workflow runs

The daily rhythm is simple and offline-first.

Morning: The field team synchronises master data and the daily work plan. The phone pulls the latest blocks, TPH, workers, vehicles, and planned activities for the day.

During the day: Mandors and assistants work inside the block without signal. They record attendance, assignments, harvest tickets, bunch counts, transport documents, and maintenance locally on the Android device.

Evening: Back at the office Wi-Fi, the app synchronises automatically in the background. Xerpium Sawit ERP receives the transactions, validates them again, posts them, and returns a per-transaction status to the phone.

This means FFB data is captured at the point of work, not reconstructed later from memory or paper notes.

From harvest ticket to weighbridge reconciliation

The mobile app centres on the harvest ticket and the bunch count.

  • Harvest ticket: One-tap creation per block, TPH collection point, or harvester.
  • Bunch count input: Large +/- buttons make janjang counting fast and less error-prone in the field.
  • Kg estimate and premium: The ticket calculates estimated kg and premium directly from the recorded bunches.
  • SPB transport document: The team records the vehicle, driver, destination, and scans the QR ticket before TBS leaves the block.

When the TBS arrives at the mill, the weighbridge number is reconciled against the SPB and the original harvest ticket. In ERP, the harvest ticket feeds production per block and per harvester, premium, and estimated tonnage. SPB and transport records feed TBS transport to the palm oil mill and weighbridge reconciliation.

This same field data then flows into Panen & Produksi TBS modules such as FFB Collection, Weight, Quality Grade, Transport, Production Estimate, and Cost per Kg. Downstream, the mill side sees TBS Reception and Grade Check, CPO Output, Kernel Output, OER, and KER. KPI dashboards, operational dashboards, and financial reports inherit the same field numbers.

Why the numbers are accurate and auditable

Xerpium Sawit Mobile adds a strong integrity layer around every harvest transaction.

  • Trusted time: The app uses server time from the last synchronisation plus the internal phone clock. Changing the phone clock does not affect transactions. If the phone reboots before synchronisation, the transaction is flagged.
  • Location and fake-GPS detection: Every transaction records coordinates and accuracy. The app detects mock location and checks whether the position is inside or outside the afdeling polygon.
  • Hashed camera photos: Field evidence is captured only from the in-app camera—no gallery access. Photos are compressed to a maximum of 300 KB and hashed with SHA-256. The hash, time, and location cannot be changed.
  • Device integrity: Root, emulator, and hooking detection triggers a warning banner and flags transactions for assistant review.
  • Append-only records: Once sent, transactions cannot be edited. A correction is a new transaction that references the original, enforced at database level.
  • Secure access: First login is online, then offline PIN access. The app locks automatically, limits wrong-PIN attempts, and encrypts the local database with SQLCipher.

Together, these controls make the FFB number auditable from harvester to weighbridge.

Offline is not a problem

Connectivity gaps do not stop the flow. Synchronisation is idempotent—resending does not duplicate data. It tolerates disconnects and preserves sequence: attendance first, then harvest, then SPB. When Wi-Fi is available, synchronisation runs automatically in the background.

The app always shows the number of unsent records and per-transaction status: queued, sent, received, rejected, or posted, with a rejection reason. Field teams know exactly what has reached the ERP and what still needs attention.

Business benefits of mobile harvest calculation

  • Faster harvest closing: Field data reaches the ERP the same day, and daily reports are automatically recapped with on-screen signatures.
  • More accurate premiums: Per-harvester bunch counts and kg estimates are tied to the same harvest ticket.
  • Stronger audit trail: Hashed photos, trusted time, location checks, and append-only corrections protect the record.
  • Clearer reconciliation: SPB and weighbridge numbers align against the original harvest ticket.
  • Better assistant decisions: Risky items are flagged and recorded with an approval trail.

Conclusion

Xerpium Sawit Mobile makes palm harvest calculation easier and more accurate by digitising the field where the number is born—not after it has been rewritten on paper. It fixes the harvest ticket, bunch count, premium, SPB, and weighbridge reconciliation into one append-only flow, so the mill, lab, dashboards, and financial reports work from a single auditable FFB record.
