# ERP Reporting & Dashboard: Real-Time KPIs and Cross-Module Analytics with Xerpium

For many B2B organizations, reporting is still a backward-looking exercise. Finance and operations teams export data from multiple systems, consolidate spreadsheets, reconcile currencies, and present numbers that are already outdated by the time they reach decision-makers. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a Reporting & Dashboard module that is built into the core platform and connected to every other module. It delivers real-time KPI visibility, custom reports, cross-module analytics, multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail, and integration with the Xerpium AI Assistant.

Common Reporting Challenges in ERP Environments

Before adopting a unified reporting approach, many companies face recurring obstacles:

  • Fragmented data across modules and entities: Information sits in separate systems or spreadsheets, making consolidated reporting slow and error-prone.
  • Manual multi-company and multi-currency consolidation: Finance teams often rework figures for different legal entities and currencies before producing a single view.
  • Delayed KPI visibility: Static reports only show what happened in the past, reducing the ability to respond to current operational conditions.
  • Custom report bottlenecks: Business users depend on technical teams to build or adjust reports, slowing down analysis.
  • Limited audit traceability: When data moves between spreadsheets and systems, it becomes difficult to verify where a number originated or how it changed over time.

These challenges create friction between day-to-day operations and the insights needed for planning, compliance, and performance management.

How Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard Solves These Challenges

Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard is a base module within Xerpium ERP. Because it is native to the platform, it does not operate as an isolated BI tool. Instead, it is designed to unify data from all other Xerpium modules, giving users a consistent reporting layer across the business.

Real-Time KPIs and Live Dashboards

The module provides real-time KPI tracking and live dashboards. Instead of waiting for end-of-period reports, managers can monitor operational and financial metrics as they change. This supports faster review cycles and more responsive decision-making.

Real-time dashboards also reduce the need for repeated manual data extraction. Teams can rely on a single source of ERP data that reflects current activity across the organization.

Custom Reports and Cross-Module Analytics

Xerpium supports custom reports and cross-module analytics. Users can build reporting views that combine data from different parts of the ERP without exporting into external tools. This makes it easier to analyze relationships between operational, financial, and transactional data.

Because the Reporting & Dashboard module is unified with all other Xerpium modules, the source data remains consistent. A custom report can pull information from different functional areas while preserving the same definitions, currencies, and structures used across the system.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail Support

For organizations operating across multiple legal entities or regions, the module supports multi-company and multi-currency reporting. This allows consolidated views without manual currency conversion or fragmented entity-level spreadsheets.

The module also includes audit trail capabilities. Users can trace reported values back through the underlying ERP data, supporting internal reviews and external compliance needs. Audit trail support is particularly valuable when reporting must be verified or when historical changes need to be understood.

Integration with AI Assistant Xerpium

Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard is integrated with AI Assistant Xerpium. This connection places reporting within the broader AI-assisted ERP experience, allowing users to work with reporting and dashboard data alongside the assistant already available in the Xerpium environment.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard

Organizations using Xerpium for reporting and dashboards can expect several practical benefits:

  • Faster decision-making: Real-time KPIs and live dashboards reduce the gap between operational activity and management insight.
  • Reduced manual effort: Multi-company and multi-currency support reduce spreadsheet-based consolidation work.
  • Consistent cross-module data: Since the module is a base part of Xerpium, reports draw from the same unified data as the rest of the ERP.
  • Audit-ready reporting: Audit trail support provides visibility into data changes and report traceability.
  • Flexible reporting without technical bottlenecks: Custom reports allow business users to build views that match their operational needs.

Example Use Case: Multi-Entity Performance Review

Consider a company that operates several regional entities, each transacting in a different currency. The finance team previously collected monthly exports from separate systems and manually consolidated them into a single performance view.

With Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard, the team builds a custom report that combines cross-module operational and financial data. The dashboard shows real-time KPIs for all entities in a consolidated multi-currency view. Managers can review current performance without waiting for a month-end close. Because audit trail is part of the module, any figure can be traced back to its source for validation.

This reduces the time spent on reporting preparation and gives leadership a more accurate, current picture of the business.

Conclusion

Reporting should not be a separate, disconnected process. Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard brings real-time KPIs, custom reports, cross-module analytics, multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail, and AI Assistant integration directly into the core ERP. The result is a more efficient reporting workflow and a clearer view of business performance.
