# Theme Park & Recreation ERP: Unify Ticketing, F&B, and Maintenance with Xerpium

A theme park or recreation venue does not operate as a single business. It runs a ticketing business, a food and beverage business, a retail business, and a safety-critical asset operation every day. Without an integrated platform, these functions often live in separate systems, creating reconciliation delays, inaccurate stock counts, and gaps in safety documentation.

Xerpium ERP for Theme Park & Recreation brings ticketing, visitor management, F&B, retail, ride maintenance, accounting, HR, and AI-powered reporting into one connected system. The result is a clearer operating picture across every gate, outlet, and attraction.

Common Challenges in Theme Park Operations

Theme park operators typically face several recurring issues:

  • High-volume ticket sales across counters, websites, and group bookings that must be validated in real time.
  • Multi-outlet F&B and retail operations with fast-moving inventory and frequent promotions.
  • Safety-critical ride maintenance that requires daily inspections, preventive schedules, and clear downtime tracking.
  • Complex revenue recognition for annual passes, group deposits, and deferred income.
  • Limited visibility into cost and revenue performance per ride, outlet, or event.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Theme Park & Recreation

Xerpium ERP addresses these challenges through a set of integrated modules designed for the industry.

Ticketing & Visitor Management

Xerpium supports both on-site and online ticket sales. The Point of Sale module handles ticket counter sales with multi-cashier and multi-payment options, including cash, QRIS, card, and e-wallet. Online Ticketing provides a website e-commerce experience for date-specific ticket booking.

At the entrance, Visitor Check-in scans barcode or QR tickets, validates them in real time, and tracks entry counts. The system supports multiple Ticket Types such as adult, child, family pass, annual pass, promo, group, and corporate.

Capacity Control helps operators set daily quotas per ride or zone and monitor real-time occupancy. Queue Management adds virtual queue, fast pass, and estimated wait time capabilities to improve the guest experience.

F&B & Retail Operations

Food and beverage operations run through a multi-outlet POS system covering restaurants, kiosks, food courts, and food trucks. Menu Management tracks recipes, food cost percentages, and pricing per outlet. Kitchen Display routes orders to the kitchen and records preparation status and serve time.

Inventory covers raw materials, beverages, snacks, and merchandise across multiple warehouses. The Souvenir Shop uses retail POS with barcode scanning and real-time stock updates. Promo & Bundle supports combo meals, member discounts, happy hour offers, and seasonal promotions.

Ride Maintenance & Safety

Maintenance is a critical control point for any theme park. Xerpium provides an Asset Registry for rides such as roller coasters, drop towers, carousels, and water rides. Each asset can store specifications and manuals.

Preventive Maintenance schedules daily, weekly, and monthly tasks with checklists and sign-off. Corrective maintenance uses work orders for emergency repairs with priority levels and downtime tracking. Safety Inspection records daily pre-operational inspections and periodic certification. Spare Parts inventory tracks ride-specific components with reorder points, while the Downtime Log captures downtime duration, cause, and repair cost.

Accounting & Chart of Accounts

Xerpium includes a theme park-specific Chart of Accounts. Revenue is classified into categories such as ticket revenue (41xx), F&B revenue (42xx), retail revenue (43xx), parking, and tenant rent. Revenue Recognition handles deferred revenue for annual passes and group deposits.

Cost Center analytics allow operators to track performance per ride, per outlet, and per event. The Balance Sheet includes ride equipment assets, accumulated depreciation, and spare parts. Tax handling supports PPN 11%, final income tax on tenant rent, and exempt fiscal positions.

Built-in Connections Across Theme Park Operations

Xerpium ERP is not a collection of disconnected modules. The system connects theme park operations directly to finance, inventory, POS, HR, dashboards, and AI.

  • Theme Park ↔ Accounting: Ticket, F&B, and retail revenue automatically post to the general ledger. The Chart of Accounts is built for theme parks, including deferred revenue for annual passes.
  • Theme Park ↔ Inventory: F&B stock, merchandise, and ride spare parts are tracked in one place with auto reorder and multi-warehouse support per outlet.
  • Theme Park ↔ POS: Multi-outlet, multi-payment, and barcode scanning work online and offline. Stock updates in real time.
  • Theme Park ↔ HR: Manage ride operators, cashiers, chefs, and cleaning service staff with shift management, attendance tracking, and overtime recording.
  • Theme Park ↔ Dashboard: Monitor visitor count, revenue per outlet, and maintenance status. AI can auto-generate dashboards from operational data.
  • Theme Park ↔ AI: Use natural language queries to ask for visitor and revenue data. AI can also generate reports and invoices.

Business Benefits

With Xerpium ERP, theme park operators can expect:

  • A single source of truth across ticketing, F&B, retail, maintenance, and finance.
  • Better capacity and queue management using real-time occupancy and wait time data.
  • Safer daily operations through preventive maintenance checklists, safety inspections, and downtime logs.
  • More accurate revenue recognition and cost visibility per ride, outlet, or event.
  • Faster decision-making with AI-generated dashboards and natural language reporting.

A Short Example: One Operating Day

On a busy weekend, the park sells date-specific tickets online and processes walk-in sales at multiple counters. At the gate, Visitor Check-in scans each QR code and validates it in real time. Inside the park, F&B outlets take orders through POS, route them to kitchen displays, and automatically update inventory. The souvenir shop processes barcode-based sales while stock levels adjust.

Before opening, the maintenance team completes daily safety inspection checklists for each ride. If a corrective work order is issued, the downtime log records the issue and repair cost. At the end of the day, ticket, F&B, and retail revenue posts to the general ledger automatically. The operations manager asks AI for visitor count and revenue per outlet, then shares the generated dashboard with management.
