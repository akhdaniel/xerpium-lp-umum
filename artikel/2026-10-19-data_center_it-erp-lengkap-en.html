Complete ERP solution for Data Center & IT System: End-to-End Operations for Colocation, Cloud, and Managed Services

October 19, 2026

Operating a data center and IT system business involves far more than keeping servers online. Finance teams need localized revenue recognition, service delivery teams need asset and ticket control, and management needs a real-time view of capacity, SLA compliance, and recurring revenue. When these functions run on separate spreadsheets, ticketing tools, and accounting software, the business loses time reconciling data and risks underbilling usage, missing contract renewals, or failing SLA commitments.

Xerpium ERP addresses this by combining localized Indonesian accounting, data center facility management, IT asset lifecycle, service contracts, ticketing, capacity planning, usage billing, and analytics in one system.

Tantangan umum di Data Center & IT System

  • Facility, rack, power, and cooling data are disconnected from IT asset lifecycle and client contracts.
  • SLA monitoring, incident management, and billing are not linked, making it difficult to prove compliance or invoice correctly.
  • Recurring revenue, one-time project revenue, usage-based charges, prepaid revenue, and IT asset depreciation require different accounting treatments.
  • Capacity planning for power, cooling, rack space, and bandwidth is often reactive rather than forecast-driven.
  • Change management and ticket escalation lack consistent approval workflows and audit trails.
  • Localized financial reporting must follow Indonesian standards, including 6-digit account codes and PPN 11% tax defaults.

Cakupan solusi Xerpium

Localized accounting and financial reporting

Xerpium provides a chart of accounts template designed specifically for data center and IT system businesses through l10n_id_data_center_it. The template uses 6-digit account codes with an account structure aligned to IT services. Key accounts include dc_120100 for accounts receivable, dc_210100 for accounts payable, dc_710200 for currency exchange gains, dc_720200 for currency exchange losses, dc_710300 for cash difference income, and dc_720300 for cash difference expense. Revenue streams cover colocation, cloud hosting, managed services, and license revenue. PPN 11% sale and purchase tax defaults are pre-configured.

The financial report structure from report_id_data_center_it includes balance sheet, profit and loss, and cash flow reports with IT asset groups such as server and network equipment. The report template syncs account groups from the COA template, parses the report structure XML, and runs a post-load hook when the chart template is applied.

Accounting for data center operations covers revenue per service type, COGS for bandwidth, power, cooling, and maintenance, depreciation for servers, network equipment, UPS, and AC, deferred revenue for annual contracts, software license cost and revenue tracking, and journal defaults with refund_sequence=True.

Operational core for data center and IT system

The xerpium_data_center_it core module brings facility management, IT asset lifecycle, service contracts, service tickets, capacity planning, change management, subscriptions, and usage billing into one ERP workflow. Teams can manage data center rooms, racks, power and cooling tracking, and the full lifecycle of servers, network devices, storage, and security equipment.

Service contracts support colocation, managed services, cloud services, support agreements, and SLA tiers. Service tickets handle NOC and SOC incident management with automatic escalation. Capacity planning tracks power, cooling, and space utilization. Change management routes IT change requests through approval workflows. Subscriptions enable recurring billing through xerpium_subscription, while usage billing meters power kWh, bandwidth GB, and storage TB.

The xerpium_dynamic_stage_data_center_it module defines stage-based workflows. The asset lifecycle moves from Draft to Available, Installed, Active, Maintenance, Decommissioned, and Disposed. The ticket pipeline moves from New to Open, In Progress, Pending, Resolved, Closed, and Cancelled. Contract stages include Draft, Active, Expired, and Cancelled, with auto-activation on enter. Change workflow stages are Draft, Submitted, Approved, In Progress, Completed, and Cancelled. On-enter methods execute business logic automatically when entering a stage, and stage configuration supports groups, confirm messages, and back/forward permissions.

AI assistant and analytics

Xerpium includes AI tools through llm_tool_data_center_it for data center and IT system operations. The Ticket Pattern Analyzer analyzes ticket patterns, auto-categorizes issues, and suggests resolutions. Capacity Forecast predicts power, cooling, and space needs from usage trends. Contract Summarizer summarizes contract terms, identifies renewal risks, and highlights SLA obligations. Asset Lookup allows natural language search for IT assets by type, location, or status.

The vit_dashboard_data_center_it dashboard provides KPI tiles for revenue, contracts, capacity, uptime, and SLA compliance. Charts show monthly revenue, cost trends, top clients, and service breakdown. The dashboard pulls data from ORM sources such as sale_order, project.project, and account_move, as well as SQL sources for monthly metrics, top clients, and service revenue analytics. It includes a global date filter with auto-apply and five-minute refresh, a sky blue color theme for IT services, and a 12-column responsive grid layout.

Dashboard analytics also cover MRR from active contracts, SLA compliance by uptime target versus actual per month, capacity metrics for rack utilization, bandwidth usage, and power consumption, top clients by revenue, revenue per service type, incident trend with resolution time, and project status with planned versus actual hours.

Alur end-to-end

A typical colocation plus managed services engagement shows how Xerpium connects the modules. A client signs an annual colocation contract with managed services and SLA tiers. The contract enters the Draft stage in xerpium_data_center_it, then moves to Active through the dynamic stage workflow. A subscription is created for recurring monthly billing, and prepaid revenue is recognized as deferred revenue under the data center accounting rules.

The operations team assigns a full rack, allocates power in kW, and provisions bandwidth and public IP addresses. IT assets move from Available to Installed and Active as servers and network devices are deployed. When the NOC receives an incident, a service ticket is created in the New stage, auto-escalated based on SLA, and moves through In Progress to Resolved. The AI Ticket Pattern Analyzer categorizes the issue and suggests a resolution. Usage billing meters any bandwidth or power overage charges, which are added to the next invoice.

At month-end, the dashboard shows MRR, SLA compliance, rack utilization, and incident trends. Financial reports reflect recurring revenue, usage revenue, COGS for power and bandwidth, depreciation for IT assets, and PPN 11% tax. The report structure syncs account groups and produces balance sheet, P&L, and cash flow statements without manual reclassification.

Langkah implementasi

  1. Persiapan data. Collect customer and vendor records, IT asset inventories, data center rooms and racks, active service contracts, SLA tiers, service catalog items, and tax settings.
  2. COA and parameter setup. Apply the l10n_id_data_center_it chart of accounts template, configure PPN 11% sale and purchase defaults, journals with refund_sequence=True, depreciation schedules, deferred revenue accounts, and dynamic stage configurations.
  3. Pilot operation. Run one service line, such as colocation or managed services, through a complete cycle: contract activation, subscription billing, IT asset installation, ticket resolution, usage billing, and financial reporting.
  4. Rollout. Expand to all service lines, activate the vit_dashboard_data_center_it dashboard and AI tools, assign access groups to NOC, facility, finance, and project teams, and train users on tickets, change management, and billing.
  5. Evaluasi. Review dashboard KPIs, SLA compliance, capacity forecasts, financial reports, and user feedback. Adjust stage rules, SLA thresholds, and accounting parameters before scaling to additional sites or service offerings.
