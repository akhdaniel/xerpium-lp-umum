# Travel, Hajj & Umrah ERP: End-to-End Operational Control with Xerpium

Hajj, Umrah, and outbound travel operators face a uniquely demanding operational environment. They must manage hotel allotments in Makkah and Madinah, airline seat quotas, pilgrim identities, passports and visas with strict expiry rules, package pricing across multiple room types, staged payments, procurement of flights and hotels, and distribution of travel equipment — often across multiple departures at the same time. When these processes run on disconnected spreadsheets or generic systems, errors, duplicate bookings, missed document deadlines, and revenue leakage become everyday risks.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a comprehensive set of travel-specific capabilities built into the enterprise ERP. It connects pilgrim registration, departure management, document verification, payment schedules, purchase orders, inventory, accounting, and reporting in one unified platform — giving Hajj and Umrah operators full control from initial registration to post-return reporting.

Common Challenges in Hajj & Umrah Operations

Operators typically struggle with several recurring problems:

  • Fragmented pilgrim data across identity, passport, visa, mahram, health, diet, and emergency contact records, making it hard to maintain a single source of truth.
  • Manual document verification for multiple document types with tight passport and visa expiry windows, often leading to last-minute rejections or compliance issues.
  • Departure capacity and quota management across package types, room types, and flight seat allocations, increasing the risk of overbooking.
  • Complex payment schedules with down payments, installments, and final settlements, requiring accurate invoicing and overpayment prevention.
  • Duplicate procurement for hotels, flights, and visas, as well as inconsistent equipment distribution across hundreds of pilgrims in a single departure.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses These Challenges

Packages, Hotels, Flights, and Pricing

Xerpium supports complete master data for hotels in Makkah and Madinah, including star rating (2–5), distance to the mosque in meters, facilities, and room capacity. Airline data covers Garuda Indonesia and Saudi Airlines with flight numbers, type (go, return, or both), and routes such as Jakarta or Surabaya to Jeddah or Madinah, including transit and duration. Seat allocation can be managed separately for economy and business class with quota per flight.

Package management includes three package types — Reguler, Plus, and VIP — each with duration, quota, hotels for Makkah and Madinah, number of nights at each hotel, and price per room type: Quad, Triple, Double, and Single. Package facilities include visa, ticket, hotel, muthawwif, transport, catering, insurance, ziarah, and manasik. Equipment templates are also defined per package type.

Pilgrim Master Data and Document Control

Each pilgrim record stores NIK (16 digits), place and date of birth, age, gender, education, occupation, passport number and validity status, visa number with issue and expiry date, mahram relationship for female pilgrims, blood type, rhesus, height, weight, wheelchair needs, dietary restrictions, and emergency contact details.

Xerpium manages nine document types: KTP, passport, 3x4 photo, vaccine certificate, marriage certificate, birth certificate, family card, health certificate, and other documents. Each document supports file upload and expiry date tracking. Verification follows a workflow from Pending to Approved or Rejected, with user and timestamp recorded. If revision is needed, the document can be reset to Pending. A computed document status automatically marks a pilgrim as document-complete when KTP, passport, photo, and vaccine certificate are all approved. The system also enforces uniqueness per document type per pilgrim.

Departure, Registration, and Payment Schedules

Departures use auto-generated codes with the format KEP/YYYY/NNNN. Each departure moves through states including Draft, Open, Full, Departed, Returned, Closed, and Cancelled, with eight dynamic stages — Persiapan, Pendaftaran Dibuka, Penuh, Verifikasi Dokumen, Berangkat, Kembali, Selesai, and Dibatalkan. Quota is taken from the package, tracked against confirmed sales orders, and shown as available and percentage used. Each departure also defines a group leader and muthawwif or guide team.

During registration, a sales order is created by selecting the package, departure, and room type. Pricing is automatically drawn from the selected room type, and quota is validated at confirmation. Confirming a sales order automatically generates three payment schedule terms by default: DP 30%, Angsuran 40%, and Pelunasan 30%. Each term can be invoiced with one click, and the system tracks total invoiced, paid, and remaining amounts while preventing overpayment beyond the package price.

Procurement, Equipment, and Distribution

For each departure, Xerpium supports four purchase order types: Hotel Makkah, Hotel Madinah, Flight, and Visa. A wizard automatically fills the vendor from hotel data and line quantities from the pilgrim count. Duplicate prevention ensures the same PO type cannot be created twice for the same departure.

Equipment distribution is driven by templates per package type. Templates include more than 15 items such as koper, tas jinjing, tas paspor, kain ihram for men, mukena for women, batik uniform, guidebook, sandals, money belt, ID card, spray, and first aid kit. Items are gender-aware — male pilgrims receive ihram and female pilgrims receive mukena automatically based on their gender. Clothing sizes from S to XXXL can be recorded per pilgrim.

Distribution records are auto-generated from confirmed sales orders per departure. The workflow moves from Draft to Confirmed, Packed, Distributed, or Cancelled. Delivery orders and outgoing stock picking automatically reduce inventory. The system also provides product summaries for packing and a per-pilgrim distributed flag for individual tracking, plus a printed distribution report.

Reporting, Reminders, KPIs, and Dashboards

Xerpium includes three main travel reports, all available in Bahasa Indonesia and English: the manifest report, payment report, and equipment report. The manifest report contains the full passenger manifest including flight, hotel, team, room summary, gender summary, and document status. The payment report shows per-pilgrim payment status, totals, percentages, and realization summaries. The equipment report shows needed versus distributed quantities and acceptance status.

Automated reminders run by email: a daily cron checks for passports expiring within six months, and departure reminders are sent at H-30 and H-7. Notifications are also sent when documents are approved or rejected, with rejection notes included.

KPI tiles provide eight instant metrics: total pilgrims, total revenue, active departures, pending documents, passports near expiry, complete documents, departures this month, and rejected documents. Charts include pilgrim distribution per package, document status, departure status, registration trend per month, revenue trend per month, upcoming departures, and passport status. Dashboard Studio offers 15 widgets covering pilgrims, revenue, documents, passports, and departures, with AI auto-generation available.

Tax, Accounting, and Multi-Company Support

Xerpium handles Indonesia-specific travel taxes, including PPN 11%, PPh 23 jasa 2% with NPWP and 4% without NPWP, PPh 26 jasa luar negeri 20% for foreign parties such as Saudi airlines, PPh final Pasal 4(2) sewa 10%, reverse charge PPN JKP luar negeri 12%, and stamp duty Rp10,000. A dedicated fiscal position maps travel, Hajj, and Umrah transactions as PPN-exempt where applicable.

Accounting is tightly connected to the travel process. Invoices are automatically generated from payment schedules for DP, installments, and final settlement. The travel-specific chart of accounts includes 180 accounts, 33 groups, and 9 tax types, supporting balance sheet, profit and loss, and trial balance reporting. Inventory integration manages equipment stock, lot and expiry tracking for medical and consumption items, and automatic picking from equipment distribution. HR integration connects group leaders and muthawwif to employee records, schedules, assignments, payroll, and guide commissions.

For larger operators, multi-company support enables multiple branch offices, consolidated financial reporting, and shared master data for hotels and packages. The AI assistant can create pilgrim registrations using natural language, check document and passport status, and generate manifest reports through chat.

Business Benefits

  • A single source of truth for pilgrim data, documents, packages, departures, and payments.
  • Automatic quota validation prevents overbooking and maintains room and flight availability.
  • Automated payment schedules and one-click invoicing accelerate cash collection and reduce billing errors.
  • Document workflow and automated reminders reduce passport and visa compliance risk.
  • Integrated purchase and inventory controls prevent duplicate POs and streamline equipment distribution.
  • Real-time KPIs, charts, and bilingual reports give management visibility over every active departure.
  • Dynamic stages and role-based access keep each departure organized from preparation to closure.

Example Use Case: One Departure End to End

A travel operator opens departure KEP/2026/0001 for a VIP package from Jakarta to Jeddah. A sales team creates a sales order by selecting the package, departure, and a Double room type. Xerpium automatically sets the price, validates the available quota, and upon confirmation generates three payment schedule terms: DP 30%, Angsuran 40%, and Pelunasan 30%. The first invoice is created with one click and sent to the pilgrim.

The pilgrim uploads KTP, passport, 3x4 photo, and vaccine certificate. The document verification team approves each file. If the passport is expiring within six months, the daily cron already sent a reminder before approval. Once the required documents are approved, the pilgrim is flagged as document-complete. The purchase team then creates four purchase orders for Hotel Makkah, Hotel Madinah, Flight, and Visa; the wizard auto-fills vendors and line quantities, and duplicate prevention stops any repeated PO.

Before departure, equipment distribution is generated automatically from all confirmed sales orders for the departure. Male pilgrims receive ihram, female pilgrims receive mukena, and stock picking reduces inventory. H-30 and H-7 departure reminders are sent automatically. After the group returns, the departure moves to Returned and eventually Closed. Management can then review the manifest, payment realization, and equipment distribution reports in both English and Bahasa Indonesia.
