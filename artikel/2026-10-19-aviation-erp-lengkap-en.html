Complete ERP solution for Aviation: Flight Ops, MRO, Cargo, and Accounting in One Platform

October 19, 2026

Running an aviation operation on disconnected spreadsheets, legacy modules, and manual handoffs creates risk at every step. A dispatcher updates a schedule while maintenance logs remain in another system. Crew qualifications expire without warning. A cargo agent recalculates chargeable weight by hand. Finance closes the month without a clear link between ticket revenue, fuel uplift, ground handling, and MRO cost. Xerpium ERP solves this by bringing airport and route data, fleet records, crew compliance, booking, cargo, maintenance, and accounting into one end-to-end ERP scope built for aviation.

Common Challenges in Aviation

Aviation operators face a distinct set of operational and financial pressures:

  • Fragmented operational data across flight scheduling, crew rostering, maintenance, cargo, and finance creates duplicate entry and version conflicts.
  • Manual compliance tracking for pilot medicals, type ratings, passports, airworthiness directives, and A/C checks leads to missed expiries and regulatory exposure.
  • Weight, balance, and ULD visibility remain decentralized, increasing the risk of load sheet errors and MTOW non-compliance before dispatch.
  • Cargo and passenger workflows move through many states—from draft PNR and AWB to flown and delivered—without a single source of truth.
  • Financial reporting lags behind operations, especially when aviation-specific accounts, Indonesian PPN 11%, and cross-module cost rollups are not connected in real time.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Xerpium covers the complete aviation back office and operational record set, grouped into several practical areas.

Airport, Route, Aircraft Type, and Fleet

The foundation starts with airport and route master data. Each airport stores IATA/ICAO codes, city, country, timezone, GPS coordinates, elevation, and hub flags. Routes define origin and destination, distance in nautical miles, block time, and type such as domestic, international, regional, or charter, with constraints that prevent origin and destination from being the same and enforce IATA three-letter and ICAO four-letter formats.

Aircraft type records capture ICAO type, manufacturer, MTOW, MLW, maximum seats, range, cruise speed, fuel capacity, and crew requirements. Fleet records track tail numbers, operator, owner, and status such as active, maintenance, AOG, stored, or retired. Airframe hours and cycles, last flight date, and upcoming A/C check dates are maintained per aircraft. A real-time MRO rollup computes YTD cost, 30-day cost, hours, and work order count. Seat templates define first, business, premium economy, and economy cabins with rows, seats per row, pitch, width, and total seats.

Crew, Qualification, and Loyalty

Crew members are managed with role, base airport, license type, medical class and expiry, and passport information. Qualifications include type rating, line check, simulator session, emergency, security, and dangerous goods, each with validity tracking. The expiry alert engine automatically scans medical, passport, qualification, and A-check dates and assigns severity levels: info, warning, critical, or expired. Loyalty tiers support Silver, Gold, and Platinum levels with minimum points, minimum spend, miles multiplier, and benefits including lounge access, baggage, boarding, and upgrades.

Flight Master, Schedule, Leg, and Crew Assignment

Flight Master records flight number, airline, passenger/cargo/charter/training type, STD/STA, duration, and international flag. Service categories cover ten types—passenger, cargo, ancillary, charter, MRO, ground handling, fuel, catering, training, and loyalty—each mapped to an income or expense account.

Flight schedules track flight date, origin, destination, and state transitions from scheduled to boarding, departed, and arrived. Flight legs capture actual operations: ATD, ATA, block time, delay, OTP with a 15-minute threshold, and load factor. Delay codes follow IATA categories such as passenger, cargo, aircraft, weather, ATC, operations, and security. Departure automatically updates airframe hours and cycles. Crew assignment per leg supports Captain, First Officer, Relief, Cabin Chief, Cabin Crew, and Flight Engineer roles, with report/release time, block time, flight time, rest time, and automatic type rating validation. Assignment states move from assigned to confirmed, completed, or removed.

Booking, E-Ticket, Seat, and Loyalty Accrual

PNR records are auto-generated with 3–8 characters and track flight, route, cabin, and state from draft to confirmed, ticketed, checked in, boarded, flown, and cancelled or refunded. E-tickets store ticket number, passenger, cabin, fare, state, SSR, and baggage. The refund wizard handles amount, cancellation fee, and refund method. The check-in wizard supports bulk check-in, seat assignment, and passenger lists.

Seat assignment uses row plus letter, cabin class, exit row, extra legroom, and occupied flags with uniqueness per aircraft and flight. Loyalty points accrue on flown records, with redemption, adjustment, and expiry ledgers per partner. Miles are calculated as total amount multiplied by the tier miles multiplier, and tier upgrades are automatic from cumulative points.

MRO, Components, Compliance, and Tooling

MRO programs include A/B/C/D checks, airworthiness directives, service bulletins, life-limited parts, corrosion tasks, and engine borescope. Work orders carry priorities for routine, non-routine, AOG, inspection, and modification types, with estimated versus actual hours and cost. Tasks include task cards, estimated and actual man-hours, mechanic assignment, and states from pending to done or blocked. The Certificate of Release to Service captures sign-off and date. Work order states progress from open to in progress, pending parts, inspection, released, and closed.

Components track part number, serial number, aircraft, position, life limits in hours or cycles, remaining life, and overhaul due dates. Spare part requirements generate quantities required, available, and to order, with supplier and automatic purchase order creation via cron. Purchase states flow from draft to requested, ordered, and received. AD/SB compliance references, types, due dates, recurrence, and states include open, complied, or N/A, with recurring records auto-resetting after compliance. Tooling records track tool number, type, calibration due date, status, and location.

Cargo, ULD, and Load Sheet

Air Waybills use a three-digit prefix plus eight-digit serial and capture shipper, consignee, agent, origin, destination, and leg. Weight fields include pieces, gross weight, volume, and chargeable weight calculated as the greater of actual weight or volume multiplied by 167. Special handling codes cover general, perishable, valuable, hazardous, DGR, human remains, and vulnerable cargo. AWB states move from draft to booked, accepted, loaded, arrived, and delivered.

ULD records define type, maximum weight, tare, maximum volume, fill percentage, and overweight alerts. Load sheets capture total passengers, baggage, cargo, and mail weight plus ZFW, TOW, and LW with MTOW compliance checks. Load sheet states flow from draft to finalized and dispatched.

Fuel, Catering, Ground Handling, and Pre-Flight

Fuel uplift records capture liters, density, mass auto-computation, price per liter, total cost, and supplier. Catering tracks meal count, special meals, beverages, suppliers, and cost. Ground handling records full, self, or partial service plus passenger count, baggage count, and cost.

Pre-flight checks support preflight, hourly, transit, daily, and weekly check types across nine categories: exterior, engine, cabin, cockpit, landing gear, fuel, flight controls, safety equipment, and cargo. Statuses include pending, OK, fail, and N/A with pass rate computation. Sign-off requires an inspector, date and time, and blocks dispatch when a check fails. Standard item templates auto-load when a check starts.

Accounting, Purchasing, HR, AI, Dashboard, and Portal

Aviation accounting uses an Indonesian aviation-specific chart of accounts with PPN 11%. Revenue streams include tickets, cargo, MRO, catering, and fuel, with balance sheet, profit and loss, and trial balance reporting. Inventory handles MRO spare parts, fuel stock, and catering stock with lot and expiry tracking plus component life limits. Sales automatically creates sale orders from bookings and includes PNR, cabin class, and travel date. Purchasing creates purchase orders for fuel, catering, ground handling, and MRO spare parts, including auto-PO from spare part requirements. HR connects crew data from employee records to qualifications, medicals, and passport expiry.

Eight AI tools provide natural language access to flight lookup, passenger itinerary by PNR, AWB tracking, work order status, OTP digest, crew roster summary, ULD availability by airport, and parts lookup by part or serial number. A 22-KPI dashboard covers revenue and accounts receivable, flight operations OTP, legs, delay, fleet size, booking volume, open purchase orders, MRO open work orders, pre-flight status, active AWBs, and spare part metrics. A customer portal enables passenger self-service for booking, ticket, and loyalty, while cargo customers can track AWBs.

End-to-End Flow

One complete passenger and cargo cycle shows how the modules connect. A domestic flight schedule is created on a route with an assigned aircraft and crew. Crew assignment validates the Captain’s type rating and alerts if a medical or passport expiry is near. Passenger bookings create PNRs, then e-tickets, seat assignments, and finally boarded and flown records. The check-in wizard confirms passengers and baggage. For cargo on the same flight, an AWB moves from draft to booked, accepted, and loaded, while a ULD is filled and checked for overweight. The load sheet is finalized with passenger, baggage, cargo, and mail weights, and ZFW, TOW, and LW are validated against MTOW before dispatch.

Before departure, the pre-flight check runs through all nine categories and requires inspector sign-off. The flight departs and the leg records actual times, delay code, OTP, and load factor. Aircraft sync automatically updates airframe hours and cycles. Crew assignment completes and rest time is logged. Fuel uplift, catering, and ground handling costs are captured and push to purchasing and inventory as needed. Loyalty points accrue to each passenger based on fare and tier multiplier. Accounting posts ticket revenue, cargo revenue, and operational costs to the aviation COA with PPN 11%. The dashboard updates OTP, revenue, AR, MRO open work orders, and cargo AWBs in real time.
