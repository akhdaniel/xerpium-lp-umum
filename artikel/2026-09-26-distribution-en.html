# Distribution ERP: How Xerpium Unifies Territory, Field Sales, and Delivery Operations

Distribution businesses operate in a uniquely complex environment. They manage multi-level sales territories, principal supplier schemes, daily delivery routes, field sales visits, on-site collections, and multi-warehouse inventory. When these functions run on separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, delays, misaligned targets, and poor stock planning become the norm.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated distribution suite that connects territory planning, principal discounts, delivery manifests, inventory transfers, seasonal forecasting, field visits, collection payments, and approval workflows in one system. The result is a cleaner operational flow from the first sales call to final payment settlement.

Common Distribution Challenges

Many distribution companies face recurring operational issues:

  • Sales territories and salesman assignments are difficult to manage across large geographic regions.
  • Principal discount schemes are applied manually, causing errors and inconsistent pricing.
  • Delivery routes, manifests, and proof of delivery are still paper-based or siloed.
  • Field visit plans and on-site collections are disconnected from accounting and inventory.
  • Seasonal demand changes make stock planning and multi-warehouse transfers reactive rather than proactive.

Core Xerpium Distribution Modules

Territory Hierarchy and Salesman Assignment

Xerpium organizes distribution coverage through a clear geographic hierarchy:

  • Region (dist.region) groups large areas such as Sumatera, Jawa, Kalimantan, and others. Each region has a unique code and an automatic area count.
  • Area (dist.area) is the intermediate sub-region, linked to a region through a Many2one relationship. It tracks zone count and active status.
  • Zone (dist.zone) is the smallest distribution unit. It links to customers via One2many to res.partner, automatically counts customers, inherits area and region context, and supports notes for special zone conditions.

On top of this hierarchy, Salesman Territory (dist.salesman.territory) assigns a salesman to a specific zone with a start and end date. The unique constraint ensures one salesman per zone, and related area and region fields are inherited from the zone.

Principal and Discount Management

Xerpium’s Principal & Discount (dist.principal) module manages principal or supplier data for distributed products. It supports mandatory discount schemes from principal to customer and integrates those discounts directly into sale orders. Purchase orders can be linked to the principal, and products are associated with the correct principal template.

This automation removes the need for manual discount calculations and ensures that every sale order reflects the agreed principal scheme.

Sales Order and Purchase Order Approval

Approval workflows help distribution companies maintain control without slowing down routine transactions.

  • Sales Order Approval supports auto-approval for orders below a configured limit, credit checks against customer receivable limits, and discount validation for values outside allowed boundaries. Stage configuration is available per company through dynamic_stage_sale.
  • Purchase Order Approval includes budget checks, principal purchase limits, multi-level approval from Supervisor to Manager to Director, and configurable stages via dynamic_stage_purchase.

Delivery Route, Manifest, and Fleet Integration

Delivery Route & Manifest (dist.delivery) handles daily route management and delivery execution. Delivery orders are generated from stock picking with a sequence, and a daily manifest groups delivery orders by route and day. Digital proof of delivery is supported, along with fleet integration for vehicle and driver assignment through the fleet module. A wizard creates delivery records directly from sale orders.

Inventory Transfer and Barcode Handling

The Inventory Transfer (dist.inventory) module supports internal transfers between warehouses with double approval. Barcode scanning for locations and goods is available through the barcodes module, and barcode labels can be printed per location. Workflow stages are configurable through xerpium_dynamic_stage_stock, giving businesses flexibility in how transfers move from request to completion.

Seasonal Forecast

Seasonal Forecast (dist.forecast) helps distribution companies plan stock based on seasonal categories such as Ramadan, Lebaran, rainy season, and back to school. The system predicts stock needs by season, runs forecast computation automatically via cron, and provides a wizard to generate forecasts from historical data. Forecast results integrate with inventory to produce purchase recommendations.

Visit Plan, Visit Log, and Collection Payment

Field operations are fully captured in Xerpium.

Visit Plan (dist.visit.plan) schedules daily salesman visits with types including sales call, canvassing, collection, and survey. It supports route optimization based on zones and tracks status from Planned to Done to Cancelled.

Visit Log (dist.visit.log) records on-site results in real time. Salesmen can add photo evidence of displays and store conditions, capture orders directly from the field, and tag geo-location coordinates for each visit.

Collection Payment (dist.collection.payment) enables on-site invoice collection at the store. It supports cash, transfer, and QRIS payment methods, links payments to account.move receivables, and updates payment status in real time.

Sales Target and Performance Tracking

Sales Target (dist.sales.target) defines monthly targets per salesman, with breakdowns per principal. The system tracks realization versus target as a percentage, integrates with distribution reports through report_id_distribution, and provides a dashboard view of sales performance against targets.

AI Dashboard and Distribution Analytics

Xerpium includes AI-powered dashboard features. Users can auto-generate dashboards from natural language via DeepSeek, convert natural language into SQL queries in real time, and receive chart insights on trends, peaks, and anomalies. The Copilot chat streams responses with dashboard context.

Distribution-specific KPIs are available through vit_dashboard_distribution, covering sales target achievement and delivery performance metrics.

The AI Assistant can also answer operational questions and perform actions, such as:

  • “Target sales bulan ini untuk salesman X”
  • “Buat delivery order dari SO/001”
  • “Tampilkan forecast stok Ramadan”
  • “Realisasi visit vs plan minggu ini”

Distribution Accounting

Xerpium’s distribution accounting capabilities include automatic principal discount posting, multi-warehouse valuation per branch or warehouse, field collection payment recording, consignment tracking, and salesman commission calculation from sales.

How Distribution Connects Across Xerpium

Xerpium’s distribution modules do not work in isolation.

  • Distribution ↔ Sales: sale orders automatically apply principal discounts, sales targets are tracked per salesman, and SO approval workflows maintain control.
  • Distribution ↔ Inventory: stock transfers between warehouses, barcode scanning, and seasonal stock forecasts keep inventory aligned with demand.
  • Distribution ↔ Fleet: delivery routes and manifests connect with vehicle and driver assignment, digital POD, and photo evidence.
  • Distribution ↔ Accounting: automatic journals from sale and purchase orders, field collection payments, and commission calculations flow into accounting.
  • Distribution ↔ Dashboard: distribution KPIs, sales target achievement, and delivery performance are visible in one view.
  • Distribution ↔ Dynamic Stage: configurable SO and PO workflows, auto-approval, credit checks, and stage-based RBAC access standardize operations.
  • Distribution ↔ Field Visit: visit plans, visit logs, on-site order capture, and field collections are integrated end to end.
  • Distribution ↔ AI Assistant: natural language queries and actions reduce time spent searching for operational data.

Example Flow: From Sales Visit to Settlement

A distribution company can run a complete cycle in Xerpium. The team defines regions, areas, and zones, then assigns salesmen to territory with valid date ranges. A principal discount scheme is set for a supplier and linked to products. When a salesman captures an order during a visit, the sale order automatically applies the principal discount. If the order value exceeds the credit limit or discount boundary, it enters the configured approval workflow.

Once approved, a delivery order is created from the sale order using the delivery wizard. The delivery is added to a daily manifest with a route, vehicle, and driver. At the customer location, the driver collects digital proof of delivery. The salesman later performs an on-site collection using cash, transfer, or QRIS, and the payment updates the receivable in real time.

For inventory planning, seasonal forecast predicts upcoming demand based on Ramadan or rainy season patterns. Purchase recommendations feed purchase orders, which go through budget and principal limit approval. Internal transfers between warehouses use barcode scanning and double approval to maintain stock accuracy.
