Building Management with Xerpium ERP: End-to-End Property Operations for Apartments, Offices, and Mixed-Use Assets

# Building Management with Xerpium ERP: End-to-End Property Operations for Apartments, Offices, and Mixed-Use Assets

Managing apartments, offices, mixed-use buildings, malls, schools, hospitals, hotels, industrial estates, and government properties involves far more than collecting rent. Property teams must handle unit configurations, resident records, monthly IPL billing, maintenance requests, work orders, security patrols, cleaning schedules, facility bookings, parking, complaints, and financial reporting. When these functions run on separate tools or spreadsheets, operational visibility suffers and routine tasks become slow and error-prone.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated Building Management suite that connects property, tower, floor, and unit data with residents, billing, maintenance, security, and accounting. This article covers common building management challenges and how relevant Xerpium modules work together.

Common Challenges in Building Management

  • Fragmented property, tower, floor, unit, and resident data across multiple systems.
  • Manual IPL and utility billing, leading to delays and limited outstanding balance visibility.
  • Unstructured maintenance and complaint handling without enforceable SLAs or clear technician assignment.
  • Limited coordination between building operations and accounting, inventory, HR, and purchasing.
  • Inconsistent reporting on occupancy, security incidents, cleaning quality, and facility usage.

Xerpium ERP Building Management Modules

Property, Tower, Floor, and Unit Management

Xerpium ERP allows property managers to record each property with name, code, address, manager, and property type, including apartment, office, mixed, mall, school, hospital, hotel, industrial, and government. Each property can have multiple towers, and each tower can define floors with number and code. Unit types are configurable, covering studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, and penthouse.

Within unit management, every unit includes name, code, property, tower, floor, unit type, owner, status, usage, and size. Statuses include Available, Occupied, Reserved, Maintenance, and Inactive. Usage can be Residential or Commercial. Size is captured in square meters with bedroom and bathroom counts. Ownership history is automatically recorded when an owner changes, supporting multi-owner history tracking.

Resident and Rental Contract Management

Resident records store identity details using KTP or Passport, phone, email, and the associated unit. Resident types include Owner, Tenant, Family, and Staff. Status can be Active, Moved Out, or Blacklisted. Move in and move out dates are tracked with history. Family members can be recorded within one unit, and vehicles can be registered per resident.

For commercial units, rental contracts manage start and end dates with automatic overlap validation. The system tracks annual rent, down payment, and balance. Down payment invoices and annual rent invoices are generated through accounting. Contract statuses include Draft, Active, Expired, and Cancelled. Unit status is automatically synced to occupied or available based on contract changes.

Billing, IPL, and Utility Management

IPL billing supports monthly invoices per unit or resident. Charge types include Service Charge, Sinking Fund, Water, Electricity, Gas, Internet, and Parking. Meter readings calculate consumption, with utility types covering water, electricity, gas, and internet. The system compares previous and current meter readings and supports tiered pricing tariffs. Consumption automatically links to billing lines.

Invoices can be generated automatically to accounting, with due dates and outstanding tracking. A monthly generation wizard enables mass billing across units.

Maintenance Request and Work Order

Residents can create maintenance requests with priorities of Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. SLAs are automatically applied based on priority, with urgent requests requiring a 1-hour response and 8-hour completion. Technicians can be assigned from HR employees, scheduled, and dispatched. Each request can include checklist lines and before/after photo documentation. Maintenance requests can also generate invoices and purchase orders.

Work orders cover Electrical, Plumbing, Civil, HVAC, Cleaning, Landscaping, and IT. Work orders can be assigned to employees or vendor contractors. Time tracking records working hours per technician, and materials can be linked to product inventory. Each work order includes task checklists with completion status and before/after documentation.

Facility Booking, Security, Cleaning, Parking, and Visitor Management

Facility booking manages facilities per property, such as gym, pool, and function room. Each facility has maximum capacity, hourly rate, and deposit. Residents can book through a calendar, and invoices are automatically integrated into billing.

Security incident management covers Theft, Vandalism, Fire, Flood, Noise, Trespassing, and Accident. Incidents are reported by user and location at the property, tower, or unit level. The workflow moves from Draft to Investigated, Resolved, or Cancelled, with action taken recorded.

Security patrol uses routes with QR code checkpoints. Patrol schedules are organized by shift, including morning, afternoon, and night. Completion rate tracks the percentage of checkpoints scanned per patrol. Each checkpoint scan logs timestamp, photo, and notes.

Cleaning management defines areas such as corridor, lobby, toilet, and parking. Schedules are organized by shift and area. Quality is scored from 1 to 5 per area, with supervisor inspections and completion rate tracking per schedule.

Parking and visitor management controls parking slots per property with types car, motorcycle, and bicycle. Slot status can be Available, Occupied, Reserved, or Maintenance. Visitors can be pre-registered, checked in and out, and issued QR code passes. Lost and found items use an item management workflow for claim and disposal. Vehicle registration is recorded per resident.

Complaint management uses configurable categories with SLAs per category. Priorities include Low, Normal, High, and Urgent, with different SLA hours. Complaints can be auto-assigned to employees or escalated. SLA deadlines are automatically calculated based on priority and category. Residents can provide 1–5 feedback, and overdue complaints are automatically detected.

Property Dashboard and Sparepart & Procurement

The property dashboard provides a pie chart of unit status and occupancy per property, a billing summary of outstanding amounts per month, open versus closed maintenance requests, complaint trends and SLA compliance, and security incident reports per property.

Sparepart request management links to product inventory. Purchase orders can be created from sparepart requests, and sparepart stock is visible in real time.

How Building Management Connects to the Rest of Xerpium

Xerpium Building Management is not an isolated module. It integrates with core ERP areas:

  • Building ↔ Accounting: IPL billing creates customer invoices automatically, maintenance invoices are generated via account.move, and rental contracts produce down payment and annual rent invoices.
  • Building ↔ Inventory: Work order materials come from stock products, sparepart requests check inventory availability, and product costs support IPL and maintenance.
  • Building ↔ HR: Maintenance technicians are assigned from hr.employee, security guards are assigned from HR records, and cleaning staff schedules are managed through HR.
  • Building ↔ Purchase: Purchase orders can be created from vendor maintenance, sparepart procurement, and material contractor purchasing.
  • Building ↔ Maintenance: Work orders are created from maintenance requests, with checklist and material tracking. Actual costs can be invoiced to residents.
  • Building ↔ Report: Report structures support building management, property financial reports, and per-property dashboards.

Business Benefits

  • Centralized property, tower, floor, unit, resident, and facility data across the entire portfolio.
  • Automated monthly IPL billing and utility consumption-to-invoice flow improve accuracy and receivables visibility.
  • SLA-driven maintenance, complaint, and security workflows provide clear deadlines and assignment.
  • Integrated accounting, inventory, HR, and purchasing remove duplicate data entry and manual handoffs.
  • Real-time dashboards show occupancy, outstanding billing, maintenance status, complaint trends, and security incidents.

Example Use Case: From Monthly Billing to Urgent Maintenance

A property manager runs the monthly IPL billing wizard, which generates invoices for all units based on service charges, sinking fund, and utility meter readings. Consumption is calculated automatically and posted as billing lines to accounting.

Later, a resident in Tower A submits an urgent plumbing maintenance request. The system creates an Urgent priority request with a 1-hour response and 8-hour completion SLA. The building team assigns an HR technician and creates a Plumbing work order. Checklist lines and before/after photos are recorded. If materials are needed, the work order consumes inventory stock or triggers a sparepart request with a purchase order. Actual maintenance costs can be invoiced to the resident.

Meanwhile, the property dashboard updates maintenance request status, open work orders, and billing summaries in real time.

Because Building Management connects natively with accounting, inventory, HR, purchasing, and maintenance, operational data flows without manual re-entry and management gets better control over daily building performance.
