# Livestock ERP: Managing Feedlot Operations and Halal Traceability with Xerpium

Commercial livestock operations — especially cattle fattening, breeding, and halal-oriented supply chains — operate under constant pressure. Feed costs shift, animal performance must be tracked individually, health events can affect sale eligibility, and buyers increasingly demand proof of origin, welfare, and halal compliance. Many operations still manage these requirements with spreadsheets, paper forms, and disconnected systems. That approach makes it difficult to know the true cost per animal, prove traceability, or improve productivity consistently.

Xerpium ERP offers a livestock module designed specifically for structured animal management, feed control, health records, halal slaughter documentation, and batch-level profitability. It connects animal operations to purchasing, sales, inventory, accounting, HR, maintenance, quality, and blockchain-ready traceability in one system.

Common challenges in livestock operations

  • Fragmented animal identity and origin data across ear tags, RFID codes, photos, supplier documents, and manual notes.
  • Limited batch-level costing because feed, medicine, labor, and overhead are not accurately allocated to a specific fattening period or pen.
  • Slow or inaccurate performance tracking for ADG, FCR, mortality, and weight gain, often caused by manual recording and duplicate weighing.
  • Weak traceability and halal evidence that cannot satisfy modern buyers, auditors, or halal certification requirements.
  • Disconnected workflows between purchase, feeding, health, slaughter, and accounting, leading to data re-entry and reporting delays.

How Xerpium ERP solves livestock management

Animal Registry and Pen Management

Xerpium starts with a structured Livestock Animal Registry. Each animal can be identified by ear tag, RFID code, or QR code, and recorded with species and breed information such as Bali, Madura, PO, Limousin, Simmental, Brahman, Angus, Friesian Holstein, and others. Origin tracking captures province, regency, and the originating farm. The system also stores photos, a gallery per animal, and purchase information including supplier, purchase order, purchase price, initial weight, and entry date.

Pen Management records each kandang with pen type — fattening, quarantine, isolation, or holding — along with capacity, current count, and utilization percentage. Each pen can have a responsible person assigned from HR employee data, a location or block, and area in square meters. Pens can be linked many-to-many to livestock batches.

Batch Management and Purchase-to-Active Workflow

A livestock batch represents a fattening period with start date, end date, and state such as draft, active, or done. Xerpium assigns pens and animals to each batch and calculates performance KPIs including FCR, average ADG, total weight gain, and mortality rate. Full costing covers feed, medicine, labor, overhead, and animal purchases, then automatically computes HPP per animal and per kilogram of live weight.

The Purchase-to-Active Workflow simplifies intake. A PO for feeder cattle captures species and expected weight. When the PO is approved, Xerpium automatically creates animal records from the line items. Pen and batch assignments from the PO are filled directly, origin province is taken from the supplier address, and the intake event is automatically recorded in traceability.

Feed, Weighing, and FCR

Feed Management supports ingredients such as forage, concentrate, BIS, bran, tofu waste, molasses, and minerals. Users can create TMR feed formulas with dry matter percentage and cost per kilogram. Feed stock is tracked per pen with quantity on hand, and daily feeding records can be logged per batch or pen by time — morning, afternoon, or evening. Dry matter is calculated automatically from ingredients or formulas.

Weighing & ADG Tracking records routine weighing per animal with date and method — manual, RFID, or digital. Average Daily Gain is computed automatically from the weight difference divided by the interval in days. The system maintains a growth curve line chart per animal, shows previous weight and interval automatically, and validates against duplicate weighing on the same date.

Feeding & FCR manages feeding schedules for morning, afternoon, evening, or night per batch or pen. In formula mode, selecting a ration formula calculates quantity automatically from daily feed per head. In ingredient mode, direct ingredient records are captured with automatic cost. Feed per head and dry matter per head are calculated automatically.

Health, Pharmacy, and SOP

Health & Medical Records track event types such as vaccination, treatment, sickness, death, quarantine, routine examination, and surgery. Medicine tracking includes product, dose, unit, cost, and withdrawal period. The system calculates the withdrawal date — the safe sale date after medicine administration — and supports preventive vaccination schedules per species with cron reminders. Photo documentation can record conditions before and after medical actions.

Pharmacy Stock tracks medicine per pen and in a central warehouse. Movements cover receipt, disbursement, and stock adjustments. Low stock alerts use minimum stock levels with an automatic low-stock flag. Expiry tracking records batch expiry dates, and stock is automatically deducted when a medical record is created.

SOP Management covers SOP types such as feed, health, cleanliness, weighing, vaccination, biosecurity, and slaughter. Each SOP includes checklist items with guidance, estimated time, and photo requirements. A daily cron automatically creates SOP executions for each pen, tracks status from draft to in progress to done or skipped, and calculates checklist completion percentage automatically.

Traceability, Halal Slaughter, and Blockchain-Ready Certificates

Traceability & Halal Chain records a chain of custody per animal: intake, active, and sold or slaughtered. Records are append-only with SHA-256 hashing and cannot be deleted after hashing. The hash chain with prev_hash structure is blockchain-ready, supporting immutable audit trails.

Halal Slaughter Record includes a checklist covering ante-mortem, bismillah, qiblat, blood drained, and post-mortem. Halal compliance is computed automatically and requires all checklist items to be fulfilled. The system also records carcass data such as live weight, carcass weight, dressing percentage, and offal, plus slaughterman assignment with manual or mechanical methods.

Blockchain-Ready Certificate manages halal certificates with number, issuing body — MUI, LPPOM, or BPJPH — type, and validity period. Validation generates an automatic SHA-256 hash. After blockchain submission, the transaction hash is stored. The system is tamper-evident and can automatically expire certificates using cron based on the validity date.

HPP, Profitability, and Accounting Integration

The livestock engine calculates feed costs from feeding records per batch, medicine costs from health events, labor costs from batch assignments, and overhead such as electricity, water, and pen depreciation. Total revenue comes from sold animals. Gross profit and margin percentage are generated automatically per batch.

Accounting features include automatic HPP per animal and per kilogram, automatic sale journal posting when an animal is marked sold, cost allocation per batch, multi-branch consolidation, and analytic accounts as cost centers per batch or pen.

AI Dashboard and Cross-Module Connectivity

Xerpium’s AI dashboard can auto-generate a complete dashboard from natural language using DeepSeek, convert natural language to real-time SQL queries, and analyze trends, peaks, anomalies, and recommendations. Growth chart components display interactive curves per animal, and conditional formatting applies dynamic colors based on values.

The livestock module integrates with Sales for special animal selection, auto mark-sold and journal posting, and delivery order or surat jalan integration. It connects to Purchase for feeder PO creation, feed ingredient purchasing, and pharmacy replenishment. Inventory tracks feed stock per pen and pharmacy stock. HR assigns labor per batch, veterinarians and officers, and pen responsible persons. Maintenance covers pen and facility maintenance, scale calibration, and cold storage. Quality monitors health event quality, halal compliance, and SOP execution. Blockchain integration covers trace record hashing, halal certificate verification, and immutable chain-of-custody audit.

Business benefits

  • Single source of truth for animal identity, origin, health, feeding, and movement.
  • Accurate HPP per animal and per kilogram of live weight with batch-level gross margin.
  • Faster operational decisions using automatic ADG, FCR, mortality, and growth curve analysis.
  • Stronger compliance with halal slaughter records and blockchain-ready traceability.
  • Reduced manual work through automation from purchase approval to animal creation, feeding, SOP execution, and sale journal posting.

Example use case: a cattle fattening batch from PO to sale

A feedlot purchases 40 feeder cattle through a PO Bakalan. Once the PO is approved, Xerpium automatically creates 40 animal records with expected weight, origin province from the supplier address, and an intake trace event. The animals are assigned to a fattening pen and a 90-day batch.

Daily feeding uses a TMR formula, with dry matter and cost captured automatically. Weekly weighing records update ADG and growth curves per animal. A health event for one animal records medicine, withdrawal date, and automatically deducts pharmacy stock. Daily SOP checklists are created by cron for the pen. At the end of the batch, Xerpium calculates feed, medicine, labor, and overhead costs, compares them with sales revenue, and produces gross profit and margin. Before sale, the system confirms withdrawal safety, and halal slaughter records generate blockchain-ready hashes for buyer verification.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP turns livestock operations from scattered manual records into a controlled, traceable, and profitable process. It connects animal identity, pen capacity, feeding, weighing, health, halal compliance, and accounting in one platform. For feedlots, breeding operations, and halal-focused livestock businesses, the result is clearer cost control, stronger compliance, and more confident decisions.
