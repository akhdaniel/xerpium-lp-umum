Complete ERP solution for Generic ERP: A Unified Xerpium Deployment Blueprint

For many businesses running generic or mixed-mode operations, daily work still depends on fragmented systems: sales teams use one spreadsheet, procurement another, finance a separate accounting package, and inventory is reconciled manually. This creates duplicate data entry, slow approvals, delayed reporting, and limited visibility from quote to cash. Xerpium ERP addresses this with an end-to-end suite that connects sales, purchasing, inventory, finance, HR, manufacturing, projects, e-commerce, and AI-assisted workflows in one platform.

Common Challenges in Generic ERP

  • Sales, inventory, and finance data live in different tools, making the quote-to-cash status difficult to trace.
  • Manual document routing and approval bottlenecks slow quotations, purchase orders, and payments.
  • Stock visibility is fragmented across warehouses, lots, serials, and delivery commitments.
  • Financial close and reporting often rely on exported spreadsheets and manual consolidation across entities.
  • HR, payroll, and project costs are disconnected from operational and accounting data, so profitability is unclear.
  • There is no single AI layer or dashboard to answer cross-module operational questions quickly.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Sales, CRM, and digital commerce

Xerpium covers the front office from lead to payment. The CRM module manages lead pipelines, opportunity kanban, probability, expected revenue, lost reasons, activity planning, and email integration. Quotation supports order types, revision tracking, and auto-cancel of expired quotes. Sale orders run through an automatic workflow with validation, invoicing, payment, and stock updates, including tier validation and multi-level approval. End-to-End Flow

A typical cycle in Xerpium connects every part of the business. A lead enters CRM, becomes a quotation with revision tracking, and is confirmed as a sale order. The sale order triggers the automatic workflow for validation, then delivery splits by commitment date or address and updates stock from the warehouse with lot or serial selection. Accounting generates the invoice, handles advance payment if needed, and recognizes revenue. On the procurement side, reorder rules create a purchase request, which becomes an RFQ or PO with multi-tier approval. Goods receipt updates inventory valuation, and three-way matching between PO, receipt, and invoice controls payment. If manufacturing is involved, the manufacturing order consumes components, posts WIP journals, and moves cost to finished goods and COGS. HR payroll posts BPJS and PPh21 automatically, while project timesheets and budgets feed profitability views. Throughout the cycle, AI can create or confirm documents, and dashboards reflect real-time status from sales, inventory, finance, and operations.

Implementation Steps

  1. Data preparation and master data: Clean and map customers, suppliers, products, chart of accounts, warehouse locations, and employee records. Define lot or serial numbering if needed.
  2. Parameter and workflow configuration: Select a relevant COA template from the 32 industry options or configure the COA, set approval tiers, Dynamic Stage rules, pricelists, reorder rules, and document sequences.
  3. Pilot core processes: Run quotation-to-invoice, purchase-to-pay, and inventory movement with a small team. Validate automated journals, delivery splits, and reconciliation.
  4. Rollout by module and team: Activate CRM, procurement, finance, HR/payroll, manufacturing, or project modules in phases. Configure dashboards and AI tools for each team’s daily work.
  5. Evaluate and optimize: Review cycle counts, budget versus actual, cash flow, report cache performance, and approval bottlenecks. Refine KPI dashboards, training, and exception rules.
