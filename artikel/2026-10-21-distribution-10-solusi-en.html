10 Best ERP Solutions for Distribution Operations

October 21, 2026

Distribution operations do not live in one system. A sales team works zones and visit plans. A warehouse moves stock between branches. A delivery team runs routes and daily manifests. Finance collects payments in the field. When these processes run in disconnected spreadsheets or separate tools, the result is late order capture, manual discount errors, unclear territory assignments, delayed POD, and stockouts during seasonal peaks. Xerpium addresses these problems through a distribution-focused ERP suite that connects territory management, delivery, inventory, field visits, collection, and approval workflows in one operational backbone.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. Salesman Territory — one salesman per zone with date range and region inheritance.
  2. Principal & Discount — automatic principal discount schemes in sales and purchase orders.
  3. Delivery Route & Manifest — daily routes, delivery orders, fleet assignment, and digital POD.
  4. Inventory Transfer — internal warehouse transfers with dual approval and barcode scanning.
  5. Seasonal Forecast — seasonal stock prediction for Ramadan, Lebaran, and rainy periods.
  6. Visit Plan — daily visit schedules, zone-based route order, and status tracking.
  7. Visit Log — real-time visit reports with photo evidence, geo tagging, and order capture.
  8. Collection Payment — cash, transfer, and QRIS collection against open invoices.
  9. Sales Target — monthly targets, principal breakdowns, and achievement tracking.
  10. Sales Order Approval — auto-approve below limits with credit and discount checks.

1. Salesman Territory

Salesman Territory prevents overlapping field assignments by assigning one salesman to a specific zone with a defined date range. The source module applies a UNIQUE constraint, so one salesman can hold a zone during a period without conflict. Related fields pull area_id and region_id from the zone record, which keeps the broader Region → Area → Zone hierarchy intact. For distribution teams, this solves the problem of two salesmen visiting the same customer or leaving a zone uncovered when a territory is changed. It gives sales managers clear ownership and makes route planning and target assignment consistent.

2. Principal & Discount

Principal & Discount manages principal or supplier data and the discount scheme that principals require for customers. The module integrates the discount automatically in sale orders and purchase orders, and links products to the relevant principal. This removes the recurrent operational problem of sales teams entering discounts manually and causing margin leakage or disputes with principals. Because the principal scheme is centralized, every transaction follows the same commercial policy, and purchasing to the principal stays aligned. Distribution companies dealing with multiple principals gain a single source of truth for trade terms and product relationships.

3. Delivery Route & Manifest

Delivery Route & Manifest turns daily shipping into a controlled operation. It manages daily routes, creates delivery orders from stock picking with a sequence, and groups delivery orders into a daily manifest per route and day. The module supports digital proof of delivery, fleet integration for vehicle and driver assignment, and a wizard to create delivery from a sale order. This solves fragmented delivery planning and missing POD evidence. Warehouses and drivers work from the same daily manifest, while back-office teams see route coverage and can resolve delivery disputes with a digital trail.

4. Inventory Transfer

Inventory Transfer handles internal stock movement between warehouses with an approval-heavy workflow. The source includes dual approval for internal transfers, barcode scanning for location and goods, printable location barcode labels, and configurable workflow stages through xerpium_dynamic_stage_stock. Distribution companies can move stock from a central warehouse to branch warehouses without losing control or traceability. The dual approval reduces unauthorized movements, while barcode scanning speeds up picking and reduces location errors. This module directly addresses the common distribution problem of inventory inaccuracy across multiple branches and warehouse locations.

5. Seasonal Forecast

Seasonal Forecast helps distribution teams prepare for demand that changes by season. The module defines season categories such as Ramadan, Lebaran, Musim Hujan, and Back to School. It forecasts stock needs based on those seasons, runs automatic computation via cron, generates forecasts from historical data through a wizard, and integrates with inventory to recommend purchase quantities. This solves the recurring problem of stockouts during peak selling periods and excess inventory after the season ends. Planners can act on forecast-based purchase recommendations before demand spikes, reducing emergency ordering and dead stock.

6. Visit Plan

Visit Plan gives field teams a structured schedule instead of ad hoc customer visits. Salesmen get a daily plan with visit types including sales call, canvassing, collection, and survey. The module supports route optimization by ordering visits based on zone, and tracks status from Planned to Done to Cancelled. For distribution operations, this solves low field productivity and uneven coverage. Managers can compare planned versus completed visits, while salesmen know which customers to visit and in what order. The zone-based ordering also reduces travel time between stops and makes daily field activity measurable.

7. Visit Log

Visit Log captures what actually happens during a field visit in real time. The module records on-site reports, photo evidence of display and store condition, order capture directly from the field, and geo tagging using coordinates. This solves the blind spot between the field team and the back office. Sales orders can be captured during the visit and sent immediately, eliminating paper order forms and delayed data entry. Photo evidence also gives supervisors a way to verify store execution without physically visiting every outlet. Geo tagging adds location proof to each visit record.

8. Collection Payment

Collection Payment brings invoice settlement to the customer’s location. The module supports on-site collection with payment methods for cash, transfer, and QRIS. It links payments to account.move records, so open receivables are updated directly. Settlement status is updated in real time. Distribution teams often suffer from slow payment reconciliation and cash leakage when collections are recorded days later in a separate system. This solution solves that by recording the payment at the point of collection and linking it to the invoice. Finance gets real-time visibility into what was collected and what remains outstanding per customer.

9. Sales Target

Sales Target makes sales performance measurable against defined goals. The module supports monthly targets per salesman and also breaks targets down per principal. It tracks realisasi versus target as a percentage, integrates with report_id_distribution for formal reports, and provides a dashboard view of sales performance. This solves the operational problem of unclear expectations and delayed performance reviews. Sales managers can see achievement gaps early, while salesmen understand which principal or product line needs more focus. The principal target breakdown is especially useful for distribution companies whose field teams must meet supplier-specific sales commitments.

10. Sales Order Approval

Sales Order Approval balances speed with financial control. The module auto-approves sales orders with a value below a configured limit. It performs credit checks against the customer’s receivable plafond and validates discounts that fall outside allowed limits, routing those exceptions for approval. Stage configuration per company is available via dynamic_stage_sale. Distribution operations need fast order capture in the field, but uncontrolled credit or unauthorized discounts create risk. This solution lets routine orders flow automatically while holding risky orders for review, so sales reps are not blocked by unnecessary approvals, and finance retains control over exposure.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Start with the field-to-back-office loop. If field teams still use paper or separate tools, prioritize Visit Plan, Visit Log, and Collection Payment together because they share zone and customer data. Second, match approvals to risk. Inventory Transfer and Sales Order Approval prevent unauthorized movements and discount leakage. Third, assess seasonality. Seasonal Forecast matters for Ramadan or Lebaran peaks, while multi-branch operations benefit from barcode-based inventory transfer. Finally, choose native integration. Delivery Route & Manifest should connect to fleet, inventory, and accounting without manual exports.

Conclusion

Distribution operations require tight coordination across territories, routes, warehouses, visits, collections, and approvals. These ten Xerpium modules map directly to those operational realities.
