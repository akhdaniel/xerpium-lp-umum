# Food & Beverage ERP: How Xerpium Connects Restaurant, Kitchen, Manufacturing, and Franchise Operations

Published: 26 September 2026

Food and beverage is one of the most operationally dense industries in ERP. A single order can touch table management, kitchen display, recipe costing, production batches, quality checks, cold chain transfers, franchise billing, and loyalty redemption. When these functions live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, revenue leaks, expired stock, slow table turns, and inconsistent quality become everyday risks.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated F&B suite that connects front-of-house service, back-of-house production, multi-branch distribution, franchise management, and financial accounting in one system. This article breaks down the core challenges and the Xerpium modules designed to solve them.

Common Challenges in Food & Beverage Operations

  • Disconnected order and kitchen workflows cause delays between ordering, preparation, and serving.
  • Manual recipe costing and batch tracking make food cost, waste, and margin control unreliable.
  • Cold chain and shelf-life risks increase spoilage, compliance issues, and customer safety concerns.
  • Multi-branch and franchise complexity makes transfers, royalties, and brand consistency difficult to manage.
  • Fragmented loyalty and promotion data limits repeat revenue and customer insight.

Xerpium Solutions for Food & Beverage

Restaurant Operations and Table Management

Xerpium supports the full dine-in journey from table to bill. fnb.restaurant.table tracks table code, name, floor or area, branch, seating capacity, minimum and maximum pax, and table type such as regular, VIP, outdoor, bar, private room, or takeaway. Each table can have a QR code identifier for self-ordering and link to multiple POS configurations. Table status moves between available, occupied, reserved, cleaning, and maintenance.

fnb.restaurant.order manages the order workflow from draft, ordering, submitted, serving, to completed. It covers dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and room service orders, records guest count, and handles order lines with product, quantity, price, discount, notes, and kitchen order links. Servers are assigned per order, and the order can link to sale.order for invoicing.

fnb.kitchen.order gives the kitchen a real-time display system. Orders move through priority levels from normal to urgent and states from new, in prep, quality check, ready, to served. Kitchens can be assigned as hot, cold, grill, or pastry. The module records timestamps for date ordered, date started, and date ready, and supports actions such as start prep, quality check, and mark ready.

Shift management is covered by fnb.restaurant.shift. Restaurants can define morning, afternoon, night, or full-day shifts, assign staff, designate a shift manager, and keep opening and closing notes. The shift lifecycle moves from draft to open to closed, with metrics for total orders and total revenue per shift.

For reservations, fnb.restaurant.reservation captures guest count, date, and table, helping restaurants plan capacity and table status ahead of service.

Catering and Event Execution

For event-based revenue, fnb.catering.order manages weddings, corporate events, and seminars. Each order includes event name, location, date, and guest count. Packages can be buffet, plated, box, or prasmanan. Order lines record menu items, quantities, and prices. The state moves from draft, confirmed, preparing, delivered, to completed. The module also tracks delivery details and equipment rental such as tables, chairs, and chafing dishes.

Recipe Costing, Batch Production, and Quality

fnb.recipe provides recipe code, name, linked product, and yield quantity. Ingredients are managed as recipe lines with product, quantity, and unit cost. Preparation steps, prep time, and cook time are included. The module categorizes recipes as main, appetizer, dessert, beverage, sauce, or base, automatically calculates total cost from ingredients, applies waste percentage per ingredient, and integrates with mrp.bom for production.

fnb.production.batch creates production batches per recipe and links them to product, kitchen, and branch. It integrates with mrp.production for manufacturing orders and tracks stock lots per batch. Expiry date tracking supports shelf-life management. Batch states move from draft, planned, in progress, completed, QC hold, to rejected. Each batch can have quality checks.

fnb.quality.check supports visual, taste, temperature, weight, microbiology, chemical, and packaging checks. Results can be pass, pass conditional, fail, or pending lab. The module captures actual and standard measurements, assigns an inspector, links to the production batch, and records check timestamps and notes.

fnb.shelf.life tracks product, lot, production date, and expiry date. It computes shelf-life days and days remaining, then classifies items as fresh, expiring soon at seven days or less, critical at three days or less, or expired. Storage types include ambient, chilled at 2–8°C, frozen at -18°C, and dry. The module also tracks storage location and batch links.

Distribution and Cold Chain

fnb.distribution.plan manages inter-branch transfers from a source branch to a destination branch. It records plan date, route, and line items with planned and loaded quantities. The state moves from draft, confirmed, loading, in transit, to delivered, and links to stock.picking for warehouse transfers.

fnb.cold.chain.log records temperature readings with timestamp, temperature in degrees Celsius, and location checkpoint. It links to stock picking or distribution plans and tracks the product being monitored. Breach detection is computed automatically when temperature exceeds the maximum threshold. Default minimum and maximum temperatures are 2°C and 8°C. Each log records the operator responsible.

Franchise Management, Loyalty, and Promotions

fnb.franchise.agreement manages franchisee contracts with branch, unique agreement number, territory exclusivity, initial fee, royalty percentage, marketing fund percentage, and annual renewal. The agreement lifecycle moves from draft, active, suspended, to terminated, and integrates with subscription billing for recurring franchise fees.

fnb.loyalty.program defines points per currency, currency per point conversion, and tiers such as bronze, silver, gold, and platinum. Each tier can carry minimum and maximum points, discount percentage, and multiplier. New members are auto-assigned a default tier.

fnb.loyalty.card records card number, partner, program, current and lifetime points balance, tier, issue and expiry dates, and transactions for earn, redeem, adjust, and expire actions.

fnb.promotion supports discount percentage, buy X get Y, minimum purchase, happy hour, and combo promotions. Promotions can apply to selected products and branches, run within scheduled date and time windows, and include terms and conditions text.

Dashboard, Analytics, and Financial Accounting

The vit_dashboard_fnb KPI dashboard provides daily and monthly revenue per branch and service type, best-selling menu items, table occupancy and turnover, average dining time, kitchen prep time and order completion rate, stock alerts, and loyalty metrics for new members and points earned or redeemed.

Financial accounting uses l10n_id_food_beverage, a chart of accounts specific to the F&B industry. Revenue streams are separated for dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and catering. COGS tracks food cost, beverage cost, and packaging cost. Depreciation covers kitchen equipment, furniture, and POS systems. Prepaid revenue supports deferred revenue for catering advance payments.

Business Benefits

  • One operational view from table orders and kitchen screens to batch production, distribution, and accounting.
  • Faster service and table turnover through integrated ordering, kitchen display, and shift management.
  • Better margin control through automatic recipe costing, waste tracking, and QC hold states.
  • Lower spoilage risk through expiry alerts, cold chain breach detection, and storage classification.
  • Scalable franchise operations with agreement, royalty, loyalty, and promotion data connected to billing.

Example Use Case

A multi-branch restaurant group uses QR self-ordering at each table. The order appears on the kitchen display system by station, and the kitchen marks items as prepared, quality checked, and ready. Recipes and production batches in the central kitchen calculate food cost, create lot-tracked batches, and pass quality checks. Chilled or frozen finished products are transferred to branches through distribution plans with cold chain temperature logs. At the outlet, shelf-life tracking automatically flags items expiring within seven days. Loyalty points and tier benefits are applied at the POS, franchise royalty fees are billed through subscription, and the F&B dashboard shows revenue, kitchen performance, stock alerts, and loyalty activity in real time.

Conclusion

Food and beverage operators cannot afford disconnected processes between service, production, distribution, and finance. Xerpium ERP brings restaurant operations, catering, food manufacturing, cold chain, franchise management, loyalty, and financial accounting into a single F&B suite. If your team is evaluating ways to unify branch operations, tighten kitchen and inventory control, and scale franchise or catering revenue, Xerpium’s F&B modules provide the foundation to do it without replacing your core ERP discipline.
