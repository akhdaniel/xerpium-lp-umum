HR Software for Modern Enterprises: How Xerpium ERP Connects People, Payroll, and Accounting

Human resources has quietly become one of the most data-intensive functions in any growing company. Every month, HR teams reconcile attendance logs, calculate BPJS contributions and PPh21 withholding, process leave requests, chase approvals, and then hand over a payroll summary to finance — often in the form of a spreadsheet that someone has to re-key into the general ledger. Each handoff introduces delay, and each manual entry introduces risk.

The problem is rarely a lack of effort. It is architectural. When attendance lives in one system, payroll in another, recruitment in an email inbox, and accounting in a separate ledger, no one has a single version of the truth. Xerpium ERP addresses this by treating HR not as an isolated administrative function but as an operational module connected directly to finance — so labour cost flows into the books automatically, and HR data stops being a monthly reconstruction exercise.

Common Challenges in HR Operations

Most HR departments, regardless of industry, run into a similar set of structural problems:

  • Fragmented employee data. Personnel records, attendance, timesheets, and performance metrics sit in separate tools, making it difficult to answer basic questions about workforce cost and productivity.
  • Payroll complexity and compliance risk. Calculating BPJS and PPh21 correctly across different employment types is detailed work. Manual calculation invites errors that surface only after disbursement — or worse, during an audit.
  • Administrative load from routine requests. Leave applications, expense claims, and pay slip requests consume HR time that could go toward recruitment, development, or retention.
  • Slow, inconsistent recruitment. Screening large volumes of CVs manually is time-consuming, and candidate evaluation varies depending on who is doing the reviewing.
  • Disconnected payroll and accounting. Salary expense is usually one of the largest cost lines in a company, yet it often reaches the ledger last — delaying month-end close and distorting interim financial reports.

Relevant Xerpium Modules for HR

Xerpium is built on Odoo, which means its HR capabilities inherit a modular architecture where each component shares the same underlying data model. The following modules address the HR lifecycle end to end.

Complete HR & Payroll

The core HR module consolidates personnel administration (SDM), payroll with BPJS and PPh21 handling, attendance, timesheets, and KPI tracking into a single connected system. Because these components share one database, attendance feeds payroll directly, timesheets support project costing, and KPI records sit alongside the employee profile rather than in a separate performance file.

Critically, this module connects directly to Accounting. Payroll is not a downstream export — it is part of the same system that produces the financial statements.

ESS Mobile App for Employee Self-Service

The Employee Self-Service application is a lightweight Flutter app designed to run comfortably on staff devices. Through it, employees can:

  • Record attendance from mobile
  • Submit leave and permission requests (cuti & izin)
  • File expense claims
  • Access pay slips
  • Complete approvals

This shifts routine transactions to the people who originate them. HR stops being a data entry bottleneck and becomes an exception handler — reviewing what needs judgement rather than processing what is already structured.

AI Recruitment

Xerpium's AI Assistant supports the hiring process through CV screening, AI interviews, and multilingual candidate assessment. For companies hiring across regions or receiving applications in more than one language, multilingual assessment removes a practical barrier that normally limits the candidate pool or slows evaluation.

The value here is consistency as much as speed: every candidate is screened against the same criteria, which makes shortlists more defensible and comparisons more meaningful.

Integration with Finance

Salary cost posts automatically into accounting journals. There is no month-end spreadsheet handover, no manual journal entry for payroll, and no gap between what HR processed and what finance recorded. Financial reports remain accurate because labour cost is captured at the source.

Business Benefits

  • Faster month-end close. With payroll journals posting automatically, finance is no longer waiting on HR to deliver a reconciled summary.
  • Lower compliance risk. BPJS and PPh21 are handled within the payroll module rather than in side calculations maintained by individuals.
  • Reduced administrative overhead. Self-service attendance, leave, expense, and pay slip access remove a large share of inbound HR requests.
  • Better hiring throughput. AI-assisted CV screening and interviews compress the early stages of recruitment without sacrificing evaluation consistency.
  • Accurate labour cost visibility. Because timesheets, attendance, and payroll share one system and feed accounting, management sees real workforce cost rather than estimates.
  • A single employee record. Personnel data, attendance history, performance KPIs, and payroll all reference the same record, eliminating version conflicts.

Example Flow: From Attendance to the General Ledger

Consider a mid-sized company running a monthly payroll cycle.

Throughout the month, field and office staff record attendance through the ESS mobile app. A project engineer submits a timesheet against a client project and files an expense claim for site travel; both route through the app for approval by the relevant manager. Mid-month, an employee requests three days of leave — approved in the same application, with the balance updated automatically.

At period end, the HR & Payroll module draws on this attendance and timesheet data to calculate gross pay, apply BPJS contributions, and compute PPh21 withholding for each employee. Pay slips become available in the ESS app, so HR does not distribute them individually.

The moment payroll is confirmed, the corresponding salary expense, statutory contributions, and withholding entries post into the accounting journals. Finance opens the ledger and finds labour cost already recorded, allocated, and ready for reporting — no import, no re-keying, no reconciliation meeting.

Meanwhile, the recruitment team is filling two open roles. Incoming CVs are screened by the AI Assistant, shortlisted candidates complete an AI interview, and assessments are produced in the candidate's working language. The hiring manager reviews a comparable set of evaluations rather than a stack of unstructured résumés.

Conclusion

HR software delivers the most value when it stops being an isolated system of record and becomes part of the operational and financial fabric of the business. Xerpium brings SDM, payroll with BPJS and PPh21, attendance, timesheets, and KPI into one connected module, extends it to employees through a lightweight ESS mobile app, supports hiring with a multilingual AI Assistant, and posts every salary cost straight into the accounting journals.
