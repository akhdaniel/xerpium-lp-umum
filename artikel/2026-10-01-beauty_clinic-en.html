Managing Beauty Clinic Operations with Xerpium ERP: From Patient Records to Doctor Commissions

# Managing Beauty Clinic Operations with Xerpium ERP: From Patient Records to Doctor Commissions

Beauty clinics operate at the intersection of healthcare, retail, and personal service. A single patient visit can involve an appointment, a consultation, a multi-session treatment plan, product consumption, prescription handling, and doctor commission calculation. When these activities live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected systems, the clinic loses time, risks data errors, and struggles to see a clear picture of daily performance.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a connected beauty clinic solution that links patient management, treatment catalogs, scheduling, admission, membership packages, inventory, prescriptions, and accounting in one system. It gives clinic owners, doctors, therapists, and front-desk staff a single source of truth for daily operations and clinical history.

Common Challenges in Beauty Clinic Management

Many beauty clinics face operational friction in similar areas:

  • Patient records are incomplete or scattered across paper files, chat messages, and spreadsheets.
  • Appointment scheduling and queue management become difficult when multiple doctors and treatment rooms are involved.
  • Multi-session treatments and membership packages are hard to track manually, especially when patients miss or reschedule sessions.
  • Product consumption for consumables, cosmetics, and medicines is not connected to stock levels.
  • Doctor commissions are calculated manually, increasing the risk of disputes and delayed payments.

These challenges are not just administrative. They directly affect patient experience, treatment follow-up, inventory accuracy, and clinic profitability.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Beauty Clinics

Xerpium brings beauty clinic operations into a unified ERP workflow. The following capabilities are built around real clinic processes.

Patient Management

Every patient is stored as a contact record with an is_patient flag and an auto-generated patient code. The biodata includes date of birth, gender, blood group, allergies, and medical notes. Emergency contact name and phone are also captured.

Xerpium maintains a medical timeline for each patient, showing appointments, treatment charts, and prescriptions. The system also tracks the patient’s visit count or admission count, giving staff a quick view of patient history.

Treatment Catalog, Doctors, and Therapists

Clinics can define a treatment catalog with treatment name, code, description, duration in minutes, and price. Treatments are grouped into categories such as Consultation, Facial, Injectable, Laser/Device, Body, Hair, and Other. Each treatment can be linked to a service product for invoicing and marked active or inactive.

Doctors are set up as contacts with an is_doctor flag, specialization, license number, and default consultation fee. Therapists can be flagged with is_therapist. Commission rules can be defined per treatment as a fixed amount or a percentage of the treatment price, and commissions are calculated automatically per appointment or treatment line.

Appointment and Queue

Appointments inherit the calendar event model, so every booking is visible in the clinic calendar. Staff must select a patient from the filtered patient list, choose a treatment from the catalog, and assign a doctor from the filtered doctor list.

Each appointment automatically receives a queue number on admission. Calendar colors are category-based, making it easy to visually distinguish consultation, facial, injectable, and laser appointments. Appointment status moves from Draft to Confirmed, In Progress, Done, or Cancelled. When an appointment is confirmed, Xerpium can automatically send an email confirmation to the patient.

Admission and Visit

A patient visit is handled as a sales order with an is_beauty_admission flag. Admissions can be walk-in or generated from an appointment. Queue numbers are auto-sequenced for each admission.

If a consultation line is missing, Xerpium can automatically add it to the order. The system records the doctor per admission and per treatment line. From a visit, staff can link to the treatment chart and membership, create prescriptions, and track product consumption for items used during the visit.

Membership Packages and Treatment Charts

Clinics can create membership packages with a package name, treatment, total sessions, price, and validity days. Each package is linked to a service product for invoicing. For each patient, the membership stores start and end dates, remaining sessions, and status such as Active, Completed, Expired, or Cancelled. Sessions are deducted automatically when an appointment linked to the membership is completed through a sale order.

For multi-session treatments such as six laser sessions, Xerpium provides a treatment chart. The chart tracks planned sessions and completed sessions per patient. The total amount is calculated as treatment price multiplied by planned sessions, with a down payment field for multi-session packages. The clinic can create invoices directly from the chart to accounting. Each chart session is linked to calendar appointments.

Prescription and Pharmacy

Doctors can create prescriptions with patient, doctor, date, and valid until date. Prescription lines include product, quantity, dosage, and instructions. Prescriptions can be linked to the relevant appointment and sale order, and prescription products can be added to the admission sales order.

Medicine preparation follows a workflow from Draft to Preparing, Ready, and Dispensed. When creating a preparation, Xerpium can automatically load lines from the prescription. Products marked as is_manufactured support compounding or racikan workflows.

Doctor Commission

Doctor commissions are generated automatically when invoices are posted. The calculation uses fixed amounts or percentages defined per treatment, based on the doctor assigned to each treatment line. Commissions are consolidated by doctor for vendor bill creation.

Commission status moves from Draft to Approved to Paid. When the related vendor bill is paid, the commission state can be set to paid automatically. This removes much of the manual work involved in tracking and paying doctor fees.

Product Consumption and Inventory

During a visit, the clinic can record consumption lines for consumable or storable products. Xerpium auto-fills the cost price from the product record and calculates subtotal as quantity multiplied by cost price. Stock is reduced in real time for consumed products.

Products can be flagged as is_medicine in the product template. This supports medicine product management and cosmetic stock tracking within the same inventory framework.

Dashboard Klinik and Patient Reports

The clinic dashboard shows daily visits, treatment revenue per period, outstanding and paid doctor commissions, active membership data, session usage, and treatment popularity.

For documentation, Xerpium provides a patient card PDF with identity and patient history, an HTML medical timeline, invoice history per patient, and a prescription report for printing doctor prescriptions.

Business Benefits of Xerpium ERP for Beauty Clinics

  • Centralized patient records with medical history, emergency contacts, and visit counts.
  • More organized scheduling with calendar-based appointments, color-coded categories, and automatic queue numbers.
  • Accurate multi-session and membership tracking with automatic session deductions.
  • Real-time inventory updates for consumables, medicines, and cosmetic stock.
  • Faster prescription handling with a clear preparation and dispensing workflow.
  • Automated doctor commission calculation and vendor bill consolidation.
  • Better financial visibility through treatment invoicing, membership invoicing, and accounting integration.

Example Workflow: A Laser Treatment Visit

A patient books a laser appointment through the clinic calendar. The appointment is confirmed and the patient receives an email notification. On arrival, the admission is created from the appointment and a queue number is assigned automatically.

The assigned doctor performs the consultation and the treatment line is recorded in the sales order. If the patient has a membership or a multi-session treatment chart, the session is deducted once the appointment is completed. Consumables used during the visit are recorded, and stock is reduced immediately. If the doctor writes a prescription, it is linked to the visit and the products can be added to the same admission sales order.

After invoicing, the doctor’s commission is calculated automatically and later consolidated into a vendor bill. The clinic dashboard reflects the daily visit, revenue, and commission movement without manual re-entry.
