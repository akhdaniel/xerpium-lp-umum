# Retail ERP: Unify POS, Marketplace Channels, and Inventory with Xerpium

Retail operations today rarely live in one place. A single business may run multiple cashier terminals, a Shopee store, a Tokopedia store, a GoFood outlet, and an e-commerce website — while also managing warehouses, procurement, accounting, and payroll. When these systems operate separately, stock levels drift, orders fall through the cracks, and financial reporting becomes a manual burden.

Xerpium ERP is designed for this reality. It connects point-of-sale, marketplace channels, inventory, procurement, accounting, HR, and AI-assisted workflows in one enterprise suite. For retail businesses that need operational control without stitching together disconnected tools, Xerpium provides a practical, integrated foundation.

Common Retail Operational Challenges

Retail teams often face a familiar set of problems:

  • POS, marketplaces, and delivery platforms run as separate channels with no unified order or stock view.
  • Offline store transactions are difficult to manage when connectivity fails.
  • Stock levels differ between physical stores, warehouses, and marketplace listings.
  • Accounting teams manually reconcile POS revenue, marketplace settlement, and tax obligations.
  • Sales commissions and shift-based payroll are hard to track across cashiers and sales staff.
  • Decision-makers lack a real-time dashboard for revenue, top products, or low-stock risks.

These challenges grow as the number of outlets, channels, and transactions increases.

How Xerpium ERP Addresses Retail Complexity

POS Multi-Outlet and POS API

Xerpium supports multi-cashier operations with per-cashier sessions, shift management, and automatic closing. Each transaction can accept cash, debit, credit, QRIS, e-wallet, or transfer. The offline mode allows stores to continue processing transactions when connectivity drops, then synchronize batch data once the system is back online.

The POS API exposes endpoints for /ping, /configs, /products, /customers, /session, and /payment_methods. It also supports batch upload of offline transactions, real-time stock updates from a POS client, and a three-tier closing fallback: normal close, force close with balancing, or hard close. Cash control includes opening balance, cash in/out, and closing reconciliation.

Discount and promotion tools cover per-item discounts, per-order discounts, buy-1-get-1, and happy hour pricing.

Marketplace and GoFood Integration

Xerpium integrates directly with Shopee and Tokopedia. Product sync updates catalog, variants, images, and descriptions from Xerpium to marketplace channels. Orders from Shopee or Tokopedia automatically become sale orders inside the system. Stock sync keeps inventory consistent across all marketplace channels in real time. Order management includes status updates, shipment tracking, and promotion management such as bundle deals, flash sales, and vouchers.

For GoBiz GoFood, Xerpium uses OAuth 2.0 credential management with sandbox and production environments. Menu sync pushes products, variants, prices, and stock to GoFood menus. Outlet control supports opening and closing outlets and subscribing to webhooks for eight events. Incoming webhook orders create sale orders automatically. Teams can accept orders, mark them ready, or reject them via API. QRIS transactions are supported for GoFood payments, and promo SKUs allow price promotions at the item level. Full API logging provides an audit trail for every request and response.

Inventory and Procurement Control

Xerpium supports multi-warehouse structures, including central warehouses, branch locations, consignment stock, and dropship. Real-time stock updates flow from POS, marketplaces, and GoFood into the inventory system. Valuation supports FIFO and average methods, with lot and serial tracking for batch traceability and expiry dates.

Barcode scanning is available for receiving, picking, packing, and shipping. Cycle counts enable periodic stock opname with automatic variance recording. On the procurement side, reorder rules based on minimum and maximum stock trigger automatic purchase orders. Purchase workflows cover RFQ, PO, receipt, and vendor bill, with three-way matching between purchase order, receipt, and invoice. Vendor management tracks rating, payment terms, and lead time, while drop shipping supports direct delivery from supplier to customer.

Retail Accounting and Financial Reporting

Retail transactions generate invoices and journal entries automatically from POS and marketplace activity. The chart of accounts includes 113 retail-specific accounts across 32 account groups. Tax handling covers PPN 11% for output and input tax, PPh 21/23/4(2), and retail exempt fiscal positions.

Xerpium produces balance sheets, profit and loss statements, trial balances, and tax reports aligned with PSAK Indonesia. Special retail accounts accommodate marketplace revenue, vendor rebates, promo support, and shrinkage. Accounts receivable and payable include aging, dunning, and reconciliation. Bank and cash management supports bank reconciliation, petty cash, and transfers. Multi-currency transactions include automatic exchange rate handling.

HR and Payroll for Retail Teams

Retail staff profiles include employee data, NIK, BPJS, and NPWP. Attendance management covers check-in and check-out, shift management, and overtime. Payroll processes basic salary, allowances, deductions, BPJS, and PPh 21. Sales commissions can be calculated automatically per transaction or per product. Leave requests for annual leave, permission, or sick leave follow workflow approval.

Dashboard Studio and AI Assistant

Dashboard Studio provides 14 KPI widgets built from 8 tiles and 6 charts for retail analytics. These include a 12-month revenue trend area chart, a horizontal bar chart for top 10 products, a doughnut chart for revenue by category, and a low stock alert list for products with stock at or below five units. Users can also generate dashboards from natural language descriptions.

The AI Assistant adds another layer of operational speed. It supports questions such as “How much sales today?” and “Create an invoice.” It can look up products, check stock, view customer history, generate sales or profit and loss reports, create POS orders, and post journal entries. The assistant includes more than 100 tools across sales, purchase, inventory, accounting, and HR, and supports 11 languages including Indonesian and English.

Business Benefits

  • One integrated system for POS, marketplaces, GoFood, inventory, accounting, and HR.
  • Real-time stock synchronization across physical outlets and digital channels.
  • Automated order creation from Shopee, Tokopedia, and GoFood reduces manual entry.
  • Offline POS capability keeps stores running during connectivity issues.
  • Retail-specific accounting and tax mapping supports PPN 11% and PSAK Indonesia reporting.
  • Commission and payroll workflows simplify shift-based retail workforce management.
  • Dashboard and AI tools give managers faster access to revenue, stock, and performance data.

Example Retail Workflow

A cashier opens a session at a branch POS, records opening balance, and processes purchases using QRIS or e-wallet. The system updates central stock immediately. Later, the store briefly loses internet; the POS continues processing orders offline and syncs them in batch when connectivity returns.

At the same time, a customer places an order on Shopee. Xerpium imports the marketplace order as a sale order and updates stock across all channels. A GoFood order arrives through webhook, creates another sale order automatically, and the outlet marks it ready via API. Each transaction generates the invoice and journal entry automatically behind the scenes.

At the end of the day, the finance team sees consolidated revenue from POS, Shopee, Tokopedia, and GoFood. Inventory runs reorder rules for fast-moving items. A supervisor asks the AI Assistant for today’s sales or top products, while the Dashboard Studio highlights low-stock items that need replenishment.

Conclusion

Retail businesses need more than a standalone POS or a basic marketplace connector. They need a system that connects storefront operations, digital channels, stock, procurement, finance, and people in one place. Xerpium ERP provides that integrated foundation with retail-specific accounting, real-time inventory, marketplace and GoFood integration, and AI-assisted reporting.
