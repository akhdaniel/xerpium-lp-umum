# Satellite City Developer ERP: Managing Townships, Kavling Sales, Infrastructure, and IPL in One System

Satellite city development is one of the most operationally complex segments in property. A developer must manage land banks, cluster planning, unit inventory, customer agreements, multi-year installment schedules, infrastructure construction, handover processes, and long-term community billing. When these functions run on separate spreadsheets or disconnected applications, delays, missed invoices, and reporting gaps become common.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a dedicated Satellite City module set built for project/township developers. It connects project planning, kavling inventory, PPJB agreements, payment schedules, infrastructure progress, handover, and IPL billing in a single system, while remaining integrated with sales, accounting, inventory, HR, dashboards, and tax tooling.

Tantangan umum

  • Kavling availability and status are scattered across spreadsheets, making it difficult to know which units are available, reserved, sold, handed over, or cancelled.
  • PPJB agreements and installment payment schedules are often tracked manually, leading to missed billing dates and reconciliation errors.
  • Infrastructure budgets, milestones, and actual costs are not consistently linked to project financial records.
  • Monthly IPL or community charges for hundreds of units require repetitive manual invoice generation.
  • Management lacks real-time visibility across multiple projects, companies, and currencies.

Project, Cluster, and Kavling Management

Xerpium’s sc.project module provides a structured project record with auto-sequence project code format SC.PRJ/####, project type (Residential, Commercial, Mixed-Use, Industrial), location, total area in hectares, master plan document, and timeline from commencement date to target completion. Project state follows a clear flow: draft → planned → in_progress → completed. Each project can be linked to an owner/developer through res.partner.

Below the project, sc.cluster manages cluster or block planning. Each cluster has a unique code per project, area in hectares, cluster type, and a direct One2many relation to the project with cascade delete. The cluster also computes real-time statistics: kavling_count, available_count, and sold_count through @api.depends.

The sc.kavling module tracks individual units or lots. Lot numbers are unique per cluster and displayed as cluster-code-number. Each kavling links to a sc.kavling.type template, includes physical attributes such as land area, building area, facing direction, corner lot, and facing road. Pricing automatically computes land price plus building price into total price. Status follows: Available → Reserved → Sold → Handed Over → Cancelled. The unit also maintains relations to agreement, partner, handover, and sale order.

sc.kavling.type acts as a reusable template with type code, building area, land area, bedrooms, bathrooms, floors, and a linked product.product for sales integration. Its display format follows Type Name (100m² / 50m²), allowing developers to generate standard kavling dimensions quickly.

PPJB Agreements and Payment Schedules

sc.customer.agreement manages PPJB or customer purchase agreements. It uses auto-sequence format SC.AGR/#### with tracking enabled. The agreement stores buyer, project, kavling, and related kavling type. Pricing includes land price, building price, discount, and net price auto-computed. Payment schemes support Cash, Installment 12/24/36/48/60 months, and Custom. Down payment percentage automatically calculates the DP amount from net price. Installment amount is computed as (net price - DP) / installment_count. The agreement state moves from Draft → Confirmed → Approved → Payment In Progress → Completed → Handed Over.

Once an agreement is completed, sc.payment.schedule auto-generates payment lines. Payment types include Down Payment, Installment, Fine, and Other. Each line has its own due date, amount, and currency with multi-currency support. An invoice link via account.move connects the schedule line to the actual invoice, while is_paid is computed from the invoice payment state.

The Payment Schedule Generator handles scheme logic: cash scheme creates a single down payment line equal to net price; installment scheme creates one DP line plus N installment lines. Sequence logic places DP at sequence 10 and installments at 11, 12, 13, and so on. Each line can be invoiced separately, and late payment fines are tracked with a separate payment type.

With Satellite ↔ Sales, approving an agreement can automatically create a sale.order using the product from the kavling type and net price as the order price. Invoice links flow from agreement.sale_order_id.invoice_ids.

Infrastructure and Milestone Tracking

sc.infrastructure manages project infrastructure such as Road, Bridge, Water Supply, Electricity, Drainage, Gas Distribution, Telecom, and Green Space/Park. Each item includes length, width, budget amount, actual cost, construction start, target completion, and actual completion date. The state follows Planned → In Progress → Completed → Cancelled. Progress is auto-computed as the average of milestone progress percentages.

sc.infra.milestone tracks progress per infrastructure item. Milestones have sequence order, progress percentage versus target percentage, completion boolean, and monetary expense per milestone. Stored related fields such as project ID allow lower-level records to carry project context for reporting.

Handover and Community Facilities

sc.handover manages unit serah terima with auto-sequence format SC.HDO/#### and name_get display [No] - Buyer Name. It links buyer, agreement, kavling, and project. Planned and actual handover dates are tracked, and actual date is automatically set when the handover is completed. Condition assessment supports Good, Minor Defect, and Major Defect. A checklist boolean and attachment count help document the process. Completing a handover automatically updates the linked kavling and agreement status to handed over.

sc.facility records community facilities such as Club House, Swimming Pool, Gym, Playground, Mosque/Musholla, Park, Security Post, and Commercial Area. Each facility can store capacity, operational status, opening date, and monthly maintenance cost, and is linked to the project with cascade delete.

IPL Billing and Multi-Project Accounting

sc.ipl.billing.line supports recurring IPL or community billing per sold/handed-over kavling. Each line stores the kavling, owner from res.partner, monthly amount, and invoice link. Bulk generation through action_generate_lines searches all sold kavlings and skips existing lines. Auto-invoice creation uses _create_invoice to generate out_invoice documents with the IPL product.

Xerpium’s property accounting features include auto-journal for kavling sales when a sale order is confirmed, separate invoices per installment term, mass IPL invoicing per period, infrastructure capitalization into assets, multi-company operations, multi-currency support with automatic BI/ECB rates, consolidation reports across projects, and tax integration covering PPN 11%, PPh Final 4% properti, and e-Faktur.

AI Dashboards and Operational Visibility

Xerpium’s AI dashboard features allow users to auto-generate dashboards from natural language via DeepSeek. A query like "Tampilkan penjualan kavling bulan ini" can be converted into SQL. AI can analyze sales trends and flag slow-moving clusters. A copilot chat provides streamed answers within the property dashboard context, while conditional formatting highlights status colors, overdue alerts, and progress bars.

The module also integrates with inventory for material consumption tracking on infrastructure, accounting with analytic accounts per project, HR and project task management, dynamic stage workflows with configurable approval states and RBAC groups, and Coretax for auto-generating e-Faktur PPN, automatic PPh Final 4% calculation, NPWP validation, and faktur returns.

  • Faster billing cycles with automatic payment schedule generation and mass IPL invoice creation.
  • Clear cash flow visibility from down payment, installments, and fine tracking.
  • Better infrastructure cost control through milestone-based progress and actual cost tracking.

Contoh alur singkat

A developer creates a mixed-use township project in Xerpium and defines its clusters. Kavling types are set up once with standard land and building dimensions. Individual kavlings are generated from those templates. When a buyer reserves unit A-01, the team creates a PPJB agreement with a down payment and 24-month installment scheme. Upon approval, the system creates the sale order and payment schedule. Each installment is invoiced and tracked independently. Meanwhile, road and drainage infrastructure milestones are updated, automatically calculating overall progress. At handover, the team records the condition assessment, completes the checklist, and finalizes the handover. The kavling and agreement statuses update automatically. From that point, monthly IPL billing lines are generated in bulk and invoiced without manual data entry.
