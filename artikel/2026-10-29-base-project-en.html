# Streamline Delivery with Xerpium Project Management Software: An Odoo-Based ERP System for Modern Teams

Delivering projects on time and within budget remains one of the most persistent challenges in any service-driven or project-centric business. Fragmented tools, disconnected spreadsheets, and manual billing processes create blind spots that erode margins and delay decisions. When task tracking lives in one application, timesheets in another, and invoicing in a third, project managers spend more time reconciling data than actually managing delivery.

Xerpium addresses this problem with a unified Project Management module built on the proven Odoo foundation. Instead of stitching together separate systems, teams gain a single environment where tasks, milestones, timesheets, and progress billing work together—fully integrated with the rest of the Xerpium ERP suite. This article explores how the Xerpium Project Management application helps organizations plan, execute, and bill projects with greater clarity and control.

Common Challenges in Project Delivery

Most project teams struggle not because of a lack of effort, but because their tools fail to keep pace with real-world complexity. The most common pain points include:

  • Disconnected data sources where task progress, time entries, and financials are stored in separate systems that rarely agree with one another.
  • Delayed or inaccurate billing caused by manual effort to translate completed milestones and logged hours into invoices.
  • Limited visibility into progress that leaves managers reacting to problems instead of anticipating them.
  • Difficulty operating across entities and currencies when projects span multiple companies, regions, or client contracts.
  • Weak audit and accountability trails, making it hard to track who changed what and when during the project lifecycle.

Each of these challenges compounds the others. Without a connected system, the cost of coordination quietly consumes the profitability of every engagement.

Relevant Xerpium Modules and Solutions

The Xerpium Project Management module is designed as a base application within the broader ERP system, meaning it integrates natively with every other module rather than existing as an isolated tool. Below are its core capabilities as delivered.

Task and Milestone Management

At the center of the application is structured task and milestone tracking. Teams break down each project into actionable tasks and tie deliverables to clearly defined milestones. This gives project managers a transparent view of where work stands and which key checkpoints have been reached, forming the backbone of both execution and billing.

Timesheets

Team members log their hours directly within the module, creating an accurate record of effort against each task. Because timesheets live inside the same system as tasks and billing, logged time flows naturally into cost tracking and invoicing without duplicate data entry.

Progress Billing

Xerpium supports progress billing, allowing organizations to invoice based on completed milestones and recorded work rather than waiting until project completion. This keeps cash flow aligned with delivery and reduces the manual effort typically required to convert work into revenue.

A Base Module That Connects to Everything

A defining strength of the Xerpium Project Management application is that it functions as a base module within the ERP—unifying with all other modules across the suite. Project data is not trapped in a silo; it connects to the wider operational and financial picture, so information entered once serves the entire organization.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

For organizations operating across several entities, the module supports multi-company and multi-currency configurations. This makes it suitable for businesses managing projects across different legal entities and client contracts in various currencies. A built-in audit trail records changes throughout the project lifecycle, supporting accountability and compliance.

Real-Time Dashboards and Reporting

Xerpium provides real-time dashboards and reports, giving managers and stakeholders immediate access to project status, progress, and performance. Decisions are based on current information rather than reports assembled days after the fact.

Integration with the Xerpium AI Assistant

The Project Management module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This connection brings intelligent support into the project environment, helping teams interact with their data and work more efficiently within the same system they use to manage delivery.

Example Use Case: From Kickoff to Invoice

Consider a consulting firm managing a multi-phase engagement for a client. The project lead creates the project in Xerpium and defines tasks grouped under three milestones. As consultants complete work, they log their hours directly into the timesheet feature attached to each task.

When the first milestone is reached, the manager reviews progress through the real-time dashboard, confirming that tasks are complete and hours are recorded. Using progress billing, the firm issues an invoice for that milestone without re-entering data into a separate finance system—because the Project Management module is unified with the rest of the ERP.

Because the firm operates across two legal entities and bills in different currencies, the engagement runs smoothly under the module's multi-company and multi-currency configuration. Every change is captured in the audit trail, giving the finance team confidence during review. Throughout, the AI Assistant supports the team as they navigate project data, reducing the friction of day-to-day administration.
