# Streamlining Manufacturing Operations with Xerpium ERP’s Manufacturing (MRP) Module

Modern manufacturers face growing pressure to deliver accurate production plans, control costs, and maintain full traceability across increasingly complex operations. Disconnected spreadsheets, isolated planning tools, and manual work order tracking often create delays, inaccurate costing, and compliance risks. Xerpium ERP addresses these challenges with its Manufacturing (MRP) module, a base module that unifies bill of materials (BOM), routing, work order management, and production cost calculation. Because it is natively integrated with all other Xerpium modules, manufacturers gain real-time visibility and control without the friction of siloed systems.

Common Manufacturing Challenges

Many production teams recognize these recurring pain points:

  • BOM and routing data stored in separate files or legacy systems, leading to version errors and production inconsistencies.
  • Manual work order creation and tracking, which slows down shop floor execution and weakens audit readiness.
  • Difficulty calculating accurate production costs when multiple companies, currencies, or cost structures are involved.
  • Limited real-time visibility into work order status, resource usage, and production performance.
  • Weak audit trails that make it hard to trace changes, approvals, and historical production data.

These issues are not just operational annoyances. They directly affect profitability, compliance, and the ability to scale manufacturing across new entities or regions.

How Xerpium’s Manufacturing (MRP) Module Addresses These Challenges

Xerpium’s Manufacturing (MRP) module is designed as a base component of the ERP suite, meaning it works seamlessly with other Xerpium modules from the start. It supports multi-company and multi-currency environments, maintains a full audit trail, and provides dashboards and reports in real time. Below are the core elements included in the module.

Bill of Materials (BOM)

A clear and accurate BOM is the foundation of any production process. Xerpium allows manufacturers to define and manage the components, raw materials, and quantities required for each finished product. Because the BOM is maintained inside the ERP, it stays connected to inventory, purchasing, and production planning data. This integration helps reduce version conflicts and ensures that the right materials are available when work orders are released.

Routing

Routing defines the sequence of operations, work centers, or production steps needed to manufacture an item. With Xerpium, routing information is structured and linked directly to work orders and cost calculations. This gives production planners a reliable view of how a product moves through the shop floor, where bottlenecks may occur, and how labor or machine time is consumed.

Work Order Management

Work orders are the operational documents that drive production. Xerpium supports the creation and tracking of work orders within the Manufacturing (MRP) module. Because work orders are integrated with BOM, routing, inventory, and financial modules, each order carries the necessary material and operational context. This reduces manual data re-entry and makes it easier to track progress from release to completion.

Production Cost Calculation

Understanding what it truly costs to produce an item is essential for pricing, margin analysis, and financial control. Xerpium includes production cost calculation as part of the Manufacturing (MRP) module. By pulling data from BOM, routing, and related operations, the system supports a more structured approach to calculating production costs. With multi-currency support, manufacturers can evaluate costs consistently across different entities or locations.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium’s Manufacturing (MRP) module is built for organizations that operate across more than one legal entity. It supports multi-company and multi-currency scenarios, which is particularly valuable for manufacturers with plants, warehouses, or sales organizations in different regions. In addition, the module maintains an audit trail. Changes and transactions can be traced, supporting internal reviews, compliance requirements, and stronger governance.

Real-Time Dashboard and Reporting

Production managers need current information, not end-of-day summaries. Xerpium provides real-time dashboards and reports within its ERP environment. This means manufacturing teams can monitor work order status, production activity, and related operational data as it happens. Real-time visibility helps decision-makers identify exceptions early and respond before small issues become costly delays.

Xerpium AI Assistant Integration

The Manufacturing (MRP) module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This integration allows users to engage with the ERP in a more conversational and guided way. While the AI Assistant can help surface information and streamline interactions across the system, its value in manufacturing comes from reducing the time spent navigating menus or searching for data. Users can rely on the assistant as part of their daily ERP workflow, supported by the same real-time data foundation.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Manufacturing (MRP)

By consolidating BOM, routing, work order, and production cost data into one ERP platform, Xerpium delivers practical benefits for manufacturing teams:

  • Improved data consistency across production, inventory, and finance.
  • Faster work order processing with fewer manual handoffs.
  • Better cost visibility through integrated production cost calculation.
  • Stronger compliance posture with a complete audit trail.
  • Scalable operations across multiple companies and currencies.
  • Real-time insight through dashboards and reports.
  • A modern, AI-assisted user experience that simplifies ERP interaction.

These benefits are particularly relevant for mid-sized and growing manufacturers that need enterprise-grade control without operating separate point solutions for every production function.

Example Use Case: From Work Order to Cost Visibility

Consider a manufacturer that produces assembled products across two entities operating with different currencies. Before Xerpium, the production team maintained BOMs in spreadsheets, while the finance team calculated costs in a separate system. When a work order was released, planners had to manually confirm the latest BOM version and routing steps. Delays were common, and cost updates often lagged behind production.

With Xerpium’s Manufacturing (MRP) module, the BOM and routing are defined once and linked directly to work orders. When a work order is created, the system pulls the relevant production data. The production cost calculation uses the same integrated data, and because Xerpium supports multi-company and multi-currency environments, costs are viewed within the correct entity context. Dashboards provide real-time status updates, while the AI Assistant helps team members quickly retrieve order or cost information. The audit trail records changes, giving management confidence in the data behind every report.

Xerpium’s Manufacturing (MRP) module brings BOM, routing, work order management, and production cost calculation into one integrated ERP environment. With multi-company and multi-currency support, a full audit trail, real-time dashboards and reports, and the Xerpium AI Assistant, manufacturers can run more controlled, transparent, and responsive operations.
