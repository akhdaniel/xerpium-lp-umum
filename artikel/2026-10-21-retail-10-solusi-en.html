10 Best ERP Solutions for Retail Operations

October 21, 2026

Retail operations fail quietly when systems don’t connect. Cashiers take cash, QRIS, and e-wallets at multiple outlets while Shopee, Tokopedia, and GoFood update separate stock counts. Procurement, accounting, and commissions still run in spreadsheets. The result is stock drift, slow reconciliation, and decisions based on outdated reports. An ERP should connect these flows in one system. Below are 10 Xerpium retail solutions that address the most frequent operational pressure points in multi-outlet and multi-channel retail.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. POS Multi-Outlet — Run multi-cashier, multi-payment, offline-capable store transactions with real-time stock updates.
  2. Marketplace Shopee & Tokopedia — Sync products, stock, prices, and orders automatically across both marketplaces.
  3. GoBiz GoFood Integration — Convert GoFood webhooks into sale orders and keep menu, stock, and QRIS payments aligned.
  4. Inventory Management — Control multi-warehouse stock, lot/expiry, barcode workflows, and cycle counts.
  5. Sales Management — Standardize quotes, orders, invoices, pricing tiers, loyalty, and credit limits.
  6. Purchase & Procurement — Automate RFQ, PO, receipt, vendor bills, drop ship, and three-way matching.
  7. Accounting & Finance — Post to a retail-specific COA with PPN 11%, AR/AP, and bank reconciliation.
  8. HR & Payroll — Manage attendance, shifts, payroll, BPJS, PPh 21, and sales commissions.
  9. Dashboard Studio — Monitor KPI widgets, revenue trends, top products, and low-stock alerts.
  10. AI Assistant — Ask for sales, stock, invoices, and reports in natural language across 11 languages.

1. POS Multi-Outlet

POS Multi-Outlet gives retail stores a unified front-end for high-volume operations. It supports multi-kasir sessions with shift management and automatic closing, so each cashier is accountable. It accepts tunai, debit, credit, QRIS, e-wallet, and transfer, eliminating payment silos. Offline Mode processes transactions when connectivity drops and syncs in batch when online. Every transaction updates warehouse stock in real time, and Cash Control adds opening balance, cash in/out, and three-tier closing reconciliation. Discount and promo rules cover per item, per order, buy-1-get-1, and happy hour. This directly solves long queues, cash-counting disputes, and stock drift between front office and back office.

2. Marketplace Shopee & Tokopedia

Marketplace Shopee & Tokopedia centralizes multi-channel commerce in one dashboard. It syncs products, variants, images, descriptions, stock, and prices from XERPIUM to Shopee and Tokopedia, preventing catalog mismatches. Orders from marketplaces become sale orders automatically, and order management handles status updates and shipment tracking. Real-time stock sync across marketplace channels reduces overselling on flash sales and bundle deals, while promo management supports bundle deals, flash sales, and voucher integration. This solves the manual copy-paste workflow that typically causes price lag, missed order updates, and customer complaints across marketplaces.

3. GoBiz GoFood Integration

GoBiz GoFood Integration connects GoFood delivery operations to back-office ERP. OAuth 2.0 manages credentials in sandbox and production environments. Menu Sync pushes XERPIUM products, variants, prices, and stock to the GoFood menu, while Outlet Control lets teams open or close outlets and subscribe to eight webhook events. Auto Order converts gobiz.order webhooks into sale orders automatically. Staff can accept, mark ready, or reject orders via API, create QRIS transactions for GoFood payments, and run promo SKU pricing with refresh status. API logging keeps a full audit trail. This removes manual order re-entry and prevents menu or payment reconciliation gaps in food retail.

4. Inventory Management

Inventory Management gives retail teams multi-warehouse control across central stores, branches, consignment locations, and dropship operations. Stock movements include receipts, deliveries, internal transfers, and adjustments, with real-time stock value under FIFO or average valuation. Lot and serial tracking supports batch tracking, expiry date management, and traceability for regulated or perishable goods. Barcode scanning streamlines receiving, picking, packing, and shipping. Cycle count supports periodic stock opname and automatic variance capture. The operational problem solved is inventory accuracy: teams avoid selling expired stock, reduce dead stock, and keep every sales channel working from the same stock position.

5. Sales Management

Sales Management runs the quote-to-invoice flow across many channels. It creates sale orders from quotations, converts them to invoices, and supports multi-channel operations including website sales and customer portal. Customer management provides segmentation, order history, and loyalty points so repeat buyers are visible. Pricing uses price lists per customer tier and volume discounts, preventing inconsistent discounting. Credit limit is enforced automatically per customer. For retail operators, this solves the problem of messy price approvals and uncontrolled credit by keeping customer terms, pricing rules, and loyalty tracking inside one record, whether the sale originates at the counter, marketplace, or website.

6. Purchase & Procurement

Purchase & Procurement automates replenishment and supplier workflows. The process follows RFQ to PO, receipt, and vendor bill. Vendor management captures rating, payment terms, and lead time so buyers can compare suppliers. Auto Reorder uses reorder rules based on min/max stock to generate purchase orders before shelves run empty. Drop Ship supports direct delivery from supplier to customer when needed. Three-way match compares PO, receipt, and invoice before posting vendor bills, reducing overpayment and receipt errors. This solves stockouts, emergency purchases, and invoice disputes by making procurement a controlled, rule-driven process rather than an ad-hoc manual task.

7. Accounting & Finance

Accounting & Finance translates retail transactions into audit-ready records. It includes a chart of accounts with 113 retail-specific accounts and 32 account groups. PPN 11% handles output tax, input tax, and prepaid tax. AR and AP modules manage aging, dunning, and reconciliation, while bank and cash cover bank reconciliation, petty cash, and transfers. Fiscal positions provide retail exempt mapping for PPN, and multi-currency supports transactions with automatic exchange rates. The operational problem solved is financial close: every POS or marketplace transaction posts to the right account without manual journal entries, reducing month-end pressure and tax filing errors.

8. HR & Payroll

HR & Payroll supports the frontline retail workforce. Employee records manage NIK, BPJS, and NPWP data. Attendance tracks check-in/out, shift, and overtime, which is essential for multi-shift stores. Payroll handles gaji pokok, allowances, deductions, BPJS, and PPh 21. Commission calculates sales commissions per transaction or product, so sales staff are rewarded accurately. Leave management covers cuti, izin, and sickness through workflow approval. This solves common retail HR problems: shift disputes, manual commission spreadsheets, and payroll errors, by keeping attendance, payroll, and commission data connected to actual sales events.

9. Dashboard Studio

Dashboard Studio converts operational data into visual controls. It provides 14 KPI widgets — 8 tiles and 6 charts — for retail analytics. Revenue Trend plots 12 months of income in an area chart. Top Products uses a horizontal bar for the top 10 products. Revenue by Category uses a doughnut chart per category, and Low Stock Alert lists products with stock at or below 5. AI Generate can turn natural language prompts into a complete dashboard. The problem solved is decision speed: store managers and finance leads no longer wait for manual Excel reports to spot revenue shifts, category performance, or low-stock risks.

10. AI Assistant

AI Assistant makes the ERP accessible through conversation. It understands natural language requests such as “Berapa penjualan hari ini?” or “Buat invoice.” Data lookup finds products, checks stock, and shows customer history. Report Generation creates sales or profit/loss reports via chat, while Automated Tasks can create POS orders or post journal entries. The assistant exposes 100+ tools across sales, purchase, inventory, accounting, and HR, and supports 11 languages including Indonesian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Arabic. This reduces repetitive data entry and gives non-technical staff faster access to operational answers.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Start by mapping your highest-volume channels. If you run Shopee, Tokopedia, or GoFood, prioritize marketplace and GoFood integration before broader modules. Second, assess inventory complexity: multi-warehouse and lot/expiry tracking matter more for perishable or multi-branch retail than a single counter. Third, review financial compliance needs — PPN 11%, PSAK-style reporting, multi-currency, and marketplace settlement reconciliation should influence the accounting scope. Fourth, consider team adoption: Dashboard Studio and AI Assistant can reduce training time, but only if the underlying POS, inventory, and accounting modules are already configured. Choose the modules that solve the most frequent operational bottlenecks first.

Conclusion

Retail operations need one system of record from the counter to the general ledger. The ten solutions above — POS Multi-Outlet, marketplace and GoFood integrations, inventory, sales, purchasing, accounting, HR, dashboards, and AI Assistant — address the disconnected workflows that cause stock errors, slow reconciliation, and delayed reporting.
