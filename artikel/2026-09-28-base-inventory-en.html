# Inventory & Warehouse Management with Xerpium ERP: Multi-Warehouse Control, Lot/Serial Tracking, and Real-Time Visibility

September 28, 2026

Inventory and warehouse operations have become increasingly complex for many B2B organizations. Teams often manage stock across multiple locations, track lot and serial numbers, coordinate putaway and replenishment, and maintain accurate records for audit purposes. When these processes run through disconnected spreadsheets or separate tools, visibility suffers, stock accuracy declines, and day-to-day warehouse work becomes harder to control.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with its Inventory & Warehouse module. As a base module, it is designed to unify inventory and warehouse processes while integrating with all other Xerpium modules. It supports multi-warehouse operations, lot/serial tracking, putaway, replenishment, multi-company and multi-currency environments, audit trails, and real-time dashboards and reporting. It also works with the Xerpium AI Assistant.

This article looks at common inventory and warehouse challenges and how Xerpium ERP helps teams manage them more effectively.

Common Inventory & Warehouse Challenges

Many organizations face similar operational pain points when managing inventory and warehouse workflows:

  • Multi-warehouse complexity — Stock is spread across different facilities, making it difficult to know where inventory is located and when to move it.
  • Lot and serial tracking gaps — Without a dependable tracking process, it becomes harder to trace batches or individual items from receiving through storage and dispatch.
  • Inefficient putaway and replenishment — Poor placement of incoming goods and manual replenishment processes can lead to misplaced stock, delays, or stockouts.
  • Limited real-time visibility — Decisions are often based on outdated information because stock movements and warehouse activity are not reflected promptly.
  • Audit and compliance pressure — Organizations operating across multiple companies and currencies need clear, traceable records to maintain accountability and compliance.

How Xerpium ERP Inventory & Warehouse Works

Xerpium Inventory & Warehouse is a base module within Xerpium ERP. That means inventory and warehouse data is not isolated from the rest of the business; it is designed to connect with the broader ERP environment and all other modules.

Multi-Warehouse Management

The module supports multi-warehouse structures, allowing companies to manage inventory across more than one warehouse from a single system. Teams can maintain a clearer view of stock availability and movement across locations without relying on separate spreadsheets or disconnected processes.

Lot and Serial Tracking

Lot and serial tracking gives organizations a structured way to follow specific batches or individual units through the warehouse. This supports stronger traceability, quality control, and recall readiness. Inventory records remain connected to the relevant lot or serial information, helping teams build a more complete history of stock movement.

Putaway and Replenishment

Xerpium supports both putaway and replenishment as part of the warehouse workflow. Putaway helps direct incoming goods to appropriate storage locations. Replenishment helps maintain stock levels by prompting teams when a location or warehouse needs additional inventory, reducing the risk of avoidable stockouts.

Real-Time Dashboards and Reports

The module includes dashboards and real-time reports. Warehouse and inventory teams can monitor stock levels, movements, and operational status as they change. This reduces dependence on manually updated records and supports faster, more informed decision-making.

Multi-Company, Multi-Currency, and Audit Trail

Xerpium Inventory & Warehouse supports multi-company and multi-currency environments. This is especially relevant for organizations operating across multiple entities or regions. The built-in audit trail provides a record of changes and transactions, supporting internal accountability, compliance, and review processes.

AI Assistant Integration

The Inventory & Warehouse module is integrated with the Xerpium AI Assistant. This connection brings AI support into the inventory and warehouse environment, helping teams work more efficiently by having an assistant available within the same Xerpium system. It allows warehouse and inventory personnel to use AI support as part of their daily workflows without leaving the ERP environment.

A Practical Inventory & Warehouse Flow

The putaway functionality helps guide the team to the correct storage location. At the same time, warehouse B is running low on the same item.
