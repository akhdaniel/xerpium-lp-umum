Complete ERP solution for Electronic OEM Manufacturing: A Unified System for BOM, KITE, Export, and Quality

October 19, 2026

Electronic OEM manufacturers operate in a highly interconnected environment. They manage customer-specific product models, version-controlled BOMs, imported raw materials, AQL inspections, yield and downtime records, KITE duty exemption, export documentation, and a dedicated chart of accounts. When these processes run on separate spreadsheets or disconnected applications, data gets duplicated, BOM versions drift, landed costs become unclear, and export compliance becomes a manual burden. Xerpium ERP addresses this by connecting the entire electronic OEM lifecycle in one system—from product model and BOM to manufacturing, quality, export, finance, and AI-assisted operational queries.

Common Challenges in Electronic OEM Manufacturing

  • Version-controlled product data: Every OEM customer may have unique specifications, BOM versions, HS codes, FOB prices, and packaging details. Keeping draft, approved, and obsolete BOM versions aligned across sales, purchasing, and production is difficult without a single source of truth.
  • Trade compliance and duty exemption: KITE facility ratios, LC terms, import values versus export values, and landed cost calculations must be tracked accurately to avoid compliance risk and margin leakage.
  • Export documentation complexity: Export delivery orders, packing lists, PEB registration through CEISA, certificates of origin, and commercial invoices must stay consistent across container, vessel, port, incoterm, and product-level weight and volume data.
  • Quality and traceability pressure: Inbound AQL sampling, reject counts, supplier certificates, yield records, and component-to-finished-good lot traceability are difficult to maintain when quality data is not linked to manufacturing orders and customer POs.
  • Fragmented finance visibility: Revenue, COGS, PPN, PPh, depreciation of SMT/Assembly/QC machines, scrap, and export versus local sales need a chart of accounts built for electronic OEM workflows—not a generic accounting setup.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Product Model, BOM, and Partner Master Data

Xerpium manages product models with customer part numbers, HS codes, FOB prices in USD, weight, volume, and linked products. Each model supports version-controlled BOMs with draft, approved, and obsolete statuses. BOM lines capture component, quantity, scrap rate percentage, supplier, and notes. Customer specifications are documented per BOM version. On the partner side, the system distinguishes OEM customers by Korean HQ, Korean subsidiary, local, and export categories, while OEM suppliers can be Korean, local, Chinese, or Singapore. Credit limits, incoterms, ports, payment terms, supplier ratings, and certificates such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001, RoHS, REACH, UL, and CE are tracked with expiry dates.

Export Operations and Trade Finance

Export delivery orders capture container, vessel, port of loading and discharge, and incoterm. The export flow connects delivery order, packing list, PEB, certificate of origin, and export invoice. PEB supports registration through DJBC and CEISA integration. Packing lists record total packages, gross and net weight, and volume per product model. Certificates of origin cover Form E for ASEAN-China, AK for ASEAN-Korea, and non-ASEAN requirements. Trade finance tools handle KITE facility target ratios and periodic realization, LC sight/usance for import and export, TT/remittance status tracking, and landed cost calculation from FOB plus freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling.

Manufacturing Orders, Quality Control, and Traceability

Manufacturing orders extend with customer PO ID and line, product model, FOB price in USD, and target ship date. Work centers include SMT, Assembly, Injection Molding, Calibration, QC Inspection, and Packing. Shift management assigns start and end times per MO. Yield records capture input, output, scrap, and yield percentage per MO and shift. Downtime records log machine breakdown, setup change, material missing, quality issues, and power outages. Quality control supports inbound AQL sampling at levels 1.0, 2.5, and 4.0 with sample size and accept/reject decisions. Lot traceability links component lots to finished lots, MOs, and customer POs. Customer complaints record defect type, root cause, action, and status.

Import, Supply Chain, and Landed Cost

Import purchase orders support USD currency, incoterm, port, ETD/ETA, and LC number. Inbound inspection uses AQL sampling at goods receipt with pass/fail results. Supplier rating scores quality, delivery, and price on a 1–5 scale and calculates an average total score. Landed cost combines FOB, freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling. Supplier certificates for ISO, RoHS, REACH, UL, and CE are tracked with expiry and attachments.

Accounting, COA, and KPI Dashboard

Xerpium provides a 168-account chart of accounts designed for electronic OEM operations. Revenue accounts cover export thermostat and parts, local sales, scrap, and discounts. COGS includes raw material, imported raw material, direct labor, overhead, and variance. Expense accounts track selling costs such as freight and commission, administrative costs such as salary and BPJS, and depreciation for SMT, Assembly, and Calibration machines. Tax handling includes PPN 11%/12% and PPh 21/23/25/26. The KPI dashboard presents total revenue MTD, open customer POs, active product models, active BOMs, active work centers, OEM customers, OEM suppliers, HS codes, monthly revenue trends, and top customers by revenue.

AI Assistant Tools

Xerpium includes AI tools for electronic OEM workflows: electronic_product_model searches product models by code or name, electronic_supplier finds OEM suppliers by name or country, electronic_customer_po locates customer POs by reference or customer, electronic_suggest_hs_code suggests HS codes from description, and electronic_check_kite_ratio checks KITE ratio by facility number.

End-to-End Flow

One connected cycle illustrates the system. A customer PO arrives for a specific product model. The manufacturing order links to that customer PO and line, pulls the approved BOM version, and sets the target ship date. Procurement creates an import PO in USD with incoterm, port, ETD/ETA, and LC number. At goods receipt, inbound AQL inspection records sample size and pass/fail. Production runs through SMT, Assembly, and QC work centers with yield and downtime recorded per shift. Finished goods inherit lot traceability back to components and the customer PO. Export operations generate the delivery order, packing list, PEB through CEISA, certificate of origin, and export invoice. Finance posts export revenue and landed cost, updates KITE realization, and the KPI dashboard reflects revenue and open POs.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth for product models, BOM versions, customer specifications, and HS codes.
  • Clear visibility into KITE ratio, LC status, landed cost, and export documentation.
  • Stronger quality control with AQL inbound inspection, yield tracking, supplier ratings, and lot traceability.
  • Faster export execution with CEISA-integrated PEB and consistent packing and invoice data.
  • Accurate electronic OEM accounting with a dedicated chart of accounts, tax handling, and COGS tracking.
  • Faster operational answers through AI tools for product models, suppliers, customer POs, HS codes, and KITE ratio.

Implementation Steps

  1. Prepare master data: Register product models, BOM versions and lines, HS codes, OEM customers, OEM suppliers, supplier certificates, and work centers.
  2. Configure finance and trade parameters: Set up the 168-account COA, PPN/PPh treatments, currency USD, incoterms, KITE target ratios, LC terms, and landed cost components.
  3. Pilot on one product family or customer: Run a controlled pilot covering customer PO, manufacturing order, import PO, inbound AQL, production yield, export documents, and accounting postings.
  4. Roll out to other models and customers: Expand the connected flow across SMT, Assembly, Injection Molding, QC Inspection, and Packing while enabling AI assistant queries.
  5. Evaluate and improve: Monitor KPI dashboard metrics, actual versus target KITE ratio, supplier ratings, downtime records, and open customer POs to refine processes.
