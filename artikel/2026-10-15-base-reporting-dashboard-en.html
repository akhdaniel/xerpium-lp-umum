# Reporting & Dashboard in Xerpium ERP: Real-Time KPIs and Cross-Module Analytics for Smarter B2B Decisions

Published: 15 October 2026

Many B2B organizations still run on fragmented reporting. Finance exports data from one system, operations pulls from another, and leadership waits for a manually assembled spreadsheet before making decisions. The result is slow insight, inconsistent numbers, and limited confidence in the data behind critical choices.

Xerpium ERP addresses this with a Reporting & Dashboard module that works as a base module across the entire ERP suite. It delivers real-time KPIs, custom reports, and cross-module analytics in a unified view. Because the module supports multi-company, multi-currency, and audit trail requirements, it is built for organizations that need both operational visibility and financial control.

Common Reporting Challenges in B2B Operations

Before adopting a unified reporting approach, many teams face a familiar set of problems:

  • Data scattered across modules and systems – teams struggle to connect finance, sales, inventory, and operational data in one place.
  • Reporting lag – manual exports and spreadsheet consolidation delay decisions and create version-control issues.
  • Multi-entity and multi-currency complexity – consolidating reporting across companies and currencies introduces errors and consumes significant time.
  • Weak audit trails – when numbers change, teams often lack a clear, reliable record of what changed and why.
  • Static dashboards – reports that are not real-time or not integrated with other modules fail to support daily operational decisions.

Xerpium ERP is designed to reduce these frictions by making reporting a native part of the ERP rather than a separate add-on.

How Xerpium ERP Reporting & Dashboard Works

The Reporting & Dashboard module in Xerpium is a base module that integrates directly with all other Xerpium modules. This means reporting is not a detached business intelligence layer; it operates within the same environment as the transactions, master data, and workflows it reports on.

Real-Time KPI and Dashboard Monitoring

Xerpium provides real-time dashboards and reports, allowing teams to monitor KPIs as data changes. Instead of waiting for end-of-day or end-of-week updates, business users can see current performance across the areas they manage.

This real-time visibility supports faster responses to changing conditions, whether that means tracking sales performance, monitoring operational output, or reviewing financial metrics.

Custom Reports for Role-Specific Insights

The module supports custom reports, giving teams the flexibility to build views that match their specific responsibilities. A finance manager, operations lead, or executive can each work with reports tailored to the data they need, without being limited to a fixed set of templates.

Custom reporting helps reduce noise and keeps attention on the metrics that matter for a given function or decision.

Cross-Module Analytics

Because the Reporting & Dashboard module is embedded alongside Xerpium’s other modules, it enables analytics across modules. Users can combine data from different parts of the ERP to understand relationships that single-module reports would miss.

For example, a report could connect order data with fulfillment and invoicing data, creating a more complete view of business performance without manual reconciliation.

Multi-Company and Multi-Currency Support

Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard supports multi-company and multi-currency environments. This is particularly valuable for B2B organizations operating across entities, regions, or currencies.

Teams can consolidate reporting across multiple companies and currencies in one place, reducing the need to manage separate spreadsheets or manually convert figures for group-level reporting.

Audit Trail for Trusted Reporting

The module includes audit trail support, helping organizations maintain a reliable record of reporting data and changes. This matters for finance teams, compliance officers, and auditors who need to understand how figures were produced and how they have evolved.

With audit trail capabilities embedded in the reporting environment, teams can approach reporting with greater confidence and accountability.

AI Assistant Integration

Reporting & Dashboard is integrated with AI Assistant Xerpium. This connection allows users to bring AI assistance into their reporting workflows, supporting faster exploration of data and more interactive reporting experiences.

While the core reporting features provide real-time data and custom views, the AI Assistant integration extends how teams can work with that information inside Xerpium.

Business Benefits of Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard

Organizations using Xerpium’s reporting capabilities can expect several practical benefits:

  • Faster decision-making through real-time KPIs and dashboards that reflect current operational and financial data.
  • Lower manual reporting effort by replacing spreadsheet-based consolidation with unified, cross-module reporting.
  • Clearer multi-entity visibility through built-in multi-company and multi-currency support.
  • Stronger audit and compliance readiness with audit trail functionality connected to reporting.
  • More relevant reporting through custom reports tailored to different teams and roles.
  • Better contextual insight with AI Assistant Xerpium integrated into the reporting experience.

Example Use Case: Consolidated Regional Reporting

Consider a B2B company operating across several regional entities. Each entity manages its own orders, expenses, and local currency transactions. The leadership team needs a consolidated view of performance without waiting days for manual reports.

With Xerpium Reporting & Dashboard, the team can:

  1. View real-time dashboards that consolidate KPIs across companies and currencies.
  2. Build custom reports for regional revenue, cost, and margin analysis.
  3. Use cross-module analytics to connect sales, fulfillment, and financial data.
  4. Rely on the audit trail to verify how reported figures were calculated.

Because the module is a base component of Xerpium, this reporting setup works across the company’s existing ERP environment rather than requiring a separate reporting tool.

Conclusion

Reporting and dashboards are only useful when they are trusted, timely, and connected to the rest of the business. Xerpium ERP’s Reporting & Dashboard module brings real-time KPIs, custom reports, cross-module analytics, multi-company and multi-currency support, audit trail, and AI Assistant integration into one base module.
