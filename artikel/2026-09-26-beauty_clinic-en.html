# Beauty Clinic ERP: Streamline Patients, Treatments, Memberships, and Doctor Commissions with Xerpium

Running a beauty clinic involves more than delivering aesthetic treatments. Behind every appointment, there is patient medical history, multi-session treatment plans, membership packages, consumable stock, prescriptions, doctor commissions, and accurate invoicing. When these are managed across spreadsheets and disconnected tools, clinics face double data entry, missed package deductions, inventory shrinkage, and slow commission reconciliation. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a unified beauty clinic management system built on integrated modules for patient records, treatment catalogs, appointments, admissions, treatment charts, memberships, pharmacy, inventory, and accounting.

Common operational challenges in beauty clinics

  • Fragmented patient data: medical notes, allergies, visit counts, and treatment history are scattered, making it difficult to provide safe and personalised care.
  • Appointment and queue management issues: clinics lose time matching patient, treatment, doctor, and available time slots, while walk-in queues are handled manually.
  • Multi-session and membership complexity: packages with validity periods, remaining sessions, down payments, and automatic session deductions are hard to track accurately outside an ERP.
  • Manual doctor commission calculations: fixed fees or percentage commissions per treatment line must be calculated, approved, consolidated, and paid without errors.
  • Inventory and consumption blind spots: consumables, cosmetics, and medicine products used during visits are not always deducted from stock in real time or costed properly.

Xerpium ERP modules for beauty clinic operations

Patient Management

Xerpium treats each patient as a partner record with an is_patient flag and an auto-generated patient code. The profile stores date of birth, gender, blood group (A+/B+/AB+/O+), allergies, and medical notes, as well as emergency contact name and phone. A medical timeline brings together appointments, treatment charts, and prescriptions. The system also tracks visit count per patient for admission history.

Treatment Catalog

The treatment catalog stores each treatment’s name, code, description, duration in minutes, and price. Treatments can be grouped into categories: Consultation, Facial, Injectable, Laser/Device, Body, Hair, or Other. Each treatment can be linked to a product template for invoicing. For revenue sharing, clinics can define doctor commission per treatment as either a fixed amount or a percentage of the price. The active flag allows treatments to be disabled without deleting historical records.

Doctor & Therapist

Doctor records are partner records marked as is_doctor, with specialization and license number for practice. Each doctor can have a default consultation fee. Therapist support uses an is_therapist flag. Commission can be assigned per appointment or per treatment line and is calculated automatically.

Appointment & Queue

Appointments are calendar events linked to a patient, treatment, and doctor. The patient field filters to is_patient records, and the doctor field filters to is_doctor records. On admission, a queue number is assigned automatically. Calendar colors are category-based, so consultation, facial, injectable, and laser/device appointments can be visually distinguished. Appointment status follows Draft → Confirmed → In Progress → Done → Cancelled, and the system can send email confirmation automatically when an appointment is confirmed.

Admission / Visit

Each visit is a sale order with an is_beauty_admission flag. Admissions can be walk-in or generated from an appointment, and queue numbers are auto-sequenced. If no consultation order line exists, Xerpium can auto-add a consultation line. The visit records the responsible doctor per admission and per treatment line, links to the treatment chart and membership, allows prescription creation directly from the visit, and tracks product consumption of items used during that visit.

Treatment Chart

For multi-session treatments such as a 6x laser plan, Xerpium provides a treatment chart per patient linked to a treatment. It logs planned sessions and completed tracking. The total amount is calculated automatically as treatment price × planned sessions, with support for a down payment amount. Clinics can create an invoice per chart to accounting. Chart status moves through Draft → In Progress → Done → Cancelled, and each session can be linked to calendar event appointments.

Membership Package

Clinics can define packages with a name, treatment, total sessions, price, and validity days, linked to a service product for invoicing the package purchase. Each patient membership records start/end dates, remaining sessions, and state. When an appointment is done via sale order, the system can auto-deduct sessions. Membership status includes Active, Completed, Expired, and Cancelled.

Prescription & Pharmacy

Prescriptions capture patient, doctor, date, and valid until date. Each line includes product, quantity, dosage, and instructions, and can be linked to the appointment and sale order. Prescription products can be added to the admission sale order. Medicine preparation status follows Draft → Preparing → Ready → Dispensed, and preparation lines can be loaded automatically from the prescription. A manufactured flag marks compounding products.

Doctor Commission

Doctor commissions are created automatically from invoice posting. Calculation is based on the doctor assigned per treatment line, using either a fixed amount per treatment or a percentage of the price. Commissions can be consolidated by doctor into a vendor bill. Commission status follows Draft → Approved → Paid, and the system can set commission status to paid automatically when the related vendor bill is paid.

Product Consumption

Each visit can record consumption lines for consumable or storable products. The cost price is auto-filled from the product’s standard price, and subtotal is calculated as quantity × cost price. Stock is reduced in real time for consumable products. A product can be marked with an is_medicine flag to differentiate medicine products in the catalog.

Dashboard & Reporting

The clinic dashboard shows daily visits, treatment revenue per period, outstanding and paid doctor commissions, active memberships with session usage, and treatment popularity. For documentation, Xerpium supports a patient card PDF report, an HTML medical timeline per patient, invoice history per patient, and a prescription report for printing doctor prescriptions.

How Xerpium ERP connects beauty clinic workflows

Beauty ↔ Accounting

Treatment invoices flow through account.move. Doctor commission can generate vendor bills automatically, and membership package purchases are invoiced through the linked service product.

Beauty ↔ Calendar

Appointments are calendar events with category-based colors and email reminders.

Beauty ↔ Inventory

Product consumption is drawn from stock, medicine products are managed in the product catalog, and cosmetic stock is tracked.

Beauty ↔ Sale

Admissions are sale orders, treatment lines are sale order lines, and doctors are assigned per line for commission.

Beauty ↔ Partner

Patients are partner records with an is_patient flag, doctors are partner records with an is_doctor flag, and the patient medical timeline is stored on the partner record.

Beauty ↔ Product

Treatments connect to product templates, medicines are products with an is_medicine flag, and consumable products are tracked for stock and cost.

Business benefits of using Xerpium ERP for beauty clinics

  • One unified patient record with medical history, allergies, emergency contact, visit count, and timeline improves safety and service speed.
  • Accurate multi-session treatment charts and membership packages reduce revenue leakage from forgotten sessions or expired packages.
  • Appointment, queue, and admission workflows reduce manual coordination and help walk-in and scheduled patients move smoothly.
  • Automated doctor commission calculation and vendor bill creation remove manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
  • Real-time consumption and inventory deduction give clear cost visibility and prevent stock discrepancies.
  • A structured dashboard shows visits, revenue, commissions, membership usage, and treatment popularity for better operational decisions.

Example workflow: from laser appointment to paid commission

A patient books a laser treatment. Front office creates an appointment by selecting the patient from is_patient records, the laser treatment from the catalog, and an available doctor. The appointment is confirmed, and an email confirmation is sent. On the visit day, the system creates an admission sale order from the appointment, assigns a queue number, and auto-adds a consultation line if needed. After the service, the team records the laser session on the treatment chart and any product consumption such as numbing cream or consumables, which reduces stock in real time. If the patient is on a membership package, the session is deducted automatically when the appointment is done. The invoice is posted, doctor commission is generated automatically based on the treatment line, and the clinic dashboard updates daily visits, revenue, and outstanding commission.

This reduces manual work, improves accuracy, and gives clinic managers the visibility they need to run daily operations and support long-term growth.
