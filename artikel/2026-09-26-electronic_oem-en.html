# Electronic OEM Manufacturing: How Xerpium ERP Connects BOM, Export Compliance, and Shop Floor Control

Electronic OEM manufacturing is a balancing act between customer specifications, multi-level bills of materials, export documentation, trade facilities, and shop floor performance. A thermostat or electronic part may move from an approved BOM to an SMT line, through quality inspection, into export packing, and finally into a customs filing—all while margins depend on accurate FOB pricing, duty exemptions, and yield data. When these processes live in separate spreadsheets, errors become expensive. Xerpium ERP brings them into one enterprise suite with an AI assistant, built specifically for electronic OEM operations.

Common Challenges in Electronic OEM Manufacturing

Electronic OEM teams often face recurring pressure points:

  • BOM and specification control across draft, approved, and obsolete versions, with customer specs tied to each revision.
  • Export documentation alignment among delivery orders, packing lists, PEB filings, certificates of origin, and commercial invoices.
  • Trade facility compliance, especially KITE duty exemption ratios between import and export realization.
  • Shop floor visibility into yield, scrap, and downtime across SMT, assembly, injection molding, calibration, QC inspection, and packing.
  • Lot traceability and quality evidence linking component lots to finished lots, manufacturing orders, and customer POs.

Xerpium ERP addresses these challenges through dedicated OEM modules that share a common data backbone.

How Xerpium ERP Supports Electronic OEM Operations

Product Model & BOM

Xerpium manages product models with fields such as model code, customer part number, HS code, FOB price in USD, weight, volume, and linked products. Each BOM version is unique per model and version-controlled as draft, approved, or obsolete. BOM lines capture components, quantity, scrap rate percentage, supplier, and notes. Customer specifications are documented per BOM version, so engineering and production always reference the correct revision.

Customer & Supplier Management

The system classifies OEM customers by type—Korean HQ, Korean subsidiary, local, or export—with credit limit, incoterm, and port of loading or discharge. OEM suppliers can be Korean, local, Chinese, or Singaporean, with supplier codes, partner types, and payment terms. Supplier ratings score quality, delivery, and price on a 1–5 scale and calculate an average total. Supplier certificates such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001, RoHS, REACH, UL, and CE include expiry tracking and attachments, reducing compliance risk.

Export Operations

Xerpium covers the export document chain from delivery order to invoice. Export delivery orders record container, vessel, port of loading/discharge, and incoterm. PEB records include DJBC registration numbers and CEISA integration for submission. Packing lists track total packages, gross/net weight, and volume per product model. Certificates of origin support Form E for ASEAN-China, AK for ASEAN-Korea, and non-ASEAN requirements. Export invoices are issued in USD with FOB value and incoterm.

Trade & Finance

For manufacturers using KITE facilities, Xerpium tracks duty exemption with a target ratio of 1.5 and periodic realization. KITE realization compares import value against export value and actual ratio against target. The system also handles letters of credit—sight or usance, import or export—with issuance, expiry, and shipping dates, as well as TT/remittance transactions with bank references and status tracking. Landed cost is calculated from FOB plus freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling.

Manufacturing Orders

Manufacturing orders are linked directly to customer PO IDs and lines, product models, FOB price USD, and target ship dates. Work centers include SMT, Assembly, Injection Molding, Calibration, QC Inspection, and Packing. Shift management records start and end times and assigns work per MO. Yield records capture input quantity, output quantity, scrap quantity, and yield percentage per MO and shift. Downtime records log machine breakdowns, setup changes, material shortages, quality issues, and power outages. Work orders include cycle time targets, scrap budget percentages, and sign-off.

Quality Control

Inbound AQL inspection supports sampling levels 1.0, 2.5, and 4.0 with sample size and accept/reject results. Quality checks extend Xerpium’s manufacturing quality module with AQL, sample size, and reject count. Lot traceability links component lots to finished lots, manufacturing orders, and customer POs. Customer complaints record defect types—cosmetic, functional, dimension, missing, or packaging—plus root cause, corrective action, and status. Supplier certificate expiry tracking reinforces incoming material quality.

Import & Supply Chain

Import purchase orders support supplier POs in USD with incoterm, port, ETD/ETA, and LC number. Inbound inspections use AQL sampling when goods arrive and record pass/fail results. Supplier rating and certificate expiry apply consistently across import and local supply, while landed cost provides a true cost picture for imported raw materials and components.

Accounting & COA

Xerpium includes a 168-account chart of accounts designed for electronic OEM. Revenue accounts cover export of thermostats and parts, local sales, scrap, and discounts. COGS accounts include raw material, imported raw material, direct labor, overhead, and variance. Expense accounts capture selling costs such as freight and commission, and administrative costs including salary, BPJS, depreciation of SMT/Assembly/Calibration machines, and ISO, RoHS, and CE certifications. The balance sheet tracks current assets for raw, WIP, and finished inventory and fixed assets for production and inspection equipment. Tax support includes PPN 11%/12%, PPh 21/23/25/26/Final, and corporate tax.

KPI Dashboard

The dashboard surfaces key operational metrics: total revenue month-to-date, open customer POs, active product models, active BOMs, active work centers, OEM customers, OEM suppliers, and HS codes. A 12-month revenue line chart and a top five customers bar chart help leadership monitor trends and concentration risk.

AI Assistant Tools

Xerpium’s AI assistant includes purpose-built OEM tools. Teams can query electronic product models by code or name, search OEM suppliers by name or country, find customer POs by reference or customer, suggest HS codes from descriptions, and check KITE ratios by facility number. These tools work through natural language chat, reducing lookup time for daily operational questions.

Business Benefits

  • Stronger version control across BOMs and customer specifications.
  • Fewer export errors through a connected DO, packing list, PEB, certificate of origin, and invoice workflow.
  • Better trade compliance with KITE realization, LC, TT, and CEISA integration.
  • Improved margin visibility through landed cost and FOB pricing tied to manufacturing orders.
  • Clearer shop floor accountability with yield, scrap, downtime, and shift-level records.
  • Traceable quality from inbound AQL inspection and component lots to finished goods and customer POs.

Example Flow: Export Thermostat Order

A Korean HQ customer issues a purchase order for thermostats. The team selects the approved BOM version and attached customer specification. Xerpium creates a manufacturing order linked to the customer PO ID and line, with product model, FOB price USD, and target ship date. Production runs across SMT, Assembly, Calibration, and QC Inspection work centers, recording yield and any downtime. Inbound AQL inspection validates incoming components, and lot traceability links component lots to the finished lot and customer PO. When goods are ready, the export flow moves from delivery order to packing list, PEB with CEISA submission, certificate of origin, and commercial invoice. The finance team checks the KITE ratio through the AI assistant and records export revenue against the OEM-specific chart of accounts.

Conclusion

Electronic OEM manufacturing demands tight coordination between engineering, production, quality, trade, and finance. Xerpium ERP provides that coordination without disconnected spreadsheets. From version-controlled BOMs and customer specs to export compliance, KITE tracking, and shop floor yield, the system gives OEM electronics manufacturers the visibility and control they need to protect margins and meet customer requirements.
