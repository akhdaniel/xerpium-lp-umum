Complete ERP solution for Beauty Clinic: A Single Platform from Patient Records to Prescription, Inventory, and Doctor Commission

October 19, 2026

Beauty clinics often operate with separate tools for scheduling, patient files, treatment menus, inventory, prescriptions, and doctor commissions. Front-desk staff update calendars, nurses track consumables manually, finance teams recalculate commissions from spreadsheets, and patients may still need repeat registration at every visit. This fragmented approach creates duplicate data entry, missed billing lines, unclear membership usage, and slow reporting.

Xerpium ERP replaces that patchwork with one end-to-end Beauty Clinic solution. It connects patient management, treatment catalog, appointments, admissions, treatment charts, membership packages, prescriptions, product consumption, doctor commissions, accounting, and clinic dashboards in a unified operational flow.

Common challenges in beauty clinic operations

  • Patient clinical, billing, and visit history are spread across different systems.
  • Treatment pricing and doctor commissions are often maintained manually.
  • Membership packages are difficult to track by session, validity, and remaining balance.
  • Product consumption for medicines and consumables is not linked to each visit or stock movement.
  • Prescription preparation and dispensing status are often disconnected from patient billing and visit records.

Xerpium solution scope

Patient management and treatment catalog

Xerpium inherits the standard partner record for patients using an is_patient flag and auto-generated patient code. Each patient record includes date of birth, gender, blood group, allergies, medical notes, emergency contact name and phone, visit count, and a medical timeline showing appointments, treatment charts, and prescriptions.

The treatment catalog stores treatment name, code, description, duration in minutes, and price. Treatments can be grouped into categories such as Consultation, Facial, Injectable, Laser/Device, Body, Hair, and Other. Each treatment links to a service product for invoicing, supports doctor commission as a fixed amount or percentage of price, and has an active flag to disable discontinued treatments.

Scheduling, doctors, and therapists

Doctors are marked as partners with an is_doctor flag, specialization, license number, and default consultation fee. Therapists are supported through an is_therapist flag. Appointments inherit calendar events, so booking uses the familiar calendar interface while requiring the patient and treatment. The doctor field is filtered to doctor partners, and each appointment receives a queue number on admission. Calendar colors follow treatment category, and appointment status moves from Draft to Confirmed, In Progress, Done, or Cancelled. Email confirmation is sent automatically when the appointment is confirmed.

Admission, treatment chart, and membership packages

Admissions inherit the sale order with an is_beauty_admission flag. A visit can be walk-in or generated from an appointment. The admission automatically adds a consultation line if it is not already present, assigns a doctor per admission and per treatment line, and links to the treatment chart and membership. Prescriptions can be created directly from the visit, and product consumption is tracked per visit.

Treatment charts support multi-session plans such as a six-session laser package. Each chart is linked to a patient and treatment, calculates total amount as treatment price multiplied by planned sessions, tracks down payment, and can create invoices to accounting. Sessions inside the chart link to calendar events, and chart status moves from Draft to In Progress, Done, or Cancelled.

Membership packages define the package name, treatment, total sessions, price, and validity days. Each patient membership stores start date, end date, remaining sessions, and status such as Active, Completed, Expired, or Cancelled. When a sale order appointment is completed, sessions are deducted automatically.

Prescription, pharmacy, and product consumption

Prescriptions capture the patient, doctor, date, and validity period. Prescription lines include product, quantity, dosage, and instructions, and can be linked to the appointment and sale order. Prescription products can be added to the admission sale order. Medicine preparation follows the status Draft, Preparing, Ready, and Dispensed, with prescription lines loaded automatically when preparation is created. Compounding is supported through an is_manufactured flag.

Product consumption tracks consumable or storable products used during a visit. The cost price is auto-filled from the product standard price, subtotal is calculated as quantity multiplied by cost price, and inventory is reduced in real time. Medicine products are flagged with is_medicine on the product template.

Doctor commission and financial integration

Doctor commissions are created automatically from invoice posting through the _post() hook. Commissions can be calculated as a fixed amount or percentage of treatment price and are assigned based on the doctor on each treatment line. Xerpium consolidates commissions by doctor and creates a vendor bill for payment. Commission status moves from Draft to Approved to Paid, and the commission is automatically marked paid when the vendor bill is paid.

On the accounting side, treatment invoices are posted through account moves, membership package purchases are invoiced, and doctor commissions become vendor bills automatically. Calendar records appointments as events with category-based colors and email reminders. Inventory connects product consumption to stock. Sale orders become admissions with treatment lines and per-line doctor assignments. Partner records become patients or doctors with the relevant flags and medical timeline. Product templates connect treatments, medicines, and consumable products to operational and financial processes.

Clinic dashboard and patient reports

The Clinic Dashboard gives management a daily view of visits, treatment revenue by period, outstanding and paid doctor commissions, active memberships, session usage, and the most popular treatments. Patient-level reporting includes a PDF Patient Card with identity and history, an HTML medical timeline, invoice history, and prescription reports for printing.

End-to-end flow

A patient registers as a partner with the patient flag and completes biodata such as allergies and emergency contact. Front desk creates a consultation appointment from the calendar, selects the treatment, assigns a doctor, and confirms the booking. The patient receives an email confirmation automatically.

When the patient arrives, the admission is created as a flagged sale order from the appointment or as a walk-in. The consultation line is added automatically, the doctor is assigned, and the selected treatment appears as a sale order line. If the patient is using a membership package, the completed appointment deducts the session. The doctor writes a prescription during the visit, and prescription products are added to the admission sale order.

Nursing or pharmacy prepares the medicine, moving preparation from Draft to Preparing, Ready, and Dispensed. Any consumable or medicine used is recorded as a product consumption line, costed automatically, and deducted from inventory. The treatment and prescription products are invoiced to accounting. Once the invoice is posted, doctor commission is created automatically, consolidated into a vendor bill, and marked paid when the bill is settled. The Clinic Dashboard updates with daily visits, revenue, commission status, membership usage, and treatment popularity.

Business benefits

  • Single patient timeline for appointments, treatment charts, prescriptions, and invoices.
  • Cleaner scheduling with queue numbers, doctor filtering, category colors, and email reminders.
  • Controlled membership packages with automatic session deduction and expiration tracking.
  • Accurate treatment revenue and doctor commission recognition linked directly to invoice posting.
  • Real-time inventory consumption for medicines and consumable products.
  • Connected prescription preparation and dispensing status for pharmacy visibility.
  • Management visibility through daily visits, revenue, commission, and treatment popularity dashboards.

Implementation steps

  1. Prepare master data — Set up patients, doctors, and therapists as partners with the correct flags. Build the treatment catalog, service products, membership packages, and medicine or consumable products with the proper flags.
  2. Configure accounting and parameters — Define accounts for treatment invoices, vendor bills for doctor commissions, product cost prices, and automatic sequences such as patient codes and queue numbers.
  3. Run a pilot — Select one clinic area or team and complete appointment, admission, treatment chart, prescription, product consumption, invoicing, and commission cycles.
  4. Roll out in phases — Train front desk, nursing, doctors, pharmacy, and finance teams. Monitor the Clinic Dashboard daily and adjust treatment catalogs or workflows before expanding.
  5. Evaluate and stabilize — Review daily visits, revenue, outstanding commissions, membership usage, and treatment popularity. Use those insights to refine pricing, packages, and operational rules.
