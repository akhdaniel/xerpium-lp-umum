# Event Organizer ERP: Unified Venue, Budget, Vendor, and Guest Operations with Xerpium

Event organizers operate in a high-pressure environment where venue availability, vendor reliability, budget accuracy, and guest experience must align on a single event date. Many firms still manage event types, venue bookings, checklists, expenses, and invoicing across spreadsheets and disconnected tools. Xerpium ERP provides a dedicated event organizer suite—from eo.event.type and eo.venue to digital invitations, QR check-in, and event-level profitability—so teams can plan, execute, and account for every event in one system.

Common challenges in event organizer operations

  • Venue double booking and capacity or facility mismatches are difficult to detect manually.
  • Vendor quality, active status, contract periods, and event history are scattered across spreadsheets.
  • Budget versus actual expense is tracked too late, making profit margin unclear during execution.
  • Timeline and checklist progress depends on individual updates, with no single operational view.
  • Sales orders and invoices are disconnected from event execution, delaying billing and revenue recognition.

Xerpium modules that solve these challenges

Event master data: eo.event.type, eo.venue, and eo.vendor

Xerpium starts with structured event master data. eo.event.type defines event types such as Wedding, Birthday, Corporate, Concert, Exhibition, Seminar, Gala Dinner, and Festival. Each type has a unique code, base price, default duration, and a computed event count for tracking volume by category.

eo.venue stores venue facilities as booleans—sound system, lighting, AC, parking, WiFi, projector—alongside hourly and daily pricing, capacity, address, city, province, country, GPS coordinates, contact person, phone, email, and a unique venue code. Critically, venue conflict detection checks for double booking during event creation.

eo.vendor manages partners by category, including Catering, Decoration, Sound System, Lighting, Photography, Security, Transport, MC, Entertainment, and Florist. Each vendor can have a 1–5 rating, price per event, contract start and end dates, active status, a linked res.partner for financial integration, and a computed event count.

Client and team records: eo.client and eo.team

Client records support Individual, Corporate, and Government types. Xerpium tracks company name, Tax ID, client rating, event count, and computed total spending. Team members are managed in eo.team with roles and per-event assignment, while both clients and teams link to res.partner for invoicing and financial integration.

Core event execution: eo.event

The central eo.event record connects every operational piece. It captures event name, client, event type, venue, project manager, start and end datetimes, actual start and end tracking, guest count, invitation count, and computed attendance count.

Financial fields include total budget, total expense, profit, and profit margin percentage—all computed. Sales integration links the event to sale_order_id and one2many invoice_ids from account.move. Vendor and team assignments are many2many, and a constraint checks for venue double booking based on venue and date.

Timeline and checklist control: eo.timeline and eo.checklist

eo.timeline structures the D-day schedule from preparation to completion. Each timeline item has start and end datetime, sequence, an assigned eo.team member, and a status of Pending, In Progress, Done, or Blocked.

eo.checklist organizes operational readiness by category—Venue, Catering, Decoration, Sound, Lighting, Photography, Entertainment, Transport, and Guest Management. Each item has low, medium, or high priority, a due date, responsible team member, and a boolean is_done flag. Checklist progress is computed per event automatically.

Contracts, sales, and payment schedules

eo.contract holds client contact, event type, date, base price plus add-ons, discount, total amount, and payment terms including DP percentage, installment count, and due dates. A sales order can be auto-created from the event using action_create_sale_order, with invoice IDs linked from the sales order.

eo.payment manages payment stages such as DP 50% and Pelunasan 50%, fully customizable. Each payment term carries a due date, amount, currency, invoice link to account.move, and status tracking for Pending, Partial, Paid, and Overdue.

Expense control and profitability per event

eo.expense tracks expense items by categories including Venue, Catering, Decoration, Sound, Lighting, Photography, Entertainment, Transport, and Talent. Each expense links to a budget line for variance tracking, a vendor or partner, receipt upload with filename, and an approval flow from Draft to Approved, Paid, or Rejected. Accepted expenses can link to a journal entry.

Profitability per event is calculated from the sum of budget line budget amounts and actual amounts. Profit equals total budget minus total expense, profit margin is profit divided by total budget multiplied by 100, and amount total is the sum of invoices linked to the event.

Digital invitations and guest management

eo.invitation auto-generates invitation numbers in the format INV/2026/00001. Invitation types include VIP, Regular, Family, Media, and Sponsor. Each invitation receives a QR code generated with the Python qrcode library and PIL/Pillow, using UUID v4 tokens for unpredictability. QR settings include error correction level H for 30% recovery and a box size of 10 pixels per module. The public check-in URL follows /eo/checkin/{token}.

Guest management in eo.guest records name, email, phone, company name, category, dietary preferences, notes, total events attended, and total invitations. Teams can run a bulk invitation wizard to select guests and send mass email, and print invitation cards through a QWeb report. Check-in captures IP address, user, datetime, and plus-one status for accurate attendance.

AI dashboard and cross-module integration

Xerpium includes a ready event organizer dashboard with 14 KPI cards under vit_dashboard_event_organizer. AI dashboard features allow users to auto-generate a full dashboard with the command “Buat dashboard event organizer,” convert natural language questions such as “Revenue bulan ini” into real-time SQL, receive chart insights on booking trends and peak season, and use a Copilot chat with SSE-streamed dashboard context. Conditional formatting highlights overdue invoices in red and completed items in green.

Event organizer operations also connect natively to accounting, inventory, CRM and sales, AI assistant, calendar, HR, and dynamic stage workflows. Accounting supports invoice per event, PPN 11% calculation, deferred revenue for event DP, expense matching as COGS per event, profit and loss per event, multi-currency transactions, and analytic accounts as cost centers.

Business benefits

  • Clear event profitability from total budget, total expense, and computed profit margin per event.
  • Reduced double-booking risk through venue conflict detection and unique venue codes.
  • Faster billing with auto-created sales orders, invoices, and PPN 11% calculation.
  • Stronger vendor and team accountability through ratings, assignments, timelines, and approval flows.
  • Professional guest experience with QR invitations, plus-one tracking, seating, RSVP, and public check-in.
  • Real-time financial and operational visibility in a 14-KPI event organizer dashboard.

Example use case: corporate gala

A corporate gala is created in eo.event using the Corporate event type with its default base price and duration. The team selects a venue and immediately checks for conflicts with the venue constraint. Catering, sound system, lighting, and security vendors are assigned based on active status and rating. The planning team builds a eo.timeline and eo.checklist with responsible team members and due dates. Invitations are generated for VIP, Regular, Media, and Sponsor guests with QR codes. On event day, guests check in through the public /eo/checkin/{token} URL, and attendance is captured automatically.

After the event, the contract creates a sales order, invoices are issued with DP and final payment terms, PPN 11% is calculated, and actual expenses are matched against budget lines. The dashboard shows revenue, margin, overdue payments, and event-level profit without manual spreadsheet work.

Conclusion

Xerpium gives event organizers a single control layer that connects venue, vendor, budget, timeline, guest, and accounting data. Instead of managing disconnected spreadsheets, teams can run events with clear visibility into profit margin, payment status, and operational progress—from first quotation to final check-in.
