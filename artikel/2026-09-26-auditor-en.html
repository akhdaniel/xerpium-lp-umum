How Audit & Tax Consultants Can Manage Engagements End-to-End with Xerpium ERP

September 26, 2026

# How Audit & Tax Consultants Can Manage Engagements End-to-End with Xerpium ERP

Audit and tax consultants operate in a high-pressure environment: multiple engagements per client per year, strict documentation standards, partner review obligations, and constant fee pressure. Without a central system, teams often juggle spreadsheets, shared drives, and separate billing tools. Xerpium ERP is an enterprise ERP suite with an AI assistant that brings engagement management, working papers, time tracking, billing, and compliance into one platform.

Common Challenges in Audit and Tax Firms

  • Engagement scope, timeline, and budget are scattered across files, making it difficult to see progress from drafting through planning, execution, review, and completion.
  • Working papers and evidence live in separate folders, so partner and director review cycles are hard to track.
  • Timesheets are not consistently connected to billable hours or internal cost rates, delaying invoices and obscuring profitability.
  • Compliance checklists for standards such as ISA, SAP, PPh, and UU Perpajakan are maintained manually, increasing risk.
  • Limited visibility into engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff makes firm-wide planning difficult.

Xerpium ERP Modules for Audit and Tax Consultants

Engagement Management

Xerpium structures each audit, tax, or advisory service as a project—per client, per year—and moves it through a clear workflow: draft → planning → execute → review → complete. Teams define scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, and team assignment. A client portal allows clients to upload documents, see progress, and download reports. The system supports multiple engagement types—audit, tax, advisory, due diligence, and forensic—in one place. Budget tracking per engagement compares estimate versus actual cost and shows profit margin.

Working Paper & Documentation

The working paper module organizes audit documentation by area, including income tax, balance sheet, and internal control. Staff can upload supporting evidence such as documents, photos, email, and notes. Standard audit checklists and compliance checks support structured sign-off. Partners or directors can review each working paper and approve or reject it. Findings are recorded with severity, recommendations, and follow-up status, creating a clear audit trail.

Time Tracking & Billing

Timesheets log hours per engagement, per task, and per staff. Billable hours are calculated automatically based on rate per staff level—partner, senior, or staff. A separate cost rate tracks internal cost per hour for profitability analysis. Invoices can be auto-generated from timesheets, and milestone billing is also supported. Fee proposals allow firms to quote fees before an engagement begins, keeping commercial terms structured from the start.

Compliance & Reporting

Compliance Check provides checklists per regulation such as ISA, SAP, PPh, and UU Perpajakan. The report structure includes 179 accounts specific to Indonesian audit firms, supporting balance sheet and profit-and-loss reporting. Turbo Report can produce client financial statements in under one minute. Dashboards show engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff. AI Advisory delivers recommendations for computerization and tax planning through natural language.

Connected Modules: Accounting, Project, HR, and Dashboard

Xerpium connects auditor operations to accounting, project management, HR, and dashboards. The 179-account chart of accounts for audit firms supports financial statements, while invoices originate from timesheets and milestones. Journals handle fee and cost allocation. Each engagement functions as a project, and each task functions as a working paper. Timesheets follow tasks, budget tracking is embedded, and review and approval workflows remain part of the system. HR records staff levels and rates, including partner, senior, and staff levels, and supports performance review per staff. Dashboards can be auto-generated by AI for faster insight.

Business Benefits

  • One system from fee proposal to final report reduces handoffs and manual reconciliation.
  • Clear engagement visibility through scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, and team assignment.
  • Stronger documentation with evidence uploads, checklists, sign-off, and structured findings.
  • Fewer billing leaks with auto-generated invoices from timesheets and milestone billing.
  • Better profitability insight through billable hours, cost rate, estimate vs actual cost, and profit margin.
  • Faster compliance and reporting with regulatory checklists and Turbo Report.
  • Improved client transparency through the client portal.

Example Workflow

A tax consultant creates a new tax advisory engagement for a client. The team sets scope, deliverables, timeline, budget, and assigns staff at the appropriate level. Staff log time per task, upload evidence, and complete checklist items. A senior reviews the working papers, and a partner approves or rejects them. Any findings are recorded with severity and follow-up status. Invoices are generated from approved timesheets and milestones. The dashboard shows engagement status, billable hours, and revenue per staff, while the client portal lets the client upload documents and download the final report.
