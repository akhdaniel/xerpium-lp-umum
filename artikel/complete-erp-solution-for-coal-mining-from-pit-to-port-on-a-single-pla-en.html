Complete ERP solution for Coal Mining: From Pit to Port on a Single Platform

November 4, 2026

Coal mining operations carry an unusual data burden. A single tonne of coal travels from a geological seam estimate, through a production order on a specific shift, into a stockpile with its own weighted-average quality, out through a delivery order and quality certificate, and finally into an export document set, an invoice, and a royalty line in a government report. Along the way it touches heavy equipment hours, fuel consumption, operator assignments, lab analysis, HSE records, and analytic cost centres per block.

Most mining companies still manage these stages in disconnected systems: spreadsheets for daily production, a separate lab logbook, a standalone accounting package, and manual compilation for ESDM reporting. The result is predictable — production tonnage that does not reconcile with stockpile balances, quality figures that differ between the lab and the delivery certificate, and month-end closing that depends on one person who knows where all the files are. Xerpium ERP addresses this by modelling the entire coal mining cycle natively, so each record is created once and reused downstream.

Common Challenges in Coal Mining

  • Fragmented production data. Daily extraction is recorded per shift and per equipment unit, but without a structured production order the link between block, seam, operator, fuel usage, and output tonnage is lost.
  • Quality inconsistency across the chain. Calorific value, ash, moisture, and sulfur are measured at the mine face, at the stockpile, and at delivery. When these are tracked separately, disputes with buyers are difficult to resolve.
  • Equipment cost visibility. Excavators, dump trucks, and drill rigs accumulate operating hours, fuel, and maintenance costs that rarely roll up into a per-site or per-block cost picture.
  • Compliance and reporting pressure. Production, sales, environmental, royalty, and export reports to regulators require consistent period data pulled from several operational areas.
  • Export and trade complexity. Shipments involve vessels, PEB submission, certificates of origin, letters of credit, and USD/IDR exposure — each handled by a different team, often with re-keyed data.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Mine Structure and Reserve Data

The foundation is the site and block hierarchy. coal.mine.site holds the site code, GPS coordinates, total area, province and district, plus permit management with permit number, type (IUP/IUPK/IPR), issue and expiry date, and computed days remaining. Site states — Active, Suspended, Closed — follow workflow actions.

Each site contains coal.mine.block records with block code, area, depth, mining type (Open Pit, Underground, Alluvial), geological zone, estimated reserve in tonnes, estimated CV, and a linked seam. Blocks progress through Exploration → Development → Production → Depleted. coal.seam stores seam thickness, depth from/to, and typical quality parameters (CV, ash, moisture, volatile matter, sulfur) used as reference data for block estimation and production quality.

Production Execution

coal.production.order is the mining MO, auto-sequenced as CPO/2026/00001. It links site, block in production, and seam, requires a shift and production date, and records planned versus actual tonnage with computed achievement percentage. Quality at extraction — actual CV, ash, and moisture — is captured directly on the order. coal.production.line breaks the order into equipment-level detail: linked coal.equipment, operator from HR, start/end time with computed duration, tonnes extracted, and fuel litres. Total fuel on the order is computed from these lines.

coal.cost.center provides an analytic account per site or block with budget tracking, linked to the production order, while coal.shift handles Day, Night, and Evening shifts for 24/7 operations.

Stockpile and Equipment

coal.stockpile tracks maximum capacity and current tonnage, with weighted-average CV, ash, and moisture. When a production order is set to done, the _add_tons method automatically updates stockpile tonnage and recalculates weighted quality. In and out movements are tracked the same way.

coal.equipment maintains a registry of excavators, dump trucks, loaders, bulldozers, graders, drill rigs, and compactors — asset code, brand, model, year, plate, serial and engine numbers, status (Active, Under Maintenance, Idle, Retired), current hours, purchase and residual value, insurance, plus maintenance schedule and history with computed total cost, total fuel, and total operating hours.

Quality, Lab, and Certification

coal.sample is auto-sequenced (CS/2026/00001) with source — mine face, stockpile, delivery, rehandle — and type: chip, grab, channel, or composite. coal.lab.result records proximate analysis (total moisture, ash, volatile matter, fixed carbon), ultimate analysis (carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen, sulfur), and gross/net calorific value, with an assigned analyst and Draft → Done → Verified states. coal.quality.certificate then issues a certificate with grade classification (A/B/C, Sub-bituminous, Lignite) based on CV, linked to the sample and to a customer for delivery.

Sales, Delivery, and Export

coal.sale.order holds customer contracts, pricing per tonne, quality specs, ordered versus delivered tonnage, and invoice integration via account.move. coal.delivery.order carries truck number, driver, vessel for barging, gross/tare/net weight, quality at delivery, dispatch and delivery dates. coal.vessel registers barge name, capacity, and dimensions.

For export, the chain extends to export DO (container, vessel, port of loading and discharge, incoterm), packing list, PEB with DJBC registration number and CEISA integration, certificate of origin (Form D ASEAN, Non-ASEAN, preferential tariff), and commercial invoice in USD with FOB value.

HSE, Compliance, and Trade Finance

coal.hse.incident covers eight incident types with severity levels, root cause analysis, corrective and preventive actions required before closing, lost-time days, witnesses, and photo attachments. coal.safety.inspection supports daily, weekly, and monthly checklists with auto-computed score, plus permit to work for hot work, confined space, working at height, and excavation.

coal.govt.report generates production, sales/PLN, environmental, royalty, and export reports per period and site, auto-computing total production, delivery tonnage, revenue, and average quality through _generate_report_json(). coal.royalty.rate configures rates per coal type with effective dates, feeding royalty calculation.

Trade finance adds LC (sight/usance, import/export), TT/remittance, landed cost, USD/IDR forex tracking with realized versus booking gain/loss, and bank reconciliation that auto-matches payments to LC or TT references.

AI Assistant and Dashboards

The vit_dashboard_coal_mining dashboard provides end-to-end KPIs, with Dashboard Studio offering 19 chart types. AI features auto-generate dashboards from a prompt, convert natural language to SQL, produce chart insights on production trends or CV decline, and support copilot chat with mine dashboard context.

End-to-End Flow

A dump truck fleet extracts from Block B-02 on the night shift. The production order records 3,200 tonnes across several production lines, each with equipment, operator, duration, and fuel litres, plus actual CV and ash at extraction. On confirmation, the stockpile receives the tonnage and recalculates its weighted-average quality, and the production journal posts against the block's analytic cost centre.

A composite sample is collected from the stockpile, analysed in the lab, and verified. A quality certificate is issued with grade classification and linked to the customer. The sale order triggers a delivery order with vessel and net weight; once delivered, revenue is recognised. For export shipments, the DO flows into packing list, PEB via CEISA, certificate of origin, and USD invoice settled under an LC. At period end, the government report aggregates production by block, sales by customer and vessel, incident counts by severity, and royalty computed from production multiplied by the applicable rate.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth from seam to shipment, with no re-keying between production, quality, and delivery.
  • Quality traceability — extraction CV, stockpile weighted average, lab result, and delivery certificate all reference the same records.
  • Cost visibility per site and block through analytic cost centres, equipment hours, fuel, and maintenance totals.
  • Faster regulatory reporting, with production, sales, environmental, and royalty data generated from operational transactions.
  • Controlled export and trade finance workflow covering PEB, CoO, LC/TT, landed cost, and forex exposure.
  • HSE discipline enforced by workflow — corrective and preventive actions required before an incident can be closed.

Implementation Steps

  1. Data preparation. Compile site master data with permits and coordinates, block definitions with reserves and mining type, seam quality parameters, equipment registry, stockpile capacities, and employee records for operators, HSE officers, and lab analysts.
  2. Chart of accounts and parameters. Configure the mining COA, analytic cost centres per site and block, shift definitions, royalty rates with effective dates, quality grade thresholds, and sequence formats for production orders, samples, and delivery orders.
  3. Pilot on one site. Run a single active site through a full cycle — production order, stockpile update, sampling, lab result, certificate, delivery, and journal posting — and validate that computed fields and auto-journals match manual figures.
  4. Rollout across sites and functions. Extend to remaining sites under multi-company configuration, then activate HSE, export documentation, trade finance, and government reporting. Configure dynamic stages where production, quality, or delivery workflows need company-specific steps.
  5. Evaluation and optimisation. Review dashboard KPIs and AI insights after the first full reporting period, tune cost centre budgets and inspection checklists, and adjust workflows based on actual operational practice.
