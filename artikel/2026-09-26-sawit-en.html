# Palm Oil Industry ERP: Managing Plantation, Mill, and Trade Operations with Xerpium

Palm oil operations run across a long and tightly connected chain: plantation blocks and afdeling, harvest and TBS transport, CPO mill processing, quality control, inventory, accounting, export documentation, and trade finance. When these functions operate in disconnected spreadsheets or generic ERP systems, plantation teams struggle to see real cost per kg TBS, mill teams cannot trace losses by processing stage, and finance teams cannot produce sawit-specific reports without manual rework.

Xerpium ERP provides an integrated palm oil industry solution with modules for land block and afdeling, TBS production, CPO mill management, quality control and lab, sawit-specific chart of accounts, dashboards, inventory, sales, export, and trade finance. This article outlines the common operational challenges and how Xerpium addresses them.

Common Challenges in Palm Oil Operations

  • Land and harvest data are scattered across blocks and afdeling, making maturity tracking, production estimates, and cost per kg TBS unreliable.
  • Mill processing without integrated TBS reception, grade check, OER/KER, and stage-wise loss tracking limits yield optimization.
  • Quality control is disconnected from lab results and certification traceability such as ISPO and RSPO.
  • Generic accounting charts force manual mapping of palm oil revenues, COGS, taxes, and plasma farmer TBS purchases.
  • Export and trade finance are managed outside the core system, creating gaps in PEB/CEISA, LC/TT, landed cost, and forex tracking.

Xerpium ERP: Palm Oil-Specific Modules

Land Block & Afdeling

Xerpium manages plantation areas in planting blocks, including land area in hectares, planting status, planting year, and afdeling grouping. It records maturity status for immature or TBM and mature or TM plants, variety per block such as DxP, and population density per hectare. The system also keeps block-level history for fertilization, maintenance, and harvest. This creates a dependable foundation for upstream planning and cost allocation.

Harvest & TBS Production

The module supports harvest schedules by block with 7/10/14-day rotation, FFB collection recording per block, weight per bunch, per employee, and per block, and quality grade classification for unripe, ripe, and overripe fruit. It also tracks TBS transport to the mill by lorry or truck, monthly or block-level production estimates, and cost per kg for harvest and transport. This links field output directly to mill intake and cost accounting.

CPO Mill Management

For the mill, Xerpium covers TBS reception at the weighbridge, quality control through sorting and grading, and processing stages including sterilizer, thresher, press, clarifier, and kernel plant. It records CPO output, palm kernel output, OER calculated as CPO/TBS × 100, KER or kernel extraction rate, and losses per processing stage. This gives mill managers real-time visibility into yield and loss points.

Quality Control & Lab

Xerpium records FFB quality before processing; CPO quality parameters such as FFA, moisture, dirt, DOBI, and iodine value; kernel quality including moisture, impurities, and broken kernel; and water quality for process water. Lab results are stored with certificate support for ISPO, RSPO, and traceability, and specification limits per parameter are maintained. This connects quality control to operations and compliance.

Sawit Chart of Accounts & Financial Report Structure

Xerpium includes a palm oil-specific chart of accounts template with a SAW prefix and 6-digit account codes. It covers assets such as saw_110100 cash/bank, saw_110900 transfer, and saw_120100 receivables; liabilities such as saw_210100 payables, accruals, and taxes payable; revenue such as saw_410100 revenue and saw_410200 sales discounts; COGS such as saw_510100 cost of goods sold by products and processes; and tax mappings for PPN 11% through tax_sale_11 and tax_purchase_11.

The financial report structure uses vit.report_structure, account groups, auto-sync with _sync_sawit_report_account_groups(), XML data from vit_report_structure_sawit.xml, prefix mapping, and post-load hooks to sync account groups after the template is applied. It is compatible with vit_turbo_report_inherit for reporting. This means financial statements match the real structure of palm oil operations without manual rework.

KPI Dashboard Sawit

The prebuilt KPI dashboard provides a production overview for CPO, kernel, and TBS processed; OER and KER; daily TBS reception in kg; quality status for FFA, moisture, and DOBI trends; cost analysis per kg CPO; sales KPIs for CPO and kernel with price trends; and financial KPIs such as profitability and revenue by product.

Operational Dashboard

The operational dashboard shows harvest progress versus target per block, TBS in transit and fleet utilization, stock levels for CPO tanks and kernel storage, mill downtime and preventive maintenance schedules, HR attendance for harvest workers and productivity, and procurement for fertilizer, chemicals, and spare parts. It also integrates with vit_dashboard_studio for custom dashboards.

Inventory & Stock

Xerpium tracks CPO stock in storage tanks, kernel stock in silos before delivery, fertilizer in estate warehouses, spare parts for mill and heavy equipment, chemicals for water treatment and lab, and fuel stock for generators, heavy equipment, and transport.

Accounting & Sales

The accounting and sales module supports sales of CPO for domestic and export, sales of palm kernel, and sales of TBS to other mills. It includes cost accounting for cost per kg TBS and cost per kg CPO, gross margin analysis per product line, operational cash flow for palm oil, and tax reports for PPN 11%, final income tax, PPh 21, and PPh 22/23.

Export Operations

Export documentation is managed as a connected flow: export delivery order with container, vessel, loading and discharge port, and incoterm; PEB with DJBC registration number and CEISA integration; packing list with total packages, gross/net weight, and volume per product; certificate of origin for Form D ASEAN and non-ASEAN preferential tariffs; and export invoice in USD with FOB value and incoterm.

Trade & Finance

Xerpium handles letter of credit for sight/usance and import/export with issuance, expiry, and shipping date tracking; TT/remittance
