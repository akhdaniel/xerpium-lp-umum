Complete ERP solution for Building Management: Unifying Property Operations, Billing, and Field Services

Building management teams typically operate across multiple properties, towers, floors, and unit types while also handling residents, rental contracts, monthly IPL billing, maintenance, security, cleaning, parking, and complaints. When each of these areas runs on separate spreadsheets, legacy property software, or disconnected accounting tools, the result is predictable: duplicate unit records, billing errors, missed maintenance deadlines, poor SLA visibility, and slow reporting. Xerpium ERP replaces this fragmented landscape with a single end-to-end ERP scope for the Building Management vertical, connecting property data, field operations, billing, inventory, HR, and accounting in one system.

Common Challenges in Building Management

  • Property, tower, floor, and unit data is scattered across multiple files, making ownership history and occupancy status difficult to trust.
  • Monthly IPL billing and utility charges are generated manually, leading to incorrect invoices, missed due dates, and weak outstanding tracking.
  • Maintenance requests and complaints are handled reactively without automatic priority-based SLAs or escalation.
  • Security patrols, cleaning schedules, facility bookings, and parking operations lack a unified audit trail and completion tracking.
  • Operational data is disconnected from accounting, procurement, and inventory, so maintenance costs and resident billing are often reconciled late or not at all.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Property, Tower, and Unit Structure

Xerpium starts with a clear property hierarchy. Each property can be recorded with name, code, address, manager, and property type such as apartment, office, mixed, mall, school, hospital, hotel, industrial, or government. Under each property, the system manages towers with unit allocation, floors per tower, and unit types including studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, and penthouse. Configuration also covers IPL products, sinking fund, and utility parameters per property.

Unit and Resident Management

Each unit is linked to property, tower, floor, and unit type. The system tracks owner per unit with multi-owner history, unit status—available, occupied, reserved, maintenance, or inactive—and usage type as residential or commercial. Unit size, bedrooms, and bathrooms are captured. Ownership history is recorded automatically whenever the owner changes. For residents, Xerpium stores KTP or passport identity, phone, email, related unit, and resident type as owner, tenant, family, or staff. Resident status can be active, moved out, or blacklisted. Move-in and move-out are tracked with dates and history, family members can be recorded per unit, and vehicles can be registered per resident.

Rental Contract

For commercial units, Xerpium supports rental contracts with start and end dates and automatic validation to prevent overlaps. Each contract records annual rent, down payment, and balance tracking. The system can generate DP invoices and annual rent invoices through accounting. Contract status moves through draft, active, expired, and cancelled, and the unit status is automatically synchronized to occupied or available based on the contract.

Billing, IPL, and Utility Management

Monthly IPL billing is generated per unit or resident. Charge types include service charge, sinking fund, water, electricity, gas, internet, and parking. Meter reading supports manual input or import for utility types such as water, electricity, gas, and internet. The system calculates consumption based on previous versus current readings and applies tariff per utility type, including tiered pricing. Consumption is automatically linked to billing lines. Xerpium can generate invoices automatically to accounting, track due dates and outstanding balances, and run a monthly wizard for mass billing generation.

Maintenance Request and Work Order

Residents can create maintenance requests with priority levels—low, normal, high, or urgent—each with automatic SLA response and completion deadlines. For urgent requests, the SLA can be set to one hour response and eight hours completion. Requests can be assigned to technicians from HR, scheduled, and dispatched. Checklist lines are supported per request, and before/after photos can be documented. Maintenance requests can create invoices and purchase orders. Work orders cover types such as electrical, plumbing, civil, HVAC, cleaning, landscaping, and IT. They can be assigned to an employee or vendor contractor, with time tracking per technician, material usage linked to product inventory, task checklists with completion status, and before/after photo documentation.

Facility Booking, Security, Cleaning, Parking, and Visitor

Facility booking manages gym, pool, function room, and other facilities per property. Each facility has capacity, hourly rate, and deposit. Residents can book through a calendar, and bookings integrate automatically to billing. Security incident management supports types such as theft, vandalism, fire, flood, noise, trespassing, and accident. Users report incidents with property, tower, or unit location, and the workflow moves from draft to investigated, resolved, or cancelled, with action taken recorded. Security patrol uses routes with QR-code checkpoints. Guards scan checkpoints per patrol, schedules are organized by morning, afternoon, and night shifts, and completion rate shows the percentage of checkpoints scanned. Scan timestamp, photo, and notes are logged per checkpoint. Cleaning management tracks areas such as corridor, lobby, toilet, and parking. Cleaning schedules are organized per shift and area, with quality scores from 1 to 5, supervisor inspection, and completion rate per schedule. Parking and visitor management covers parking slots per property with types for car, motorcycle, and bicycle. Slot status can be available, occupied, reserved, or maintenance. Visitors can be pre-registered, checked in and out, and issued a QR code pass. Lost and found items are handled with claim and dispose workflows.

Complaint Management and Dashboard

Complaint management lets users set up complaint categories with SLA per category. Complaints have priority levels—low, normal, high, or urgent—with different SLA hours. Assignment can be automatic to an employee or escalated. SLA deadlines are generated based on priority and category, overdue complaints are detected automatically, and residents can provide feedback with a 1–5 rating. The property dashboard provides a pie chart of unit status and occupancy per property, monthly billing outstanding summary, open versus closed maintenance requests, complaint trends and SLA compliance, and incident reports per property.

Cross-Module Integration

Xerpium connects Building Management with the wider ERP. IPL billing generates customer invoices in accounting, maintenance invoices are created through account.move, and rental contracts generate DP and annual rent invoices. Work orders consume materials from stock products, sparepart requests check inventory availability, and products support IPL and maintenance costing. Technicians are sourced from hr.employee, security guards and cleaning staff are assigned through HR. Purchase orders can be created from vendor maintenance, sparepart procurement, and material contractor purchasing. Work orders can originate from maintenance requests, include checklist and material tracking, and actual costs can be invoiced to residents. Reporting structures cover building management, property financial reports, and per-property dashboards.

End-to-End Flow

A commercial tenant signs a rental contract for a unit in an office tower. The contract is created with start and end dates, annual rent, DP, and balance tracking; the system validates no overlap and automatically syncs the unit status to occupied. Monthly IPL billing then runs through the wizard, pulling service charge, sinking fund, and utility lines from meter readings and tariffs, and generates a customer invoice in accounting. Later, the tenant creates a high-priority maintenance request. The system applies the SLA, assigns a technician from HR, and creates a work order with checklist and material usage from inventory. If a sparepart is needed, a purchase order is created through procurement. Actual maintenance costs are recorded and can be invoiced to the resident. Throughout the cycle, the property dashboard updates occupancy, billing outstanding, maintenance status, and SLA compliance.

Business Benefits

  • Single source of truth for properties, towers, units, owners, residents, contracts, and assets.
  • Automated IPL billing, utility consumption calculation, and accounting invoice generation reduce manual errors.
  • Priority-based SLA for maintenance and complaints improves response and completion times.
  • Security patrol, cleaning, and facility booking operations gain structured schedules, checkpoints, and audit trails.
  • Maintenance work orders, sparepart requests, and purchase orders connect field work to inventory and procurement.
  • Property dashboards and financial reports give management real-time visibility into occupancy, outstanding billing, and operational performance.

Implementation Steps

  1. Persiapan data — Collect and clean property, tower, floor, unit type, unit, owner, resident, vehicle, facility, parking slot, sparepart, and chart of accounts data for the initial scope.
  2. Konfigurasi parameter — Set up IPL charge types, sinking fund, utility tariffs and tiered pricing, SLA rules for maintenance and complaints, complaint categories, patrol routes and checkpoints, cleaning areas, and facility rates.
  3. Pilot — Run one property or tower through the full cycle: unit and resident records, rental contracts, monthly billing, maintenance requests, work orders, and accounting reconciliation.
  4. Rollout — Expand to all properties, train property managers, technicians, security, cleaning supervisors, and finance teams, and migrate historical ownership and contract data.
  5. Evaluasi — Review dashboards, SLA compliance, billing outstanding, maintenance completion, and complaint feedback, then adjust SLA parameters, tariffs, and checklist templates as needed.
