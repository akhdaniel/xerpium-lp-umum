Complete ERP solution for Palm Oil Industry: End-to-End Plantation, Mill, Export, and Financial Control with Xerpium

October 19, 2026

Palm oil operations often run on disconnected systems. Estate teams track blocks in spreadsheets, weighbridge clerks record TBS in separate logs, the mill manages processing on paper, and finance closes books in a generic accounting tool. The result is slow reconciliation, weak traceability, and difficulty answering basic questions such as cost per kg CPO, OER by block, or margin per export shipment. Xerpium ERP provides a single palm oil vertical that connects land blocks, harvest, production, quality, inventory, trade, export, and accounting without forcing a company to assemble generic modules.

Common Challenges in Palm Oil Industry

  • Estate-to-mill visibility gaps: block yield, harvest rotation, TBS grade, and transport cost per kg are often recorded manually and not linked to mill output.
  • Quality and traceability pressure: FFA, moisture, DOBI, OER, KER, and ISPO/RSPO traceability require consistent lab data and specification limits.
  • Accounting complexity: palm oil companies need a specific chart of accounts, PPN 11% and PPh handling, foreign currency sales, LC/TT instruments, and landed cost tracking.
  • Inventory silos: CPO tanks, kernel silos, fertilizer, chemicals, spare parts, and fuel are typically managed in separate spreadsheets.
  • Management reporting delays: production, quality, cost, sales, and financial KPIs are difficult to combine because operational data does not flow into accounting automatically.

Xerpium Solution Coverage

Plantation and estate management

Xerpium supports block management per divisi kebun, land area in hectares, planting status, planting year, afdeling grouping, maturity status for tanaman belum menghasilkan (TBM) and tanaman menghasilkan (TM), variety such as DxP, tree density per Ha, and block history of fertilization, maintenance, and harvest.

For harvest and TBS production, the system manages harvest schedules by block with 7/10/14-day rotation, FFB collection per block, weight per bunch, per employee, and per block, quality grade such as mentah, matang, and lewat matang, transport by lori or truck, monthly production estimates per block, and cost per kg TBS for harvest and transport.

Mill and CPO production

The mill module covers TBS reception at the weighbridge, grade check and sortation, processing through sterilizer, thresher, press, clarifier, and kernel plant, CPO output, kernel output, OER as CPO divided by TBS multiplied by 100, KER, and loss tracking per production stage. Xerpium also connects to manufacturing through bills of materials for CPO production, work orders for mill processes, by-product tracking for kernel, cangkang, and fiber, and quality control integrated into manufacturing orders.

Quality control and laboratory

Quality management includes FFB sorting grade, CPO quality parameters such as FFA, moisture, dirt, DOBI, and IV, kernel quality including moisture, impurities, and broken, water quality testing, lab result certificates, ISPO and RSPO traceability, and specification limits for each quality parameter.

Palm oil accounting and financial reporting

Xerpium includes a palm oil-specific chart of accounts template with a SAW prefix and 6-digit account codes. Examples include saw_110100 for cash/bank, saw_110900 for transfer, saw_120100 for receivables, saw_210100 for payables and accruals, saw_410100 for revenue, saw_410200 for sales discounts, and saw_510100 for cost of goods sold by products and processes. Tax mapping uses tax_sale_11 for PPN 11% and tax_purchase_11, with complete AR/AP, expense, income, cash difference, and forex mapping.

The financial report structure uses vit.report_structure and a specific vit_report_structure_sawit.xml data model. Account groups are synchronized automatically with _sync_sawit_report_account_groups() when the chart template is loaded, with prefix mapping and a post-load hook. The structure remains compatible with vit_turbo_report_inherit for reporting.

Inventory, sales, export, and trade

Inventory covers CPO tank stock, kernel silo stock, fertilizer, spare parts, chemicals, and fuel. Sales handles domestic and export CPO, kernel sales, TBS sales to other mills, cost accounting per kg TBS and per kg CPO, gross margin analysis by product line, cash flow, and tax reports for PPN 11%, final PPh, PPh 21, and PPh 22/23.

Export operations support delivery orders with container, vessel, port loading and discharge, and incoterm; PEB with DJBC registration and CEISA integration; packing lists with total packages, gross/net weight, and volume; certificates of origin including Form D for ASEAN and non-ASEAN preferential tariff; and commercial export invoices in USD with FOB value and incoterm. Trade and finance covers letter of credit for sight or usance, import or export, with issuance, expiry, and shipping date; TT/remittance for outgoing and incoming payments with bank reference and status tracking; landed cost as FOB plus freight, insurance, duty, tax, and handling; forex tracking for USD/IDR realized and booking gains or losses; and bank reconciliation that auto-matches payments to LC or TT references.

Dashboards and KPIs

The sawit KPI dashboard provides production overview for CPO, kernel, and TBS processed; OER and KER; daily TBS reception in kg; quality trends for FFA, moisture, and DOBI; production cost per kg CPO; sales KPIs for CPO, kernel, and price trends; and financial KPIs for profitability and revenue by product. The operational dashboard tracks harvest progress versus target per block, TBS in transit and fleet utilization, CPO tank and kernel storage levels, mill downtime and preventive maintenance schedules, harvest employee attendance and productivity, and procurement for fertilizer, chemicals, and spare parts. Custom dashboards can be built with vit_dashboard_studio.

Pre-integrated cross-module flows

Xerpium connects sawit operations to accounting, inventory, purchase, dashboard, manufacturing, export, trade, and HR. Purchase supports purchase requests for fertilizer and chemicals, blanket orders for fixed suppliers, TBS purchases from plasma farmers, and principal discounts for chemicals. HR supports afdeling and harvest clerk records, attendance and payroll for daily workers, productivity per harvest employee, and timesheets for mill operators.

End-to-End Flow

A complete cycle starts in the estate, where a harvest schedule rotates by block. Harvest employees record FFB collection, weight, and grade per block. TBS is transported to the mill and received at the weighbridge. Grade check and quality control data are attached to the reception.

The mill processes TBS through sterilizer, thresher, press, clarifier, and kernel plant. Work orders capture CPO output, kernel output, by-products, and losses per stage. OER and KER are calculated, and lab results for CPO and kernel quality are linked to the production batch. Finished stock is posted into CPO tanks and kernel silos, while cost accounting calculates cost per kg TBS and cost per kg CPO.

For export sales, the team creates an export delivery order, packing list, PEB through CEISA integration, certificate of origin, and commercial invoice in USD. Trade and finance manage LC or TT, landed cost, and forex. Bank reconciliation matches incoming payment to the LC or TT reference. Accounting journals are generated for TBS purchases, production, inventory movement, sales, and taxes using the SAW chart of accounts and PPN 11% mapping. Dashboards and financial reports update from the same operating data.

Implementation Steps

  1. Prepare master data: map land blocks, afdeling, maturity, variety, density, and block history; load supplier and customer records; enter opening stock for CPO tanks, kernel silos, fertilizer, spare parts, chemicals, and fuel.
  2. Configure accounting and parameters: apply the SAW chart of accounts template, set 6-digit account codes, activate report structure synchronization, configure PPN 11% and PPh taxes, define QC specification limits, and set OER/KER targets.
  3. Run a pilot: start with one afdeling or one mill process covering harvest, TBS reception, processing, QC, inventory, sales, and accounting before scaling.
  4. Roll out operations and integrations: activate purchase requests, blanket orders, plasma TBS purchases, export documents, CEISA submission, LC/TT, and HR attendance workflows.
  5. Evaluate and refine: compare actual harvest versus production estimate, monitor OER/KER and losses, review margin by product line, and customize dashboards with vit_dashboard_studio for management needs.
