Purchase & Procurement with Xerpium ERP: From PR and RFQ to 3-Way Matching

# Purchase & Procurement with Xerpium ERP: From PR and RFQ to 3-Way Matching

Procurement teams in B2B companies often work across disconnected spreadsheets, email threads, and legacy systems. Purchase requests, supplier quotes, purchase orders, and invoice verification become separate steps with different owners, making it difficult to maintain consistency, control spend, and prepare for audits. Xerpium ERP addresses this with a Purchase & Procurement module that connects PR, RFQ, PO, vendor management, and 3-way matching in one base system. Because the module is integrated with all other Xerpium modules, procurement data flows into the wider ERP environment without manual re-entry.

Common procurement challenges

  • Manual or disconnected PR-to-PO processes slow down approvals and create visibility gaps.
  • Vendor data and RFQ responses are scattered across emails and spreadsheets, making supplier comparison inconsistent.
  • 3-way matching between purchase orders, goods receipts, and invoices is often manual, increasing the risk of payment errors.
  • Multi-company and multi-currency operations add complexity to procurement, reporting, and compliance.
  • Weak audit trails make it difficult to reconstruct procurement decisions and prove process compliance.

How Xerpium Purchase & Procurement helps

Xerpium’s Purchase & Procurement module is a base module that works with all other Xerpium modules. It supports multi-company, multi-currency, and audit trail requirements, while providing real-time dashboards and reports. The module also integrates with the Xerpium AI Assistant.

PR, RFQ, and PO in one workflow

Xerpium supports purchase requisitions (PR), requests for quotation (RFQ), and purchase orders (PO) as part of the same procurement flow. A request can move from internal need to supplier quotation and then to an approved purchase order without leaving the system. This keeps the procurement history aligned and gives teams a single source of truth for what was requested, quoted, and ordered.

Vendor management

Vendor management is included within the Purchase & Procurement module. Teams can maintain supplier-related procurement activity in one place, which supports clearer supplier comparison and better oversight across the purchasing cycle. Because vendor data sits inside the ERP rather than in separate files, it becomes easier to keep procurement decisions consistent.

3-way matching

The module provides 3-way matching as part of the purchase-to-pay process. It aligns the purchase order, goods or service receipt, and supplier invoice so that discrepancies are visible before payment. This helps reduce overpayments, duplicate invoices, and manual reconciliation work.

Multi-company, multi-currency, and audit trail

Xerpium supports multi-company and multi-currency procurement. That means organizations operating across different entities or currencies can manage purchasing within one ERP environment instead of relying on disconnected local processes. Audit trail is built into the module, helping teams track changes and maintain a clear record for internal reviews or external audits.

Real-time dashboard and AI Assistant

The module includes dashboards and reports that provide real-time visibility into procurement activity. Instead of waiting for month-end spreadsheets, procurement and finance teams can monitor open POs, vendor activity, and related information as it happens. Xerpium’s AI Assistant is also integrated into the Purchase & Procurement module, giving users an additional way to interact with procurement data and tasks.

Business benefits

  • A unified PR-to-PO process that reduces manual handoffs and scattered documentation.
  • Stronger vendor visibility through centralized vendor management.
  • More reliable payment verification with 3-way matching against the PO and receipt.
  • Easier multi-company and multi-currency procurement without separate systems.
  • Clearer compliance and accountability with audit trails.
  • Real-time dashboards and reports that support faster operational decisions.
  • AI Assistant integration for a more natural way to support procurement workflows.

Use case: multi-company procurement with 3-way matching

Consider a manufacturing group that operates across several companies and currencies. A site team raises a purchase requisition for raw materials in Xerpium. The procurement team uses the RFQ step to compare supplier responses, selects a vendor, and issues a purchase order. When the goods arrive and the supplier invoice is received, the team performs 3-way matching against the purchase order and the receipt. Because the module supports multi-company and multi-currency, the transaction remains consistent across entities. The real-time dashboard gives finance and procurement leaders visibility into open commitments, while the audit trail keeps a clear record for review.

Conclusion

Xerpium ERP brings purchase requisitions, RFQs, purchase orders, vendor management, and 3-way matching into one connected procurement process. With multi-company, multi-currency, audit trail, real-time dashboards, and AI Assistant integration, the Purchase & Procurement module helps B2B organizations move away from fragmented purchasing and toward a more controlled, visible, and audit-ready operation.
