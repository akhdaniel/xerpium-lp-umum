# Rental Management with Xerpium ERP: Streamlining Fleet, Heavy Equipment, and Asset Rentals

Rental operations can appear straightforward: assign an asset, track usage, and invoice the customer. In practice, rental businesses managing vehicles, heavy equipment, long-term assets, and general rental items deal with far more complexity. Availability checks, condition documentation, dynamic pricing, depreciation, late-return penalties, and tax handling must all work together. Xerpium ERP brings these processes into one connected system, supported by accounting, inventory, purchasing, HR, and AI-assisted workflows.

Common Challenges in Rental Operations

  • Asset availability and condition are scattered across spreadsheets, making it difficult to know which units are ready to rent.
  • Pickup and return evidence such as odometer readings, fuel level, and physical condition is often manual and inconsistent, leading to disputes over usage or damage.
  • Hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly pricing across multi-item contracts increases the risk of billing errors.
  • Late returns, damage penalties, and VAT are frequently tracked outside the main rental system or applied inconsistently.
  • Rental execution is disconnected from invoicing, spare part inventory, purchase orders, and driver payroll, creating delays and duplicated work.

Xerpium Rental Modules and Features

Vehicle Rental Management

Xerpium integrates fleet data from its fleet module for vehicles such as cars, trucks, dump trucks, and excavators. The system records odometer readings at pickup and return, and automatically calculates distance traveled. Fuel level is tracked using Empty, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, or Full at both pickup and return. Rental rates can be configured per vehicle for hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly billing. Vehicle records also include brand and model information such as Komatsu, Caterpillar, Mitsubishi, Toyota, Hino, and Isuzu.

Heavy Equipment and Machinery Rental

For heavy equipment and machinery, Xerpium connects to the maintenance module to manage items such as generator sets, air compressors, welding machines, and water pumps. Condition is tracked at pickup and return using New, Good, Fair, or Damaged. Serial number tracking through stock.lot supports traceability for each unit. Safety inspections are recorded before and after rental to document equipment condition and support accountability.

Long-Term Asset Rental

Xerpium supports rental of long-term assets such as buildings and land through integration with its Accounting Kit. This includes depreciation tracking alongside rental revenue, real-time asset valuation, and visibility into rental price versus depreciation. Contracts can run monthly or yearly, and each asset receives a unique asset code for identification.

General Rental Items

Beyond vehicles and heavy assets, Xerpium handles general rental items such as chairs, tents, sound systems, and party equipment. Pricing can be customized per hour, day, week, month, or a custom period. The product catalog supports a rent_ok flag, meaning any product can be enabled for rental when needed. A single contract can combine multiple types of rental items.

Rental Contracts and Dynamic Stages

Every rental contract receives a sequential auto-number such as RENT/2026/00001. Contracts support many rental items with individual pricing for each line. Pricing models cover hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly rates, while duration is automatically computed from the start date and return date. Contracts move through dynamic stages: Draft, Confirmed, Ready for Pickup, Active, Pending Return, Returned, Closed, and Cancelled. A Kanban view provides a visual drag-and-drop board with color-coded stages, and stages can be supported by Python hooks and role-based access control.

Pickup and Return Wizards

The pickup wizard records odometer readings for vehicles, fuel level, condition, notes, and photos before the rental starts. Once completed, the contract automatically moves to Active Rental. The return wizard records the final odometer, calculates total distance traveled automatically, checks fuel level, inspects condition, and documents any damage. Extra daily charges for late returns are applied automatically based on product configuration. The contract then moves to Returned.

Billing, Tax, KPIs, and Financial Reporting

Each rental line calculates a subtotal using quantity multiplied by unit price and duration. VAT 11% or exempt tax handling is integrated into the system, and multi-currency is supported. Extra daily charges and damage fines can be added to contract totals.

Xerpium provides KPI tiles for Total Rental Revenue, Total Rental Orders, Total Fleet Units, Outstanding AR, Total Clients, Rental Items Available, Invoiced Amount, and Invoices This Month. Charts include a Fleet Status doughnut chart, Top Rental Items horizontal bar chart, Monthly Revenue Trend area chart, Monthly Orders Trend line chart, Top Rental Items list, and Top Clients list.

Financial reporting includes balance sheet, profit and loss, and trial balance views. The balance sheet covers current assets such as cash, receivables, and spare parts, as well as fixed assets like fleet units, heavy equipment, and buildings. Profit and loss covers rental revenue, cost of goods sold, salaries, unit maintenance, depreciation, and net profit. Tax reporting supports VAT at 11% for input and output transactions, along with final withholding tax scenarios.

Connected Back-Office Processes

Xerpium connects rental operations with the broader ERP environment:

  • Accounting: Invoices are generated automatically from rental contracts. A dedicated chart of accounts includes 181 accounts across seven groups: cash, receivables, spare parts, fleet, revenue, cost of goods sold, and expenses.
  • Inventory: Tracks spare parts, tires, oil, and consumables. The system also monitors rental unit availability and supports minimum stock alerts with automatic purchase orders.
  • Purchase: Creates purchase orders for spare parts, new units, and insurance. Vendor management and three-way matching between purchase order, goods receipt, and invoice are supported.
  • HR: Uses hr.employee data for driver and operator records, shift scheduling, assignment, payroll, and driver commissions.
  • Dashboard Studio: Supports AI auto-generated dashboards with KPI cards, fleet status, top rental items, and revenue trends.
  • AI Assistant: Allows users to create rental contracts through natural language, check unit status and availability, and generate rental invoices.
  • Multi-Company: Supports multi-branch rental operations, inter-branch unit transfers, and consolidated financial reporting.
