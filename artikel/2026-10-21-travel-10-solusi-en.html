10 Best ERP Solutions for Travel, Hajj & Umrah Operations

October 21, 2026

Managing Hajj and Umrah operations involves far more than selling a travel package. Agents must coordinate hotel and flight inventory, track pilgrim documents and passports, enforce departure quotas, collect staged payments, procure hotel rooms and visas, distribute equipment, and report to management. When these tasks run on separate spreadsheets or generic accounting tools, data gets duplicated, document deadlines slip, and departure capacity is often oversold. An integrated ERP designed for travel removes those disconnects by linking operational data into a single workflow.

The 10 Solutions at a Glance

  1. Hotel & Penerbangan — centralizes hotel, flight, route, and seat quota data to prevent itinerary errors.
  2. Paket & Fasilitas — structures Reguler, Plus, and VIP packages with room pricing and included facilities.
  3. Jamaah — consolidates pilgrim identity, passport, visa, mahram, health, and emergency contact data.
  4. Dokumen — manages nine document types with approval workflow and automatic completeness status.
  5. Keberangkatan — tracks departure lifecycle, stages, quota usage, and assigned team members.
  6. Registrasi (Sale Order) — combines package selection, auto-pricing, quota checks, and payment scheduling.
  7. Payment Schedule — auto-generates DP, installment, and final payment terms with one-click invoicing.
  8. Purchase Integration — creates hotel, flight, and visa purchase orders without duplicate bookings.
  9. Template Perlengkapan — standardizes gender-aware equipment templates for each package type.
  10. Distribusi Perlengkapan — executes equipment handover, stock picking, and packing summaries per departure.

1. Hotel & Penerbangan

This module centralizes hotel and flight master data for Hajj and Umrah operations. It handles Makkah and Madinah hotels from 2 to 5 stars, including distance to the mosque in meters, facilities, and room capacity. Airlines such as Garuda Indonesia and Saudi Airlines are stored with flight numbers, go/return/both types, origin and destination, transit, duration, and economy/business seat quotas per flight. The display uses a clear format such as “Garuda Indonesia GA-981 (Jakarta → Jeddah).” By consolidating this data, travel agents reduce manual mismatches between package promises and actual inventory.

2. Paket & Fasilitas

Paket & Fasilitas structures the three package types—Reguler, Plus, and VIP—with duration days and quota per type. Each package defines different hotels for Makkah and Madinah, number of nights in each city, and room pricing for quad, triple, double, and single occupancy. It also records included facilities: visa, ticket, hotel, muthawwif, transport, catering, insurance, ziarah, and manasik. Equipment templates are attached per package, covering items such as koper, ihram, mukena, seragam, and buku panduan. This prevents pricing inconsistencies and clarifies exactly what each pilgrim receives.

3. Jamaah

Jamaah centralizes pilgrim identity and compliance data. It captures 16-digit NIK, place and date of birth, age, gender, education, occupation, passport number and expiry with automatic six-month validity check, and visa number with issue and expiry tracking. For female pilgrims, the module links a mahram relationship to a male companion. Health records include blood type, rhesus, height/weight, wheelchair needs, and special diet. Emergency contact name, phone, and relationship are stored. This single source of truth reduces duplicate data and helps staff anticipate passport, visa, and health-related risks before departure.

4. Dokumen

Dokumen manages nine document types—KTP, passport, 3x4 photo, vaccine certificate, marriage certificate, birth certificate, family card, health certificate, and others—with file upload and expiry tracking. A workflow moves each document from pending to approved or rejected, recording the verifying user and timestamp; rejected documents can be reset to pending for revision. The module enforces one document per type per pilgrim and computes document_complete status when KTP, passport, photo, and vaccine are approved. This eliminates manual checklists and gives team members a clear verification audit trail.

5. Keberangkatan

Keberangkatan manages the departure lifecycle. It auto-generates codes such as KEP/2026/0001 and tracks states from draft to open, full, departed, returned, closed, and cancelled. The dynamic stage view covers eight stages: Persiapan, Pendaftaran Dibuka, Penuh, Verifikasi Dokumen, Berangkat, Kembali, Selesai, and Dibatalkan. Quota is calculated from the package, consumed by confirmed sale orders, and displayed with available and percentage. Group leaders and muthawwif are assigned from hr.employee, and go/return flights can differ from package defaults. This gives operational teams live quota visibility and clear stage-based accountability.

6. Registrasi (Sale Order)

Registrasi (Sale Order) turns package and departure selection into a booking record. Users choose a package, departure, and room type; the price is automatically taken from the selected room type. A quota check validates availability at confirmation. Once confirmed, the system auto-generates a three-term payment schedule with DP 30%, installment 40%, and final 30%. Each term can be invoiced with one click, and the module tracks total invoiced, paid, and remaining amounts. It also flags document status from pilgrim document completeness. This reduces manual pricing errors and gives a clear financial position per registration.

7. Payment Schedule

Payment Schedule automates recurring and configurable payment terms. The default terms are DP 30%, Angsuran 2 40%, and Pelunasan 30%, but percentages can be adjusted. When a sale order is confirmed, the schedule is generated immediately. Each line moves from pending to invoiced to paid, synchronized from payment invoices. One-click invoice creation per term removes repetitive billing work, while overpayment prevention validates that total payments never exceed the package price. For Hajj and Umrah agents with many pilgrims, this ensures receivables are tracked accurately and late payments are easier to identify.

8. Purchase Integration

Purchase Integration connects departures to procurement. It supports four purchase order types: Hotel Makkah, Hotel Madinah, Flight, and Visa. A wizard auto-fills vendors from hotel data and auto-fills line quantities from the pilgrim count for the departure. The module prevents duplicate purchase orders of the same type for the same departure, which avoids double-booking hotels or flights. Purchase order tracking shows purchase count and status per departure, and product descriptions are generated automatically with departure context. This brings procurement under operational control and reduces errors when many groups depart close together.

9. Template Perlengkapan

Template Perlengkapan standardizes equipment by package type. Each package—Reguler, Plus, or VIP—has its own template with 15+ items, including koper, tas jinjing, tas paspor, kain ihram, mukena, seragam batik, buku panduan, sandal, sabuk uang, ID card, spray, and P3K. The template is gender-aware: male pilgrims receive ihram and female pilgrims receive mukena, filtered automatically. Clothing sizes S through XXXL are captured per pilgrim. This ensures consistent pilgrim supplies and avoids last-minute shortages or wrong-size distribution during peak departure windows.

10. Distribusi Perlengkapan

Distribusi Perlengkapan executes equipment delivery from confirmed sale orders. Lines are auto-generated per departure, and the workflow moves from draft to confirmed, packed, distributed, or cancelled. Stock picking creates outgoing delivery orders and auto-reduces inventory. A product summary aggregates quantity per product variant for packing, while a per-pilgrim distributed flag tracks individual acceptance. The module also provides a printable distribution list report. For agents handling hundreds of pilgrims, this closes the loop between booking, inventory, and physical handover without manual spreadsheets.

How to Choose the Right Solution

Prioritize operational integration rather than point solutions. Look for a platform where package configuration, departure quota, pilgrim documents, payment schedules, and purchase orders share one data model; otherwise you will still re-enter data. Confirm the solution handles Indonesian travel tax rules—PPN 11%, PPh 23 at 2% or 4%, PPh 26 at 20%, final PPh 4(2) at 10%, reverse-charge PPN JKP LN at 12%, and stamp duty. Check document automation: passport six-month warnings, approval workflows, and computed completeness reduce manual follow-up. Finally, assess multi-company consolidation and reporting so branch offices can be managed from one dashboard.

Conclusion
