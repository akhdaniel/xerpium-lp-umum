# School and Vocational Education Management with Xerpium ERP

Running a school or vocational institution involves far more than classroom teaching. Behind every report card, schedule, invoice, and attendance record is an administrative process that must remain accurate, transparent, and repeatable across academic years. When admission data, curriculum planning, assessment, student affairs, and payments live in separate spreadsheets or disconnected applications, schools lose time and expose themselves to errors.

Xerpium ERP offers a unified education management suite for schools and vocational institutions. It connects the student lifecycle—from prospective student admission to active learning, assessment, graduation, and parent billing—inside one system with clear workflows and audit trails.

Common Challenges in School and Vocational Administration

Before implementing an education ERP, many institutions face similar operational bottlenecks:

  • Fragmented admission processes. Prospective student data, documents, parent records, and selection status are often managed manually across waves and capacities.
  • Inconsistent curriculum and teacher assignments. Lesson plans, minimum passing criteria, and teacher schedules may not be formally linked to subjects, semesters, or academic levels.
  • Complex assessment and report generation. Multiple test types, weighted scoring, and grade conversions create a heavy administrative burden at the end of each term.
  • Scattered attendance and scheduling data. Class attendance, per-lesson presence, academic calendars, and teacher workloads are difficult to consolidate.
  • Manual billing and payment reconciliation. Monthly tuition, re-registration fees, and other education payments often require separate follow-up and manual matching.

How Xerpium ERP Supports the Full Student Lifecycle

Xerpium ERP structures school administration into connected modules that work from admission through graduation.

Admissions and Student Placement

The admission module manages the entire PSB process per academic year with configurable waves and capacities. Schools can record prospective student details such as NISN, NIS, place and date of birth, gender, blood type, religion, and ethnicity. Parent or guardian data includes education, occupation, income, and status. Address data is captured through a structured province, city, district, sub-district, and postal code selection.

Documents such as KTP, family card, birth certificate, diploma, and photos can be uploaded by document type. The system also records student hobbies and economic conditions, including categories such as poor, less fortunate, or able.

For selection, each PSB process supports five customizable score fields and two customizable contribution fields. Admission waves are managed as groups with capacity and filled count. Placement follows a structured workflow from Draft to Open to Done, allowing staff to filter passing students and place them into destination classes. Once placement is completed, student-class records are created automatically and the student becomes active in academic data.

Curriculum and Lesson Planning

The curriculum module handles subjects as mandatory or additional, linked to teachers and report card values. Teacher assignments include the subject, semester, decree reference, and effective date.

For each subject and level, schools can maintain:

  • RPP (lesson plans) with weight per level and semester
  • KKM (minimum passing criteria) per subject, level, and academic year
  • Testing types such as UTS, UAS, quizzes, assignments, and practice
  • Assessment aspects such as affective, cognitive, concept understanding, psychomotor, and practice

Xerpium also includes AI support for lesson planning. Using a GPT model, the system can generate draft RPP documents to accelerate teacher preparation.

Assessment and Report Cards

Assessment follows a controlled workflow from Draft to Open to Done. Teachers input grades per student and per RPP, with support for multiple test types. Each grade detail includes weight, date, and notes, and the average score is calculated automatically.

Weight rules determine how quizzes, assignments, practice, UTS, and UAS contribute to the final score. These rules move from draft to verified status. Grading is configurable, with a standard example of A for 90–100, B for 80–89, C for 70–79, D for 60–69, and E for 0–59.

The report card process is also workflow-based. Final numeric and letter grades are calculated automatically, and teachers can add subject comments as well as spiritual and social attitude comments with predicates and score ranges.

For online exams, Xerpium can generate questions via GPT and link them to a survey.

Attendance, Scheduling, and Student Affairs

Attendance is managed at two levels. Class presence runs monthly per class and tracks statuses such as present, sick, permission, absent, and leave. Lesson presence captures per-session data by subject, teacher, period, material, and teacher notes. The system automatically computes attendance recaps per student and the number of active school days per month. Teachers can manually search for students or auto-populate all students in a class.

Academic calendars record events by date, including national holidays, exams, and report card distribution. Learning periods define each period number with start and end times. Class schedules link day, period, subject, and teacher per class. Teacher schedules and total teaching hours can be recapped through a workflow.

Student affairs cover class transfers, grade promotion, student mutation, graduation, and transcripts. Each process follows a structured workflow and is recorded in the mail thread for audit trail.

Payments with Xendit Integration

Xerpium supports payment collection through Xendit, including public key, secret key, and webhook token configuration. Invoices are created using the Xendit API v2 with a redirect payment URL. Webhook statuses automate reconciliation:

  • PAID marks the transaction done, auto-reconciles, and sends email
  • PENDING keeps the transaction pending
  • EXPIRED or FAILED cancels the transaction

Each transaction links to the accounting record and stores the invoice URL. Recurring payments cover monthly SPP, education fees, and re-registration. Multiple payment methods are available through Xendit, including QRIS, virtual account, and credit card.

Student and Parent Portals

The student portal provides a dashboard with schedule, recent grades, and announcements. Students can view lesson schedules, attendance, grades, assignments, and online report cards. They can upload assignment answers and communicate with teachers and homeroom teachers.

The parent portal gives families a dashboard with their child’s schedule, attendance, and invoices. Parents can review daily schedules, monthly presence recaps, grades, and semester report cards. They can pay invoices directly through Xendit, view statistics on grade and attendance trends, register prospective students for PPDB, and message teachers or school staff.

Teacher Data and Teaching Load

Teacher records extend employee data with NIP, NUPTK, NRG, academic titles, nickname, gender, place and date of birth, marital status, identity number, religion, and ethnicity. Teachers can be categorized by department, such as subject teacher, honorary staff, assistant, or administration, with configurable employment status and start date.

Teaching load is managed through semester-based subject assignments, teacher schedules, and total teaching-hour recaps. Lesson presence records each session, including teacher attendance, notes, and session hours.

Example Workflow: From Admission to Report Card and Payment

A vocational school opens a new PSB process for the 2026/2027 academic year with two admission waves and a defined capacity. Candidate data, documents, parent records, and selection scores are collected in the system. After the selection workflow, staff filter passing students and place them into classes. Placement done automatically creates active student records.

Teachers are assigned to subjects for the semester. They create RPP documents, set KKM values, and define assessment weights. During the term, class attendance and lesson attendance are recorded, while assignments and tests are scored in the assessment module. At the end of the term, final report cards are generated automatically with numeric and letter grades. Parents view the report through the portal and pay the SPP invoice online using Xendit.

Business Benefits of Xerpium ERP for Schools and Vocational Institutions

  • Single source of truth for admissions, student data, teacher assignments, grades, attendance, and payments
  • Controlled workflows from draft to open to done, reducing unauthorized or premature data changes
  • Automated calculations for score averages, attendance recaps, active school days, and report card grades
  • Faster teacher preparation with AI-generated RPP drafts and AI-generated exam questions
  • Better parent experience through self-service schedules, grades, attendance, invoices, and online payment
  • Clean audit trail through mail thread records and transaction links to accounting

Xerpium ERP helps schools and vocational institutions replace fragmented administrative work with a connected system that follows each student from admission to graduation. By standardizing processes, automating calculations, and integrating payments and portals, schools can focus more on learning outcomes and less on manual coordination.
