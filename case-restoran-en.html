Restaurant & F&B

Manage tables, kitchen, inventory, and loyalty in one restaurant ERP.

A unified control center for multi-branch restaurant operations

Xerpium’s F&B suite connects front-of-house table and order management with back-of-house kitchen display, production, and inventory across branches.

  • Real-time table status, capacity, table type, and QR self-order per branch
  • Order workflow from draft to completed for dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and room service
  • Shift management with staff assignment, shift manager, and opening/closing notes
  • KPI dashboard for daily revenue, top products, and table turnover

From recipe costing to kitchen display and batch production

Control food cost and prep consistency with structured recipes, production batches, and quality checks fed directly to the kitchen display system.

  • Recipe management auto-calculates total cost from ingredients with waste percentage
  • Kitchen orders are prioritized with states from new to ready, assigned by kitchen station
  • Production batches track lot, expiry date, and QC hold or rejection
  • Quality checks cover temperature, weight, packaging, and visual or taste results

Scale with franchise, loyalty, and cold-chain distribution

Extend beyond a single outlet with franchise agreements, member loyalty programs, promotions, and branch distribution that protects product integrity.

  • Franchise agreements manage territory, initial fee, royalties, and subscription billing
  • Loyalty programs with points, tiers, and member cards support earn and redeem transactions
  • Promotions schedule discounts, happy hours, and combos by branch and product
  • Distribution plans and cold chain logs track branch transfers with temperature breach alerts

Run tighter margins and consistent quality across every outlet

Shelf-life tracking, stock alerts, and per-shift metrics give operators the visibility to reduce waste and improve service speed.

  • Shelf-life states flag expiring soon, critical, and expired items by storage location
  • Dashboard stock alerts highlight low stock and expiring soon items
  • Shift metrics report total orders and total revenue per branch
  • Kitchen performance averages prep time and order completion rate
