Managing Township Complexity from Land to Handover

Real estate developers need to track projects, clusters, kavling inventory, and sales agreements across multiple developments. Xerpium ERP provides a unified project structure that connects every unit, buyer, and payment schedule.

  • Project/Township master with type, location, timeline, and linked developer/owner
  • Clusters and blocks with computed kavling availability and sold counts
  • Kavling units with unique lot numbers, pricing, status, and physical attributes

Structured Sales Agreements and Payment Automation

The PPJB agreement module manages buyer, unit, pricing, and payment scheme, while payment schedules are auto-generated for cash or installment terms.

  • Auto-sequence agreements with state flow from Draft to Handed Over
  • Down payment percentage auto-calculates DP amount and per-installment amounts
  • Payment schedule generator creates DP line plus 12/24/36/48/60 installment lines
  • Invoice creation per installment with is_paid tracking and fine lines

Infrastructure, Handover, and Community Operations

Track infrastructure milestones, unit handover conditions, and community facilities all linked to projects. IPL billing lines can be generated in bulk for sold/handed-over kavlings.

  • Infrastructure items with budgets, actual costs, and auto-computed progress from milestones
  • Handover checklist with condition assessment and auto-update of kavling/agreement status
  • Facility types with capacity, opening date, and maintenance cost
  • IPL billing lines with bulk generate and auto-invoice per period

Scalable Accounting, AI Insights, and Multi-Project Consolidation

Xerpium ERP enables multi-company and multi-currency property operations with tax integration and consolidated reporting. AI dashboards turn natural language into SQL and chart insights for sales and slow-moving clusters.

  • Auto-journal for kavling sales and installment tracking with separate invoices
  • Infrastructure capitalization to assets and IPL auto-invoice mass generation
  • Multi-company and multi-currency with BI/ECB rates and consolidation reports
  • AI dashboard with NL-to-SQL, chart insight, and conditional formatting for status/alerts
