Precision from BOM to Export for Electronic OEMs

Xerpium unifies product models, version-controlled BOMs, OEM customer and supplier data, and export documentation for electronic OEM manufacturers.

  • Product Model with customer part number, HS code, FOB price USD, weight, volume, and linked product
  • BOM Version states draft/approved/obsolete with unique model linkage to mrp.bom
  • OEM Customers: Korean HQ, Korean Subsidiary, Local, Export with credit limit, incoterm, port of loading/discharge
  • OEM Suppliers: Korean, Local, Chinese, Singapore with supplier rating and certificates (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, RoHS, REACH, UL, CE)

Manufacturing Execution and Quality Control

Track manufacturing orders linked to customer POs, yield, downtime, AQL inspections, and lot traceability across SMT, assembly, injection molding, calibration, QC inspection, and packing.

  • MO Extension with customer_po_id/line, product_model, fob_price USD, target_ship_date
  • Work Center: SMT, Assembly, Injection Molding, Calibration, QC Inspection, Packing with shift start/end and assignment per MO
  • Yield Record: input qty, output qty, scrap qty, yield % per MO & shift; Downtime Record: machine breakdown, setup change, material missing, quality issue, power outage
  • AQL Inspection inbound sampling levels 1.0/2.5/4.0 with sample size, accept/reject, and Lot Traceability from component lot to finished lot, MO, customer PO

Export, Trade Compliance, and Finance Automation

Streamline export operations with CEISA-integrated PEB, packing lists, certificates of origin, commercial invoices, and manage KITE duty exemption and LC/TT payments.

  • Export DO with container, vessel, port loading/discharge, incoterm; PEB with DJBC registration number and CEISA integration
  • KITE Facility with duty exemption, ratio target 1.5, periodic realization, and KITE Realization import value vs export value
  • LC (Letter of Credit) sight/usance, import/export, issuance/expiry/shipping date; TT/Remittance outgoing/incoming payment with bank reference and status tracking
  • Landed Cost calculation: FOB + freight + insurance + duty + tax + handling

Scalable OEM-Specific Accounting and AI-Powered Insights

Use the 168-account OEM chart of accounts, landed cost calculation, KPI dashboards, and AI assistants to monitor revenue, open POs, BOM health, and compliance.

  • KPI Dashboard: Total Revenue MTD, Open Customer POs, Active Product Models, Active BOMs, Active Work Centers, OEM Customers, OEM Suppliers, HS Codes
  • Monthly Revenue line chart 12 months and Top Customers bar chart top 5 by revenue
  • AI Assistant Tools: electronic_product_model, electronic_supplier, electronic_customer_po, electronic_suggest_hs_code, electronic_check_kite_ratio
  • OEM Accounting: 168 accounts, PPN 11%/12%, PPh 21/23/25/26, COGS raw material, import raw, direct labor, overhead, variance; export revenue, local sales, scrap
