From Pit to Port: One Source of Truth for Mining Operations

Xerpium ERP consolidates site permits, blocks, seams, and cost structures into a single operational backbone for coal mining.

  • Manage mine sites with GPS coordinates, area, permit types (IUP/IUPK/IPR), expiry, and computed days remaining.
  • Track blocks by mining type, geological zone, estimated reserve ton and CV, with status from Exploration to Depleted.
  • Reference coal seams for thickness, depth, and quality parameters such as CV, ash, moisture, volatile matter, and sulfur.
  • Use cost centers per site/block and day/night/evening shifts for 24/7 operations.

Production Control That Captures Every Ton and Hour

Production orders and lines bring together planning, execution, equipment, and stockpile updates in one workflow.

  • Auto-sequence production orders (CPO/2026/00001) with site, block, seam, shift, date, and responsible employee.
  • Record planned vs actual ton and computed achievement %, plus actual CV, ash, and moisture at extraction.
  • Capture per-line equipment, operator, start/end time, duration, tons extracted, and fuel used.
  • Auto-add tonnage to stockpile on done; track weighted average CV, ash, moisture, and stock movement.

Quality, Delivery, and Compliance Built In

From sampling to lab analysis, certificates, delivery, and HSE, Xerpium keeps compliance and quality traceable end-to-end.

  • Coal sample workflow from Draft to Reported, with sample types and sources like mine face, stockpile, delivery, or rehandle.
  • Lab results cover proximate, ultimate, gross/net CV, and analyst; quality certificates grade A/B/C/Sub-bituminous/Lignite.
  • Delivery orders track truck/vessel, gross/tare/net weight, quality at delivery, and dispatch/delivery states.
  • HSE incident reports by type and severity with investigation, corrective/preventive action, safety inspections, and permit to work.

Scale with Audit-Ready Reporting and Royalty Automation

Xerpium turns operational data into government-ready reports and financial accuracy for growing mining operations.

  • ESDM reports auto-compute production ton, delivery ton, revenue, and average CV/ash/moisture per period.
  • Royalty rates configured per coal type with effective dates; royalty amounts calculated from production x rate_per_ton.
  • Equipment registry with current hours, fuel, maintenance history, and total cost computed.
  • Sales orders track ordered vs delivered tonnage, vessel registry, and invoice integration.
