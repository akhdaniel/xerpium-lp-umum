Fishery

Trace every catch, grade, and shipment with Xerpium Fishery ERP.

From Catch to Cold Chain in One System

Fishery operations require precise species data, vessel tracking, and compliant fishing areas. Xerpium consolidates these master data points for end-to-end operational control.

  • Scientific name, unique code, default product, and multi-language per fish species
  • Vessel registration, captain, capacity, and fleet integration
  • Fishing ground, GPS reference, area classification, and track record

Trip Management and Auto-Grading

From trip departure to receiving and grading, Xerpium automates catch management and quality checks. Grading rules and pricing rules remove manual guesswork.

  • Trip lifecycle: Draft → Departed → Returned → Received → Closed
  • Crew management with roles (captain, engineer, crew) and share percent
  • Auto-grade computation based on temperature and freshness score
  • Pricing rules compute price per kg by species, grade, and size

Cold Storage, Processing, and Lot Traceability

Cold storage integrity and processing yield are managed in real time with IoT and batch tracking. Extended lot traceability links every lot from vessel to customer with QR codes.

  • Cold room types (chilled, frozen, blast freezer) with temperature thresholds and auto-alert
  • IoT temperature logging and cold chain events from receiving to delivery
  • Processing batch types: fillet, loin, steak, block, whole frozen, fish meal; yield with byproducts and waste
  • Extended stock lot with species, vessel, trip, area, grade, temperature; parent-child traceability and public QR
