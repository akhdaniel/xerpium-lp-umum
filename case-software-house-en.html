Agile, Waterfall, and Hybrid in One Project Workspace

Software houses must handle Scrum, Waterfall, and Kanban across multiple clients without losing control. Xerpium lets each project choose its methodology while uniting backlog, phases, tasks, and budgets.

  • Scrum support includes product backlog, story points, sprint planning, velocity tracking, and completion rate.
  • Waterfall provides sequential phases with gate approval, predecessor validation, and milestone deliverables.
  • Kanban board supports task types like Feature, Bug, Technical Debt, and Spike, all linked to backlog or phases.

From User Stories to Sprint Velocity

Xerpium turns product backlogs into measurable sprints with clear acceptance criteria and automatic velocity history. Teams see capacity, goals, and task breakdown in one flow.

  • User story format As a... I want... So that... with acceptance criteria, story points, priority, tags, and assigned developer.
  • Sprint workflow Planning → Active → Closed limits one active sprint per project and offers a close wizard for review.
  • Task breakdown links user stories to project tasks, with sprint assignment and backlog traceability.
  • Velocity tracking auto-computes completed story points per sprint and backlog completion rate.

Budget, Timesheet, and Team Allocation That Protect Margin

Actual hours from timesheets flow into cost tracking and profitability per project, while team allocation prevents overbooking. Contract type and sale order link keep billing aligned.

  • Supports Fixed Price, Time & Material, and Milestone Billing, with project linked to sale order for invoicing.
  • Profitability computed as (budget - actual cost) / budget × 100 using configurable hourly rate (default Rp 150,000).
  • Allocate employees per project by role—Project Manager, Business Analyst, Developer, QA Engineer, DevOps, UI/UX Designer—with hours per week and date validation.
  • Total hours computed from weeks × hours_per_week, and actual hours auto-computed from timesheet.

Scale with Technology Stack and Cross-Project Reporting

Software houses can showcase their technology portfolio and use built-in reports to improve delivery and profitability. Xerpium provides the visibility needed to scale without losing oversight.

  • Technology catalog categorized into Frontend, Backend, Database, DevOps, Mobile, Other with color tags and unique names.
  • Map technologies to projects (many2many) and filter projects by stack or view stack diversity.
  • Reports include sprint velocity, project profitability, phase completion, and team allocation utilization.
