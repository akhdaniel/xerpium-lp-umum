Managing Complexity Across the Feed Value Chain

From corn, soybean meal, and premix procurement to finished broiler, layer, ruminant, and aquaculture feed, Xerpium unifies operations.

  • Multi-supplier PO and long-term commodity contracts for corn, soybean meal, premix, rice bran, pollard, CPO, molasses, minerals, vitamins, and additives.
  • Multi-warehouse stock: corn silo, SBM warehouse, premix cold storage, and finished feed warehouse.
  • QC sampling at receiving for moisture, protein, fat, and fiber content.
  • Sales channels: distributors, direct farmers, feed stores, modern trade, and toll manufacturing.

From Formula to Finished Bag with Batch Traceability

Xerpium connects ration formulation, multi-level BOM, and manufacturing orders through grinding, mixing, pelleting, cooling, and bagging work centers.

  • Multi-level BOM: grinding → mixing → pelleting → cooling → bagging.
  • Manufacturing orders from formulations with QC at each stage.
  • Costing: HPP per ton with cost rollup from raw materials, labor, and overhead.
  • Quality Control lab: raw material, in-process, finished product, and Certificate of Analysis per batch with lot traceability.

Commercial and Financial Control for Feed Distribution

Distributor credit, delivery tracking, and feed-specific financial reports keep cash and margins visible.

  • Customer credit: distributor receivables, credit limits, due dates, and aging reports.
  • Delivery: DO, delivery note, and fleet tracking to farmers/distributors.
  • Profit & Loss by feed type (broiler/layer/ruminant/aquaculture), HPP per ton, operating expenses, and gross profit.
  • Tax: PPN 11% and exempt fiscal position for animal feed.
