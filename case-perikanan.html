Perikanan

Kelola tangkapan, proses, dan lacak hasil laut dari kapal ke pelanggan.

Kompleksitas dari Kapal hingga Konsumen

Xerpium menangani spesies, grade, area tangkap, dan rantai dingin dalam satu sistem.

  • Fish Species: nama ilmiah, unique code, dan multi-language untuk traceability.
  • Fishing Trip Management: estimasi tangkapan vs aktual, biaya fuel, ice, logistik, dan profitabilitas per trip.
  • Cold Storage & Cold Chain: threshold suhu, auto-alert, dan log temperatur dari IoT.

Manajemen Trip dan Penerimaan Ikan Terintegrasi

Kelola trip penangkapan, ABK, biaya, dan penerimaan dengan grading otomatis serta harga otomatis.

  • Fishing Trip Management: lifecycle Draft → Departed → Returned → Received → Closed, crew management dengan share percent, cost tracking fuel/ice/logistic/wage/maintenance.
  • Fish Receiving & Grading: flow Draft → Weighing → QC → Approved → Posted; gross/tare/net, temperature log, freshness score, auto-grade rules.
  • Auto Pricing System: pricing rules priority supplier > grade > size > default, date range musiman.

Lot Traceability dan Rantai Dingin End-to-End

Lacak setiap lot dari kapal, penerimaan, pemrosesan, hingga pengiriman dan pelanggan, termasuk integrasi cold chain.

  • Extended Stock Lot: species, vessel, trip, area, grade, temperature; parent-child lots untuk traceability hulu-hilir.
  • Processing Batch: yield management input → output + waste, process types fillet/loin/steak/block/whole frozen/fish meal, auto-create mrp.production.
  • Cold Chain Events: track receiving → storage → transfer → delivery; QR code generation untuk public traceability.

Skalakan Operasi dengan Fitur Enterprise dan Integrasi

Dukungan multi-perusahaan, multi-gudang, multi-mata uang, serta integrasi ke penjualan, pembelian, akuntansi, fleet, HR, quality, dan bea cukai.

  • Enterprise Features: multi warehouse, multi company/branch, multi user, multi currency, consolidation reports.
  • Fishery ↔ Sales/Purchase/Inventory/Accounting/Fleet/HR/Quality/Bea Cukai: auto-create vendor bill, picking auto-post, crew cost accrual, HS code export, INATRADE.
  • AI Dashboard Features: natural language → SQL, chart insight, copilot chat, conditional formatting untuk analisis real-time.
