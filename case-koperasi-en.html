A Cooperative ERP Built for Member Trust and Multi-Business Scale

Cooperatives manage strict member KYC, multi-branch operations, and both financial and commercial units. Xerpium unifies the base, savings, loan, and business modules into a single chart of accounts.

  • Central member registry with NIK, KTP, address, member type, and status
  • Multi-branch support including head office and Jakarta branches
  • Role-based access from member to admin
  • Auto-generated member and transaction sequences like KOP/YYYY/NNNNN

Automated Savings, Lending, and Member Self-Service

Xerpium handles the full simpan pinjam lifecycle with automated accounting and member-facing portal access. Interest posting, amortization, and overdue penalties run on scheduled jobs.

  • Savings: five products with interest rates up to 7.5%, transaction numbering, and withdrawal approval workflow
  • Loans: four flat/reducing products with application workflow, amortization schedules, and guarantor documentation
  • Self-service portal: member dashboard, savings and loan views, online loan application, mobile-ready routes
  • GL integration: deposit, withdrawal, disbursement, and payment entries automatically posted to the single chart of accounts

Integrated Business Units Beyond Simpan Pinjam

Serba usaha modules run on the same ERP, connected through inter-unit transfers and member cross-sell. This supports retail, distribution, logistics, production, agriculture, and service operations without separate systems.

  • Retail POS with multi-payment, product catalog, stock management, membership discounts, promotions, and e-commerce
  • Distribution and logistics: sales order, route planning, fleet management, warehouse operations, cold chain, freight forwarding
  • Production: multi-level BOM, manufacturing orders, work centers, quality control, raw material auto-purchase
  • Agriculture, services, and cleaning: crop/livestock traceability, project management, service scheduling and quality scoring

Consolidated Reporting, SHU, and the Path to Scale

A single chart of accounts and consolidated reporting give management one view of all units. Unified dashboard KPIs and SHU distribution close the loop back to members.

  • Single chart of accounts combines simpan pinjam and operational business
  • Consolidated balance sheet and P&L for all business units
  • SHU distribution from all units to members
  • Dashboard KPIs: total revenue, total purchases, outstanding AR, top members, top suppliers
